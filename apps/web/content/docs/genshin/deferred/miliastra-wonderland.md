# Miliastra Wonderland

Miliastra Wonderland is the game's user-generated mode: players build stages in the Miliastra Sandbox editor on PC, and others play them in lobbies of up to twenty, as Manekins dressed in cosmetics earned or crafted there.

**Why deferred:** what it plays is other players' stages, published to and served from HoYoverse's servers, in lobbies shared with other players. The world keeps no server and has no other players ([co-op](/docs/genshin/deferred/co-op)), and an editor without anyone's stages to play would be a different product rather than the game's mode.

**Revisit when:** co-op is revisited, so a stage has players to share it with and a server to publish it on.
