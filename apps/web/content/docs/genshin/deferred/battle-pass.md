# Battle Pass

The game's Battle Pass runs a period at a time, opened from Adventure Rank 20. Daily, weekly and period missions give its experience, a thousand a level for fifty levels, with a weekly cap on what the daily and weekly missions give. Its free tier, the Sojourner's Battle Pass, gives a reward plan's materials; its paid tier, the Gnostic Hymn, adds more.

**Why deferred:** its periods follow HoYoverse's live version schedule, and its period missions mostly ask for the events active in that period, which the world does not run ([events](/docs/genshin/deferred/events)). The community's dump keeps the periods' dates (`BattlePassScheduleExcelConfigData`) only for periods already announced, so a world replaying them would run out of periods, and its missions would point at events that are not there.

**Revisit when:** the world keeps events of its own, so a period's missions have something to ask for. Only the free tier is revisited then: the Gnostic Hymn is bought with money, and nothing in the world is ([Genshin](/docs/proposals/genshin)).
