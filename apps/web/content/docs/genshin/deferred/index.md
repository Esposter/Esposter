# Deferred

Ideas we chose not to build yet. Check here before proposing.

- [One instanced terrain draw](/docs/genshin/deferred/terrain-instanced-draw) — the visible ground in one draw over a height texture array; waits until the terrain's draws show in a frame.
- [Underwater window](/docs/genshin/deferred/underwater-window) — the sky's window through the surface seen from below, and caustics on every surface; waits on Fontaine.
- [Water screen-space reflections](/docs/genshin/deferred/water-screen-space-reflections) — the shore mirrored in the water; waits on a region whose reference shows it.
- [Rising stone ramp](/docs/genshin/deferred/rising-stone-ramp) — the login stone's solved toon ramp held to rise toward its lit end; waits on the deferred pass's highlight and the normal maps being in the light's solve.
- [Specular ramp](/docs/genshin/deferred/specular-ramp) — a second ramp giving wet stone and metal a hard highlight band; waits on the first region with either.
- [Co-op](/docs/genshin/deferred/co-op) — players sharing one host's world, friends, realm visits and duels between players; waits on an account keeping the world on the server.
- [Events](/docs/genshin/deferred/events) — limited-time events in windows HoYoverse's servers open; waits on an event chosen as permanent content.
- [Battle Pass](/docs/genshin/deferred/battle-pass) — its free tier's periods and missions; waits on the world keeping events, and its paid tier never comes.
- [Miliastra Wonderland](/docs/genshin/deferred/miliastra-wonderland) — other players' stages in shared lobbies; waits on co-op.
