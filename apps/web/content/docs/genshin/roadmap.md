# Roadmap

Open work only, the biggest gap to the game first. What was decided against is in [deferred](/docs/genshin/deferred); read it before adding an item. The order is `genshin:parity rank`'s first table over the login's four frames, re-read each pass, and an item moves when its ceiling does. A stand-in's gap from the game's own exports, `rank`'s second table, never orders the work, since it can narrow pass after pass while the frame stands still ([parity](/docs/genshin/parity)). Each item names the measure that says it is done: the clouds by their statistics (`genshin:parity clouds`, `cover`), the music by `listen`, and everything else by `rank` and `compare` against its reference, checked by eye ([parity](/docs/genshin/parity)).

## Next

- [ ] **Each hour's light, through the game's own deferred pass.** The stone is lit as the pass lights it, its toon ramp and its sky harmonics solved per hour by `genshin:parity calibrate` over the exports' binned pixels, with no god rays, grade or bloom over the frame ([login screen](/docs/genshin/login-screen)): the form explains about half again as much of the light as the old sun and ambient did, and the day's frame fell by four hundredths. `rank`'s light rows barely moved for it, since they price the exports' error pixel by pixel and most of that is texels and carving that do not line up, which no light reaches; the light's own measure is `calibrate`'s binned residual, the frames' FLIP its check. Each hour's haze now thins with height as its frame shows, solved with its light over bins split by height (`calibrate --haze`, [login screen](/docs/genshin/login-screen)). `rank`'s third table prices what is left to a light: corrected to the recording over bins of depth, facing and how far a face turns up, the dawn's frame gains about a hundredth, over the same bins split by height about four, and smoothed over a few pixels a tenth. What is left:
  - [ ] What varies with height past the haze. One falloff left about three hundredths of the dawn's frame to height, and `rank`'s height bands by depth split it in two: the far stone stands darker than a haze hiding all of it draws, at every hour and further off the deeper it lies, which the night's and the dusk's most opacity now hold ([login screen](/docs/genshin/login-screen)); and the stone over the walkway stands about an eighth darker than ours at every depth, a light that dims with height. The dawn's own most opacity scored its frame worse, its cloud sea paling less, so the dawn waits on the cloud sea under the haze. A light from the cloud sea fading with height solved as well as the most opacity over the dawn's bins, but only by emptying the haze, which the cloud sea's pale sheet needs; a light that dims with height over the stone alone, as the reflection pass's clustered probes would cast it, is the next measure.
  - [ ] The cloud sea under the haze. The dawn's and the night's dense haze drown the cloud sea into one pale sheet where the recordings show its billows, since the sea takes the haze as the stone does. Drawn clear of the haze the billows scored both frames about four hundredths worse, so the pale sheet stands nearer the recordings than our billows do: the sea now stands where the game's middle emitter does, 14 metres under the walkway, read off its effect (`Eff_SeaOfCloud_Login`) at the login's `CloudEffect` anchor, and its two side banks stand under 2 metres beneath the walkway some 215 metres to either side, which ours does not draw. Those banks, and each emitter's spread, which its particle system holds without fields, are the next measures.
  - [ ] The reflection pass's ambient: the light probes' clustered ambient and the reflection cube (`Hidden/DeferredReflections`), set at run time from no asset. Like the clamp and the highlight after it, it is a light over depth and facing, which `rank`'s third table caps at about a hundredth of the dawn's frame, so it waits on the height term. The sun's own pass clamps its ramp's facing at none, so a face turned away reads the ramp's middle; clamping ours scored the dawn and the night two hundredths worse, its turned-away towers losing most, since the unclamped ramp's lower half stands in for the light these probes add. Once they are modelled the clamp follows, then the pass's highlight: GGX capped at 12 over a dielectric's specular colour, about a tenth of the lit stone at its peak, with the stone's smoothness fitted from its mask without `_GlossMapScale`, which the stone program never reads, and the rim glow's pixels, which the pass lights as shading model 13. The day's and the dusk's ramps fall toward their lit ends, and holding them to rise scored the dusk worse ([rising stone ramp](/docs/genshin/deferred/rising-stone-ramp)), so what the falling ramp stands in for is looked for here and in the normal maps first.
  - [ ] The dusk sun's direction, by its shadows' edges. The light's binned residual cannot read it, since the sky's harmonics take up whatever the ramp's facing term would ([rising stone ramp](/docs/genshin/deferred/rising-stone-ramp)). A tower's shadow lies over the wings before the door where the recording's are lit, so the shadows' edges against the recording's are the measure left.
  - [ ] The phone's door frame scores worse under the PC client's day light, 0.453 to 0.478, the phone build lighting its door apart; a PC frame of the door by day settles which light the door takes.
- [ ] **Each hour's sky, solved under its clouds.** The clear sky is the next term, a fifth to three tenths of each frame's FLIP. `genshin:parity sky` reads only the pixels the cloud mask finds clear, the mask split by Otsu's threshold over the clear sky fitted under the clouds (`fitClearSky`), and every hour's sky ships from it, the night's with the glow round its moon; drawn through the tone mapping alone, the dawn's stands about five hundredths off its frame's clear sky. What is left:
  - [ ] The dusk's sky under its clouds: low toward the sun the recording is all cloud and glow, so no clear pixel holds the bottom colour there, and it solves to a red no sky shows. Over its upper sky the solve fits two thirds of the clear pixels to 0.04 but all of them to 0.10, the trimmed third the recording's thin bright cloud and gold glow through it, which no shape of the sky draws and its sun's place does not move: thin cloud over the clear sky is the next measure, then a frame that shows the sky under it.
  - [ ] The cover at the horizon and overhead: every hour's shares and the bands' heights are solved with our clouds read where the renderer draws them (`genshin:parity cover --heights`), which scored the dawn five hundredths better and the door recording three. Within 3 degrees of the horizon our sky still draws well under the recordings' cover (the dawn's a half against nine tenths, the day's none against a half), where their haze reads as cloud, and above 25 degrees no hour but the dusk draws any cloud where the recordings show a tenth: the haze at the horizon and a band reaching overhead are the next measures. Their colours solved by spread (`genshin:parity clouds`) scored the door recording worse, as they did the day: that match is blind to place, so brighter clouds pay where the recording has none, and it still reads our clouds by our own split, as the cover solve no longer does.
- [ ] **Where the parts stand.** A part a pixel or two off costs each frame about four thousandths of its FLIP: `rank` charges an outline only the error past what its two sides carry anyway, which was nearly all of the seventh to a fifth it once priced there. A part standing a dozen pixels off or more is charged to the light rows over its area instead, and some are: the dawn's left tower stands wide into the sky the recording shows, and the dusk's middle towers each stand a few to fifteen pixels off, in no one direction, so it is each tower's place and not the row's. `place` on edges runs a row metres up, and a mask of the reference's sky by colour and grain reads its cloud sea as hazy stone, so the measure is still to be found, one that holds the upper sky's outline of each tower against ours.
- [ ] **The clouds' kind.** The reference's clouds hold up to a sixth of a frame's FLIP, and being scattered at random they are priced at their colours' share alone. Every hour's cover is solved on its frame (`genshin:parity cover`), our clouds read where the renderer draws them against our sky drawn with none, so their colour no longer decides how many there are.
  - [ ] The clouds' brightness: every hour's stand dimmer over their sky than the recordings' (the night's under half as bright, the day's a sixth under) and softer edged. The day's and the dusk's colours solved by spread (`genshin:parity clouds`) ship; the dawn's and the night's solve to shades far darker and score their frames worse, so their brightness over the sky is the measure to match first.
  - [ ] The day's horizon: the recording's sky is cloud within 8 degrees of it, ours about three fifths, its haze washing our low clouds into the clear sky.
  - [ ] A cloud's inside: the recordings paint a lit crown grading into a pale shade, with cirrus wisps between; ours fill with two flat colours.

- [ ] **The login's music, by its score.** The login plays its music ([music](/docs/genshin/music)): its notes agree with the game's on most frames, closer than the game a quarter second off itself, but its octave bands sit about five to seven decibels off, as far as the game somewhere under two seconds out of step with itself: the notes and the swells are right and the sound is not. The first piece's lowest band is overfilled by about six decibels by the noise, which every sounding note plays in step from one buffer. Each note's own start in the loop, on a whole sample frame, fixes the read-back (`genshin:parity noise`) but leaves the second piece's top bands short. The larger gap is the sound itself, so the notes are to play from recordings of real instruments ([sampled instruments](/docs/proposals/genshin/sampled-instruments)), which retires the noise and its read-back with the synthesizer. The fourth pass scored each mix of pitch-keeping instruments under an expression of its own, against the synthesizer under its shipped one: the second piece held its pitch two tenths of a decibel behind, and the first fell behind on both. What the recordings lack is spectrum, which a fixed equaliser closed on the second piece alone, and the first piece's pitch lies past every mix of them, by level, layering or an equaliser on each voice, so what plays that piece is the next choice. The synthesizer has moved since: its notes died about twice as fast as the game's, heard as scattered single notes, and the decay refitted to the notes' median curve rings them as the game's ring. The second piece lost some pitch agreement for it, all through its top voice, whose notes are mostly overtones of lower ones a transcription heard as notes, so telling those from a doubled melody is the synthesizer's next unknown.

## Later

- [ ] **The stand-ins' surfaces, once the frame ranks them.** Each stand-in's own row in `rank`'s first table reads under a hundredth on every frame: the exports drawn in place of our towers, walkway or door recover next to nothing while the light, the sky and the placement above them stand where they are. Their gaps from the exports (the second table) are real, and are what picks each one's representation once the frame ranks it again.
  - [ ] **The login towers' carving, as the game carves it.** The towers carry their traced surfaces ([login screen](/docs/genshin/login-screen)): each band's tone, the paint, the recesses, the gilding and the open colonnades. What a lathe still cannot show:
    - [ ] The lantern tower's crown stands as an open colonnade of columns and arches over an inner wall; ours is a solid drum painted with them.
    - [ ] Relief that tilts the light along a recess's edge: neither three's bump map over the atlas nor over a blurred height canvas of the layers' depths moved the towers against the exports, and the traced colours score best drawn a quarter as far from the stone, so the relief the game lights is its geometry's: carved geometry of our own, the crown's colonnade first.
    - [ ] The stone's grain: the exports' diffuse noise reads as a fine texture on every face, ours as flat paint. A procedural grain of our own, fitted to its scale and contrast.
    - [ ] Gilding as metal: drawn as metal it read dark at every hour, since nothing reflects the sky into it. It waits on an environment map of the login's sky.
  - [ ] **The walkway's surface.** Its carving is drawn as relief from the game's own normal map ([login screen](/docs/genshin/login-screen)). What is left: its middle lane's own paler stone, where ours is one stone throughout; its rims' width, drawn over two centimetres where the game's are one, since the paving's canvas resolves no finer; the navy of a rim turned from the sun, which is the deferred pass's ramp and sky light; and the dais's wings, which the recording shows lit where a shadow lies over ours.
  - [ ] **The door's surface at rest.** On the phone's door frame the door is the largest stand-in gap against its exports: its panel's relief is painted, at three fifths of its read contrast where painting scores best over both door frames together and this frame alone 0.005 worse, and the game's is carved and lit.
- [ ] **The health notice's face.** Signika's letters are proportioned apart from the game's, which is its whole remaining gap; no size or spacing moved it. An open face nearer the game's, measured as Signika was chosen.
- [ ] **A current mainland recording.** Mainland China's title splash and age rating are judged on an older build's launch, drawn on full white where the current PC client's white is a step under it; a current recording settles both.
- [ ] **The day frame's letterbox.** Its recording masks its top and bottom rows, read as a mask over a full frame; a recording of the day at its full height confirms that against a window of that shape.
