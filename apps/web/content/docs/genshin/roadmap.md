# Roadmap

Open work only, the biggest gap to the game first. What was decided against is in [deferred](/docs/genshin/deferred); read it before adding an item. The order is `genshin:parity rank`'s first table over the login's four frames, re-read each pass: each term's ceiling, the most of the frame's FLIP it could recover, and an item moves when its ceiling does. A stand-in's gap from the game's own exports (`rank`'s second table) picks its representation once the first table ranks it and orders nothing, since it can narrow pass after pass while the frame stands still. Each item names the measure that says it is done: the clouds by their statistics (`genshin:parity clouds`, `cover`), the music by `listen`, and everything else by `rank` and `compare` against its reference, checked by eye ([parity](/docs/genshin/parity)).

## Next

- [ ] **Each hour's light, through the game's own deferred pass.** The stone is lit as the pass lights it, its toon ramp and its sky harmonics solved per hour by `genshin:parity calibrate` over the exports' binned pixels, with no god rays, grade or bloom over the frame ([login screen](/docs/genshin/login-screen)): the form explains about half again as much of the light as the old sun and ambient did, and the day's frame fell by four hundredths. `rank`'s light rows barely moved for it, since they price the exports' error pixel by pixel and most of that is texels and carving that do not line up, which no light reaches; the light's own measure is `calibrate`'s binned residual, the frames' FLIP its check. What is left:
  - [ ] The sun's direction at the day and the dusk. With the ramp's dark end at none, both hours' ramps fall toward the lit end, faces turned to the sun standing darker than faces turned away: their directions were set by hand off the faces they light, the day's sun off its frame and the dusk's glow apart from its light, and a ramp held to rise is what tells the right direction, solved with the light.
  - [ ] The pass's highlight and reflection: a GGX highlight of roughness squared smoothness, capped at 12, and the reflection cube on upward smooth faces, which the stone's smoothness and specular colour wait on, and the rim glow's pixels, which the pass lights as shading model 13.
  - [ ] The normal maps: the witness's G-buffer writes the meshes' own normals, so a solve read back from our render returns its light to within about a ninth; the exports' normal maps in the normal target close that.
  - [ ] The phone's door frame scores worse under the PC client's day light, 0.453 to 0.478, the phone build lighting its door apart; a PC frame of the door by day settles which light the door takes.
- [ ] **Each hour's sky, solved under its clouds.** The clear sky is the next term, a fifth to three tenths of each frame's FLIP. `genshin:parity sky` reads only the pixels the cloud mask finds clear, the mask split by Otsu's threshold over the clear sky fitted under the clouds (`fitClearSky`), and every hour's sky ships from it, the night's with the glow round its moon; drawn through the tone mapping alone, the dawn's stands about five hundredths off its frame's clear sky. What is left:
  - [ ] The dusk's sky under its clouds: low toward the sun the recording is all cloud and glow, so no clear pixel holds the bottom colour there, and it solves to a red no sky shows. It waits on a frame that shows that sky, or on the cover that hides it as the recording's does.
  - [ ] The dusk's cover: `genshin:parity cover --heights` solves the heights each band stands between on every hour's frame at once, and the dusk ships them with its shares, which score the door recording better. Its residual stays near three tenths, from 8 to 15 degrees about a seventh against seven tenths: at the recording's split our clouds stand too little over their sky to count, and the bands' distances are not yet solved. Their colours solved by spread (`genshin:parity clouds`) scored the door recording worse, as they did the day: that match is blind to place, so brighter clouds pay where the recording has none. Where each band's clouds stand comes before their colour.
- [ ] **Where the parts stand.** The silhouettes' placement and pose is a seventh to a fifth of every frame's FLIP. `place` on edges runs a row metres up, so the row's offset and each tower's place need a measure on the sky's outline against each part's instead.
- [ ] **The clouds' kind.** The reference's clouds hold up to a sixth of a frame's FLIP, and being scattered at random they are priced at their colours' share alone. The dawn's and the dusk's cover is solved on their frames (`genshin:parity cover`); the day's and the night's waits on their clouds' kind, since more of them scored those frames worse.
  - [ ] The day's clouds read grey where the recording's are white, about twice their clear sky's brightness against two and a half, and the colour solve (`genshin:parity clouds`) scored the day worse; the night's read dark against the recording's pale ones. Their colours, then their cover.
  - [ ] The day's horizon: the recording's sky is cloud within 8 degrees of it, ours about three fifths, its haze washing our low clouds into the clear sky; the dawn's overhead holds twice the recording's cover however few top clouds it draws, the cloud sea's nearest billows standing up past 25 degrees.
  - [ ] A cloud's inside: the recordings paint a lit crown grading into a pale shade, with cirrus wisps between; ours fill with two flat colours.

- [ ] **The login's music, by its score.** The login plays its music ([music](/docs/genshin/music)): its notes agree with the game's on most frames, closer than the game a quarter second off itself, but its octave bands sit about five to seven decibels off, as far as the game somewhere under two seconds out of step with itself: the notes and the swells are right and the sound is not. The first piece's lowest band is overfilled by about six decibels by the noise, which every sounding note plays in step from one buffer. Each note's own start in the loop, on a whole sample frame, fixes the read-back (`genshin:parity noise`) but leaves the second piece's top bands short. The larger gap is the sound itself, so the notes are to play from recordings of real instruments ([sampled instruments](/docs/proposals/genshin/sampled-instruments)), which retires the noise and its read-back with the synthesizer. The fourth pass scored each mix of pitch-keeping instruments under an expression of its own, against the synthesizer under its shipped one: the second piece held its pitch two tenths of a decibel behind, and the first fell behind on both. What the recordings lack is spectrum, which a fixed equaliser closed on the second piece alone, and the first piece's pitch lies past every mix of them, by level, layering or an equaliser on each voice, so what plays that piece is the next choice. The synthesizer has moved since: its notes died about twice as fast as the game's, heard as scattered single notes, and the decay refitted to the notes' median curve rings them as the game's ring. The second piece lost some pitch agreement for it, all through its top voice, whose notes are mostly overtones of lower ones a transcription heard as notes, so telling those from a doubled melody is the synthesizer's next unknown.

## Later

- [ ] **The stand-ins' surfaces, once the frame ranks them.** Each stand-in's own row in `rank`'s first table reads under a hundredth on every frame: the exports drawn in place of our towers, walkway or door recover next to nothing while the light, the sky and the placement above them stand where they are. Their gaps from the exports (the second table) are real, and are what picks each one's representation once the frame ranks it again.
  - [ ] **The login towers' carving, as the game carves it.** The towers carry their traced surfaces ([login screen](/docs/genshin/login-screen)): each band's tone, the paint, the recesses, the gilding and the open colonnades. What a lathe still cannot show:
    - [ ] The lantern tower's crown stands as an open colonnade of columns and arches over an inner wall; ours is a solid drum painted with them.
    - [ ] Relief that tilts the light along a recess's edge: neither three's bump map over the atlas nor over a blurred height canvas of the layers' depths moved the towers against the exports, and the traced colours score best drawn a quarter as far from the stone, so the relief the game lights is its geometry's: carved geometry of our own, the crown's colonnade first.
    - [ ] The stone's grain: the exports' diffuse noise reads as a fine texture on every face, ours as flat paint. A procedural grain of our own, fitted to its scale and contrast.
    - [ ] Gilding as metal: drawn as metal it read dark at every hour, since nothing reflects the sky into it. It waits on an environment map of the login's sky.
  - [ ] **The walkway's surface.** Its carving is drawn as relief from the game's own normal map ([login screen](/docs/genshin/login-screen)). What is left: its middle lane's own paler stone, where ours is one stone throughout; its rims' width, drawn over two centimetres where the game's are one, since the paving's canvas resolves no finer; the navy of a rim turned from the sun, which is the deferred pass's ramp and sky light; and the dais's wings, which the recording shows lit where a shadow lies over ours.
  - [ ] **The door's surface at rest.** On the phone's door frame the door is the largest stand-in gap against its exports: its panel's relief is painted, at three fifths of its read contrast where painting scores best over both door frames together and this frame alone 0.005 worse, and the game's is carved and lit.
- [ ] **The health notice's face.** Signika's letters are proportioned apart from the game's, which is its whole remaining gap; no size or spacing moved it. An open face nearer the game's, measured as Signika was chosen.
- [ ] **A current mainland recording.** Mainland China's title splash and age rating are judged on an older build's launch, drawn on full white where the current PC client's white is a step under it; a current recording settles both.
- [ ] **The day frame's letterbox.** Its recording masks its top and bottom rows, read as a mask over a full frame; a recording of the day at its full height confirms that against a window of that shape.
