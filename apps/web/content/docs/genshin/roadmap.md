# Roadmap

Open work only, part by part of the game. What was decided against is in [deferred](/docs/genshin/deferred); read it before adding an item. Each part's items run in the [recreation passes](/docs/proposals/genshin/recreation-passes)' order, each pass answering one kind of unknown from its most exact source, judged by its own measure and frozen once it holds: an item waits for every earlier pass however large its own gap, since a later pass's solve absorbs an earlier one's error. Inside a pass, `genshin:parity rank`'s first table orders the items over the part's own frames, re-read each pass: the opening's over the login's four frames, the world's over Windrise's once it has references, since a rank prices one scene and never orders two against each other. Each item names the measure that says it is done: a placement, a shape or a surface by its gap from the game's own exports, the clouds by their statistics (`genshin:parity clouds`, `cover`), the music by `listen`, a sound by its bands against the game's own, and the frame by `compare` against its reference once every pass before it holds, checked by eye ([parity](/docs/genshin/parity)).

The towers' row stands where the blocks lay it, its 5 metres under the walkway the walkway's own lift, which `genshin:parity passes` holds; these frames are older builds', so each is checked again on a current build's frame of its hour.

- [ ] **Display: the game's own transform from scene colour to the screen.** The uber pass's tone curve is drawn in place of the neutral one, its contrast held by `passes` on the current build's recording and each hour's stone light solved again under it ([rendering style](/docs/genshin/rendering-style)). - [ ] **Light: each hour's, through the game's own deferred pass.** The stone is lit as the pass lights it, its toon ramp and its sky harmonics solved per hour by `genshin:parity calibrate` over the exports' binned pixels, with no god rays, grade or bloom over the frame ([login screen](/docs/genshin/login-screen)): the form explains about half again as much of the light as the old sun and ambient did, and the day's frame fell by four hundredths. `rank`'s light rows barely moved for it, since they price the exports' error pixel by pixel and most of that is texels and carving that do not line up, which no light reaches; the light's own measure is `calibrate`'s binned residual, the frames' FLIP its check. Each hour's haze now thins with height as its frame shows, solved with its light over bins split by height (`calibrate --haze`, [login screen](/docs/genshin/login-screen)). `rank`'s third table prices what is left to a light: corrected to the recording over bins of depth, facing and how far a face turns up, the dawn's frame gains about a hundredth, over the same bins split by height about four, and smoothed over a few pixels a tenth. Screen-space occlusion now darkens the creases of the day's, the dusk's and the night's frames ([login screen](/docs/genshin/login-screen)), the first of what varies inside a part. The light is now held to lights that can be, none below none, by non-negative least squares ([parity](/docs/genshin/parity)): solved free, its lights cancelled one another and turned the night's towers to a rainbow up their height. Held so, the dawn scores level, the day, the phone's door frame and the dusk about five thousandths worse and the night nine, since the free solve's negative light was cancelling a haze too bright over the far stone. The stone now hazes in colours of its own, solved with its light, and every hour's frame scores better than under the free light ([login screen](/docs/genshin/login-screen)); and a light varying across the scene from side to side or with depth explains a hundredth to two of the residual, too little to build. What is left:
  - [ ] The light solved apart from the haze. `calibrate` solves each hour's haze profile and the stone's haze colours together with its light, so either can take up the other's error, as the free light once cancelled a haze too bright over the far stone. The light is solved and frozen on its own measure first, over bins the haze barely reaches, and the haze is then solved under it in the atmosphere pass, below.
  - [ ] What varies with height past the haze. One falloff left about three hundredths of the dawn's frame to height, and `rank`'s height bands by depth split it in two: the far stone stands darker than a haze hiding all of it draws, at every hour and further off the deeper it lies, which the night's and the dusk's most opacity now hold ([login screen](/docs/genshin/login-screen)); and the stone over the walkway stands about an eighth darker than ours at every depth, which a light fading with height over tens of metres, as the reflection pass's clustered probes would cast it, now holds at every hour ([login screen](/docs/genshin/login-screen)). The dawn's own most opacity scored its frame worse, its cloud sea paling less, so the dawn waits on the cloud sea under the haze. A light from the cloud sea fading fast with height solved as well as the most opacity over the dawn's bins, but only by emptying the haze, which the cloud sea's pale sheet needs. The light fading with height solves to a different colour each hour, blue at night with its red below none and red at dusk with its blue below none, so it carries more than the probes' light, and what else it stands in for is the next measure. By day the stone 20 metres up and 40 to 80 out reads over twice as bright as the recording's where every other cell stands within a fifth, and `rank`'s light map finds it on the lantern tower's crown and the two wide towers top left, standing where the recording shows sky and one thin tower: the day's is a placement, below, not a haze.
  - [ ] The reflection pass's ambient: the light probes' clustered ambient and the reflection cube (`Hidden/DeferredReflections`), set at run time from no asset. Like the clamp and the highlight after it, it is a light over depth and facing, which `rank`'s third table caps at about a hundredth of the dawn's frame; the light fading with height stands in for its rise up the towers. The sun's own pass clamps its ramp's facing at none, so a face turned away reads the ramp's middle; clamping ours scored the dawn and the night two hundredths worse, its turned-away towers losing most, since the unclamped ramp's lower half stands in for the light these probes add. Once they are modelled the clamp follows, then the pass's highlight: GGX capped at 12 over a dielectric's specular colour, about a tenth of the lit stone at its peak, with the stone's smoothness fitted from its mask without `_GlossMapScale`, which the stone program never reads, and the rim glow's pixels, which the pass lights as shading model 13.   - [ ] The dusk sun's direction, by its shadows' edges. The light's binned residual cannot read it, since the sky's harmonics take up whatever the ramp's facing term would ([rising stone ramp](/docs/genshin/deferred/rising-stone-ramp)).   - [ ] The phone's door frame scores worse under the PC client's day light, 0.453 to 0.478, the phone build lighting its door apart. - [ ] **Atmosphere: the haze and the cloud sea.** Each hour's haze is solved with its light by height and depth today ([login screen](/docs/genshin/login-screen)); once the light is frozen on its own, the haze is solved again under it by its own measure, by depth and height, and then the sea under it.
  - [ ] The cloud sea under the haze. The dawn's and the night's dense haze drown the cloud sea into one pale sheet where the recordings show its billows, since the sea takes the haze as the stone does. Drawn clear of the haze the billows scored both frames about four hundredths worse, so the pale sheet stands nearer the recordings than our billows do: the sea now stands where the game's middle emitter does, 14 metres under the walkway, read off its effect (`Eff_SeaOfCloud_Login`) at the login's `CloudEffect` anchor, and its two side banks stand under 2 metres beneath the walkway some 215 metres to either side, which ours does not draw. Those banks, and each emitter's spread, which its particle system holds without fields, are the next measures.
- [ ] **Atmosphere: each hour's sky, solved under its clouds.** The clear sky is the next term, a fifth to three tenths of each frame's FLIP. `genshin:parity sky` reads only the pixels the cloud mask finds clear, the mask split by Otsu's threshold over the clear sky fitted under the clouds (`fitClearSky`), and every hour's sky ships from it, the night's with the glow round its moon; drawn through the tone mapping alone, the dawn's stands about five hundredths off its frame's clear sky. The game draws a cloud layer over its sky that the scene does not: a dome under `Enviro_Cloud_Layer_Mat`, its Voronoi density scrolled and bent by curl, lit by a normal map between its light and dark colours, with cirrus wisps over it, its coverage, opacity and colours set at run time. The dusk's clear sky already stands on its frame's mean, so what its top third still holds, the largest term a light reaches on the door recording, is that layer's thin cloud and wisps; the layer, ported with textures of our own and its settings solved by hour, comes before each item below. What is left:
  - [ ] The dusk's sky under its clouds: low toward the sun the recording is all cloud and glow, so no clear pixel holds the bottom colour there, and it solves to a red no sky shows. Over its upper sky the solve fits two thirds of the clear pixels to 0.04 but all of them to 0.10, the trimmed third the recording's thin bright cloud and gold glow through it, which no shape of the sky draws and its sun's place does not move: thin cloud over the clear sky is the next measure, then a frame that shows the sky under it.
  - [ ] The cover at the horizon and overhead: every hour's shares and the bands' heights are solved with our clouds read where the renderer draws them (`genshin:parity cover --heights`), which scored the dawn five hundredths better and the door recording three. Within 3 degrees of the horizon our sky still draws well under the recordings' cover (the dawn's a half against nine tenths, the day's none against a half), where their haze reads as cloud, and above 25 degrees no hour but the dusk draws any cloud where the recordings show a tenth: the haze at the horizon and a band reaching overhead are the next measures. Their colours solved by spread (`genshin:parity clouds`) scored the door recording worse, as they did the day: that match is blind to place, so brighter clouds pay where the recording has none, and it still reads our clouds by our own split, as the cover solve no longer does.
- [ ] **Atmosphere: the clouds' kind.** The reference's clouds hold up to a sixth of a frame's FLIP, and being scattered at random they are priced at their colours' share alone. Every hour's cover is solved on its frame (`genshin:parity cover`), our clouds read where the renderer draws them against our sky drawn with none, so their colour no longer decides how many there are.
  - [ ] The clouds' brightness: every hour's stand dimmer over their sky than the recordings' (the night's under half as bright, the day's a sixth under) and softer edged. The day's and the dusk's colours solved by spread (`genshin:parity clouds`) ship; the dawn's and the night's solve to shades far darker and score their frames worse, so their brightness over the sky is the measure to match first.
  - [ ] The day's horizon: the recording's sky is cloud within 8 degrees of it, ours about three fifths, its haze washing our low clouds into the clear sky.
  - [ ] A cloud's inside: the recordings paint a lit crown grading into a pale shade, with cirrus wisps between; ours fill with two flat colours.

- [ ] **Audio: the login's music, by its score.** The login plays its music ([music](/docs/genshin/music)): its notes agree with the game's on most frames, closer than the game a quarter second off itself, but its octave bands sit about five to seven decibels off, as far as the game somewhere under two seconds out of step with itself: the notes and the swells are right and the sound is not. The first piece's lowest band is overfilled by about six decibels by the noise, which every sounding note plays in step from one buffer. Each note's own start in the loop, on a whole sample frame, fixes the read-back (`genshin:parity noise`) but leaves the second piece's top bands short. The larger gap is the sound itself. Layered over the synthesizer, recordings of real instruments have taken the second piece from 6.5 dB to 5.6 dB ([sampled instruments](/docs/genshin/sampled-instruments)), while the first ties the synthesizer under every layering tried, so a fixed equaliser over each layered mix, scored held out across time, or another openly licensed library read through the same pitch ceiling, is what that piece tries next. The synthesizer has moved since: its notes died about twice as fast as the game's, heard as scattered single notes, and the decay refitted to the notes' median curve rings them as the game's ring. The second piece lost some pitch agreement for it, all through its top voice, whose notes are mostly overtones of lower ones a transcription heard as notes, so telling those from a doubled melody is the synthesizer's next unknown.

- [ ] **Audio: every sound of the login, matched completely.** The door now sounds as it opens ([sound effects](/docs/genshin/sound-effects)); the clicks on the screen's buttons, the door's sound as it assembles itself and the wind are left, each found among the game's own sounds by matching the recording that plays it, fitted by its octave bands and started when the game starts it, as the door's opening is.
