# Comments on published resources

Comments or reactions from viewers on a published resource's public `/view/[type]/[id]` page — feedback on a dashboard, discussion under a webpage.

## Why deferred

Public pages are deliberately anonymous and rate-limited; comments need identity (or open the abuse box wide), storage, owner moderation tools, and notification of new comments — a social subsystem esbabbler already is. Bolting a second discussion system onto view pages duplicates it.

## Revisit when

The [public discover feed](/docs/platform/deferred/public-discover-feed) materializes — a public audience surface is when on-page discussion makes sense, and by then the esbabbler machinery (auth, moderation, notifications) should be reused, not reinvented.

## Cheaper interim

Share the link into an esbabbler room and let the discussion happen there, next to the people who care.
