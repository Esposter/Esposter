# Voxel map

The proposal put a minimap in the corner of the voxel world, turned with the camera, and a full map over the world on M. Both were drawn from the terrain seed in the chunk worker a column at a time, so the player could find the room again once the open world had taken them away from it.

**Why not:** The chunk worker, the seed and the room are gone with the voxel world. The console's world is the [Genshin](/docs/proposals/genshin) recreation, whose map is drawn from its region catalogue: the [world map](/docs/genshin/world-map) sets the coordinates and landmarks, and [exploring](/docs/proposals/genshin/exploring) draws the overlay on M the way the game does.
