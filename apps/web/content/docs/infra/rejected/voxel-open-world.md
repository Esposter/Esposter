# Voxel open world

The console's voxel room stood in terrain generated from a seed: rolling grass over dirt over stone, one kind everywhere. The proposal gave that ground climate-chosen biomes (forest, river, lake and snow) in a realm of the repository's own invention, and set the console's views, such as the collector harbour, down in it as places the player walked to.

**Why not:** The voxel world is gone. The console's world is the [Genshin](/docs/proposals/genshin) recreation, whose geography is the game's own continent, read off the official map rather than invented. Its [terrain](/docs/genshin/terrain) and [world map](/docs/genshin/world-map) pages hold everything this page wanted from biomes, at the continent's scale.
