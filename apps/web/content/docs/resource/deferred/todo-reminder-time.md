# Todo Reminder Time

Microsoft To Do keeps two times on a task: **Add due date** picks the day it is due, and **Remind me** picks, separately, "later today, tomorrow, next week, or a date and time you pick" ([Add due dates and reminders](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/add-due-dates-and-reminders-in-microsoft-to-do-064d9696-08d1-4433-bfdd-f661dc97491f)). A reminder the evening before a morning deadline is the case it serves.

## Why deferred

A TodoList due date carries a time of day, and [due reminders](/docs/resource/todolist-due-reminders) push when it arrives, so one field already answers both "when is it due" and "when am I told". A second time is a second field on every todo, a second scheduled message per save, and a second thing the Calendar blade has to choose between. The lean list keeps one.

## Revisit when

A reader asks to be reminded ahead of a due time — the reminder then becomes an offset before the due date rather than a second free time, so it stays one decision.
