# Genshin Engine

The design, from each module's job to the order a frame runs in, is `openspec/specs/genshin/engine-architecture/design.md`; this skill is the rules a change to the engine or its world follows.

## Settled — do not re-propose

- **A rigid-body physics engine** (Rapier, cannon) for collision. Movement is kinematic, so collision is a query service the controller asks; a solver fights the feel (`openspec/specs/genshin/character-controller/design.md`, "A kinematic capsule on queries, no physics engine").
- **An `ignorePatterns` entry for the engine's TSL.** The fluid simulator's file stays the only exclusion (`.agents/skills/oxlint/references/ignore-patterns.md`).
- **Shipping assets taken from the game** — models, textures, sounds or art, extracted or converted. Every mesh, texture and shader a build draws is generated by our own code, re-derived from the game's exports as closely as they allow (`openspec/specs/genshin/scene-derivation/design.md`); the developer's own exports are drawn under `nuxt dev` alone, through a base URL only the development server hands the world (`openspec/specs/genshin/derived-assets/development-exports/design.md`).
- **Hosting HoYoverse's official MMD packs** — on Blob Storage, a CDN or any public address, however content-addressed or credited. Every pack's terms say 「请勿二次配布」, do not redistribute (`openspec/rejected/genshin/hosting-official-character-packs/design.md`).
- **The word "teyvat" in any name** — package, folder, route, page or prose. The world is implicit in "Genshin".
- **A second renderer path for WebGL.** `WebGPURenderer` falls back to WebGL 2 on its own.

## Rules

- **A world system lives in its own composable; the screen only composes them.** Each system of `WorldScreen` is one `use<System>` composable under `composables/`, and a cross-system reaction goes through `WorldEventMap` on the screen's one `WorldEvents`, never a call into another system's state (`openspec/specs/genshin/engine-architecture/design.md`).
- **The engine has no Vue, no store and no DOM beyond a canvas, and no place.** `genshin-engine` is the renderer and the generators, and knows no region by name. `genshin-world` is the game's world on it: the catalogue, the regions' data, and the TresJS layer — components and composables such as `usePostPipeline` — that creates the engine's modules. The world's screen (`WorldScreen`) owns its canvas; the app keeps only the opening (`components/Genshin/Index.vue`), its stores, and the bundler's seams, which the world takes as props: the terrain worker's factory, the URL region data is fetched from, and the tuning panel's visibility.
- **A generator is pure and seeded, and returns typed arrays** — `references/generators.md`.
- **Nothing allocates per vertex or per frame** — `references/generators.md`.
- **Every generator and per-frame selection is benched at two scales or more** — `references/generators.md`.
- **Light is shared uniforms, and the sky writes them** — `references/lighting.md`.
- **Environment surfaces use `createToonMaterial` over the world's one ramp texture** — `references/lighting.md`.
- **A character is its official MMD pack, read at run time by the engine's own PMX reader** — `references/characters.md`.
- **The frame is drawn by the engine's post pipeline** — `references/post-pipeline.md`.
- **A mask the post chain reads is drawn from stand-ins, never from the world's materials** — `references/post-pipeline.md`.
- **One outline material serves every toon material, and the view compiles before it shows** — `references/post-pipeline.md`.
- **An instanced mesh is `createInstancedMesh`, never three's `InstancedMesh` constructed by hand** — `references/materials.md`.
- **A material is made once per kind, not per landmark** — `references/materials.md`.
- **Ground is not outlined** — `references/post-pipeline.md`.
- **The sun casts through cascades; the god rays read their own sun** — `references/lighting.md`.
- **A look value is tuned in development's panel, then copied into the region's constants.** `useGenshinTuning` writes uniforms and regenerates textures live and saves nothing.
- **The ground streams; nothing places a heightfield by hand** — `references/terrain-and-vegetation.md`.
- **There is one wind** — `references/terrain-and-vegetation.md`.
- **Ground is painted by its layers, placed where its terrain places them** — `references/terrain-and-vegetation.md`.
- **A change of detail cross-fades by dither, never pops** — `references/materials.md`.
- **Weather is written after the sky and before the water** — `references/lighting.md`.
- **One input, read once a frame, bound in one map** — `references/controls-and-screens.md`.
- **What moves the camera runs ahead of the floating origin by priority, and the body moves in the world's own coordinates** — `references/controls-and-screens.md`.
- **The engine depends on three alone, so it dispatches over an enum by an `if` chain or a map, never a `switch`**: lint's `default-case` wants a default, and the engine has no `exhaustiveGuard` to give one.
- **A screen over the world is a `ScreenKind`** — `references/controls-and-screens.md`.
- **A game rule that moves over time is a deterministic step in the world** — `references/game-rules.md`.
- **Combat is one set of rules every feature calls into** — `references/game-rules.md`.
- **Region data and kits are the world package's, and a kit is its hosted record joined to one behaviour module**, loaded with its character from a derived map, as `references/regions-and-kits.md` gives.
- **Game data is published, never imported.** A table, a word, a fitted scene or an authored world file is read by its key from the hosted game data, awaited at a boundary and handed down to the functions over it (the `genshin-data` skill).
- **The world package is published and builds through the Vue tsdown configuration** — `references/world-package.md`.
- **A system that holds player state adds its slice to the save schema in the same change.** The save is `genshinSaveSchema` in `genshin-world`: one key per system, each slice's Zod schema beside its model, storing only what the player did as ids and counters, with the rules deriving everything else on load. Nothing in a slice is a derived value, and a change to a slice's shape is a backfill (`openspec/specs/genshin/save-data/design.md`).
- **One function per file, `#src/*` imports, explicit return types** — the package is published with isolated declarations, so every exported function states its type.

## Reference pages

- `references/regions-and-kits.md` — when adding a region, its data or landmarks, a character's kit, or a building's kit.
- `references/generators.md` — when writing a seeded generator, a per-vertex or per-frame loop, or the bench that proves its cost.
- `references/lighting.md` — when a material reads light, the sky or the weather writes its colours, or a sun shadow or the god rays are drawn.
- `references/characters.md` — when a character's model, its MMD pack, or its materials and outline are in question.
- `references/post-pipeline.md` — when a post pass, the outline, a mask for the post chain, or the view's compile before it shows is in question.
- `references/materials.md` — when a mesh is instanced, a material is made for a kind of thing, or a change of detail cross-fades.
- `references/terrain-and-vegetation.md` — when a region's ground streams or is painted, or anything moves in the wind.
- `references/controls-and-screens.md` — when input is read, the camera moves with the floating origin, or a screen opens over the world.
- `references/game-rules.md` — when a rule moves over time, an enemy's AI steps, or a hit is priced.
- `references/world-package.md` — when the world package's build, or the Tres tags its templates compile to, is in question.
