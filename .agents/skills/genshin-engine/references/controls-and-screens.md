# Controls, camera and screens

Read when input is read, the camera moves with the floating origin, or a screen opens over the world.

- **One input, read once a frame, bound in one map.** The world screen owns the one `createInput` and reads it on the canvas's `beforeLoop`, ahead of every frame callback, handing the state down as `inputState`; a key, mouse button or pad button is an `InputAction` chord in `InputActionBindingMap`, never a listener of a component's own (`openspec/specs/genshin/controls/design.md`). The touch controls are the one component listening to pointers, and they move nothing themselves: they write into that input through `press`, `release`, `setTouchStick` and `turn` (`openspec/specs/genshin/touch-controls/design.md`).

- **What moves the camera runs ahead of the floating origin by priority, and the body moves in the world's own coordinates.** A frame callback that moves the camera registers at `CAMERA_FRAME_PRIORITY`, so the origin's shift reads where the camera now stands whatever order its component mounts in. The character's body moves in the world's own coordinates against the world's one ground (`getGroundHeight`, which the session builds with `createWorldHeight` from Windrise's ground its gate read and each region's as it arrives) and the landmark collider, never shifted, and what is drawn on it stands in the world group (`openspec/specs/genshin/character-controller/design.md`).

- **A screen over the world is a `ScreenKind`.** Its effect on the world is its `ScreenBehaviourMap` row, its shortcut an `InputActionScreenKindMap` entry, and its component a slot of `MenuScreen` named by its kind, which the world screen fills with what the screen needs; a kind with no slot opens as a placeholder. A talk (`ScreenKind.Dialogue`) is the exception, which `openspec/specs/genshin/screens/design.md` states. Every fixed-step loop and clock in the world stands still while the open screen's `isHeld`, through an `isHeld` prop (`openspec/specs/genshin/screens/design.md`).
