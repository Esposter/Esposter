# Regions and kits

Read when adding a region, a region's data or landmarks, a character's kit, or a building's kit. The one-line rule is in `SKILL.md`; this page is its reasoning.

- **Region data and kits are the world package's**: heights, colours, look and a region's own kits live in `packages/genshin-world/src/services/<place>/` with their models in `src/models/<place>/`, its landmarks in `src/data/regions/<region>.json` (served by the app's server at `GENSHIN_REGION_DATA_BASE_URL`), and the engine keeps only the generic primitives they are built from (`kits/architecture`, the tree, the statue). Every region shares the world's one barrel, so a region's exports carry its name (`createLiyueBuildingGeometries`, `FONTAINE_STOREY_HEIGHT`): two regions exporting one name is a `TS2308` in the build. A building landmark names its region's kit, and `BuildingKitPartsMap` is where a kit is drawn (`openspec/specs/genshin/region-buildings/design.md`).
- **A character's kit is its hosted record joined to one behaviour module** (`openspec/specs/genshin/kits/README.md`). Every value it plays by, a frame, an area, a talent parameter, is in its `kits` record, published by `genshin:assets kits` from gcsim at v2.47.2, the wiki and the tables; the module holds only behaviour over the shared primitives in `services/kit/effects` and reads its values from the record's reader as `buildKit` builds the kit, so a module that divides by 60, names a proud skill group or calls `getTalentMultiplier` fails `kitModules.test.ts`. A value no source holds is an entry of the kit's overlay in `src/data/kits`, naming its cause from `KitProvisionalCause` or the source it chose, and `Unclassified` is an entry nobody has yet read against its module's comments. A kit page's Sources cites gcsim at v2.47.2, the tag its record reads. Each module is loaded with its character from `CharacterIdCreateKitBehaviourMap`, generated from the id the module declares, never written by hand, and left out of the barrel so it is a chunk of its own. A character's skill and burst icons are its entry of the hosted `skillGlyphs` index, read with its kit, so the entry holds no character's icons.
- **A change to a gcsim reader owes a run over every config at `GCSIM_TAG`.** `pnpm ai:gcsim:configs` from the repo root parses each character's `config.yml` at v2.47.2 with `parseGcsimYaml` and names any it refuses, so a reader that stops on one shows here before a kit publish does.
