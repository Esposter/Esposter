# Deriving a screen

Read when deriving a screen, a surface or a scene in passes. The one-line rules are in `SKILL.md`; this page is their reasoning.

- **The surface is seen before it is built.** Before a screen, a dialog or an interaction is built, a public clip of it is watched (the clip tool, frames read as images), so its shape is the game's own and the clip is its reference: the World Level's tips open a dialog from an info icon, not a hover tooltip, and the clip shows that.

- **Derive a screen in passes** (`openspec/specs/genshin/parity/passes/design.md`, the `recreation-tooling` skill's `references/passes.md`): published sources first, then the screen's blocks through an indexed asset, its `DerivedAssetComponentMap` entry (names, roots, `interface` root and anchor, `clipPattern`), then `genshin:assets extract`, `shaders`, `inventory`, `interface`, `clips` and `witness`. An interface piece is placed from its rect in the interface tree and moved by its decoded clip; a 3D piece goes through layout, camera, shape, motion and surface against the exports (a recording's camera, and motion no clip holds, read off it by the exports' landmarks alone), then the scene's display, light and air (`references/scene.md`); every sound through the audio pass (`references/sound.md`, `references/music.md`). Each pass holds on its own measure, never on the frame's score, before the next begins and is then frozen; `compare` scores the frame only after the last, as acceptance. Exact data outranks measurement, and what a recording may still measure is the page's list.
