# Sound effects

Read when a sound the game plays beside its music (a click, a door, the wind) is found, fitted, played or timed. How the door's works and why is `apps/web/content/docs/genshin/sound-effects.md`; this page is the audio pass's rules for every sound effect. Music is `references/music.md`.

- **Every sound a screen plays is matched, none left out.** A screen's audio pass holds when each sound the recording plays (each button's click, each part's arrival, the ambience under it) is found among the game's own sounds and plays as the game's does; a sound heard in the recording with no match is the pass's open row, never a sound of our own invented to fill it.
- **A sound is found by matching, never by its name.** The banks hold sounds by id alone, so `genshin:assets sounds` scores every sound of a package (`Minimum.pck` holds the login's) against the recording's window by its octave bands' levels at every start, over the level of what plays before the window, and prints the best set of each size the highest scorers make together at their stored levels, with what each leaves unexplained; the sound is the smallest set past which a further sound buys only hundredths of a decibel, its sounds and offsets what `fitSoundEffect` takes. A set is judged under one gain over every band, never a gain a band, which lets one sound stand in for another's octaves. Search the published recordings for one that plays the sound clearly before recording the game (`references/motion.md`).
- **The game's sound is the measure, the recording only finds it.** A recording carries the music, the interface's other sounds and a codec's losses, so a sound's levels are fitted from the decoded bank sound, and the recording only says which sounds play and when.
- **A sound with no pitch is its bands' levels over time, in both channels**: three noises a band of `SOUND_EFFECT_BAND_EDGES`, the one the channels share and each channel's own, read from the matched sounds' mix (`fitSoundEffect`) and played by the engine's own noise (`computeSoundEffectSamples`). Never read through a mono downmix, which misstates a wide sound's level and drops its width. A sound with a harmonic series is a note, fitted as the music's are. Nothing of the game's audio ships, in any format.
- **A sound starts when the game starts it.** Its delay after what sets it off (a click, a stage, a part settling) is read off the recording frame by frame, the sound's own rise against the event's first frame, and kept as a constant beside the stage it belongs to.
- **The pass's measure is each band against the game's**: `genshin:parity effects`, our render against the decoded game sounds in both channels every 5 milliseconds, and its onset against the recording's, gated at the recording's own frame timing. It holds once it sits as near the game's as two renders of our own noise sit to each other; past that the gap is the noise's grain, which only the game's samples would close.
