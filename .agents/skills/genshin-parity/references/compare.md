# Compare and the snapshot

Read when a screen's `compare` score is judged against its target, or its snapshot row is rewritten.

The one-line rule is in `SKILL.md`; this page is its reasoning.

Target a mean of hundredths of a percent, with any remainder explained (antialiasing along an edge). The run rewrites its row of the committed `ParityReferenceMap.snapshot.md` (`--all` every row), which is committed with the change that moved it and is the only score a session quotes.
