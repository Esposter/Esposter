# OpenSpec

Each open change's tasks and its spec delta name the same scenarios (the `openspec` skill's Process rule 4), held by `scripts/src/workspace/openspecScenarioTasks.test.ts`. A row here is a change that does not agree yet. Once it agrees, its row is dated by the commit that reconciles it (`Ledger: openspec | <change>`, dated by `pnpm ai:sweep:ledger-coverage`) or deleted, and the test fails until one of the two is done.
