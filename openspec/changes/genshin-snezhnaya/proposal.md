# Snezhnaya

## Why

Snezhnaya is the newest nation in the game, and its region is built on the terrain, water, sky and weather passes that the other nations use. Its look is what sets it apart: cold is the standing weather, warm torches light its settlements against it, and its birches and tundra grass cover the land. Its capital waits on the game's data, so the region lands in order, starting with Everfrozen Earth once Snezhnograd is found.

## What Changes

Snezhnaya's region looks and reads as the cold, torch-lit nation it is, with its areas listed as the game names them.

- Cold is the standing weather: the chill of each area thickens its fog and snowfall, and regular snow weather holds where it is milder.
- Each settlement's Kresnik's Torch shows as a warm light, the brightest point in a pale scene.
- Birch forests, dark conifers on the peaks and tundra grass through the snow give the region its vegetation.
- The world map lists Snezhnaya's areas by the game's names.
- Everfrozen Earth is built with Snezhnograd and its palace once the game's data places the capital.

## Capabilities

- **genshin/snezhnaya** (modified)

## Waiting on

- The `snezhnaya-capital` re-extraction finding Snezhnograd in the game's data.
- Game data: `game-exports` and `parity-page` (per the change's needs).

## Impact

The region's terrain, vegetation, water and weather are built on the other passes, and this change adds Snezhnaya's cold, torches, birches and areas on top of them. The world map's area list and the torch landmarks are touched. Its palette is sampled from references once the surface pass reaches it.
