# Bosses

## Why

A boss is an enemy whose reward is claimed rather than dropped. A normal boss gives the material each character ascends with, and a weekly boss the material talents need past level 6, so the character screen's ascension and the talents depend on them. The enemies already place a boss's camp, respawn it at once and drop nothing for it, leaving its reward to a blossom that does not yet exist. The respawn after a claim and the weekly reset and count are built; the blossom, the fights and the weekly gating are not.

## What Changes

Bosses fight by their own moves and leave a claimable reward in place of the drop.

- **BREAKING:** a normal boss no longer returns at once after its defeat. It returns five seconds after its Trounce Blossom is claimed, and while the blossom is unclaimed it stays defeated until the player teleports away.
- A normal boss's Trounce Blossom is claimed for 40 Original Resin and pays Mora and its own drop by World Level.
- Each boss fights by its own attacks and phases, and resists displacement and cannot be frozen by Frozen.
- A weekly boss is fought in its trounce domain once the quest that introduces it is done, and its reward is claimed once a week.
- From Adventure Rank 40, a locked weekly boss can be challenged from the handbook.
- A weekly boss's first clear of its highest difficulty gives one of each of its materials.
