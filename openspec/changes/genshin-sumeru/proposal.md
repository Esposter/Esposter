# Sumeru

## Why

Sumeru is the nation of the Dendro Archon, and its two halves are so different that they read as two biomes: a dense rainforest and a desert, joined along a wall of cliffs. Its region is built on the terrain, vegetation, water, sky and weather passes which the other nations use, and its look is what makes it Sumeru. The design sets out how each half reads and the order its areas are built in.

## What Changes

Sumeru's region reads as the rainforest and desert it is, with each area named as the game names it.

- The rainforest is dense and humid: canopy trees, broad leaves, hanging vines and giant mushrooms, with rivers and still pools under a green-gold haze.
- At night the rainforest's plants and fungi glow, and rain and mist are its regular weather.
- The desert is dunes shaped by ridge lines and wind-carried sand, with oases of water and palms, and no rain falls in it.
- The Mausoleum of King Deshret and Khaj-Nisut stand as landmarks, visible from afar.
- Sumeru is all surface: the Ashavan Realm and the Realm of Farakhkert are surface areas, and the world map lists the areas by the game's names.
