# Fishing

## Why

Fishing is one of the game's pastimes, and its points, stocks, fish, rods and the cast's and reel's rules are built. A player still cannot fish: the bite, the reel, the screen, the catch into the bag and the Fishing Association's exchanges are not wired, so the rules hold no catch.

## What Changes

A player casts at a fishing point, waits for a fish to nibble and bite, strikes, reels the fish in by holding its zone, and keeps the catch.

- Fishing opens at a point once the Serenitea Pot is owned and its quest is done.
- A fish nibbles for a time within its range and bites, and a strike that comes too early scares it off.
- A strike on a biting fish starts the reel, and a fish left unstruck lapses.
- A line that stays out of the reel's zone too long breaks.
- A catch puts the items its reward row gives in the bag, with no proficiency counted.
- Each bait draws only its own fish, and each point draws from its region's pools.
- Proficiency is exchanged at the region's Fishing Association for its rewards.

## Capabilities

- **genshin/fishing** (modified)

## Waiting on

- `openspec/changes/genshin-crafting/`: bait is crafted from its recipe.
- `openspec/changes/genshin-shops/`: the Fishing Association's exchanges are made at its shop.
- A recording of the reel (`fishing-reel.mkv`) to set the tension rate, the allowance and the zone's travel. Until it lands these stay provisional and gate nothing.

## Impact

The world gains a fishing service, a bite and a reel model, and a fishing view. The interaction prompts offer fishing at a point in reach, the input bindings take the cast, strike and reel, and the bag takes each fish caught. The fish, pool, stock and rod tables are read from the game's data, and the skill, bait and proficiency tables are read too, each bait with the fish it draws.
