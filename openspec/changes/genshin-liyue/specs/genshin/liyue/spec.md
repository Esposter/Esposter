## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Karst and stone forests rise as rock within their areas

Liyue's karst pillars and stone forests SHALL rise as layered rock within their areas' outlines, placed as landmarks or scattered by rule.

#### Scenario: Jueyun Karst rises as pillars within its outline

- **GIVEN** the player is in Jueyun Karst
- **WHEN** the player looks across the karst
- **THEN** layered rock pillars rise within the area's outline
- **AND** each pillar has a flat top that carries trees and grass

### Requirement: A sea of clouds lies below Liyue's high peaks

The area around Jueyun Karst and the high peaks SHALL show a layer of stepped cloud below the summits.

#### Scenario: Jueyun Karst shows a sea of clouds below its summits

- **GIVEN** the player is at a summit around Jueyun Karst
- **WHEN** the player looks down
- **THEN** a stepped layer of cloud lies below the summits

### Requirement: Dihua Marsh is reed beds and shallow water

Dihua Marsh SHALL be drawn as reed beds and shallow water.

#### Scenario: Dihua Marsh shows reed beds and shallow water

- **GIVEN** the player is in Dihua Marsh
- **WHEN** the player looks across the marsh
- **THEN** reed beds stand in shallow water

### Requirement: Liyue Harbor climbs its cliff in tiers

Liyue Harbor SHALL be a set of landmarks on stepped streets up its cliff, with its wharves below.

#### Scenario: Liyue Harbor's streets climb its cliff in tiers

- **GIVEN** the player is in Liyue Harbor
- **WHEN** the player walks up from the wharves
- **THEN** the streets rise in stepped tiers up the cliff
- **AND** the harbour's landmarks stand on those tiers

#### Scenario: Liyue Harbor's landmarks match the game's reference

- **GIVEN** the player stands at a harbour landmark's reference view
- **WHEN** the player looks at the landmark
- **THEN** the landmark matches the game's reference of that view

### Requirement: The Chasm's underground mines are a separate layer

The Chasm's surface and its underground mines SHALL be drawn as separate layers, the underground dark except for its lamps and glowing ore.

#### Scenario: The Chasm's underground mines are a separate layer

- **GIVEN** the player enters the Chasm's underground mines
- **WHEN** the player moves beneath the surface
- **THEN** the mines are drawn as their own layer
- **AND** only the lamps and the glowing ore give light

### Requirement: Chenyu Vale is tea slopes, mist and white-walled villages

Chenyu Vale SHALL be drawn with terraced tea slopes, waterfalls, low mist in its valley and villages of white walls and dark tiles.

#### Scenario: Chenyu Vale shows its tea slopes and mist

- **GIVEN** the player is in Chenyu Vale
- **WHEN** the player looks across the valley
- **THEN** terraced tea slopes rise above low mist in the valley
