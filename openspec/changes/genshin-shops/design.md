# Shops

The game's shops are its vendors in the cities, the Souvenir Shops that take each region's Sigils, the Fishing Associations, and the shops in the Paimon menu, Paimon's Bargains among them. They turn Mora, Sigils, Starglitter and Stardust into ingredients, recipes, materials, Fates and characters. They spend from the [inventory](../../specs/genshin/inventory/design.md)'s wallet and stand at the vendors placed in region data.

The rule over Paimon's Bargains' Fates and the Mondstadt grocery's goods is built, as the [shops](../../specs/genshin/shops/design.md) page sets out. This page keeps what is not.

## Decisions

- **Every shop's goods are the game's.** `ShopGoodsExcelConfigData` holds each good: the shop it belongs to, the item and how many, its price in Mora or in items, its buy limit, its refresh, the Adventure Rank it shows from and the dates it sells between. `ShopExcelConfigData` names the shop, and `ShopRotateExcelConfigData` a rotation's order.
- **Restocked by its refresh.** What was bought is kept with when it was bought, and a good's limit comes back when its refresh comes round, each good by its own row. The table names four refreshes, none, daily, weekly and monthly, most goods taking none. There is no every-two-or-three-days kind in the table, and the wiki's [Shop](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Shop) page names only daily and weekly restocks, so that cycle is not proposed. The weekly one waits on its vendors.
- **Paimon's Bargains' rotation.** The wiki gives the schedule: two characters a month from a six-month cycle of twelve, at 34 Starglitter each; a set of five weapons, Royal or Blackcliff, every two months at 24 Starglitter; and a monthly set of top materials and the Crown of Insight. The table holds the characters as rotations 10201 and 10202, six orders each, and the weapons as 10203 to 10207, with the goods naming no item of their own. Each set's price is read from its rotation's goods rows, which give the characters 34 Masterless Starglitter at one a month and the weapons 24 at three a month (`characterPrice`, `weaponPrice`), matching the wiki's Starglitter Exchange section on the Paimon's Bargains page. The rotation's item ids are joined, not dead: each character's item is a card whose item use (`ITEM_USE_GAIN_AVATAR`) gives the avatar id in its first parameter, and each weapon's item is the weapon's own id. `buildPaimonsBargainsRotation` publishes `shops/paimonsBargainsRotation` from those joins: the six pairs of rotations 10201 and 10202 at each order, and the weapon sets at order one (Royal) and order two (Blackcliff) of rotations 10203 to 10207. The anchor is the wiki's month table, read from its MediaWiki API (`api.php?action=parse&page=Paimon%27s_Bargains&prop=wikitext`): January is Fischl and Xiangling, the fourth of the six pairs, so a game-time month `m` offers pair `(m + 2) % 6` and the weapon set is Blackcliff in odd months and Royal in even ones (`getPaimonsBargainsOffer`, on the game-time month counted from the daily hour). A character whose constellations are full cannot be bought (`checkIsPaimonsBargainsCharacterBuyable`). The names of the characters and weapons wait on the bag's definitions, and the screen on the bag holding them. Fates for Primogems are the [inventory](../archive/2026-10-18-genshin-inventory/design.md) proposal's.
- **Souvenir Shops take Sigils.** Each region's main city trades its own Sigils for materials, blueprints and Mora, never restocking. The table's Souvenir rows price in item 305 for Mondstadt and 307 for Liyue. The wallet's Sigils are this change's, not the [inventory](../archive/2026-10-18-genshin-inventory/design.md)'s: the inventory names no Sigil, so `Currency.ts` gains them here. Since 2.0 a region's shop opens only once its region's offering is at its last level, as Sumeru's waits on Vanarana's Favor ([offering systems](../genshin-offering-systems/design.md)). The dump names the Sigils Anemo Sigil (305) and Geo Sigil (307). The Souvenir goods are found by the Sigil they are priced in, because the table's shop numbers 1002 and 1009 name an Aster activity shop, not a Souvenir Shop; `buildSouvenirGoods` publishes them, each region's, with the count the dump gives. `checkIsSouvenirShopOpen` is the closed-until-last-level rule as a pure function. The eight region Sigils are in `Currency.ts`, `CurrencyItemIdMap` and `ShopItemCurrencyMap`, so the trade is built. The closed gate is wired to Mondstadt's offering, the Frostbearing Tree: Marjorie's talk opens shop 1002 only once the tree's levels are read and its last is reached, as the decision below sets out. The other regions' Souvenir goods are built from the Sigil each is priced in, the Cryo Sigil pricing none, so Snezhnaya has no slice.
- **Nothing for Genesis Crystals.** The Gift Shop and the outfit shop sell only for Genesis Crystals, which the world never sells, so their goods are never offered, as the [Genshin](../genshin-program/design.md) proposal rules out any payment.
- **A Reputation discount is the price's.** The table gives a city's named shops a Mora discount at its reputation level four, a rate of 90 in the table, which is ten percent off. The rounding is the proposal's: in the player's favour, to a multiple of five Mora ([reputation](../genshin-reputation/design.md)). The wiki's Blanche (Mondstadt) page bears it out: Salt at 60 is 50 after the discount, Pepper at 80 is 70 and Tomato at 120 is 105. The rule is `discountShopGood` in genshin-world: a good priced in Mora whose discount level the player's Reputation level reaches is priced at the discounted Mora, and every other good keeps its price. The screen prices and charges with that one good, so the price shown is the price taken. The good's discount level is read from the table's city rows by `readCityDiscountLevel`, the level four of Mondstadt's city 1, and the save's single Reputation progress is the level the caller passes in, read as Mondstadt's, which the [reputation](../genshin-reputation/design.md) page has to confirm.
- **A vendor is a resident.** A city's vendor is placed as a resident, and their shop opens from their talk, as the game opens it.
- **A vendor's shop is found by the map from its NPC id.** The dump links no NPC to a shop, so `VendorShopTypeMap` in `services/shop/` names each city vendor's shop by the vendor's NPC id, written from the wiki's vendor pages region by region, Mondstadt first, and provisional. Mondstadt's entries are Blanche (1423) on shop 1004, the general goods, as above, and Marjorie (1435) on shop 1002, whose goods all take the Anemo Sigil, as her [wiki page](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Marjorie) says she trades for them. The table names 1002 an Aster activity shop and 1004 a Dragon Spine one, so both names conflict with their goods, and the owner settles the conflict. The other Mondstadt vendors are matched by their stock, as the wiki's Flora, Chloris, Shiliu, Charles, Draff, Brook and Schulz pages list it by name (read through the MediaWiki API, `api.php?action=parse`, and the Mondstadt General Goods page for Blanche's): the table's goods shops 1011 to 1018 hold those names, one shop per vendor, Flora 1011 (NPC 1425), Charles 1012 (NPC 1465), Shiliu 1013 (NPC 1640), Schulz 1014 (NPC 1638), Brook 1015 (NPC 1541), Draff 1017 (NPC 1537) and Chloris 1018 (NPC 1657), each provisional. Shop 1077 repeats Shiliu's five goods under another number and is left out, and Charles's shop 1012 holds the Adventure Rank recipe the wiki lists apart, which the map keeps. The shop table names no row for shops 1011 to 1018, so none takes a city discount. `buildMondstadtVendorGoods` publishes them as seven `shops/mondstadt<Vendor>` datasets, 31 goods in all. The main session publishes them with `pnpm genshin:assets shops`, and the map and `SHOP_GOODS_KEYS` name them. Nantuck (NPC 1673) sells from shop 1064, the Fishing Association's, built as `shops/mondstadtFishing` and paid in fish from the bag. The other seven Fishing Associations are matched by their stock the same way, since the wiki's Fishing Association page lists one member per region: Jiawei (NPC 2615) on shop 1065, Liyue; Kujirai Momiji (3200) on 1066, Inazuma; Loumelat (4176) on 1071, Sumeru; Delaroche (5073) on 1090, Fontaine; Oroluk (6340) on 1122, Natlan; Hedvika (7086) on 1137, Nod-Krai; and Zherebtsov (7418) on 1156, Snezhnaya, each provisional. The NPC table also has Nantuck under 167301 with the same body id, which the map does not name, since the resident's id is the one placement records. No member is placed as a resident in the region data, so no talk opens from the world yet, and task 4.4 stays open until one is.
- **A Souvenir Shop opens at its region's offering.** Mondstadt's Souvenir Shop is gated by the Frostbearing Tree, the offering the [offering systems](../../specs/genshin/offering-systems/design.md) page places in Dragonspine, within Mondstadt's region axes: `checkIsVendorShopOpen` keeps shop 1002 closed until the tree's level, from the save, reaches the last of the levels the game's table holds (`checkIsSouvenirShopOpen`, the table loaded beside the tree's places as `frostbearingTreeLevels`), and its talk is a dialogue meanwhile. Liyue's offering is not built, so its shop opens from the start, provisional. The owner confirms Mondstadt's entry against the Frostbearing Tree.
- **The Mondstadt general goods vendor is Blanche, on shop 1004.** `ShopExcelConfigData` does name shop 1004, as `SHOP_TYPE_ACTIVITY_DRAGON_SPINE`, so no name in the dump links it to the grocery. The link is read from the table's order, as the [shops](../../specs/genshin/shops/design.md) page sets out, and the conflict with the name stays open for the owner to settle. The wiki's Blanche (Mondstadt) page confirms it without a recording: her shop lists the same eight goods at the same Mora prices and daily limit of 100 as shop 1004, and is open at all times of the day.
- **The Shop screen is measured from a clip.** Its pixels are read from the public Paimon's Bargains purchase clip (`aJq1MnTPtZ4`, 12 seconds): the panel at 505 by 250, the four goods columns of 310 with 32 gaps, the detail's picked card at 570 by 345 and the Buy button at 1135 to 1345 by 728 to 790, which the frame shows on the same pixels. The frame is kept outside the repo, and only these numbers are in code.
- **A good priced in an item the wallet does not trade is paid from the bag.** A fish is spent from the bag as the price, through `payShopGoodFromBag`, and never held in the wallet, which would count it twice against the bag. `ShopItemCurrencyMap` maps only the wallet's currencies, so a fish is not in it; the Shop screen draws such a good too, its price shown as the item with the count of it the bag holds, named from the materials table. `countShopPrice` reads that count: the wallet's for a currency, the bag's for an item.
- **A fish is named from the materials table, which the items build reads for the Fishing Associations.** The Shop screen names a bag price through `getItemDefinition`, and the materials table holds only the items the items build names. That build now also names each Fishing Association's goods and the fish they are bought with, read from the goods table, so each one resolves. `items` publishes before `shops`, and the seven new keys in `SHOP_GOODS_KEYS` are published with it, as the lock names each; `readShopGoodMap` reads every key together, so one missing dataset fails every city shop, and a key joins the map only with its publish.
- **A furniture formula is filed with the furnishings.** Six of the Fishing Associations' goods are furniture formulas, typed `MATERIAL_FURNITURE_FORMULA`, which `MaterialType` did not hold, so the materials schema would refuse the whole table. `MaterialType` gains `FurnitureFormula`, filed under the Furnishing tab. The wiki gives the furnishing's category rather than the formula's bag tab, so the filing is provisional until a recording confirms it.
- **A good that is no currency goes into the bag.** A purchase whose item the wallet does not hold adds it to the bag through `addInventoryItem`, as `claimExpedition` adds a reward, and a purchase the bag cannot take whole is refused with nothing spent.
- **A purchase is kept once for each limited good.** The save's `shopPurchases` holds, for each good with a buy limit, how many were bought in the refresh window of its latest purchase and the moment of that purchase. A purchase in a later window starts the count again at one, so the save holds at most one entry for each limited good, bounded by the goods the shop table gives a limit. A good with no limit keeps no entry, since no limit counts it. The purchases of the two copies of a save merge by keeping, for each good, the purchase made later. A save written before this slice existed reads as no purchases, and one written before the Sigils were added reads each Sigil at zero: the reader defaults them and no stored save is backfilled.

## Scope and order

**Today:** the wallet holds the currencies. Paimon's Bargains opens from the Paimon menu's Shop entry on its own screen, offering the Fates and the month's pair and weapon set, each bought at its rotation's price and limit: a character joins the roster, or gains a Stella Fortuna when held, and a weapon goes into the bag. Its tab labels are not built yet, since each has several text ids in the game text, so its goods share one grid. The Mondstadt grocery's Mora-priced goods are offered by the rule, with no screen. The grocery's goods give ingredients the bag does not hold yet, so the rule refuses them until it does.

**This adds, in order:**

1. **The grocery's purchases into the bag.** The bag's materials hold only what drops, expeditions and the forge name, so the grocery's eight ingredients have no definition yet.
   - `scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/items/buildItems.ts` adds the item ids of the `shops/mondstadtGeneralGoods` record to the ids it builds; `pnpm -C scripts genshin:assets items` republishes `items/materials` from the dump's `MaterialExcelConfigData`, and `pnpm -C scripts genshin:text names` adds their names to the existing name-text chunks.
   - `packages/genshin-world/src/services/shop/buyShopGood.ts` takes the bag (`Inventory`) and a getter of an item's definition by its id beside the wallet: a shop passes `(itemId) => getItemDefinition(itemId, names, materialDataMap)` over the material table the world reads as it opens, and the Realm Depot passes its furnishing's definition, since a furnishing has no material row. An item in `ShopItemCurrencyMap` still goes to the wallet; any other is added with `addInventoryItem(inventory, getGoodItemDefinition(itemId), itemCount)`, and an addition with any overflow returns undefined. The result gains `inventory`.
   - `packages/genshin-world/src/services/shop/buyShopGood.test.ts` gains two cases on good 203001 (item 100075, 60 Mora): bought into an empty bag it adds one item and takes 60 Mora, and into a bag with no room for a new kind it is refused with the wallet unchanged. The existing Fate cases pass the bag through unchanged.
2. **Paimon's Bargains' screen, its rotation, weapons and materials**, once the bag holds the items' definitions.
3. **Each city's vendors and Souvenir Shop**, as its region is built, with the Sigils in the wallet.
4. **The Fishing Associations**, with fishing.

## Data and measures

- **Read from the game's tables:** `ShopExcelConfigData`, `ShopGoodsExcelConfigData`, `ShopRotateExcelConfigData` and `ShopSheetExcelConfigData`, the goods' item rows, and which resident runs which shop, which no table here yet names.
- **Read from the wiki:** Paimon's Bargains' rotation, the names of its characters and weapons, and the Mondstadt grocery's stock, where the table leaves them open.
