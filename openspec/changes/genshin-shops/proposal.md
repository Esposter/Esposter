# Shops

## Why

The rule over Paimon's Bargains' Fates and the Mondstadt grocery's goods is built, but the grocery's ingredients have no definition in the bag yet, so the rule refuses them, and no shop is on screen. Each vendor's goods, their restocks and the Souvenir Shops that take Sigils remain. Shops are where the player turns Mora, Sigils and Starglitter into ingredients, recipes and characters.

## What Changes

The player can buy what the game's shops sell for Mora, Sigils and Starglitter, with each good restocking on its own refresh.

- Each shop offers its goods at the game's price, buy limit and sale dates, and a good is not offered outside its sale dates.
- A purchased good comes back when its own refresh comes round: daily or weekly, or never for a good with no refresh.
- The Mondstadt grocery's ingredients go into the bag when bought, and a purchase the bag cannot take whole is refused with nothing spent.
- Paimon's Bargains offers two characters a month and a set of weapons every two months; a character with full constellations cannot be bought.
- Each region's Souvenir Shop trades its Sigils for materials, blueprints and Mora, never restocks, and stays closed until the region's offering is at its last level.
- A city's named shop takes ten percent off its Mora prices at reputation level four, rounded down to a multiple of five Mora.
- A talk with a city's vendor opens their shop.
- Goods paid in Genesis Crystals are never offered.

## Impact

The shop purchase path takes the bag beside the wallet, so a good can add an item to the bag. The item definitions for the grocery's ingredients are published from the game's material table. The shop, Paimon's Bargains and Souvenir Shop screens, the city residents who run shops, and the Reputation discount are touched. Fates for Primogems stay with the inventory proposal.
