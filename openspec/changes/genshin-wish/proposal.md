# Wish

## Why

The pull, the rates, the guarantees, the Epitomized Path's rules, the returns and the screen are built, with the standard and beginners' pools. The event banners the game runs in their windows are not, nor the second character banner, and the wish screen keeps no history. The event banners' windows come from the wiki's banner pages, since the game's data does not hold them.

## What Changes

The wish offers the banners the game runs in their windows, keeps every draw in a history and gives each new character its place in the roster.

- An event banner is offered only within its window, read from the wiki's banner page at the Asia server's offset, and beside the beginners' and standard banners.
- Two character event banners can run side by side, sharing the character banners' counters.
- The history lists each draw's kind, item and time, and keeps draws past a year.
- A character drawn for the first time joins the roster; a repeat draw counts a copy and returns its Starglitter.
- The beginners' wish draws Noelle as its featured four-star on its eighth wish only.
- A Fate costs only earned Primogems, and a schedule naming an item the names do not hold fails the build.

Chronicled Wish is out of scope and gets its own page when its turn comes.

## Impact

A new schedule record of event banners is built from the wiki and published through the game data tooling, and read by key beside the stat tables. The wish screen gains a second character banner and the history. The banner model gains each banner's own id.
