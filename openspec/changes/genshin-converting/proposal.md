# Converting

## Why

The bench's conversion rows are the alchemy that turns one material into another of its family with a catalyst, and the crafting design leaves them out. Dream Solvent turns a boss material or a billet into another of its kind, and Dust of Azoth turns an elemental sliver, fragment, chunk or gemstone into the same tier of another element. A player holds both catalysts and has no bench recipe that uses them.

## What Changes

Build the conversions on the crafting bench as recipes of their own.

- A conversion takes one material and the catalyst it names, makes one item and costs no Mora. It is open from the start, with no rank and no instruction.
- The bench lists each conversion section under tabs of its own, apart from the craft tabs of the same combine type, each named by the game's own text.
- The save's cap on recipes rises to hold every recipe the bench now holds. No save field changes, so no save needs a backfill.

## Capabilities

- **genshin/crafting** (modified)

## Waiting on

Nothing blocks the build. The conversion section's layout and the achievement count of a conversion are provisional until a PC recording confirms them.
