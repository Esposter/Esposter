# Offering systems

## Why

Beside the Statues of The Seven, many areas keep an offering that takes items found in their place and gives rewards for levels. The shipped rule covers only the Frostbearing Tree, so the game's other offerings cannot yet be offered to or rewarded.

## What Changes

Every offering in the game follows the one rule the Frostbearing Tree uses, with its items, levels and place.

- Each offering's levels, thresholds and rewards come from its own level table or from the wiki's page for it.
- Each offering stands at the point the official map gives it in its region.
- An offering's items are found in its place, at its spawned places or from its region's quests.

## Capabilities

- **genshin/offering-systems** (modified)

## Waiting on

- Where each offering's items stand comes with the spawned places: `openspec/changes/genshin-spawned-places/`.

## Impact

Every region's offerings in the world: the offering prompt in reach, the bag the offered items come from, and the offering's landmark in the world. The Frostbearing Tree's own landmark waits on the world's drawing and is not changed here.

Size kept as the source page's own: splitting it is a later decision.
