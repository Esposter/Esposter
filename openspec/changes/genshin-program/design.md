# Genshin

Genshin Impact is set on one continent: seven nations, each with its own element, climate and architecture, plus the borderlands around them. This proposal rebuilds it in the app with TresJS and Three.js's WebGPU renderer. The goal is a recreation you can explore, as close to the game's regions as careful reference can make it. The world comes first: its look, its sky, its ground, water and plants, then the regions one by one. After that, the game's features are copied one at a time, starting with the character and how it moves.

## Decisions

- **A fan work, unmarked on its pages.** Region, place and landmark names are the game's own, since the point is the game's world. The page does not pass itself off as HoYoverse's and carries no mark of its own; each package's README states the fan-work status instead. This area still never counterfeits official art and claims no rights of its own. This supersedes the [voxel open world](../../rejected/infra/voxel-open-world/design.md), whose realm was original, since the realm is now the game's own.
- **The bundle carries only what is authored here.** Geometry is generated in code from parameters, textures are procedural nodes in the Three.js Shading Language (TSL), and shaders are written in the repository. No model, texture, sound or image file taken from the game or its media is bundled into the app or served by its server. The one exception is decided: the interface matches the game's exactly, so its glyphs are traced from the game's own marks into paths of our own, the way the [parity](../../specs/genshin/parity/README.md) loop does it. The other is decided too: the music's voices, which no oscillator of ours makes sound like an orchestra, may play public-domain recordings of real instruments layered over the synthesizer, never the game's ([sampled instruments](../../specs/genshin/sampled-instruments/design.md)). The characters are drawn from HoYoverse's official MMD models, which it publishes for fans apart from the game. They are not bundled either: the models' own terms forbid redistributing them, 「请勿二次配布」, so the app publishes each pack under the owner's statement that HoYoverse permits it ([published packs](../../specs/genshin/characters/published-packs/design.md)), and the browser downloads each pack once, at the startup loading screen, keeping it for later visits. The characters page holds how a character is drawn from it ([characters](../../specs/genshin/characters/README.md)). The world stays generated, which is also what makes the project a showcase of the renderer rather than an asset viewer. Generated is not approximate: a scene is re-derived from the game's own assets as closely as they allow, by our own shaders, generators and data, with no fidelity ceiling short of copying the game's files ([scene derivation](../../specs/genshin/scene-derivation/design.md)).
- **The game's files are references, never bundled.** The installed game keeps its content in encrypted asset bundles. AnimeStudio, a third-party exporter, reads them locally with the game closed, and its exports sit in the references folder beside every screenshot and recording, outside the repository ([derived assets](../../specs/genshin/derived-assets/design.md)). Nothing is injected into the running game, which an anti-cheat driver watches. The game's code metadata may be decrypted offline from the user's own install where what it decodes, such as the tables' real field names or a script's type data, is worth far more than the dumps give, and nothing it reads is committed (the `genshin-parity` skill). What a player can see stays a reference too, gathered as [parity](../../specs/genshin/parity/README.md) gathers any: the community wiki and published recordings searched first, and the game recorded only for what nothing published shows. None of the references is committed, and none of them reaches the reader. The reader receives only what the game supplies and the bullets above carry: the game's text, committed as generated slices; the hosted game data the world reads by key, which the browser downloads at the startup loading screen; and the official packs, downloaded from their own published container.
- **As close to the real region as the game's own data allows.** Every scene is rebuilt in the [recreation passes](../genshin-recreation-passes/design.md): its layout read from the game's terrain tiles and streaming records, its shapes and surfaces fitted against the game's own exports, a recording's camera read off it by the exports' landmarks, and only its display transform, light, air and sounds read off a recording's colour, each pass judged by its own measure and frozen ([scene derivation](../../specs/genshin/scene-derivation/design.md)). The frame's perceptual score against its references is its acceptance, never its loop.
- **An engine of modules.** The [engine architecture](../../specs/genshin/engine-architecture/design.md) divides the world into a package of single-purpose modules that the app mounts, so each system is built, tested and replaced on its own; a page adds the module it first needs.
- **WebGPU through TresJS.** `TresCanvas` takes a renderer factory, so the scene runs on three's `WebGPURenderer` while components stay declarative. Materials and post-processing are TSL node graphs, as the [fluid simulator](../../specs/fluid-simulator/README.md) already does. TresJS comes first, and raw Three.js is used only where TresJS and cientos have nothing, with the reason given on the page that does it.
- **TSL takes the full lint.** The engine's modules import the same `three/webgpu` and TSL types and lint in about a second, so the engine takes no exclusion, and a new one is added only for a file measured to hang (the `oxlint` skill, `references/ignore-patterns.md`).
- **The world keeps a budget.** Its cost grows with what the camera can see, never with the size of the continent. Every page here applies the rules the [engine architecture](../../specs/genshin/engine-architecture/design.md) sets out: instancing, level of detail, off-thread generation, and transferable buffers.
- **The game's systems settle their open questions this way.** The spawned places, such as chests, Oculi, puzzles, camps and shrines, are read from the official interactive map's public point data, fitted to world coordinates on the statues and waypoints both sources hold. Poise and the other frame-level combat numbers come from gcsim's data up to v2.47.2, its last MIT release, cross-checked against KQM. The Serenitea Pot editor, the card game and Imaginarium Theater are in scope, as the whole world is. Time-limited events and a server-backed world for co-op stay [deferred](../../deferred/genshin/). Each character's kit is written from its talent descriptions, never decompiled.

## How it works

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  R[The game's own data: terrain tiles, placements, exports] --> RP{Recreation passes: each gated by its own measure}
  RP --> A[Fitted world data: heights, placements, kits' parameters]
  A --> W[Worker: terrain tiles, scatter, landmark meshes]
  C[Camera position] --> Q{CDLOD quadtree: which tiles, at what detail}
  Q --> W
  W -->|transferable buffers| S[Scene: terrain, water, vegetation, buildings]
  T[Clock and weather] --> K[Sky, sun, fog, wind]
  K --> S
  S --> M[Toon materials and outlines]
  M --> P[Post-processing: bloom, god rays, grade, anti-aliasing]
  P --> F[Frame]
```

The work is ordered into phases, each with an exit gate, and every page below is held by one of them. The [phases design](../genshin-phases/design.md) states the gates, the definition of done for each kind of unit and how the next unit is taken, so this page names the phases and no more.

## Scope and order

1. **The recreation passes' runner and measures first.** Every later page is judged by them, so they are the base the rest stands on. The rest of the engine follows, each page built when Windrise's passes reach what it draws and shown first there, in the scene the [rendering style](../../specs/genshin/rendering-style/design.md) is built in.
2. **Mondstadt first among the regions.** It is where the game begins, and its opening areas are the ones the Windrise scene already holds. It is phase 4, and the other regions are phase 5, built in the game's release order.
3. **Then the play features.** The [menu screens](../genshin-menu-paimon/design.md) are phase 2, and their Paimon is drawn as a character is once characters stand. The [character controller](../../specs/genshin/character-controller/design.md) (run, sprint, jump, climb, glide, swim and stamina) and its [follow camera](../genshin-follow-camera/design.md), the [HUD](../genshin-hud/design.md), whose stamina meter the controller needs, and [characters](../genshin-characters/design.md), from HoYoverse's official MMD models, which the startup loading screen downloads from the [published packs](../../specs/genshin/characters/README.md) before the world shows, are phase 3. Each feature is built when its turn comes, not before, so nothing is built against an engine that does not yet exist; a feature's page may be written ahead of its turn and states only its proposed scope until it is built.
4. **Then the game's systems.** A system whose work serves every region is phase 6, and one that a region's work opens sits in that region's phase, as the [phases design](../genshin-phases/design.md) classes it. The [character kits](../genshin-character-kits/design.md) come first, since every character's strike is a kit's and every growth system, reward and challenge after them acts through a kit. The systems' pages are written ahead of their turn as the map of what is left, each holding only what the game's tables and the wiki settle, and each is re-read against the engine when its turn comes.

## What this does not propose

- **Assets from the game.** Nothing exported is committed, served or converted into a file of ours; only what our own fits and generators write ships.
- **Multiplayer.** The world is the person's own, as the game's is outside co-op ([co-op](../../deferred/genshin/co-op/design.md) is deferred).
- **A monetised or official-looking product.** No payment, no HoYoverse branding in the chrome, and no claim to be the game.

## Notes

- **As-built pages form a Genshin area.** Each engine page, once built, is rewritten in the [Genshin](../../specs/genshin/README.md) area.
- **The geography is the game's own, fitted.** Heights and placements are read from the game's terrain tiles and streaming records on its own coordinates and fitted by our generators ([scene derivation](../../specs/genshin/scene-derivation/design.md)), so no region is laid out by hand and none needs a transform from a map's pixels.
