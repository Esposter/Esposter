# Reputation

Each nation keeps a Reputation, raised by work done for its people. The [Reputation](../../specs/genshin/reputation/design.md) page holds Mondstadt's levels and the three-a-week claim limit across nations. What remains is what puts them in the world, and the later nations.

## Decisions

- **Requests run as world quests.** From level 2, requests are taken from the nation's keeper and run as world quests: deliver the items asked for, or clear the camp named. Each pays its Reputation EXP and Mora, and the three-a-week limit already counts them across nations.
- **Bounties are hunted with Elemental Sight.** From level 2 (level 1 in Natlan), the keeper offers bounties. The player goes to the place named, finds the target's trail with [Elemental Sight](../genshin-elemental-sight/design.md), defeats it and claims the bounty back with the keeper. The target is spawned when the bounty is taken.
- **Exploration and quests feed it.** Each nation's exploration progress, its chests, puzzles and statue offerings, raises its Reputation at the thresholds `ReputationExploreExcelConfigData` sets, and its Reputation quests give theirs once. A nation's exploration percentage is read off its areas as the [exploration progress](../genshin-exploration-progress/design.md) proposal settles, which also pays Mondstadt's thresholds; a threshold pays once, when the percentage passes it.
- **Mondstadt's keeper is Hertha, NPC 1663.** The wiki names each nation's keeper (Hertha, Ms. Yu, Madarame Hyakubei, Effendi, Euphrasie), and `genshin:text find "^Hertha$"` with the NPC table gives her id. Her id is the NPC table's row for the keeper, whose billboard is the reputation icon. The hosted `sceneNpcBorn/scene3` dataset holds no birth for NPC 1663 among its 1005, so her place is not in the dump's births. The [scene group record](../../specs/genshin/scene-groups/design.md) marks the spot she keeps, the scene's `Reputation` building point, and the wiki places her in Mondstadt by the Knights of Favonius Headquarters, so her place is an estimate marked provisional.
- **The Reputation screen is the keeper's.** Talking to the keeper opens the nation's Reputation screen: its level and EXP toward the next, each level's reward, and the four sources (bounties, requests, exploration and quests). It is drawn from a public clip, opened by the keeper's talk once she stands; until then it is reached only through its fixture on the parity page.
- **A discount is the shop's own.** The shops apply it to a named shop's prices once they have a screen, and each discount's shops are the ones its function's text names, joined to the shops' ids then.
- **Natlan's tribes and supply notices.** Natlan keeps a Reputation per tribe, and its supply notices take donated items, three a week shared between tribes, each 160 to its tribe and a Sanctifying Unction, as the wiki gives them.
- **A Reputation quest is bound to the quest its parentQuestId names.** A Reputation quest row pays when the quest its `parentQuestId` names finishes, and a request pays when the quest its `questId` names finishes. The reward's own row names no quest, so the binding is the quest's id, and the reward is paid on the move that finishes that quest, never on a move from a progress already finished. Settles task 2.3. `getFinishedReputationRewards` is that rule; nothing calls it yet, since the Mondstadt slice does not hold the Reputation quest rows and the quests they bind are not carried.
- **A discount applies only at the shop it names, once a shop has a screen.** The shop rows whose `vipFuncID` is the function the level names are the named shops: 203 (the grocery) and 204 (the food shop) for function 1002, each with `cityDiscountLevel` 4. The grocery's goods are filed under shop type 1004, which the shops page reads as the grocery, and no dump field joins that type to shop row 203, so the join is the shops page's mapping. The food shop's goods are not published. The Shop screen is mounted now, and only the grocery's goods carry the discount level, as the next note on task 3.2 says.
- **Requests and bounties open at level two, on the dump's one-based levels.** The dump's `level` starts at one: its row for level 2 holds the Mining Outcrop Search (function 1001) the wiki gives level two, so no offset applies. The unlock is the wiki's "unlocked upon reaching Reputation Level 2", not the level of the first row that names a request group, since the rows for levels 1 to 4 all name group 5 and a gate read off them would open requests at level one. A request is drawn by weight from the group the nation's current level's row names.
- **A delivery request is carried as the world quest its row names, a Collect of the items it asks for.** The Mondstadt request rows name twelve sub quests that are deliveries: each has a Giving row with the items and count, and its accepting NPC is in the sub quest's accept condition. The carried quest's id is the request's `questId` (for example 7070101, the sub quest, so the reward binding matches the quest's id), its title and description are its main quest's (the sub quest's `mainId`), its one step is a Collect of each item by the Giving row's count, the same objective `QUEST_CONTENT_OBTAIN_ITEM` already maps to, and it has no offer talk, since the keeper's request board offers it. The hand-in is not an event in the world yet, so a delivery counts the items picked up, provisional. The three requests on Manly Jack's board (7070901, 7071001, 7071101) are not camps: each Giving row is a group (`GIVING_METHOD_GROUP`) that asks for any of a giving group's items, and the wiki's request pages name no camp for them. They are carried as group deliveries, the next decision. Task 2.1 adds the three to `QuestId` and to the carried rewards' list, the one shared seam the quests change also touches, so each is one additive line.
- **A group delivery is one Collect of any of the group's items.** A giving by group asks for `givingGroupCount` of any one of the items its `GivingGroup` row names: 7070901 asks three of 108012 or 108013, 7071001 two of 108008 or 108009, and 7071101 three of 108096 or 108097. The objective is one Collect whose `targetId` is the group's first item and whose `alternativeTargetIds` holds the rest. That field is optional, so the objectives already carried and saved keep their shape. `advanceQuest` counts a pickup of any of those ids toward the one objective, and the request pays on the move that finishes it, as the exact deliveries do. Provisional: the wiki says any one of the items, and the first item is read from the group's order in the table.
- **An accepted request starts its quest.** `startQuests` takes the id of an accepted quest and starts it at its first step, once, if it is carried and not yet started. The accept rule is tested, but the Reputation screen has no offer or accept control, so nothing calls it yet. That is task 2.1's blocker.
- **A bounty's target is drawn from the wiki's list for its difficulty, and spawned at its region's centre.** The wiki's Targets table names each tier's possible enemies, three, four and five stars. The dump's bounty difficulties are those tiers by their Reputation: `HUNTING_DIFFICULTY_EASY` (the default, 120 EXP) is three-star, `MEDIUM` (160) four-star, `HARD` (200) five-star. Each named enemy resolves to one monster by its English name through the dump's monster names: the lowest id whose name is the wiki's, else the lowest whose name ends in it, since the wiki's family names (Abyss Mage, Mitachurl) are the dump's element variants. A taken bounty draws its target from its difficulty's list by the roll and spawns at the centre of its hunting region, which the dump places for all four Mondstadt regions. Provisional: the variant and the draw stand until the game or the wiki names them. The nearest-camp fallback for a region the dump does not place is not built, since no Mondstadt region lacks one; it needs the kinds' families and the spawned camps. The kinds' table held only the camps' kinds, so 30 of the 34 Mondstadt bounty kinds were not in it. `collectEnemyKindIds` now takes the bounty kinds beside the camps' kinds, once each, and `buildEnemyKinds` reads each one's codex row and curves, which the dump holds for all 34. The main session republishes the enemies dataset. Task 2.2 stays open on the target's spawn in the world (the Enemies component is outside this change) and on the Elemental Sight trail.
- **A finished request pays through the quest-finished event.** The world emits `finishQuest` with the quest and its progress before and after, on the one move that finishes it. The Mondstadt requests bind their quests (`getReputationRequestBindings`, a quest the groups repeat bound by its first row) into `getFinishedReputationRewards`, which pays each reward through the same payment the exploration thresholds make. Only requests pay: the Reputation quest rows that bind by `parentQuestId` are not read yet, so task 3.1 stays open on that half.
- **Task 3.2 is built for the grocery by the shops change, and waits on the food shop.** The Shop screen is mounted (`ScreenKind.Shop` in `World/Session`), and `discountShopGood` prices a good at its discounted Mora, its two scenario tests named as the task's verify asks. Only the grocery (shop type 1004) carries the discount level, read from its city's shop rows by `readCityDiscountLevel` in the shops commit `275fddffe6`: no other Mondstadt shop's builder passes one. The food shop (204) is named by its function's text but has no goods builder, so the task stays open on it, the wait named in `x-waiting`.
- **Task 4.1 waits on three things.** Natlan's keeper has no placed resident, its bounties have no hunting rows (the dump's `HuntingRefresh` names no city 6), and the notice-to-tribe mapping is not in the tables read. Nothing of Natlan is built yet.
- **One change, one `x-waiting`, naming only what blocks.** Tasks 2.1 and 2.2 wait on the screen's offer, 2.2 also on the target's spawn and its trail, 3.1 on the Reputation quest rows, and 4.1 on Natlan's data. The change is not split: the offer joins 2.1 and 2.2, and the rest of the change is built on the same screen. The metadata rule now names a partly blocked change's wait, as the roadmap's decision settles.

## How it works

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  BOUNTY["A bounty: trail by Elemental Sight, target defeated"] --> CLAIM["Claimed back with the keeper"]
  REQUEST["A request: items delivered or a camp cleared"] --> CLAIM
  CLAIM --> EXP["Reputation EXP for its nation, under the week's limit"]
  EXPLORE["Exploration thresholds, Reputation quests"] --> EXP
```

## Scope and order

**Today:** Mondstadt's Reputation is data and rules, and its reward page is a component with a fixture on the parity page. Nothing places the keeper, draws a bounty's trail, counts exploration or applies a discount.

**This adds, in order:**

1. **Mondstadt's keeper**, placed in region data, opening the screen and offering its requests from level 2. Her place is estimated from the wiki's description of where she stands in Mondstadt, by the Knights of Favonius Headquarters, marked provisional; the record's `Reputation` building point already marks the spot she keeps.
2. **Requests**, as world quests the quest reader carries.
3. **Bounties**, once Elemental Sight draws a trail and the target spawns.
4. **The discount at the shops**, its grocery's part built by the shops change, its tests here.
5. **Each later nation**, and Natlan's tribes and supply notices, as their regions are built.

## Data and measures

- **Read from the game's tables, still to read:** `ReputationQuestExcelConfigData`, the Reputation quests with their rewards, once the quest reader carries them. Its rows name a quest by `parentQuestId` and a reward, but not the quest that pays, so the binding is the quest reader's to settle.
- **Read from the wiki:** which shops each discount covers, as the ids of the shops its function's text names.
