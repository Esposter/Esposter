# Spiral Abyss

The Spiral Abyss is the game's standing combat challenge: a domain at Musk Reef, reached through the wormhole off Cape Oath, of twelve floors with three chambers each, cleared against the clock for stars and Primogems. Its first eight floors, the Abyss Corridor, are cleared once; its last four, the Abyssal Moon Spire, change their enemies and reset every month. Its rules are built as the [Spiral Abyss page](../../specs/genshin/spiral-abyss/design.md) records, read from the game's own tower tables. What remains is how a chamber is fought and entered, and it is fought with the [character kits](../genshin-character-kits/design.md) and the [party](../genshin-party/design.md)'s teams, entered as a [domain](../genshin-domains/design.md) is, so this page waits on those too.

## Decisions

- **A chamber's waves are camps of its scene.** Each half's enemies are the table's list for that half, placed as the enemy camps of the chamber's own domain scene, and a half is defeated when its camps are. A two-team chamber with no second list, as the monolith chambers have none, takes its second half's enemies from its scene the same way.
- **A chamber is fought in a stand-in arena beside the entrance, on the domain's camp mechanism, until its scene is read.** A half's monsters spawn as one camp (`createAbyssHalfCamp`): each monster is the kind its describe is fought as (`spiralAbyss/enemyKinds`), at the chamber's monster level, standing on a circle of `ABYSS_ARENA_SPREAD` (6, PROVISIONAL) round the entrance. The camp is the domain arena's own mechanism, so the defeats of the camp in play count, the half's last enemy advances the chamber, and the next half's camp spawns; a chamber a half of which lists no describe is not entered, so the current period's chambers, which carry no lists, stay closed. The chamber's clock runs while the world is open (`stepAbyssArena`): a clock run out fails the chamber, drops its camp and awards no stars, and a clear stops the clock at the seconds left, from which its stars are recorded (`recordAbyssChamberClear`) as progress for the world session. Not yet built: the stars are not saved, so they are lost on leaving the world; the clear's rewards are recorded as given but their items are not paid, as `claimAbyssRewards` is not called; and the carry is not applied to the next chamber, which waits on the save. The second half is not fought until the scene is read (tasks 2.2). The period's blessing applies no effect: its module is Stardread's, which waits on the world's reaction event (the blessing bullet above names the blocker), so the period's blessing applies no effect in this change.
- **The chamber's end is carried on.** Each member's health and energy are recorded when a chamber completes, and the next chamber starts from those records, as the wiki's Spiral Abyss page gives them, with the healing after the clear not recorded. The record is a snapshot taken at the clear, so what the party does after it, its healing included, does not change what the next chamber starts from: the snapshot, not the party's live state, is what carries. `recordAbyssChamberClear` takes the party's members and returns the snapshot as `carry`; the next chamber's start applies it with `applyAbyssCarry` to the members still in the party, and the carry is held for the session only, not saved.
- **A reward id is its reward row's items, written beside the floors' rewards.** The tower table's rewards name 232 reward ids, each resolved through the shared reward reader to its items (floor one's nine-star reward is 100 Primogems and 25,000 Mora), and the items go into the existing rewards slice rather than a new one. A claim gives each item as a pick up does, every wallet currency into the wallet and the rest into the bag, refused whole when the bag has no room for all of it, as an expedition's claim is. `settleAbyssClear` pays a clear's first-time rewards and records the clear only when that claim goes through, so a clear the bag cannot take is not recorded and the chamber can be cleared again (PROVISIONAL).
- **A chamber is entered by a party set of one team per half.** Each half is fought by one team of the player's party set, with a character in it, and no character is in both teams, as the wiki's Spiral Abyss page gives it under Divergence. A set that does not hold that has no challenge, so a chamber is not entered without one.
- **A cycle's stars and claimed rewards are saved with the save.** The Corridor is one cycle that never resets, and each Moon Spire period is its own, saved under `spiralAbyss` by the key `corridor` or the period's id. A cycle keeps each chamber's cleared state and most stars, and the reward ids its clears have claimed. The merge of two copies keeps each chamber cleared if either copy cleared it, the most stars either holds, and either copy's claimed rewards. A save written before this key has none, so a backfill adds an empty `spiralAbyss` to each save (dev run here; prod owed to the main session). The save's length ceiling counts 256 cycles of the longest cycle, the Corridor's 24 chambers (PROVISIONAL).
- **A period's blessing comes with the period, and its effect is one module per blessing.** Each Moon Spire period's schedule row names its blessing by its name's and its description's text hashes and an icon, and gives the instants it opens and closes. The periods record publishes them, and the period offered at an instant is the one whose opening covers it, read in the game's time zone. A blessing's effect is a module over the engine's reactions and damage, written once a source gives it. The current period's, Stardread's, is written first: a shockwave at an opponent's position once a character triggers Superconduct, Cryo Swirl, Frozen or Shatter on it, at most once every 2 seconds. The shockwave's damage is in no source read, so the module decides when it fires and not what it deals, and the Stellar reactions the wiki also names are not in the engine, so they do not trigger it. The kit's reactions reach the world as `reactionTriggered`, which `applyAbyssBlessingReaction` answers in a Moon Spire chamber for the arena's camp only, with the shockwave recorded in the arena's blessing state; no damage or visual reads that state, as the source gives neither.
- **A chamber's Benedictions are the three weighted lists its level row names.** Each `towerBuffConfigStrList` entry is a list of Benediction ids with their weights, and the `spiralAbyss/benedictionChoices` record publishes all three lists of each chamber as the table gives them, keyed by the chamber's level id. `readAbyssBenedictionChoices` reads it as published, and the chamber passes its own level's lists to `pickAbyssBenedictionList`. The wiki's Spiral Abyss page says the choice refreshes at each daily reset and offers one of three, and names no rule for which list a day draws. PROVISIONAL: `pickAbyssBenedictionList` takes the game day's index in turn among the three lists, until a recording or a source settles it.
- **A Benediction's constant stat is an attribute line for the party, where its description gives one (PROVISIONAL).** `getAbyssBenedictionAttributeLines` holds the stat and amount of the 19 Benedictions whose description names a constant stat (tower buff ids 1 to 16, 22, 28 and 38), each read off its buff's description in the dump: attack, crit rate and crit damage, physical damage, elemental mastery, energy recharge, defense, max HP, the seven element resistances and physical resistance, healing bonus, and cooldown reduction. It is keyed by the tower buff id, because the published choices carry no modifier, so the table is the world's only source of the stat until a publish carries one. The Session holds the chamber's lines as `partyBonusLines`, and `getCharacterAttributeLines` gives them to every character's attribute lines, so the party holds them for the chamber alone. The 17 the table leaves unbuilt are named here: sprint stamina, move speed and attack speed (17, 18, 32) and heavy attack stamina (36), which have no attribute; the instant heals on a trigger (19, 31, 34); the damage of a skill or attack kind (20, 21, 23) and of a weak point (27), which have no attribute; the timed or chance-based effects (24, 35); and the HP-conditional attack and defense (25, 26, 29, 30).

- **The Abyss's words are the world's name text, except its fixed words.** The blessing's name and description and each Benediction's description are text hashes, so `genshin:text names` publishes them into the name text, reading them through `NAME_TEXT_ID_KEYS` beside the item and character names. The choices record carries each Benediction's description hash, joined from the tower buff table. The fixed words the screens show (the Abyss Corridor, the Abyssal Moon Spire and the Benediction hint) are `GameTextKey` entries, since each has a manual text id.

- **A Moon Spire chamber shows its period's blessing and no Benedictions, which the wiki does not match.** The change's scenarios name the period's blessing for a Moon Spire chamber and the choices for a Corridor chamber, so `getAbyssChamberBlessing` gives one or the other. The wiki's Benediction section says every chamber's start offers one, Moon Spire included, so a Moon Spire chamber's Benedictions are left unoffered until a source settles which is true.

- **The entrance is provisional, at the centre of Cape Oath.** The wormhole's own place is in the game's scene export, which is not read yet, so the Spiral Abyss's entrance stands at the centre of Cape Oath's outline in the catalogue, inside it. The F key on it opens the Moon Spire's entry with the period's blessing. It moves to the wormhole's place once the scene export places it.
- **A chamber's enemies are its monsters, one list per half, by describe id.** The dump lists them for the Corridor's chambers and for some Moon Spire chambers, and for none of the current period's. Those wait on the game's scene group export for their dungeons, the Moon Spire's dungeons of the current period's floors.
- **A describe is fought as its base variant, the monster of it with the lowest id (PROVISIONAL).** A describe's monsters are a family of variants, and the dump gives no rule for choosing among them. The lowest id agrees with every one of the 48 kinds the regions place, each being its describe's base variant, so the rule keeps every placed fight. The enemies dataset publishes the base variants of the describes the chambers list, with the regions' kinds. A kind with no hand-written traits takes its family's, the poise and the drop family that the kinds of its family and type share (the poise of its type where they share none), and no element, attack element or energy drops, which are its own. The describe-to-kind record `spiralAbyss/enemyKinds` publishes this mapping, and `readAbyssEnemyKinds` reads it once published. A describe with no monster row (21 Moon Spire describes) or no archive entry (15, Moon Spire only) is left out, and every Corridor describe resolves: 53 of 53.

## Scope and order

**Today:** the Abyss's floors, chambers, clock, halves, monolith, stars, unlocks, rewards and periods are built as pure rules, so the [as-built page](../../specs/genshin/spiral-abyss/design.md) holds them. Nothing enters a chamber, fights one, or shows the Abyss.

**This adds, in order:**

1. **The rewards' items.** `buildSpiralAbyss.ts` builds the `spiralAbyss/rewards` record as `{ floorRewards, rewardItems }`: the floor rewards as now, and every reward id they name resolved through `readRewardMap` and `toRewardItems` (`scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/rewards/`) to its items, each `{ count, itemId }`, keyed by reward id. `readAbyssFloorRewards.ts` parses the new shape. A new `services/spiralAbyss/claimAbyssRewards.ts` takes a clear's `givenRewardIds`, the items map, the bag, the wallet and the names, and gives every item through `pickUpDroppedItem`, returning undefined, with nothing changed, when any item overflows the bag. Tests: `claimAbyssRewards.test.ts` (a reward of Primogems and Mora lands both in the wallet and nothing in the bag, a material goes into the bag, and a bag with no room refuses the whole claim).
2. **The chambers' scenes and their enemy camps**, with each half's wave defeated by its camps, once the domain scene is derived ([scene derivation](../../specs/genshin/scene-derivation/design.md)).
3. **The screen and the chamber's loop**: entering a chamber with the party set, the clock run over real play, a half advanced on its last enemy defeated, and the health and energy carried into the next chamber.
4. **The blessings**: the Moon Spire's blessing of each period, and the Corridor's blessing choices, each written once its effect is read from a source.
5. **The wormhole at Cape Oath**, once Musk Reef's scene is derived.

## Data and measures

- **Read from the wiki:** the wiki's API answers (`api.php?action=parse&page=<title>&prop=wikitext`, read 2026-10-18) where its pages refuse a scripted fetch. Its Spiral Abyss page gives the clock (300 seconds on floors 1 to 4, 600 from floor 5), the opening at Adventure Rank 20, the monolith to be kept intact, the six stars that open the next floor, the two teams of a divergent floor, and the party's HP and energy: each character's are recorded when a chamber completes, and the next chamber starts at the recorded level, with healing after the clear not recorded. The clock and the opening rank are provisional until a recording measures them. Each period's blessing wording is on its own page, `Spiral Abyss/Blessing of the Abyssal Moon/<date>` (the category Blessings of the Abyssal Moon lists them), and the Benedictions, the Corridor's choices, on `Spiral Abyss/Benediction of the Abyss`: one of three each chamber, refreshed daily, lasting the floor, the chamber or immediately.
- **Read from the game's tables:** each chamber's domain scene; `TowerBuffExcelConfigData`, whose 36 Benedictions carry a text hash and a lasting type; each `TowerLevelExcelConfigData` row's `towerBuffConfigStrList`, published as `spiralAbyss/benedictionChoices`; and each `TowerScheduleExcelConfigData` row's `buffnameTextMapHash`, `descTextMapHash`, `icon` and `closeTime`, published on the periods record beside the open time it already carried. Which of a chamber's lists a day offers is not settled by any source read yet.
- **Measured:** the chamber's clock and the two-half carry, off recordings, as the [roadmap](../genshin-roadmap/tasks.md)'s References sought list states. The Abyss's opening rank is the wiki's, not yet measured.
