# Spiral Abyss

## Why

The Spiral Abyss is the game's standing combat challenge at Musk Reef: twelve floors of three chambers each, cleared against the clock for stars and Primogems. Its rules are built as pure rules, so nothing yet enters a chamber, fights one or shows the Abyss. A chamber is fought with the character kits and the party's teams and entered as a domain is, so the Abyss waits on those as well.

## What Changes

The player can enter the Abyss's chambers, fight their two halves, claim the rewards of their stars and carry their health into the next chamber.

- A clear's rewards are given as items: wallet currencies to the wallet and everything else to the bag, with a claim refused whole when the bag has no room for all of it.
- A chamber is entered with a party set, its clock runs over real play, and its stars are awarded against the time left.
- Each half of a chamber is defeated when its enemy camps are, and a two-team chamber's second half takes its enemies from its scene.
- Each character's health and energy at a chamber's clear carry into the next chamber.
- Each Moon Spire period's blessing and the Corridor's blessing choices are offered once their effects are read from a source.
- The wormhole at Cape Oath leads to Musk Reef once its scene is derived.

## Impact

The rewards record gains its items, and the claim takes the bag beside the wallet. The world session gains entry to the Abyss's chambers and the return from them. The enemy camps and the party's health and energy are carried between chambers. The blessings and the wormhole are added as their sources and scenes are read.
