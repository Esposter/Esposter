# Character Trials

## Why

A Character Trial lets a player try a character they do not own. The trial is a short domain of its own: the character comes at a preset level with the talent level and artifacts its trial row gives, and the first clear of the domain pays a reward. The game's trial rows are in the 7.1 exports, and the Wish screen's banners already name the characters they feature, so a trial can be reached from the banner of its character.

## What Changes

- A banner whose featured five-star character has a trial shows a trial button, which opens that trial's domain.
- A trial's party is its character at the trial's level, with the talent level and artifacts its template gives.
- A trial's enemies stand in the domain arena, and the first clear of a trial gives its reward once.

The trial's weapon, its enemies and its constellations are not in the trial rows, so they are named as blocked in the tasks rather than guessed.
