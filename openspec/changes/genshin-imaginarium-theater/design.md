# Imaginarium Theater

The Imaginarium Theater is the game's roguelike combat challenge, held in the Mondstadt Library's Theater Lobby. Each season names three elements and a few characters; a player brings a cast of their own of those elements and plays through acts, each character able to perform twice, choosing events between battles that add characters or blessings. It is fought with the [character kits](../../specs/genshin/kits/README.md) and opens at [Adventure Rank](../archive/2026-10-22-genshin-adventure-rank/design.md) 35 with its quest, so this page waits on both.

The [as-built page](../../specs/genshin/imaginarium-theater/design.md) holds the season schedule, the difficulties and their level floors, Vigor, Blessing Level, the opening character's bonus and the Stella rewards. What stays here is the cast's makeup, the stage, the acts and events, and the screen.

## Decisions

- **The stages' enemies come from the wiki's season pages, joined to the game's monsters by exact name, codex entry and curve.** Each season's page (`Imaginarium Theater/Seasons/<day the season begins>`, one per season the wiki has written up, listed by the wiki's own `allpages` query and cached through `fetchEnemyWikiPages`) gives every battle's domain enemies block: each variant's enemy names, and its Easy Mode level. Only the Easy variants are stages here, the lowest difficulty: a variant the wiki gives no Easy level (the Defense Sequence and High-Pressure Defense battles) is not an Easy stage and is left out, 657 of the 957 variants. A name resolves to a codex entry, the describe id the enemies builder reads for each kind's describe row, when every monster whose English name (`readMonsterNames`, read by text id from the dump's English text map) is that name shares one describe id. A name whose monsters span several entries, or that no monster holds, is refused with its count of entries (`entries`), never guessed. The entry's canonical monster is the one its monster codex row names, filed under the same entry (`findCanonicalMonsterRow`); an entry whose codex names no such row, or whose monster has no base health, attack and defense curve, is refused (`codex`). The enemy's stats at the stage's Easy level are its monster row's base health, attack and defense times the multipliers its grow curves give at that level, read from the dump's monster curve table (`toImaginariumStageEnemy`), as the game computes them. The record is `imaginarium/stages`, one entry per Easy stage with its season, act, battle, variant, Easy level, its enemies (monster id, describe id, health, attack and defense) and its refusals. A stage with no refusals is complete.
- **A Companion event draws its offers at runtime from the run's eligible characters of its pool's element.** The wiki's Events page gives the three Companion events' pools: Select offers two characters of an element, Random two characters of any element, and Invite six of any element, for 80, 80 and 100 Fantasia Flowers. The dump's two Companion effect rows are `REFRESH_ADD_AVATAR_CARD_EVENT_CASE`, effect 1 `1,2,1,80` and effect 17 `1,3,1,80`, both 80 flowers, so Select's 80 agrees with the dump, and the element tags that name the pools are the Open item (b)'s chain. The dump holds no row for Random or for Invite: their prices are the wiki's, and Invite's 100 has no row in the dump. The draw is `drawImaginariumCompanionOffers`: the characters the season admits at the difficulty (`checkIsImaginariumCastEligible`) of the pool's element, or of any element when it names none, each given a draw from the world's one seeded random source (`createSeededRandom`), the first `offerCount` by draw taken. The price is `spendImaginariumCount`'s. The draw takes the pool's element as its input, so the element of each Companion event is read from the event's own pool row when a run dispatches it, which is not built.
- **The cast by the season's rules.** Four members perform each stage. The Alternate Cast is the player's own characters of the season's three elements, plus its four special guests whatever their element, at least level 60 for the lower difficulties and 70 above, as many as the difficulty asks. The opening characters are the player's own or their trial versions. A cast's stats are fixed once chosen: each member carries its priced Max HP and its Burst's energy cost, and every member starts each stage at full health and energy, a health share of one over its Max HP and its energy at its Burst's cost (`performImaginariumStage`).
- **Events between battles.** A Battle Event gives Fantasia Flowers and the choice of one of two Alternate Cast members to join the Principal Cast. The other events are paid in Fantasia Flowers: a Companion event invites a character, a Brilliant Blessing grants a reaction's buff, and a Mystery Cache grants an effect, some for the worse. Events can be rerolled, and a run can be rewound to the end of its last boss stage a limited number of times.
- **Vigor comes back only from a Mystery Cache's option.** A character with none left cannot perform again this run.
- **Stella for each act's star challenge.** An act's star challenge, enemies defeated in time, a number defeated, or a monolith kept above its health, gives one Stella, and none when it is not met (wiki, Stella). `gainImaginariumStella` answers it. The run's Stellas are counted and their rewards given as the built rule counts them.
- **Five difficulties**, each with its acts and cast, from the season's tables. The acts are the dump's `actCount` (`GOMPDDJNDAE`, see the difficulty fields below), and the cast's count is read from `alternateCastMinimum` and `alternateCastExtra`, also below. The five levels are read already.
- **A season's cast is the role combat avatar table's.** `RoleCombatAvatarConfigExcelConfigData`, joined to a schedule row by its `avatarConfigId`, holds each season's three elements, its six opening characters and its four special guests, under the dump's keys at 7.1, named by what they hold: `elementCodes` is `FMNCKJDCADH` (three codes, then a trailing 0), `openingCharacterIds` is `MFNFDFPOAMC` (the openers' avatar ids) and `specialGuestIds` is `FKFHFHFGMEM` (the guests' avatar ids). The wiki's elements, openers and guests match these in every season the schedule holds, the guests by name. The codes read 2 Pyro, 3 Hydro, 4 Dendro, 5 Electro, 6 Cryo, 7 Anemo and 8 Geo, as `ElementCodeElementMap` holds them. The writer keeps the check that each season's openers hold exactly its three elements (`checkIsOpeningElementsMatched`), so a renamed key or a shifted code fails the run rather than writing a wrong cast. Its six trial ids (`openingTrialIds`, `GCLLBFHCLFK`, the trial versions of the openers) are read from the trial avatar table as the season's `openingTrials`, each giving its opener's character and level; the writer keeps the check that they are the openers (`checkIsOpeningTrialsMatched`).
- **The cast's floor is the difficulty's.** A character joins the Alternate Cast when its element is one of the season's three or it is one of the season's special guests, whatever its element, and its level is at least the difficulty's level floor, which the difficulty slice already holds.
- **What this leaves out.** The Supporting Cast, characters lent by friends, needs other players and is left with [co-op](../../deferred/genshin/co-op/design.md).

- **Vigor and performers from the wiki, provisional.** Four stage members perform each stage, every stage costs each performer one of its two Vigor, and Vigor is restored only by a Mystery Cache option (wiki, [Imaginarium Theater](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Imaginarium_Theater), Cast). The constants are `IMAGINARIUM_PERFORMER_COUNT` and `IMAGINARIUM_VIGOR`.
- **Fantasia Flowers from the events page, provisional.** The Battle Event gives 90 (Battle: Normal; Elite Assault and High-Pressure Defense 110, the Climactic Showdown 125). A Companion event costs 80 (Companion: Select and Random; Companion: Invite costs 100). Brilliant Blessings cost 60 and the rare Mystery Cache 40 (wiki, [Imaginarium Theater/Events](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Imaginarium_Theater/Events), List of Events). Only the Battle Event's 90 and Companion's 80 are built; the others wait on their events. `IMAGINARIUM_BATTLE_FLOWERS` and `IMAGINARIUM_COMPANION_FLOWER_PRICE` hold them.
- **A Battle Event offers two Alternate Cast members.** The two are the lowest-keyed of one seeded roll each over the Alternate Cast, so no member is offered twice.
- **A Mystery Cache's full restore, provisional.** The option "Surprise: Explosive Excitement" fully restores one character whose Vigor is not full (wiki, Mystery Cache). `restoreImaginariumVigor` gives that restore; the options that restore one Vigor are not built.
- **Every counted spend goes through one primitive.** A paid event and a rewind both spend a count only when it covers the cost, so `spendImaginariumCount` answers both.
- **A stage is fought on the arena's rules.** `fightImaginariumStage` refuses a stage unless four performers each hold Vigor, and a fought stage spends each performer's one Vigor and gives its outcome through `resolveDomainChallenge`, so a stage's clear, time limit and party fall are the domain challenge's. Its enemy waves and time limit are the caller's: the wiki names no stage enemies, so none are read yet, and the kit fight that deals the damage is not built.
- **The rewind's limit is the caller's, open.** The wiki says a rewind may be used "a limited amount of times" without a figure. The run states its rewinds left and each rewind spends one through `spendImaginariumCount`, so the figure is set once it is read from the game. The run's checkpoint, the Principal Cast at the end of its last boss stage, is set by the caller, since the screen that ends a boss stage is not built: `rewindImaginariumRun` returns the run to it, and `startImaginariumStage` leads to that rewind when the cast cannot perform.
- **The difficulty fields, named.** `actCount` (`GOMPDDJNDAE`) is the acts a difficulty runs: 3, 6, 8, 10 and 10 at levels one to five. The wiki names Easy's three acts in 26 of its 29 seasons and Normal's six in 21; Hard, Visionary and Lunar are not labelled per act, so their 8, 10 and 10 are read, not confirmed. `alternateCastMinimum` (`DKAOJONAPHB`) is the Alternate Cast a difficulty needs beyond its six opening characters: 2, 6, 10, 16 and 22, which is the wiki's minimum to start of 8, 12, 16, 22 and 28 less six at every level. `alternateCastExtra` (`ADJGFDEJKFN`) is what a difficulty allows beyond that minimum: 2, 2, 4, 4 and 4, the wiki's maximum of 10, 14, 20, 26 and 32 less its minimum at each level (the dump leaves it out of the first seasons' rows, and the table reader writes it as protobuf's 0 there, so the record holds 0). So a difficulty's cast runs from six plus `alternateCastMinimum` up to that plus `alternateCastExtra`.
- **Rerolls, provisional.** The events page says the player "can reroll the events if they need to" and gives neither a base count nor a price, so a reroll spends one of the run's rerolls (`IMAGINARIUM_REROLL_COST`), and the run starts with the count it is given, which is open. A Mystery Cache's "Surprise: Reroll!" gives two chances (`IMAGINARIUM_REROLL_CHANCES`; wiki, [Imaginarium Theater/Events](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Imaginarium_Theater/Events), Mystery Cache). `rerollImaginariumEvents` spends one and takes the act's fresh set from the caller's draw, and `gainImaginariumRerolls` gives the two chances.

## How it works

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  SEASON["The latest season begun: elements, openers, guests"] --> CAST["The cast chosen, stats fixed"]
  CAST --> ACT["An act: choose an event"]
  ACT -->|"battle"| STAGE{"Four with Vigor left?"}
  STAGE -->|"yes"| FIGHT["Performed: one Vigor each, Flowers won"]
  STAGE -->|"no"| REWIND["Rewind to the last boss stage"]
  ACT -->|"Flowers spent"| BUY["Companion, Brilliant Blessing or Mystery Cache"]
  FIGHT --> ACT
  BUY --> ACT
  FIGHT -->|"star challenge met"| STELLA["A Stella"]
```

In the world, `models/imaginarium/ImaginariumSeason.ts` gains the three fields in its interface and schema, and a new `services/imaginarium/checkIsImaginariumCastEligible.ts` answers whether a character of an element and a level may join a season's Alternate Cast at a difficulty. Tests: `toImaginariumSeason.test.ts` (the codes `[2, 5, 6, 0]` read Pyro, Electro and Cryo, the trailing 0 dropped), `checkIsOpeningElementsMatched.test.ts` (the openers' elements matching and differing from the season's three) and `checkIsImaginariumCastEligible.test.ts` (a character off the season's elements refused, a special guest of any element taken, a level under the floor refused).
2. **A stage**, at the lowest difficulty, fought with the character kits.
3. **The acts and Battle Events.**
4. **The paid events**, with the Mystery Cache's Vigor.
5. **The rewind, the star challenges and the other difficulties.**
6. **The Theater Lobby**, once the Mondstadt Library's interior stands.
