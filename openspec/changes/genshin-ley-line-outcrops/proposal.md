# Ley line outcrops

## Why

Ley line outcrops are the open world's resin challenges, and the rules for them are built. What is missing is the challenge itself: an outcrop that is touched and fought, the blossom that appears once its enemies fall, the claim that pays it, and the outcrop's move afterwards. Without these, the resin the game spends on outcrops has nothing to buy.

## What Changes

Touching an outcrop fights it, and its blossom pays its reward by the reader's World Level.

- Touching an outcrop spawns its region's enemies at the World Level's level, within its fight radius.
- The Ley Line Blossom appears once every enemy of the outcrop is defeated.
- Claiming a blossom with resin pays the reward row for the reader's World Level, the last row standing for every World Level past the list.
- A cleared but unclaimed outcrop stays where it is until the daily reset.
- A claimed outcrop moves to the next place in its group.
- The daily reset starts each open outcrop kind over at newly drawn places.
- Whether Liyue opens its blossoms at rank 8 and 12, or at 18, and whether a claim grants the previews' 100 Adventure EXP are left open for a decision.

## Impact

The open world's outcrops and their enemies, spawned on a touch. The blossoms and their claims, paid by World Level. The region data that holds each region's outcrop places, and the daily reset the outcrops share with the enemies.
