# Original Resin

## Why

Original Resin's count, regeneration, refills, claim prices and rewards are shipped, but the claim itself is not offered in the world. A cleared challenge leaves a blossom with no way to claim it, so the resin cannot be spent where the game spends it.

## What Changes

Pressing F at a cleared challenge's blossom offers its claims, and choosing one spends resin and grants its rewards.

- Pressing F at a blossom offers the claims the held resin affords, Condensed Resin among them at a ley line or a domain.
- Choosing a claim spends its resin and draws its rewards into the bag and the wallet.
- A cleared challenge that is never claimed gives nothing.
- Fragile Resin used from the bag restores sixty Original Resin.

## Capabilities

- **genshin/original-resin** (modified)

## Waiting on

- The first blossom a challenge places, from the ley line outcrops (each outcrop placed at a Ley Line Blossom point of the official map): `openspec/changes/genshin-ley-line-outcrops/`, or from the bosses: `openspec/changes/genshin-bosses/`.
- Using an item from the bag, which Fragile Resin needs: `openspec/changes/genshin-inventory/`.

## Impact

The F prompt at a blossom in reach, the list of offers, the spending of resin and the rewards that go into the bag and the wallet, and Fragile Resin's use from the bag.
