# Crafting

## Why

The as-built crafting page holds the recipes, their rules, the instructions that open them, the characters' crafting talents, the bench's screen and the save's crafting slice. Nothing places the bench in the world, so a player cannot craft at one, and the bench does not yet spend from the bag and wallet or draw Condensed Resin's resin from the Original Resin wallet.

## What Changes

Place the crafting bench in the world and open the crafting screen from it.

- A city's bench stands at the place its scene group record gives, and is used through the interaction prompt.
- Crafting at the bench spends the recipe's materials from the bag and its mora from the wallet.
- Condensed Resin takes its resin from the Original Resin wallet.
- The bench shows the recipes the party has learned and their crafted counts.

## Capabilities

- **genshin/crafting** (modified)

## Waiting on

None.

## Impact

The world gains the bench and its prompt, and the crafting screen opens from it with the party's crafter picked. The bag, the wallet and the Original Resin wallet are spent from. The keeper stays a resident placed on his own page, and the essential oils and the Xiao Lantern stay with the quests.
