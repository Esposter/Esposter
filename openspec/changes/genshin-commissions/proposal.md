# Commissions

## Why

The daily commission tasks, their dealing, their claims and Katheryne's bonus are built. What a commission does in the world once it is dealt is not: a scene task does not yet swap its area's enemies, a quest task does not finish with its quest, the day's four are not kept with the moment they were dealt, and the handbook does not show them. Each of those is part of how a daily task reaches the player.

## What Changes

A commission acts in the world once dealt, and the day's four are kept until the daily reset.

- A scene task replaces its area's enemies and objects with its own while it is open, and the area's own return when the task is done and the area reloads.
- A commission is not dealt at a place a quest in progress holds.
- A quest task finishes when its quest completes.
- The Adventurer Handbook's Commissions tab lists the day's four with the preferred region.
- The day's four are kept with the instant they were dealt, and a day dealt before the latest daily reset is dealt again.
- A region's commissions join the deal once its quest is done.
- A claim sends its Mora, items and Companionship EXP to where each is kept.
