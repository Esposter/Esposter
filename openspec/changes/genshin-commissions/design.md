# Commissions

The as-built [commissions](../../specs/genshin/commissions/design.md) page holds the daily tasks, their dealing, their claims and Katheryne's bonus. What remains is what a commission does in the world once it is dealt: the area it swaps, the quest it finishes with, the day the player keeps, and the handbook that shows the four.

## Decisions

- **The scene swap is the [commissions page](../../specs/genshin/commissions/design.md)'s rule.** Its old groups stand aside for its new ones until it is done and the area reloads, so a commission replaces the enemies, objects and chests that stood there. The camps and objects a scene task swaps in stand where the [spawned places](../genshin-spawned-places/design.md) fit them, around the task's own named point.
- **A commission is blocked where a quest stands.** A task whose place a quest in progress holds is never dealt. Dealing takes that set of places, and the set is read from the quests in progress.
- **A region's pool joins on its opening quest.** The dump's `CityTaskOpenExcelConfigData` names the main quest each city opens with: Liyue's 2211102, Inazuma's 7280801 and Sumeru's 7380201. Mondstadt's 2003202 is not carried: its sub quest holds no words and no objective, so the world has no quest to finish for it. A region's tasks join the deal once that quest is among the finished main quests, and a region no quest is named for (Natlan, Fontaine and Nod-Krai) joins as its tasks are read. The finished set is read from the quests the world holds, so until a save keeps its finished main quests, the three named regions do not join a session's deal.
- **Each region's daily tasks are one record a region**, `commissions/<region>`, as the [hosted game data](../../specs/genshin/hosted-game-data/design.md) keys a region. The builder writes one for each city the shared city-to-region map names, each holding that region's tasks with the reward tiers and bonus every region shares.
- **City 7 is Nod-Krai, and city 8 is filed under no region.** The dump's expedition icon for city 7 is `UI_ChapterIcon_NodKrai`, so the shared city-to-region map files city 7 under Nod-Krai. City 8 is named by no region in the map. Open: its icon, `UI_ChapterIcon_Snezhnaya`, names a catalogue region, so the mapping may be Snezhnaya's rather than Nod-Krai's. Until that is confirmed, the publish has no Snezhnaya daily-task record and no Snezhnaya fishing stock.
- **The handbook shows the day's four.** The Adventurer Handbook's Commissions tab lists them with the preferred region, as the game shows them, each title and description the game's own words by text id. Each commission carries its preferred region, read from the dump's city of its task through the city-to-region map the fishing pools share, and the text id of its title, so the tab names both from the game's own words. Nod-Krai has no region name key in the dump yet, so its commissions list no region until one is read.
- **Kept with the player's progress**, and every unfinished one replaced at the daily reset, which the world reads from the game's day as the enemies' respawn does. The alternative, dealing the four afresh on each visit, is rejected: a reload would reroll the day's commissions, so the player could choose the ones they want.
- **The kept day is the moment it was dealt.** The save holds the day's four, their counts, the claimed ids and the instant they were dealt, and nothing derived; a day dealt before the latest daily reset (`computeLastDailyReset`, 04:00 on the server's clock, the reset every system reads) is stale and is dealt again, as the weekly count reads its week from `computeLastWeeklyReset`. A day dealt with no commissions in it is dealt again too, since the rank may have reached the unlock since it was dealt.
- **A quest task finishes with its quest.** Its finish count is its quest's one completion, which the builder writes into the record, since the dump leaves most quest tasks' count at zero. `useWorldQuests` emits `finishQuest` with the quest's id when a quest in progress completes, and `advanceCommissionForQuest` advances the dealt, unclaimed quest task whose quest it is by that one doing, so `checkIsCommissionFinished` reads it finished. The session's listener advances the dealt task once the slice is read, and earns the quest's Encounter Points whether or not a day is dealt.
- **A claim's rewards are routed as a blossom's claim is.** `routeCommissionClaim` sends the claim's Mora and the Mora its Adventure EXP pays past rank 60 into the wallet, its Adventure EXP into the rank through `gainAdventureExp`, its Companionship EXP to the deployed team's friendship through `gainCompanionshipExp`, and every other item into the bag through `addInventoryItem`. A bag that cannot take an item whole refuses the claim: it returns undefined, nothing is routed, and the claim is not recorded, as `claimResinReward` refuses a claim the bag cannot hold. Encounter Points are not in the wallet, so the claim's count is the caller's to keep.
- **An Archon or Story quest's completion earns four Encounter Points.** `getQuestEncounterHalfPoints` gives the amount by the quest's kind, and the other kinds give none until their amounts are settled. The wiki marks shorter Archon quests and some Event Story quests as giving less, so their amounts stay open with the other sources (section 6). The amounts are held in half points, so a half point from a chest or a collected item is held whole, and the claim's count is the whole points held.
- **The points are held for the daily reset they were earned on, up to the storage.** The save holds `encounterPoints` as the reset they were earned on (`earnedOn`) and the half points held. `readEncounterPoints` reads a balance earned before the latest daily reset (`computeLastDailyReset`) as none, and `computeEncounterPointStorage` caps the held half points at four points less one for each daily commission finished that day, as the wiki's storage rule has it. Points held stay when the storage has since shrunk below them. Points past the storage are kept as Long-Term Encounter Points (the next decision), and `claimCommissionWithPoints` spends a claim's point from the daily points (the decision after), and the handbook's Commissions tab makes the claims (the decision below).
- **The claims are made on the handbook's Commissions tab.** The game's Adventure Encounters panel sits at the foot of the Adventurer Handbook's Commissions tab, under "Encounter Points Stage Rewards" (`GameTextKey` `UI_ADVENTURE_INVESTIGATION_DAILY_ATTENDANCE_REWARD`), and it holds both the Encounter Points claim ("Use Long-Term Encounter Points" converts one for 30 Original Resin, per the game's own text) and the commission claims that spend a point. Each row's claim is `claimCommissionWithPoints`, shown only where `toHandbookDayCommissions` finds it claimable; the foot's button is `convertLongTermEncounterPoint`. Katheryne's bonus is claimed at her branch once the four are claimed, and that is built (`takeKatheryneBonus`, see the [commissions](../../specs/genshin/commissions/design.md) page). The claim of a commission's own reward at that branch is not built here: the game's text sends a player there for the rewards of a finished commission, which `claimCommissionWithPoints` also pays, so it is a second surface for the same call. Provisional until a recording measures the panel's layout, and the panel opens at Adventure Rank 24 on the wiki (`checkIsAdventureEncountersUnlocked`): below that rank the handbook gives the rows no point claim and the panel is hidden. The panel's daily count is the whole points held over the storage's limit, shown bare, because the dump has no label for the count; its layout waits on the same recording.
- **A claim spends a whole point from the half points held.** `claimCommissionWithPoints` reads the points held at `now`, passes their whole points (`floor(halfPoints / 2)`) to `claimCommissionReward`, and takes two half points back for each whole point the claim spent. A claim that `routeCommissionClaim` refuses spends none, so the point stays held.
- **The points past the storage are kept as Long-Term Encounter Points, reset with the major version.** `computeEncounterPointOverflow` takes what the storage turns away from an earn, and `earnLongTermEncounterPoints` adds it to the long-term points held in `LONG_TERM_MAJOR_VERSION`, so points earned in an earlier major version read as none (`readLongTermEncounterPoints`). The constant is set by hand at 7, the major version of the 7.1 data, because the client carries no game version and no published data records one (the scripts read the install's version at build time, for the city areas only), so it moves with each major version. `convertLongTermEncounterPoint` turns one long-term point (two half points) into a daily point for `LONG_TERM_RESIN_COST` (30) Original Resin, and only where the daily points are a whole point short of the storage.
- **Collected items and the first Oculus earn half points.** A gathering point picked up into the bag emits `gatheringPickedUp` with its item's id, and a Crimson Agate, Lumenspar or Spirit Carp (dump ids 107010, 107016 and 107026) earns the half point `getCollectedEncounterHalfPoints` gives, as the wiki gives 0.5 each. An Oculus earns one on its first collection, through the `oculusFound` event `useWorldStatues` emits. Mnemonic Cluster is named and earns none, as the wiki gives no amount for it, and the Plumes of Purifying Light earn none, as the dump's tables name no id for them. A chest's item and a dropped item raise only the quest event `questEvent`, which earns nothing here: the wiki counts a chest once, as a chest (task 6.4), so its items must not earn on top of it.
- **The later reset's points win a merge.** A save merged from a guest and an account keeps the balance earned on the later daily reset, and on the same reset the larger count.

## Scope and order

**This adds, in order:**

```text
packages/genshin-world/src/
├── models/commission/CommissionDaySave.ts
└── services/commission/
    └── checkIsCommissionDayCurrent.ts
```

1. **The kept day.**
   - `CommissionDay` (`packages/genshin-world/src/models/commission/CommissionDay.ts`) gains `dealtAt: Temporal.Instant`.
   - The daily reset is `packages/genshin-world/src/services/time/computeLastDailyReset.ts`, the server's 04:00 at or before `now` in the [reset clock](../../specs/genshin/reset-clock/design.md), so this change adds no copy of it.
   - `checkIsCommissionDayCurrent.ts`: whether a day's `dealtAt` is at or after the daily reset at or before `now`.
   - The save: `CommissionDaySave.ts`, the schema of the day with `dealtAt` as an ISO string (`z.iso.datetime()`, as `AchievementProgressSave` keeps `finishedAt`) and the dealt and claimed lists each at most `COMMISSIONS_PER_DAY` long; `genshinSaveSchema` gains `commissionDay: commissionDaySaveSchema` with no default, the stored saves backfilled to it when it lands, and a new player's save holding an empty day dealt at the epoch; `GenshinSaveState` gains `commissionDay`, read in `readGenshinSave` and written in `toGenshinSave`.
   - Tests: the reset clock's `computeLastDailyReset.test.ts` (a moment before 04:00 reads the day before's reset, one at 04:00 reads its own) and `checkIsCommissionDayCurrent.test.ts` (dealt before the reset is stale, after it current), beside each; `readGenshinSave.test.ts` gains a day read back from its save with its `dealtAt`.
   - No caller deals yet, so nothing in the session changes.
2. **Scene tasks**, swapping their area's camp while open. Waits on the area's camps, placed from the official map's enemy marks as the [spawned places](../genshin-spawned-places/design.md) proposal sets out.
3. **Quest tasks**: a quest's completion reaching its commission, once the quests report a completion.
4. **The handbook's Commissions tab** and the commissions' words.
5. **Routing and the other regions**: a claim's Mora, items and EXP each sent where it is kept, Companionship EXP through the [friendship grant](../../specs/genshin/companionship/design.md), and the other regions' pools.
6. **Encounter Points earned**: the quests' points, the storage they are held in, the daily reset they are kept to, and the save field. The other sources follow with their amounts (section 6).

## Data and measures

- **Read from the game's tables:** the scene points the tasks stand at, and the groups a scene task swaps, which the area's extraction holds once it is run.
- **Placed by the spawned places:** the camps and objects a scene task swaps in.
- **Read from the recordings:** the Adventure Treasure Pack that the wiki lists on Katheryne's bonus, which the dump's preview names at a count of zero, is settled by a clip of the bonus being claimed ([roadmap](../genshin-roadmap/tasks.md)).

## Open questions

- **A failed load keeps yesterday's day.** When the save's day cannot be read, `packages/genshin-world/src/composables/useWorldCommissions.ts` keeps the saved day as `commissionDay`, so yesterday's dealt commissions are offered as today's. Its header comment promises the opposite, that a failed load deals no day. Whether a failed load clears the saved day is the open decision; until it is taken, the comment and the code disagree.
