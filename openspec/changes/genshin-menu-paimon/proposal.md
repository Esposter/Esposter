# Menu Paimon

## Why

The Paimon menu's shell, its pause of the world and its entries are built, and the HUD's Paimon button opens it. What the menu lacks is Paimon floating beside it, which waits on an official model, and the profile card's fields, which wait on the systems behind them. The menu is whole without her, but its profile card shows only a name.

## What Changes

Paimon floats beside the menu once an official model is held, and the profile card gains its fields as the systems behind them land.

- Paimon appears to the right of the menu with her idle animations, drawn from an official model the reader holds, and the menu works whole without her.
- Community, Version Highlights, Feedback and Survey are drawn as disabled entries, and Training Guide is left out of the contents.
- The profile card shows the signed-in account's name and avatar, or the default name Traveler when signed out.
- The profile card gains Adventure Rank and World Level once the rank system lands, and its signature, namecard, birthday and UID each join when the system behind it lands.
- The menu's side bar opens the Time, Settings and quit screens, which are their own proposals.

## Capabilities

- **genshin/menu-paimon** (new)

## Waiting on

- Paimon's official model pack, which the user downloads among the developer's extracted packs, read as every character's is.
- The characters' reader, which draws Paimon beside the menu: `openspec/changes/genshin-characters/`.
- The Adventure Rank system, which supplies the profile card's rank and World Level: `openspec/changes/genshin-adventure-rank/`.
- The signature, namecard, birthday and UID systems, each joining the profile card as it lands.

## Impact

The Paimon menu in the open world, its layout and its icons, Paimon beside it, and the profile card's fields. The menu's block in the derived assets and the parity reference map, which lists each screen's recordings.
