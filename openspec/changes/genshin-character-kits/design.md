# Character kits

Every character on the [character kits](../../specs/genshin/kits/README.md) index has a module of its own, and the framework those modules run on is built. What is left is the element a statue gives the Traveler, the passives and constellations the modules leave out, the five swap watchers older modules keep, Hu Tao's Spirit Soother heal under half HP with her Constellations 4 and 6, Nefer's kit, which gcsim has no folder for, and the world's entry back under its size ceiling. It comes before the rest of the game's systems that act through a kit, such as talents, constellations, weapons' and artifacts' effects, and every challenge after them. It builds on [character attributes](../../specs/genshin/character-attributes/design.md), whose sums price a hit, and on the [character controller](../../specs/genshin/character-controller/design.md), whose body a kit acts from.

## Decisions

- **The Traveler's skill and burst take the element her statue gives.** She holds no element until her first resonance with a Statue of The Seven, as the game's prologue has her, and then holds the element of the statue she resonated with last ([statues page](../genshin-statues-of-the-seven/design.md)). Her skill's hit names Anemo in her record, so her behaviour's `takesWielderElement` drops that name and the hit takes the wielder's element, as her burst does by naming none. The table has one set per element form, so the statue's element would pick the set and its cooldowns and energy cost; only the Anemo set is in the record, so the other sets wait for their game data and her cooldowns and energy cost stay the Anemo set's. The skill's particles are the wiki's, not the table's, as the kits index records.
- **Passives are written with their character's module.** Each unbuilt passive is added to its character's module and opened by the ascension phase its table names, so a passive adds to its kit without a change to the framework.
- **A shared effect is built by the first module that needs it, never before.** The Traveler's kit uses a hit, and a heal and a hit's element sit with the party's damage.
- **Amber's Fiery Rain arrows are radius 4, and Every Arrow Finds Its Target widens them to 5.2.** Her kit page prices an arrow at 4 round the body, and the Ascension 1 passive widens the area by 30%, so the base arrows publish radius 4 and the passive's arrows 5.2, both citing the kit page in place of `Unsourced`; the radius 2 the record reads now is the reader's miss, not a value either source gives.
- **A periodic discharge's spawns are each held in its record.** The records already hold the lists the shared periodic-hits helper used to build, so each record holds every spawn's list, Ineffa's two discharges included, and the seven modules read their hits from it in place of building them inline. No helper comes back to rebuild what the record holds.
- **The kit reader carries a float flag.** A parsed literal written with a decimal point, a `float64(...)` conversion, and any arithmetic with a float operand yield a float value, and a division is whole-number only when both operands are whole, as Go has it; a truncated `3.0 / 2` is a wrong number read silently, which a value model has to rule out.
- **Targeting's further coefficients wait.** The view, current-target and priority coefficients wait for a camera's frustum, a kept target and a boss.

## Scope and order

**Today:** a character without a module of its own fights with the Traveler's kit until one is written, as `readCharacterKit` falls back to the Traveler's record and `createTravelerKitBehaviour`.

**This adds, in order:**

1. **The Traveler's skill and burst** for the element a statue gives them, once the [statues page](../genshin-statues-of-the-seven/design.md) holds the resonance, then one module for each playable character not yet on the [character kits](../../specs/genshin/kits/README.md) index.
2. **Passives**, each written with its character's module and opened by the phase its table names.
3. **Hu Tao's Spirit Soother heal** under half HP, with her Constellations 4 and 6 and Blood Blossom, once a kit's actions can read party HP and an enemy's status at their start.
4. **The swap watchers onto the kit events.** Klee's Sparks 'n' Splash, Xiao's Bane of All Evil, Ningguang's Star Jades and Razor's Wolf Within each end through a field that ticks on every step to see whether their character still holds the field, built before the [kit events](../../specs/genshin/kits/README.md); each moves onto `CharacterSwapped`. Xingqiu's Raincutter, set going as a normal attack starts, moves onto the same channel once the delay gcsim gives each character's attack before it is read.
5. **The rolls a hit's strike can now draw.** Klee's Explosive Spark at 50% and her Chained Reactions, and Yelan's Breakthrough Barb at 34% on each Lingering Lifeline hit, each roll from the world's random source as the hit strikes, which `KitContext.random` reaches.
6. **Nefer's kit and its overlay.** gcsim has no folder for her at v2.47.2, so her record is built from the tables' groups 12231, 12232 and 12239 and the wiki, its frames, hitmarks and areas provisional until a recording measures them.

## Data and measures

- **Still to read:** each talent's name and description by their text ids. The multipliers are read from `ProudSkillExcelConfigData` already.
- **Still to choose:** the Traveler's per-element sets. The game data holds them: each element form's skill and burst have their own proud skill groups, and the cooldowns and energy costs sit in those groups' entries. The published talent kit names one group per slot, and the published multipliers hold only the Anemo form's groups, so the per-element sets wait on a publish that carries each form's groups and on a record key per form. Until then the Anemo set's values hold for every element, and the element itself is the statue's, read from the [statues page](../genshin-statues-of-the-seven/design.md).
- **Still to find:** a source for each strike's, the charged attack's and the plunge collision's poise damage. gcsim's Anemo attack file has none.
- **Measured:** the least height a plunge starts from is what the wiki does not give, so it waits on the References sought list's `world-attacks.mkv`. Until then it is a provisional constant with its character's module.
- **Wanderer's normal attack.** gcsim sets its hitmarks and frames in `init()` (`attackReleaseNormal`, `attackFramesNormal`), which the builder does not evaluate, so the strikes have no source for their hitmarks, frames or element.
- **Mualani's charged attack.** gcsim's hitmark is `shortChargeHitmark` plus a windup that depends on the state the charge starts from, which the builder does not read, so the charge has no hitmark source or element.
- **Klee's normal attacks.** Her record holds zero hits per strike, where gcsim's `attackHitmarks` gives three, so her normal attacks deal nothing until the overlay's count is read against them.
- **Lyney's Prop Arrow is not aimed.** The kit's charged attack lost its `isAimed` flag when the record replaced the hand-written kit: `lyney.json` holds no `chargedAttack.isAimed`, because the gcsim candidate that sets it (`addGcsimChargedAttackCandidates`) is emitted only when gcsim cites an aimed hitmark for Lyney, so the body turns to its target instead of the camera's aim while the aim is held. Restoring it is a decision on the data pipeline and its publish, which the record's lock makes an explicit act.
- **Venti's and Tighnari's charged shots are not aimed.** Like Lyney's, their hand-written kits set `isAimed` on the charged attack and the records that replaced them hold no `chargedAttack.isAimed` (`venti.json`, `tighnari.json`), so the body turns to its target instead of the camera's aim while the aim is held. Tartaglia's record holds the flag, so the fix is the same publish that restores Lyney's, not a per-kit code change.
- **Xinyan's shield DoT reads its multiplier by position.** Rave's DoT hit takes its talent multiplier from the Constellation 2 pulse at the index the Riff Revolution DoT hits end on, a lookup into a sibling list, so reordering or adding a Riff Revolution DoT hit silently changes which multiplier the shield uses. The record should hold the multiplier on `hitFields.shieldDot`, which needs the record generator to emit it and a publish, so the module reads it by name.
- **Amber's Fiery Rain arrows are radius 2, where her kit page prices them at 4.** The record holds each arrow's area at radius 2 and `Unsourced` (`cc8796d10b`), the base arrows and the Every Arrow Finds Its Target arrows alike, so Ascension 1's 30% widening no longer shows in `amber.json`. The [Amber kit](../../specs/genshin/kits/amber/design.md) states an arrow is priced at 4 round the body and widened by 30%, which would be radius 4 and 5.2. Choosing between them is a design decision, and either one publishes the `hits.fieryRainArrows.*.area` and `hits.everyArrowFindsItsTargetFieryRainArrows.*.area` entries through the `kits` record, a republish that `kitOverlays.test.ts` holds the overlay to.
- **Radii gcsim holds in a table or a variable.** Itto's Saichimonji Slash and Arataki Kesagiri take their circle's radius from `chargeHitboxes[burstIndex][slash][0]`, and his high and low plunges from `highPlungeRadius` and `lowPlungeRadius`, set to their burst values by a branch. The area reader resolves only a literal radius on the circle's own line, so these areas are `Unsourced` though gcsim gives radii of 3 and 4 for the charge and 6 and 4 for the plunges under the burst. Their labels are wrong by the definition `Unsourced` carries, and the fix is for the reader to resolve a table index and a branch-set variable, a feature the area reader does not yet have, after which the entries cite gcsim and the record republishes.
- **Periodic discharge schedules.** Seven modules (Ineffa, Xingqiu, Yae Miko, Gorou, Fischl, Diluc and Durin) each build their hits from a count and an interval inline, since the shared periodic-hits helper went with the records that now hold the lists. Ineffa's two discharge lists differ by the spawn each action gives Birgitta, so the choice is whether each record holds every spawn's list or a shared helper builds them again; that choice spans seven kits.
- **Go's float division.** The constant evaluator reads a number by its value alone, so a division of two whole-valued operands truncates, as `float64(3) / 2` and `3.0 / 2` do, where Go gives 1.5. Honouring it means a float flag carried through the parsed literals, the conversions and `GoValue`, which is a choice of value model for the kit reader, not a fix to one line.

## What this does not propose

- **Talent levels and constellations.** The kit reads its talent levels and constellations; raising them is the [talents](../genshin-talents/design.md) and [constellations](../genshin-constellations/design.md) pages'.
- **The effects drawn.** A slash's trail, a field's circle and a burst's animation are the character's motion and the world's effects, matched in the [recreation passes](../genshin-recreation-passes/design.md).
