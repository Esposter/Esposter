# Genius Invokation TCG

The card game's rules engine is built, so [Genius Invokation TCG](../../specs/genshin/genius-invokation/README.md) is what stands. This proposal is what is left of it: each card's module, the duel screen opened from a resident's talk, and the duels, decks and rewards the game gives beyond the tutorial. The talk it is opened from is the [dialogue](../../specs/genshin/dialogue/design.md)'s, which is built.

## Decisions

- **Each card's effect is a module.** The game keeps its cards' behaviour in configs under `BinOutput/GCG`, which are written as each card's module over the engine's shared effects, from the card's own description where the configs are not read, as a character's kit is. A skill that names a shared effect, such as `Effect_Damage_<element>_<n>`, needs no module of its own; a character's own `Char_Skill_<id>` script is one.
- **Every card is the game's own row.** Characters, skills, costs and cards come from the `GCGCharExcelConfigData`, `GCGSkillExcelConfigData`, `GCGCostExcelConfigData` and `GCGCardExcelConfigData` tables, and names and descriptions are the game's by text id.
- **Duels are against the game's opponents.** The residents the game seats for an Invitational and the tavern's challengers play the decks `GCGDeckExcelConfigData` gives them. Winning earns their cards and Lucky Coins, the Card Shop sells cards for Lucky Coins, and the player's Player Level grows by duels, as the game earns each.
- **Opened at Adventure Rank 32 with its tutorial quest**, as the wiki gives it.
- **The Card Shop sells in Lucky Coins.** Shop 217's goods are filed under shop type 2000 and numbered from 217000, and every one is priced in Lucky Coins (107021). The bag holds Lucky Coins as a material, so `buyShopGood` pays them from the bag with no change to the purchase rule. Its 226 goods that sell no card are published with the cards, since the shop sells them in the same coin.
- **The Player Level is the game's level table.** `GCGLevelExcelConfigData` holds ten levels, each with the EXP that passes it (the tenth passes none) and its reward id. A player who has finished the tutorial quest holds level one at no EXP. A challenger's games open by the level its week row names: Marjorie's `levelCondList` opens game 1021 at level 1 and the next four at levels 3, 5, 7 and 10, so a challenger offers each game once the Player Level reaches its level. The ladder is read as the Friendship Levels' is, by one shared rule, never a twin of it.
- **Seating is estimated from the wiki.** The challengers are absent from the dump's NPC birth records: neither NPC 9701 nor 9704 is among the NPCs the open world places there, so the [scene group record](../../specs/genshin/scene-groups/design.md) holds no member for either. Each one's seat is estimated from the wiki's location for that challenger, marked provisional.
- **A further deck is a record of the hosted game data.** Each is a `gcg/deck<id>` record and more card text in every language, published by `genshin:assets gcg` and `genshin:text gcg`, with an entry of `GcgDeckLoaderMap` reading it by key. Its card modules are code and are written with the deck they serve.
- **A deck's created cards are the ones its skills name.** The dump has no field for them, so `GcgDeckIdCreatedCardIdsMap` takes them from the `$[C<id>]` tokens in the skill text of the deck's characters and its cards. Decks 11105 and 12005 take that set. The nine decks built earlier keep their hand-picked sets, which agree with the derived set for 1, 3, 7, 11002, 30111 and 30112. For 2, 4 and 11005 the set is the derived one plus the Oceanic Mimics `122011` to `122013`: the Oceanid Mimic `122010` that the skills name is summoned as one of those three kinds (its text lists them; Rhodeia's summons `Char_Skill_22012` and `Char_Skill_22013` make them through `summonGcgOceanicMimics`). Deck 4 also takes Niwabi Enshou and Aurous Blaze, which Yoimiya's skills name, and deck 11005 takes Cat-Claw Shield `111021`, which Diona's Icy Paws names.
- **A summon's end-phase piercing is a card field.** `endPhaseStandbyPiercing` on a card module is dealt by the end-phase runner before the card's own hook damage, so a card module never imports the damage path, which imports the card map.
- **A card that switches a character in takes the switch path.** `switchOnPlay` on a card module is resolved by the duel after the card's `play` hook and before its skill, through `switchGcgActiveCharacter`: the field's switch hooks fire, but no die is paid and no turn passes, because the card's own cost is what it pays.
- **A talent card runs its own skill script.** Lightning Stiletto runs `Char_Skill_14032_Ex_1`, the card's own script, which grants Keqing Electro Infusion and creates no further Lightning Stiletto, while Keqing's Stellar Restoration (`Char_Skill_14032`) creates one in hand, or, with a Lightning Stiletto already in hand, discards every copy there and grants Electro Infusion. The Electro Infusion is the status card 114032 (provisional: the card text's `$[K1002]` token is not resolved in the dump).
- **No duel against another player.** Duels with friends need other players, which [co-op](../../deferred/genshin/co-op/design.md) defers; the game gives them no reward either.
- **The Player Level and the Friendship Level read one rule.** `computeLevelReached` counts the levels an EXP total has reached over any table of `{ exp }` rows, so the card game's level and the companionship level are the same function.
- **The duel holds its random source.** `GcgDuel.random` is the seeded source its dice are rolled from and its decks shuffled with. The functions that roll or shuffle read it from the duel, so a caller passes none, and a card's draw or roll reads the duel's the same way. A choice a player makes when a card is played, such as the dice Toss-Up rerolls, is a typed argument on the play, which the duel screen supplies.

## Scope and order

**Standing:** the [as-built page](../../specs/genshin/genius-invokation/README.md) holds the rules engine, the decks already built and the duel board, which opens from a resident's talk. No duel is seated yet: the two challengers whose decks are built, Marjorie and Ellin, have no resident row in the regions' data. Every resident holds their standing talk, which offers a seated challenger's duel as a reply.
**This adds, in order:**

1. **The Player Level and the games it opens.** Built: `computeGcgPlayerLevel` (zero until the tutorial quest is finished, then the level its EXP reaches), `services/gcg/readGcgLevels.ts` reading `gcg/levels` with `gcgLevelsSchema`, and `buildGcgLevels`, which `genshin:assets gcg` writes from `toGcgLevels.ts`. Left: the real publish of `gcg/levels`, which the main session makes so the reader typechecks against the lock, and the tutorial's start, which waits on quest 70665 ("Come Try Genius Invokation TCG!") being carried, since the world has no finish event for it. **Publish, for the Mac:** the `gcg` command's dry run adds one record, `gcg/levels`, and moves no other.

2. **Seating the challengers**, at the wiki's estimated seats. Marjorie (NPC 9701, game 1021 with deck 11005) and Ellin (NPC 9704, game 1031 with deck 11002) are seated on their NPCs once the region that holds their residents is written. Each seated challenger's talk is their standing talk, published with every resident's by `genshin:text standing-talks`, and its opening line offers the duel reply `191219274`, so no talk is written for a seat.
3. **The other opponent decks**, the invitationals', the tavern challengers' and the rest the duel rows name, each given the modules its cards and skills need. The decks the early duels play are built, and the as-built page records them. Each is published as its `gcg/deck<id>` record and read through `GcgDeckLoaderMap`. Left: the 7.1 dump holds a card list (`cardList`, a plain name the reader reads as it is) for 783 of its 1132 decks and none for the other 349, among them the challengers' decks 12103, 13001, 14002, 12105 and 13003. No other table or field holds deck cards, so no mapping recovers them. Of the 349, 252 are all-monster decks, the shape of Ellin's 11002, whose spec says it holds no cards; the other 91 hold player characters and no list, which the dump does not explain. A deck with no list is not published with cards until those 91 are explained.
4. **Invitationals, tavern challengers, the Card Shop, and the EXP the duels pay into the Player Level.** Built: the Card Shop's slice, `shops/gcgCardShop`, takes shop 217's 330 goods, all priced in Lucky Coins (104 sell a card, and 226 sell items the card table does not hold). A bought card goes into the bag, since no collection state exists yet. Of the normal week-level challengers' 38 decks (Marjorie and Ellin excluded), 10 hold a card list and 28 hold none. Built: 11105, with every card modelled, and 12005, with its created cards modelled too (Sacred Cryo Pearl, Ice Lotus, Jade Screen, Stellar Restoration and Lightning Stiletto). Lightning Stiletto's Electro Infusion and its discard of copies already in hand are not modelled, and the duel plays it as a fast action, not the combat action the game gives it. Left: the other eight decks with lists hold 55 unmodelled cards, and their created cards name 29 more.

## Data and measures

- **Read from the game's tables:** the `GCG*` tables and the configs under `BinOutput/GCG`. The tables are fetched into the dump beside the standard rule's, and only the slices a duel loads are written into the world.
- **Read from the wiki:** each card's effect where its configs are not read, and each duel rule's detail.
- **Measured:** the duel board's layout against a whole-frame public recording of the board, which scores its tints, words and places. Its card art, ornate frame and profile plates are the game's and are not drawn, so they stay in the score until the user accepts the layout.
