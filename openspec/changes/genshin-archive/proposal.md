# Archive

## Why

The Archive's seven sections, their entries read from the game's codex tables, the bag's opening, the artifact sets' rule and the unlock after its quest are built. What remains is what the other sections open by doings the world does not have yet: viewpoints taken in at their places, enemies defeated, and tutorials shown. Until those doings reach the Archive, its entries for them stay locked.

## What Changes

Archive entries open and count from the doings the world does.

- Reaching a viewpoint's place offers it to be viewed, and viewing it opens its entry with a picture the world draws from its place. A viewpoint with no known place is not offered.
- Each defeat of an enemy counts toward its Living Beings entry, and the first defeat opens that entry.
- A tutorial's entry opens when its tip is first shown.
- Opened entries and their counts are kept with the player's progress.

## Capabilities

- **genshin/archive** (modified)
- **genshin/save-data** (modified)

## Waiting on

- The wildlife's strike, which the animals' Living Beings entries and their turning models need.
- A shown tip, which the Tutorials section's opening needs.
- Books placed in the world, since no world drop is a volume's material yet.

## Impact

- The Archive's sections in the Paimon menu, the viewpoints in the open world, and the enemies' defeats.
- The save, which keeps the opened entries and the defeat counts.
