# Domains

## Why

The domains page holds the opening rules, each kind by its Adventure Rank and by the day of the week, but nothing enters a domain, gives a domain's materials or runs its challenge. A domain is the instance where artifacts, talent materials and weapon ascension materials come from, the materials every growth page spends, so the domains stay closed until their entrances, screens, challenges and rewards exist.

## What Changes

Make each domain entrance a place the player can open and enter, run its challenge, and pay its reward.

- An unlocked domain entrance is a waypoint the map and jump list can jump to.
- F at an unlocked entrance opens the domain screen, listing the levels open at the player's Adventure Rank and the day's materials.
- Entering a domain fades into its scene; clearing it, timing out or losing every member returns the player to the entrance.
- A ley line disorder changes the fight as its description says.
- A cleared domain raises a Petrified Tree, which is claimed with Original Resin for its rewards.
- A one-time domain gives its reward on the first clear only.

- The scene export the other machine is making, which lays out each domain's scene.

## Impact

The world gains domain entrance landmarks and their waypoint jumps, a domain screen, the fade into and out of a domain's scene, and the challenge's waves and time limit. The landmark kinds, the jump list and the enemy camps of a domain's waves are extended. The scene points, the domain entries and the daily dungeon table are read from the game's tables, and the wiki supplies each domain's rolled drops and its weekday schedule.
