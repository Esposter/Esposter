# Combat

## Why

Auras, reactions by the game's priority, damage, the internal cooldown, shields, energy and the level multipliers are built, and so are the Lunar reactions. One family the game added later is still missing: the Stellar Glimmer reactions, Stellar-Conduct and Stellar Swirl. Each is turned on by a Stellar Jubilee passive that only a few kits carry, so neither reaction can happen in the world yet.

## What Changes

The Stellar Glimmer reactions take effect once the party holds an enabler from a Stellar Jubilee passive.

- Stellar-Conduct and Stellar Swirl deal damage by the Glimmer formula: each contributor's damage is worked out alone with standard crit, and the four highest are weighted 0.6, 0.3, 0.05 and 0.05.
- A Cryo Swirl becomes Stellar Swirl while the party enables it, and a Superconduct becomes Stellar-Conduct while the party enables that.
- With no enabler held, no Stellar reaction occurs and each stays its ordinary reaction.
