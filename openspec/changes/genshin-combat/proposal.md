# Combat

## Why

Auras, reactions by the game's priority, damage, the internal cooldown, shields, energy and the level multipliers are built, and so are the Lunar reactions. One family the game added later is still missing: the Stellar Glimmer reactions, Stellar-Conduct and Stellar Swirl. Each starts from a gadget that only two kits place, so neither reaction can happen in the world yet.

## What Changes

The Stellar Glimmer reactions take effect once a kit places the gadget they start from.

- Stellar-Conduct and Stellar Swirl deal damage by the Glimmer formula: each contributor's damage is worked out alone with standard crit, and the four highest are weighted 0.6, 0.3, 0.05 and 0.05.
- A Swirl becomes Stellar Swirl where a placed kit gadget stands.
- Stellar-Conduct needs its placed gadget; with no gadget placed, no Stellar reaction occurs.

## Impact

- The reactions in the Genshin world's combat, the transformative damage formula, and the gadgets a kit places in the open world.
