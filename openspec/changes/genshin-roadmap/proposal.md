# Roadmap

## Why

Open work for Genshin part by part, each in the recreation passes' order and, inside a pass, the order its own rank prices it. The opening — the passes' missing measures, then where the parts stand, the camera, the stand-ins' shapes, the walkway's rise, their surfaces, the game's display transform, each hour's light, its haze, sky and clouds, and the login's music and every sound it plays. The world — Windrise's references, its scene re-derived from the game's own data, and the god rays and bloom a recording prices.

## What Changes

The area's open work that is not yet a change of its own, in priority order, kept as `tasks.md`. Each item that grows a design becomes its own change under `openspec/changes/`.
