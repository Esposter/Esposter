# Roadmap

Open work only. What was decided against is in [deferred](../../deferred/genshin/); read it before adding an item. Inside a phase the items of a scene run in the [recreation passes](../genshin-recreation-passes/design.md)' order, each pass answering one kind of unknown from its most exact source, judged by its own measure and frozen once it holds: an item is worked only once every earlier pass holds, however large its own gap, since a later pass's solve absorbs an earlier one's error, while a queued run waits only on the earlier passes its measure reads (the `genshin-parity` skill's `references/compute-queue.md`). Inside a pass, `genshin:parity rank`'s first table orders the items over the scene's own frames, re-read each pass: the opening's over the login's four frames, the world's over Windrise's once it has references, since a rank prices one scene and never orders two against each other. Each item names the measure that says it is done: a placement, a shape or a surface by its gap from the game's own exports, the clouds by their statistics (`passes --pass Atmosphere`, `layer`, `cover`), the music by `listen`, a sound by its bands against the game's own, and the frame by `compare` against its reference once every pass before it holds ([parity](../../specs/genshin/parity/README.md)).

The compute queue's runs owed sit in [the compute folder](../../specs/genshin/compute/README.md), one page per area, and the fleet reads them from there.

Genshin comes first. Until the Genshin recreation is complete, no work outside Genshin is taken on any machine, and each machine's fleet profile lends the Genshin area alone (the owner's decision). When no Genshin item is runnable, the effort goes to unblocking the waiting Genshin items, never to another area.

A screen's reference clips are replaced by the interface export once the screen's blocks are extracted: `genshin:assets interface` places its pieces and `genshin:assets clips` plays their motion, with the copied captures for the look.

The roadmap is grouped by phase: each item sits under the phase whose exit gate fails without it, the lowest such phase, so a phase is a gate and not a ranking. Within a phase the items keep the order they had, and the phases, their gates and the rule that sorts an item into one are set out in [the phases change's design](../genshin-phases/design.md).

## References sought

The References sought are the items below that name a clip or still file (a `.mkv` or `.png`) and the unit they wait on, each under that unit's phase. They are the clips and stills a value needs, which agents find in published material (see [parity](../../specs/genshin/parity/README.md)); the user records none of them. A found one leaves the list once its source and the file it is saved as under `captures` are written into the design it waits on, and the work reading it takes that file in place of the line's own name. Until one is found, its value ships provisional. A found clip is checked against its line's settings (the English PC client at 1080p and 60 frames a second unless it says otherwise), and a near one is taken with its difference noted.
