# Roadmap

Open work only, part by part of the game. What was decided against is in [deferred](../../deferred/genshin/); read it before adding an item. Each part's items run in the [recreation passes](../genshin-recreation-passes/design.md)' order, each pass answering one kind of unknown from its most exact source, judged by its own measure and frozen once it holds: an item is worked only once every earlier pass holds, however large its own gap, since a later pass's solve absorbs an earlier one's error, while a queued run waits only on the earlier passes its measure reads (the `genshin-parity` skill's `references/compute-queue.md`). Inside a pass, `genshin:parity rank`'s first table orders the items over the part's own frames, re-read each pass: the opening's over the login's four frames, the world's over Windrise's once it has references, since a rank prices one scene and never orders two against each other. Each item names the measure that says it is done: a placement, a shape or a surface by its gap from the game's own exports, the clouds by their statistics (`passes --pass Atmosphere`, `layer`, `cover`), the music by `listen`, a sound by its bands against the game's own, and the frame by `compare` against its reference once every pass before it holds, checked by eye ([parity](../../specs/genshin/parity/README.md)).

The compute queue's runs owed sit in [the compute folder](../../specs/genshin/compute/README.md), one page per area, and the fleet reads them from there.

A screen's reference clips are replaced by the interface export once the screen's blocks are extracted: `genshin:assets interface` places its pieces and `genshin:assets clips` plays their motion, with the copied captures for the look.

## Conventions

- [ ] **Where a Genshin spec's source paths live: in design.md, as the openspec rule says, or in spec.md beside each requirement, as the cites-its-built-claims series writes them.** [Scene groups](../../specs/genshin/scene-groups/spec.md), [Sumeru kits](../../specs/genshin/sumeru-kits/spec.md) and [Terrain shape height](../../specs/genshin/terrain-shape-height/spec.md) name source files in a requirement or their Purpose, and so do [Constellations](../../specs/genshin/constellations/spec.md), [Login-screen sky](../../specs/genshin/login-screen/sky/spec.md), [Login-screen towers](../../specs/genshin/login-screen/towers/spec.md), [Parity commands](../../specs/genshin/parity/commands/spec.md) (a scenario's THEN), [Parity references](../../specs/genshin/parity/references/spec.md), [Parity visual suite](../../specs/genshin/parity/visual-suite/spec.md), [Parity witness](../../specs/genshin/parity/witness/spec.md), [Wish](../../specs/genshin/wish/spec.md) and [Weapon enhancement](../../specs/genshin/weapon-enhancement/spec.md), whose requirement cites `{data-root}` dump paths outside the repository as well. Each sits against the rule in [openspec](../../../.agents/skills/openspec/SKILL.md) that paths belong in design.md. The owner decides it: the citations test scans every page, so either placement passes it, and a move would undo the series' direction. Done when the rule and the series agree in one place, and the series is swept to it in one change.
- [ ] **Where a Genshin spec's Open items live: once in design.md, or in both spec.md and design.md as 75 capability pairs repeat them now.** [Interface library](../../specs/genshin/interface-library/spec.md) repeats its Open items verbatim in design.md, and the hover-fade item with them, so one unsourced claim is tracked in two pages that must be edited together. [Constellations](../../specs/genshin/constellations/spec.md) and [Login-screen sky](../../specs/genshin/login-screen/sky/spec.md) hold the same kind of pair, the sky's tone-curve item with them. Done when the owner picks the one page, the rule says so, and the pairs are swept to it in one change.
- [ ] **Where a compute frame score's Bar sentence lives: restated on six compute pages, or one pointer at the compute bars.** [Fontaine compute](../../specs/genshin/compute/fontaine/design.md), [Inazuma compute](../../specs/genshin/compute/inazuma/design.md), [Liyue compute](../../specs/genshin/compute/liyue/design.md), [Snezhnaya compute](../../specs/genshin/compute/snezhnaya/design.md) and [Sumeru compute](../../specs/genshin/compute/sumeru/design.md) each end their frame-score item with "Bar: none numeric; reported, not gated: the passes above carry the bars, and the frame is checked by eye." [Natlan compute](../../specs/genshin/compute/natlan/design.md) and [Nod-Krai compute](../../specs/genshin/compute/nod-krai/design.md) already point at the [compute bars](../../specs/genshin/compute/README.md#bars). The duplicate-prose check does not read headings, and it skips any run that other than two pages share, so it passes today with the copy on six pages: five with the item in Queue and Mondstadt with its item under Waiting. Moving the five to Waiting, as the compute README requires once they wait on every pass, changes no page count, so the check still passes; the move is owed by that rule, not by the check. Done when the frame score's bar is one pointer on every page, the five items move to Waiting naming every other pass of their region, and the duplicate-prose check passes.
