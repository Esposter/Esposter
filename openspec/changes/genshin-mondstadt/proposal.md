# Mondstadt

## Why

Mondstadt is where the game begins, and the rendering style's Windrise scene already stands in it, but the region has only its terrain, vegetation and weather rules. Its windmills, its walls, the city's landmarks and its snow are still unbuilt, so the region does not yet look or move as the game's does.

## What Changes

Mondstadt is drawn as the game draws it, from the wind over its meadows to its landmarks and its snow.

- Its meadows show the strongest wind of any region, with gusts rolling across the grass, and Stormterror's Lair raises the wind to a gale.
- Windmills stand with sails that turn in the wind.
- Mondstadt City's gate bridge, walls, cathedral and Anemo Archon statue plaza are drawn landmark by landmark against the game's references.
- Dragonspine has snow ground, ice, frozen falls and snowstorms once snow weather exists.
- Its areas keep the game's names, Windrise stays in Galesong Hill, and its trees stand in their areas.

Mondstadt's ground, vegetation and wind, its windmills and city landmarks, its snow weather on Dragonspine, and the area names the world shows. Each scene is rebuilt in the recreation passes.
