# Inazuma

## Why

Inazuma's islands, their terrain, water, sky and weather are built on the shared region engine, but its landmark buildings and its Enkanomiya layer are not. The Grand Narukami Shrine, the torii gates, Tenshukaku and the Enkanomiya ruin need their own generators, and Enkanomiya needs its own light and sky. Without them the nation is a set of islands without its shrines, gates or underworld.

## What Changes

Finish the Inazuma region: its storm as an area setting, each island's palette, the landmarks and torii of its capital, and Enkanomiya as a layer with its own light and ruins.

- The permanent thunderstorm over Inazuma and Yashiori Island is an area setting: lifted by default and restorable from the hour and weather control.
- Each island shows its own palette, from Narukami's sakura to Tsurumi's grey fog.
- Torii gates line Inazuma City at the game's count of 32.
- The Grand Narukami Shrine and Tenshukaku are matched to the game's landmarks pose by pose.
- Enkanomiya is entered at its gate beneath Watatsumi Island and lit by its artificial sun, not the sky's clock.
- Enkanomiya's white stone ruins are drawn from a kit of their own.
