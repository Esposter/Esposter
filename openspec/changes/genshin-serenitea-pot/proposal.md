# Serenitea Pot

## Why

The Serenitea Pot's making, Trust Rank, Adeptal Energy and stores are built, but the world still holds no realm: nothing is placed, entered or seen. Without a realm the player has nothing to furnish and no one to live with them there. The realm, its placement editor, its companions and the Realm Depot are what remain.

## What Changes

The player gets a realm to furnish and live in, entered from the Serenitea Pot once the player reaches Adventure Rank 28.

- The Serenitea Pot gadget, placed on a valid surface and interacted with, enters the player's realm; the gadget or any teleport in the open world leaves it, back to where the player went in.
- The placement mode places, moves, turns and stacks furnishings. A placement that would take an area past its load is refused, and the load shows green, orange or red as the area fills.
- Characters other than the Traveler can be invited to live in the realm, up to the count the player's Trust Rank allows, and a favoured gift set gives a companion its dialogue and one-time rewards.
- Tubby's Realm Depot sells its goods for Realm Currency, which joins the wallet.
- Gardens and ponds yield crops and fish as real time passes, and companions earn Companionship EXP as Realm Bounty.

## Capabilities

- **genshin/serenitea-pot** (modified)

## Waiting on

- The load each realm area holds. No table read names it, and the placement editor waits on it.
- Recordings the page lists as not yet found: the realm stores' fill rate, the Trust Rank queues and an area's load.
- Game data: `game-exports` (per the change's needs).

## Impact

The world session enters and leaves the realm's scene. The realm's progress holds its companions, its placements and its Realm Currency. The wallet gains Realm Currency, whose game text name is keyed by the game text tooling. The furnishing editor, the Realm Depot screen and the companions' Trust Rank counts are new surfaces.
