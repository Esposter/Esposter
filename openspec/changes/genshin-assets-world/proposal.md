# World scenery asset family

## Why

The game holds the whole scenery of its nine regions on the Mac that runs this recreation: the ground, the buildings, the water, the rocks, the ruins, the trees, the plants and the statues are all exported. The recreation draws almost none of it from those exports. Windrise has its fitted scene, every other region has a generated capital and stand-in capsules and boxes, and most of the work that would close that difference already sits in open region and compute changes. What no open change names is a short list: the plants and rocks of each region beyond Windrise, the ruins and statues the game places, the cloud bands' measured spread, the sub-areas that have no root to derive from, and the art the scenery cannot ship without a record. Until those are written down and given their conversion steps, the scenery cannot be rebuilt from the install alone.

## What Changes

- Writes the scenery gaps that no open change owns as a short list of tasks: a plant fit and a rock fit for every region, the Sumeru stilt huts and desert ruins, each nation's Statue of the Seven, the cloud band spread, the sub-area derivation roots, and a roadmap record for the art the scenery cannot ship.
- Links every other scenery gap to the open change, roadmap item or compute page that already plans it, or to the change it waits on, and writes no task for it.
- Adds the requirements the new placements and measures deliver, each stated as what a player sees.

## Capabilities

- **genshin/world-scenery** (new): the grass, rocks, ruins, statues, cloud bands and sub-area ground a player sees in a region.

## Waiting on

None. The tasks read the exports already on the Mac. The still water and the rivers wait on the water prefab exports that the flowing-water change names, and this change does not carry that wait.

## Impact

The fit registry and the component map in the asset tools, the world package's plant, rock, ruin and statue components, the landmark kinds, the cloud measure beside the parity measure, and the roadmap's open work. The owning specs of each piece (the water, sumeru and snezhnaya pages and the derived assets page) are updated in the same change that ships their behaviour.
