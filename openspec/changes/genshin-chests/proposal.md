# Chests

## Why

Chests' places and the Primogems and Mora an opening pays are built, so a chest opened on F gives the player some of its reward. An opening does not yet give the weapons, artifacts and materials it holds, and the locked, buried and sealed chests in the world cannot be opened. An opened chest also does not count toward the achievements or the exploration of its area.

## What Changes

Opening a chest gives its full reward, and the locked, buried and sealed chests join the world.

- Weapons, artifacts and Character EXP materials pour out as drops to pick up, or go straight to the bag where a drop would fall away.
- Adventure EXP and Sigils go to the player's wallet.
- A chest near an enemy camp or a puzzle stays locked until that camp is cleared or that puzzle is solved.
- A buried chest offers Dig at its place and appears once dug; a dug or freed chest pays no reward until its tier is measured.
- A chest sealed in Dendro vines or ice is freed by Pyro, and one sealed in rock by a blunt attack or one that deals poise damage.
- Luxurious and Remarkable chests stay unopened until their rewards are settled.
- An opened chest counts once toward its area's exploration and its region's chest achievements.
