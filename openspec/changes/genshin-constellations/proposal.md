# Constellations

## Why

The constellation table, its activation rules, the talent levels the third and fifth add, and the Stella Fortuna a wish brings are built. The Constellation tab draws the six constellations but cannot be pressed, no constellation's effect is read by its character's kit, and the Traveler has no element set chosen. Without the press and the effects, a constellation a player earns does nothing.

## What Changes

Constellations can be activated from the character screen, and each active constellation takes effect in its character's kit.

- The Constellation tab's Activate button activates the next constellation and spends one Stella Fortuna; it is refused when all six are active or no Stella Fortuna is held.
- A wish's Stella Fortuna is added to the characters it belongs to as the draw lands.
- Each constellation's switch is written in its character's kit and applies only when active.
- The Traveler's element set is chosen by the statue it resonated with, and each set is unlocked by its source.
- The wish screen shows the Stella Fortuna each draw brings on its result cards.
