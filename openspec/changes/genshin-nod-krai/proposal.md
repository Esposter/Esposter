# Nod-Krai

## Why

Nod-Krai is an autonomous archipelago at Snezhnaya's southern edge, with a culture drawn from the Baltic and dieselpunk machinery, and it is not yet built. Its moon, isles, two kits and statues are its own, so the region cannot be drawn from another region's kits or sky.

## What Changes

Nod-Krai's sky, isles, settlements and statues are drawn as its own, with its moon larger at night.

- At night in Nod-Krai the moon is drawn larger and brighter than in the other regions.
- Lempo Isle, Hiisi Island and Paha Isle stand as separate isles joined by the Moontide Sea, which is part of the region.
- Its ports and towns are built from a dieselpunk kit, and the Frostmoon Scions' enclaves and stone circles from a second kit.
- Statues of the New Moon stand where other regions have Statues of The Seven, and serve as waypoints.
