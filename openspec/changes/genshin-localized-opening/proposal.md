# Localized opening

## Why

The opening already speaks the reader's language: its words come from the game's text in fifteen languages, and its title splash draws each language's logo. Its other pictures and layouts do not. They are measured off recordings of the English client and one Japanese client, while the Chinese, Japanese and Korean clients may set their words differently and show their own publisher's logo.

## What Changes

The opening's publisher splash and screen layouts follow each client's language where a recording shows the client differs.

- A client that shows a different publisher's logo shows its own logo on the publisher splash.
- A language's screen layout differs from the English layout only where that language's recording shows a difference.
- The visual suite gains an image only for a language whose screen differs.
- A localized reference is compared in its client's own words.

## Waiting on

- The recordings of the Japanese and Korean PC launches, `japanese-launch.mkv` and `korean-launch.mkv`, which no public clip shows: their publisher and title splashes, their health notices and their doors all wait on them.

## Impact

The publisher splash and its logo per client, the opening's screen layouts keyed by language where they differ, and the visual suite's images per language. The parity reference map that names each screen's recordings.
