# Quests

## Why

The quests page holds the quest model, progression, the quest screen, navigation and the start and finish of quests, but the world does not yet give the carried quests their go-to places or list the world quests' series. A go-to step has nowhere to be reached, and a world quest's series is not shown, so the carried quests cannot be finished in the world or read in their series.

## What Changes

The carried quests' go-to steps get places in the world, navigation points to them, and the quest screen lists each world quest under its series.

- Each carried go-to step has a place in the world, completed when the player reaches it within its radius, and navigation points to that place.
- A world quest's series is listed in the quest screen under its chapter's title and number, with its words.
- A series' quests unlock one after another, each offered once the one before is finished.
