# Natlan

## Why

Natlan is the nation of the Pyro Archon, shaped by volcanism and home to six tribes, and it is not yet drawn. Its volcanic ground, lava, eruptions and tribal settlements are what set it apart from the other regions, so each has to be built as the game shows it.

## What Changes

Natlan is drawn with volcanic ground and lava, with each tribe's own settlement, and with its landmarks.

- Lava is a bright flow that glows and lights the rock beside it, and hot springs and vents send up steam.
- An eruption suspends rain in its area until it ends, and rain resumes afterward.
- Each of the six tribes' lands has its own walls, roofs, murals and palette, built from one building kit.
- Natlan's landmarks, such as the Stadium of the Sacred Flame and Ochkanatlan, are matched to the game's references.
- Natlan's ground, flowers and springs draw in the saturated palette of the region.
