# Minimap

A small overview of the whole board in a corner, with the visible part marked, for moving around a large board.

## Why not

The whiteboard's [original design](../../../changes/archive/2026-11-02-resource-whiteboard/design.md) drops the minimap, since a whiteboard does not need one and Excalidraw has none. The fit to content control and the zoom buttons answer the same need, finding the content of a large board, in one press.
