# Webpage site settings

A site's own settings applied to a webpage: a favicon of its own, a custom 404 page, a global canonical address, a sitemap of its pages and a language code, as Webflow's site settings hold ([SEO checklist](https://university.webflow.com/resources/checklists/seo-checklist), [Build and launch checklist](https://university.webflow.com/resources/checklists/build-and-launch-checklist)).

## Why not

Each of them belongs to a site, a set of pages under one domain, and a webpage here is one page at its own address on the app's domain ([a page is a second webpage](../webpage-site-pages/design.md), rejected for the same reason). The favicon and the 404 page are the app's, one set for every address it serves. A canonical address of a page that exists once is the address it is at. A sitemap lists the pages of a site, and an owner's webpages are separate pages the explorer already lists. The page's language is the app's. What a single page does own, whether it is listed in search, is its own switch, the search-visibility setting that [a change](../../../changes/resource-webpage-search-visibility/design.md) proposes.
