# Page Switcher Menu

SurveyJS's Creator has a page menu that jumps to any page of a survey from a list, so an author does not scroll the design surface to reach one. This record covers that menu.

## Why not

Choosing a page row in the outline already selects that page, and the canvas scrolls to it ([inline-editing change](../../../changes/resource-survey-creator-inline-editing/design.md)). A second control listing the same pages beside the canvas would give an author two routes to one page. The inline-editing change rejects it for that reason, and the outline stays the one place a page is chosen.
