# Pin resources to a dashboard

Azure lets you pin resources/blades as tiles on portal dashboards — the analogue would be pinning resource tiles into our Dashboard resource type.

## Why not

Our Dashboard is a data-visualization product (visuals bound to datasets), not a portal homepage; mixing navigation tiles into it muddles both. Quick access is what [favorites & recents](../../../specs/resource/favorites-and-recents/design.md) are for.
