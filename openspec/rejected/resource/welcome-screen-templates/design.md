# Welcome screen and templates

A welcome screen with a gallery of board templates, shown when a board is new.

## Why not

A new board opens on an empty canvas, as the [whiteboard spec](../../../specs/resource/whiteboard/spec.md) says. Blueprints are the platform's route to starting a resource from prepared content: a blueprint's manifest deploys as real resources through the standard write paths ([blueprint resource](../../../specs/resource/blueprint-resource/design.md)). A template gallery would be a second route to the same starting content, beside the one the platform already has.
