# Voxel day and night

The proposal turned the voxel world's one fixed hour into Minecraft's day: the sky, the fog and the sun and moon turning, the ground lit by a sky light and a torch light baked into each vertex beside its shading, so night darkened the open ground while the room's torches kept it warm. A bed in the room slept through to morning.

**Why not:** Its light was a voxel technique, baked per block face, and its day was Minecraft's. The console's world is the [Genshin](../../../changes/genshin-program/design.md) recreation, whose [sky and time](../../../specs/genshin/sky-and-time/design.md) gives it the game's own day and painted sky through shared light uniforms rather than baked vertices.
