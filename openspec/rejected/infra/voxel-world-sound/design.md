# Voxel world sound

The proposal gave the silent voxel world its sounds through one Web Audio graph: footsteps chosen by the block under the player, a landing, and the room's door opening and closing where it stood, with a volume in the world settings.

**Why not:** Every sound was keyed to a voxel world's parts: the block underfoot, the player's jump, the room's door. All of them are gone. The [Genshin](../../../changes/genshin-program/design.md) world has no character or door yet, and sound there is written as its own page when something in it makes one, not carried over from a world it does not resemble.
