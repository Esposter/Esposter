# Voxel open world

The console's voxel room stood in terrain generated from a seed: rolling grass over dirt over stone, one kind everywhere. The proposal gave that ground climate-chosen biomes (forest, river, lake and snow) in a realm of the repository's own invention, and set the console's views down in it as places the player walked to.

**Why not:** The voxel world is gone. The console's world is the [Genshin](../../../changes/genshin-program/design.md) recreation, whose geography is the game's own continent, read from the game's own data rather than invented. What this page wanted from biomes becomes the continent's own areas, at its scale: the [world map](../../../specs/genshin/world-map/design.md) describes them, and the [terrain](../../../specs/genshin/terrain/design.md) takes their shapes from the game's own tiles, fitted region by region ([scene derivation](../../../specs/genshin/scene-derivation/design.md)).
