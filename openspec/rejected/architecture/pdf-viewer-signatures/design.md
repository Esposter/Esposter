# PDF viewer signatures

Signing a document with a typed, drawn or imaged signature, and showing a digital signature's properties, as the reference viewer's editor does ([pdf.js viewer markup](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mozilla/pdf.js/master/web/viewer.html)).

## Why not

A signature is an act with legal weight, and a drawn mark on a rendered page proves nothing about who made it or that the file was not changed after. A digital signature needs a certificate and a signing service, which a chat attachment viewer does not have. A reader who must sign a document does it in a tool made to hold that proof and sends the result as a new attachment.
