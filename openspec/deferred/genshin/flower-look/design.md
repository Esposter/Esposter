# Flower look

The flowers on a finest terrain tile are scattered from a seeded stream, each stood on the ground, turned and sized at random, in one of the region's colours, as the [scatter](../../../specs/genshin/scatter/design.md) page describes. Their spacing, sizes and colours' spread are provisional, since being random they are matched to the game's flowers by their statistics rather than by any one of them. Those statistics are what this idea sets, and the [trees and scatter](../../../changes/genshin-trees-and-scatter/design.md) page holds it deferred.

**Why deferred:** the match is to the statistics of the game's flowers at a view the scatter draws, and the change names no recording of them to measure against. Matching without one would tune the flowers to a guess.

**Revisit when:** a recording of the game's flowers at a view the scatter draws is in hand, so their spacing, sizes and colours can be measured against it.

**Cheaper interim:** the flowers the seeded stream places in the region's colours, as the scatter page already draws them.
