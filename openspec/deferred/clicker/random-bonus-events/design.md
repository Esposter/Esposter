# Random Bonus Events

Timed clickable bonuses that appear at random intervals and grant a short production or click multiplier — the mechanic that makes players keep the tab focused.

**Why deferred:** Real engagement value, but it needs buff state the game doesn't have (temporary effects with expiry interacting with the effect engine) plus art/sound. [Milestone achievements](../../../specs/clicker/game-loop-and-saves/design.md) have shipped, but the mid-game still has little to spend bursts on while upgrades exist for only a couple of buildings.

**Revisit when:** active sessions are rewarding enough to boost — after [all-building upgrades](../all-building-upgrades/design.md).
