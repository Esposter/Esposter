# Note inline equations

An equation written in a note as LaTeX and drawn typeset, as Notion's inline and block equations do.

## Why deferred

Typesetting needs a renderer such as KaTeX, its stylesheet and fonts, and a sanitizer allowance for its markup in the published render, in an app whose other resources draw no mathematics. The Sheet's cells hold the numbers a note would show, and a pasted picture of an equation is already a note image.

## Revisit when

A second resource needs typeset mathematics, or a note is asked for equations by an owner whose documents are mathematical, so the renderer is paid for once.
