# vue-pdfjs-headless

`packages/vue-pdfjs-headless` (npm `vue-pdfjs-headless`) is a PDF viewer's loading, page drawing and viewer state with no styling: composables that take the elements a consumer renders and return refs and functions. The renderer is the consumer's, passed in, so the package never imports `pdfjs-dist` at runtime and a consumer picks the build it needs, the legacy one included.

The package's capabilities, each with its own design page and requirements:

- [Document](document/design.md) loads a document from a URL, from bytes or from a file, through the consumer's renderer, with its progress, its password prompt, its failure and a superseded load.
- [Viewer](viewer/design.md) draws the pages, their overlays, zoom, search, outline and thumbnails, rotation, download and print.
- [Annotations](annotations/design.md) keeps form values and drawn annotations in the saved bytes, reports each drawing, lists comments, exchanges free texts as XFDF, and stamps a watermark on the download alone.

The app's own use of the package is the [PDF viewer](../architecture/pdf-viewer/design.md): the renderer's worker and folders, the thumbnail and the dialog's look.

## Upstream

Both wrappers' trackers get one verdict per issue, open or closed, read with its comments. The tables keep the in-scope issues, and the others are counted at the end of this section. `vue-pdf-viewer`'s source is closed, so its tracker is the only evidence for it, and its features are answered by the viewer's decisions. A feature the dialog does not draw yet keeps its in-scope verdict, which judges the viewer rather than one consumer of it, and its row links the capability building it, or the deferred idea that holds it. The page drawing's in-scope defects carry tests in the package: #52 in the scale function's suite and #20 in the page composable's. #28 and #261 are fixed upstream by the wrapper's own verdict, so no test here proves an open defect. The worker-syntax issues in `vue-pdf-embed` (#125, #266, #277, #283, #291) are answered by the [document](document/design.md) capability, which takes the renderer's legacy build from its consumer.
