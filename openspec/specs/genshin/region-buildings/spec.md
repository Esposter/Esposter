## Purpose

A building landmark generated by its region's own kit, from that kit's options, in its region's colours, standing on the world's one ground, Windrise's fitted ground with every region's laid over it. Each region's capital, or Natlan's first tribe, stands as one such building at a provisional place read off the game's waypoints, until the fit writes the place exactly.

## Requirements

### Requirement: A building is built by its region's own kit from that kit's options

A building landmark SHALL be built by its region's own kit from the options that kit takes, and SHALL be left undrawn when its options do not match its kit.

#### Scenario: Options match the kit

- **GIVEN** a building landmark whose options are those its region's kit takes
- **WHEN** the region loads
- **THEN** the building is drawn by its region's kit

#### Scenario: Options do not match the kit

- **GIVEN** a building landmark carrying options from another region's kit
- **WHEN** the region loads
- **THEN** the region is logged as failed
- **AND** the region is left undrawn

### Requirement: A building's parts take the region's palette colours

A building's parts SHALL take their region's palette colours wherever the palette samples them, and their constant colours wherever it does not.

#### Scenario: Palette colour sampled

- **GIVEN** a region whose palette samples a colour a part uses
- **WHEN** the building is drawn
- **THEN** the part takes the palette's colour

#### Scenario: Colour left out of the palette

- **GIVEN** a colour the palette leaves out
- **WHEN** the building is drawn
- **THEN** the part keeps its constant colour

### Requirement: Every building stands on the world's one ground

Every building SHALL stand on the world's one ground, with every region's ground laid over Windrise's.

#### Scenario: Building placed on the ground

- **GIVEN** a building in any region
- **WHEN** it is placed
- **THEN** it stands on the world's ground at its place

### Requirement: A region with no fitted ground stands on plateaus

A region no fit has drawn SHALL stand on its plateaus over the world's base, and a region a fit has drawn SHALL stand on its own fitted hills, features and residual.

#### Scenario: Region with no fit

- **GIVEN** a region no fit has drawn
- **WHEN** the world's ground is built
- **THEN** the region's plateaus stand over the world's base

#### Scenario: Region with a fit

- **GIVEN** a region a fit has drawn
- **WHEN** the world's ground is built
- **THEN** the region's fitted hills, features and residual stand on their own base

### Requirement: Each region's capital stands at its city's waypoint place

Each region's capital, and Natlan's first tribe, SHALL stand as one building at the place its city's waypoints give in the game's data, until the fit writes that place exactly.

#### Scenario: Capital placed from its waypoints

- **GIVEN** a region's capital and its city's waypoints in the game's data
- **WHEN** the capital is placed before the fit writes its place
- **THEN** it stands at the place read from its waypoints

### Requirement: Snezhnograd stands at its set place until its waypoint is read

Snezhnograd SHALL stand at its set place beside Nasha Town until its place is read from the game's data.

#### Scenario: Snezhnograd placed before its waypoint is read

- **GIVEN** the game's data does not yet hold Snezhnograd's waypoint
- **WHEN** Snezhnograd is placed
- **THEN** it stands at its set place beside Nasha Town

### Requirement: The fit places every capital exactly at its waypoints

The fit SHALL place every capital exactly at the median of its waypoints, and SHALL raise an unfitted plateau to its capital's height.

#### Scenario: Fit run for landmarks

- **GIVEN** a region's capital and its waypoints in the game's data
- **WHEN** the fit runs for landmarks
- **THEN** the capital stands at the median of its waypoints
- **AND** an unfitted plateau under it is raised to its height

### Requirement: Every plateau stays within the terrain's bounds

Every plateau SHALL stay within the bounds the terrain stands between, and the check SHALL fail for any plateau or fitted ground outside them.

#### Scenario: Plateau outside the bounds

- **GIVEN** a plateau whose height lies outside the terrain's bounds
- **WHEN** the check runs
- **THEN** the check fails

### Requirement: Capitals are placed in the game's own coordinates

Capitals SHALL be placed in the game's own coordinates, with Inazuma east of Liyue and Fontaine to its north-west.

#### Scenario: Regions placed on the map

- **GIVEN** the capitals placed in the game's coordinates
- **WHEN** the map is viewed
- **THEN** Inazuma lies east of Liyue
- **AND** Fontaine lies to the north-west

### Requirement: A region's ground loads when the camera comes within reach

A region whose data has not loaded SHALL load its ground and its capital when the camera comes within reach of it.

#### Scenario: Camera comes within reach

- **GIVEN** a region outside the camera's reach
- **WHEN** the camera comes within reach of it
- **THEN** the region's data and ground load
- **AND** its capital is drawn
