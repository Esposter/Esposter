# Enemies

The world's enemies follow the rules the wiki documents for the game's open world. A camp placed in a region's data spawns when the region comes into reach. Its enemies notice whoever comes near, fight together, give up when led too far from home, and come back on the game's own timers once beaten. All of it is `genshin-world`'s, since it is the game's rules rather than the engine's. Until a model is built for each kind, an enemy is drawn as a capsule, tinted by its AI's state so the states can be watched (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/World/Enemies/Index.vue`).

## How it works

```mermaid
stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> Idle
  Idle --> Alert: target within aggro range, or a camp-mate engaged
  Idle --> Chase: hit
  Alert --> Chase: alert seconds pass, or hit
  Chase --> Windup: target within attack range, cooldown over
  Windup --> Recovery: windup passes, striking a target in reach
  Recovery --> Chase: recovery passes, cooldown starts
  Chase --> Stagger: poise or shield broken
  Windup --> Stagger: poise or shield broken
  Recovery --> Stagger: poise or shield broken
  Stagger --> Chase: stagger passes
  Chase --> Return: past the leash, or no target
  Return --> Idle: home, healed to full
  Chase --> Dead: health reaches zero
  Windup --> Dead: health reaches zero
  Recovery --> Dead: health reaches zero
  Stagger --> Dead: health reaches zero
  Idle --> Dead: health reaches zero
  Alert --> Dead: health reaches zero
  Dead --> [*]: its death passes, its drops are rolled
```

- **One deterministic step.** `stepEnemy` reads an enemy, the target on the ground (or none) and the step's seconds, and writes the enemy's next state, position, heading and poise in place (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/stepEnemy.ts`). It reads no clock and no random source, so a test steps it exactly as the world does. Idle is standing at its spawn, or walking its patrol's points in a loop.
- **A hit is combat's to compute.** `damageEnemy` takes the damage and poise damage combat's formula computes for a hit (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/damageEnemy.ts`). The enemy's health falls, and each energy threshold it falls to drops its energy, once for the enemy's life however it is healed. A shield it holds (Stormterror's, a fifth of its max HP, `packages/genshin-world/src/services/bosses/stormterror.ts`) takes the damage first and breaks once drained, staggering it until the stagger ends and it stands again at full shield. A hit on a broken poise, or one that breaks it, staggers the enemy and cancels its attack; any other hit sets an unengaged enemy on its attacker. A returning or dead enemy is immune.
- **Hit by the kit, striking back.** A kit's hit lands through `strikeEnemy`: a blunt hit shatters a Freeze first, an elemental hit applies its element under the internal cooldown kept on the enemy for each attacker and tag, and the damage is priced by combat before `damageEnemy` takes it (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/strikeEnemy.ts`). A dead or returning enemy is passed by. An enemy's own strike lands on the member on the field through `strikePartyMember`, its ATK less what its statuses cut of it. The team's shields take the damage first, all at once, a rule the Open list records as unconfirmed, and what none of them absorbs takes the [party](../party/design.md)'s HP, by its share of Max HP (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/strikePartyMember.ts`, `packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/computeEnemyStrikeDamage.ts`). An enemy within aggro range of a live taunt strikes the nearest one instead, and the taunt's HP takes the damage (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/selectEnemyTaunt.ts`, `packages/genshin-world/src/composables/useWorldCombat.ts`).
- **Elements it keeps.** Each enemy carries an `ElementalState` (`packages/genshin-world/src/models/enemy/Enemy.ts`), advanced at each of its fixed steps so its auras decay and its Electro-Charged and Burning tick between hits (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/combat/aura/advanceElementalState.ts`). The reactions an enemy's elements bring on their own wait on the enemy (`elementalReactions`), to be struck by the kit on the field, as [combat](../combat/design.md) describes. `hitSeconds` counts the seconds since the enemy was last hit, which is what draws a hit.
- **A launching hit throws a staggered enemy airborne.** The wiki's stagger levels run Mute, Shake, Light, Heavy and Air, each attack's set by its impulse type (`{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Page_Interruption_Resistance%2FStagger.json`, its Stagger Level and Common Impulse Type tables), and an Air hit knocks an enemy it leaves staggered airborne (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/strikeEnemy.ts`). A kit marks such a hit `KitHit.isLaunching` (`packages/genshin-world/src/models/kit/KitHit.ts`), from impulse types 5 to 9, which the wiki's common impulse table gives the Air stagger level, as Skyward Sonnet's 7 does (`{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Venti_Talents.json`, Skyward Sonnet's Advanced Properties, `impulse = 7`), and a launching hit that leaves the enemy staggered gives it the `ENEMY_LAUNCHED_STATUS_ID` status for the stagger's seconds (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/strikeEnemy.ts`, and `ENEMY_LAUNCHED_STATUS_ID` in `packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/constants.ts`), which a kit reads as the enemy being airborne. gcsim models no launch: its Venti leaves Constellation 2's airborne cut as a TODO (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/venti/cons.go`, line 37).
- **Camps wake together.** After every step, `wakeEnemyCamps` alerts each idle enemy whose camp has a member engaged, so a whole hilichurl camp turns at once (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/wakeEnemyCamps.ts`).
- **Leashed to home.** An enemy farther from its spawn than the leash distance, or one left with no target, walks home. It is immune on the way and arrives healed to full, with its shield and poise restored (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/stepEnemy.ts`, `packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/damageEnemy.ts`).

## Kinds and their stats

A kind is the game's own monster, named by the game's monster id (`EnemyKindId`). Its row of the game's table holds its type, its name's text id, its base HP, ATK and DEF with the level curve each grows by, and its resistance to each element and to physical damage (`{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/MonsterExcelConfigData.json`, row 25010201, with `hpBase`, `propGrowCurves`, `elecSubHurt` and `physicalSubHurt`). `genshin:assets enemies` (`scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/enemies/buildEnemyKinds.ts`) publishes that row for every kind a region's camps place, and every curve they name, from the community's dump of the game's tables, to the [hosted game data](../hosted-game-data/design.md). The world reads both records as it opens (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/readEnemyTables.ts`), and every rule over a kind takes the tables as an argument: the spawn, the strikes both ways, the drops, the Archive's count and the sight's name tags. The family is the archive's group, the entry its describe id names (`scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/enemies/buildEnemyKinds.ts`), and the type is the table's `securityLevel`: elite or boss, and common where the row has none. Both are spelt as the game spells them, so the tables parse into the world's enums.

`computeEnemyStats` scales each base stat by its curve's multiplier at the enemy's level, as gcsim's HP does (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/enemy/types.go`, line 42): max HP and ATK by their curves, and DEF by one plus a hundredth of the level (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/computeEnemyStats.ts`; the defense curve is one plus a hundredth of the level at all 200 levels of `{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/MonsterCurveExcelConfigData.json`, 1.01 at level 1).

What the game's table does not hold is authored from the wiki in `EnemyKindTraitsMap` (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/EnemyKindTraitsMap.ts`): the kind's poise type, the element its energy carries, its energy drops in falling order, and the family whose Mora and materials it drops. Each poise type's bar (its length, refill speed, endurance and reset time) is the wiki's table: the nine enemy rows of `packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/PoiseTypeSettingsMap.ts` match its Poise Type table (`{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Page_Interruption_Resistance%2FPoise.json`) row for row.

## Spawns and respawn

A region's data places its enemies in camps, each member with its kind, its level at World Level 0, its spawn and its patrol. The world spawns each member at the level its World Level raises it to, as the [Adventure Rank](../adventure-rank/design.md) page sets out, and a change of World Level spawns every camp anew. The world spawns a camp when its region's data arrives, and drops it when the region leaves reach (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/World/Enemies/Index.vue`). A member defeated earlier is spawned again only once its respawn time has passed (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/computeEnemyRespawnTime.ts`, `packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/checkIsEnemyRespawned.ts`), so an enemy never comes back in view:

| Camp                   | A defeated member comes back                                                                                                                     |
| :--------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Common enemies only    | 12 hours after its defeat (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/constants.ts`)                                                             |
| With an elite among it | at the next daily reset, 04:00 on the server's clock (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/computeEnemyRespawnTime.ts`)                    |
| With a boss among it   | 5 seconds (`BOSS_RESPAWN_AFTER_CLAIM_DURATION`) after its Trounce Blossom is claimed, and while unclaimed at the first teleport after its defeat |

The wiki's Reset page (`{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Page_Reset.json`, its Other Resets section) gives the first two rows' timers: a common enemy outside an elite's group comes back 12 hours after its defeat, and the commons of an elite's group come back with the elite at the daily reset, as the wiki gives for elites with limited spawn points per day. The boss row's five seconds is the build's `BOSS_RESPAWN_AFTER_CLAIM_DURATION` (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/bosses/constants.ts`), applied by `packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/computeEnemyRespawnTime.ts`; the Normal Boss page gives the game's figure as roughly five seconds after the claim of the Trounce Blossom that a defeated boss leaves (`{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Page_Normal_Boss.json`, its description and the paragraph after it), so the build uses that page's figure without its "roughly".

The daily reset is the server's 04:00, UTC+8, as the [reset clock](../reset-clock/design.md) sets out (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/time/constants.ts` for the hour and the zone, and the "REFRESH_DAILY" rows of `{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/RefreshPolicyExcelConfigData.json` with `14400`, the 4:00 hour).

A common or elite member's return is saved as the [save data](../save-data/design.md) page keeps it (`packages/genshin-world/src/models/enemy/EnemyRespawnSave.ts`). A boss camp's defeat is saved as its moment of defeat (`packages/genshin-world/src/models/enemy/BossDefeatSave.ts`), beside the blossom it left and the last teleport, so a reload reads the same return.

The world keeps every spawned enemy in one map by its spawn key, the camp's id and the member's id (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/World/Enemies/Index.vue`). A camp's leaving removes its enemies from the map and a spawn's return adds it back, and the character's kit and the enemies' steps read the same map.

## Drops

A defeated enemy's drops are rolled by `computeEnemyDrops` from its level's band of five levels, the last band holding every level from 90 (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/computeEnemyDrops.ts`, `packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/constants.ts`). Its family's base Mora is scaled by the band's share: a random amount in the band's range for a common enemy, and the band's one share for an elite (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/constants.ts`). Character EXP is the band's, for common and elite enemies (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/constants.ts`). Each material drops its whole expected count and one more at the chance of its fraction (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/computeEnemyDrops.ts`), the second tier starting at level 40 and the third at 60; the wiki's drops data writes a fixed set as a whole count and a chance, such as "4 + 20.00%" for Raw Meat, and its material tier rates start the second and third tiers at the bands 40 to 44 and 60 to 64 (`{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Page_Module:Drops_Table%2Fdata.json`). A boss drops nothing, since its reward is claimed from its blossom (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/computeEnemyDrops.ts`). Enemies give no Adventure EXP: the game awards it only for claiming a boss's reward. Where the Mora and the materials lie, and how the player picks them up, is the [interaction](../interaction/design.md) page's.

## The stand-in

The World Enemies component (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/World/Enemies/Index.vue`) steps every enemy in reach on the engine's fixed-step loop, and draws them all as one instanced capsule, stood on the ground, turned to its heading and tinted by its state. Its target is the character's feet, which the world screen hands down, so every enemy turns on the body wherever it goes. Where no character stands, as on a reference's held camera or a witness render, the enemies have no target. A screen holding the world holds its enemies too, and a witness render draws none, since the references it is judged against show none.

A capsule that a hit landed on is tinted white for 0.15 seconds (`ENEMY_HIT_TINT_SECONDS`) after the hit, and a flash of radius 0.3 metres (`ENEMY_HIT_FLASH_RADIUS`) stands at its middle for 0.1 seconds (`ENEMY_HIT_FLASH_SECONDS`), drawn as a second instanced mesh. Both timings, the flash's size and the tint's colour are provisional, as `packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/constants.ts` marks them.

## Cost

- **Two draws for all enemies.** The capsules are one instanced mesh and the hit flashes a second, their matrices and tints written each frame from scratch objects, so a frame allocates nothing for them (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/World/Enemies/Index.vue`, `packages/genshin-world/src/services/world/composeHeadingMatrix.ts`).
- **The AI costs what is in reach.** Only the camps of regions in reach (`packages/genshin-world/src/composables/useRegionData.ts`) are stepped (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/World/Enemies/Index.vue`), and waking a camp compares each idle enemy with its camp-mates, which a camp's handful keeps cheap.

## Notes

- **The published tables hold a partial dump.** The `enemies/kinds` record holds the Hilichurl Fighter as the game's table has it, and `enemies/levelCurves` every level of each curve they name (`scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/enemies/buildEnemyKinds.ts`, lines 97 to 107), which `packages/genshin-world/src/models/enemy/EnemyLevelCurves.ts` reads from level 1.
- **The AI's numbers are provisional.** Its ranges, speeds and timings, the leash and the capsule's size are starting points marked in `packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/constants.ts`, each to be measured off a recording or the Hilichurl's own clips.
- **Character EXP is computed, not yet given.** The party's levels take it, since a quest's finish gives its Character EXP through `raiseCharacterByExp`, but nothing grants a defeat's roll yet, so a defeat's EXP is rolled and left. A defeat's drops are placed and picked up by the world's screen, and the component emits `strike` and `defeat` for it (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/World/Enemies/Index.vue`).
- **Windrise's camp stands where we put it.** Its place and level are ours until the game's own spawns are read (`packages/genshin-world/src/data/regions/mondstadt.json`).
- **A launch lasts the stagger, and every enemy can be launched.** The game's airtime follows the impulse's vertical force against the enemy's weight, and an Air hit on the ground falls to Heavy when its vertical force is under 5.5 times the defender's real weight (`{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Page_Interruption_Resistance%2FStagger.json`, its Real Stagger Level table), or does nothing for the kinds immune to Air staggers. Neither weight nor that immunity is built, so a launching hit launches every enemy it staggers, an open gap the Open list carries (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/strikeEnemy.ts`), for the provisional `ENEMY_STAGGER_SECONDS` (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/constants.ts`), and a hit on an enemy already in the air restarts its launched status rather than adding one (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/addEnemyStatus.ts`).
- **The party's combat leaves after a provisional grace.** The party is in combat while an enemy is in an engaged state on it, and leaves once none has been for `COMBAT_CLEAR_SECONDS` (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/constants.ts`), 5 seconds, provisional until a recording measures it (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/enemy/stepCombat.ts`): the wiki gives the clear timers but no figure.
