# Parity commands

From `scripts/`, as `pnpm genshin:parity <command>`, each with its own `--help` (`scripts/src/services/genshinParity/commands/genshinParityCommand.ts`, every command registered); the parity page from `packages/genshin-world`, as `pnpm parity` (`packages/genshin-world/package.json`). The video clip is `recordings clip` among these: it downloads through the generic `pnpm recreation clip`, which writes no manifest row, and writes the row itself.

The commands group by the step of the loop they serve: fetching and packing references, scoring and approving a screen, the witness commands that draw the game's exports under our camera, the pose and scroll solves, the image readers, the sky, light and fog analyses, the music scores, the benchmark and load timings, and the recording commands that capture the installed game. Each command's flags, defaults and printed lines are its own `--help` and its file under `scripts/src/services/genshinParity/commands/`; [the commands spec](spec.md) holds what each does.
