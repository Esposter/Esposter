# Combat

The game's combat is one small set of rules every character and enemy shares, and its community has documented them to the decimal. They live in `genshin-world` under `packages/genshin-world/src/services/combat`, as functions over plain state with no rendering, no input and no three.js, since the engine knows no rule of the game as it knows no place. The world calls into them from its fixed steps: the character on the field's kit prices each of its hits, and the [enemies](../enemies/design.md) age their elements and strike the party through them.

## How it works

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  HIT["A hit: its element, gauge and internal cooldown tag"] --> ICD{"applyInternalCooldown: does this hit apply its element?"}
  BLUNT["A blunt hit"] --> SHATTER["applyBluntHit: poise drains the Freeze, and what is left shatters"]
  SHATTER --> APPLY
  ICD -->|"its share of the gauge"| APPLY["applyElement: the element's reactions, in the game's priority"]
  APPLY --> STEP{"An aura this step reacts with?"}
  STEP -->|"yes"| CONSUME["Consume the auras by coefficient × what is left of the trigger; record the reaction once"]
  CONSUME --> LEFT{"Any trigger left?"}
  LEFT -->|"yes"| STEP
  STEP -->|"none left to try"| AURA{"Left over, and nothing it consumes remains?"}
  LEFT -->|"no"| AURA
  AURA -->|"yes"| TAX["Left as an aura: taxed to 0.8, decaying"]
  AURA -->|"no"| EC
  TAX --> EC{"Electro now beside Hydro?"}
  EC -->|"yes"| TICK["Electro-Charged ticks at once"]
  EC -->|"no"| OUT["The reactions"]
  TICK --> OUT
  FIXED["Each fixed step"] --> ADVANCE["advanceElementalState: auras decay; Electro-Charged and Burning tick"]
  ADVANCE --> OUT
  OUT --> DAMAGE["getDamage, getAmplifyingMultiplier, getCatalyzeBonus or getTransformativeDamage"]
```

Each target holds an `ElementalState`: its auras, its own clock, and when its Electro-Charged and Burning last ticked and it last crystallized. `createElementalState` makes an empty one.

### Auras

An attack applies a gauge of its element, in gauge units. Pyro, Hydro, Electro, Cryo and Dendro left as an aura are taxed to 0.8 of that gauge and decay evenly over 2.5 seconds a unit of the attack plus 7, so Bennett's 2U tap leaves 1.6U of Pyro for 12 seconds. Anemo and Geo leave no aura. A second attack of the same element keeps the larger gauge at the first aura's rate, except Pyro: a larger Pyro replaces the aura with its own rate.

Not every aura comes from an attack.

- **A self aura** is an element put on a target with no attack, as a slime's seeds or an enemy's elemental shield put one: `applyElement`'s last parameter, the seconds its source lasts. It reacts as an attack of its gauge would, but what is left of it is untaxed and decays at its source's gauge over those seconds, or not at all where they are `Infinity`, as most elemental shields' do. A Large Dendro Slime's Toxic Seeds leave 4U of Dendro losing 0.8U a second, and the Hydro Samachurl's 2U of Soothing Rain for 8 seconds keeps 1.6U after vaporizing 0.8U of Pyro, losing 0.25U a second.
- **An immutable aura** is an enemy's innate element, set on its state with `isImmutable`. Its gauge takes its share of a trigger as any aura's does, but it is never consumed, never decays and gives Electro-Charged's ticks nothing, so what is left of the trigger goes on to the auras beside it. A Cryo Slime's 2U of Cryo takes 2U of Beidou's 4U of Electro as Superconduct, and the 2U left quicken the Dendro beside it; a Cryo Hypostasis's 4U takes all of it. An attack of its own element leaves it as it is.

The Hilichurl Fighter, the one kind the world places yet, has neither: an enemy kind's innate element and its shield come with the kind.

### Reactions, in the game's priority

`ElementReactionStepsMap` holds, for each applied element, the reactions it tries in the order the game tries them. The coefficients of the table's cells that gcsim's `reduce` calls name match them (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/overload.go`:23,30, `superconduct.go:34,40`, `vaporize.go:25,32`, `melt.go:19,20,29`, `bloom.go:31,32,40`, `catalyze.go:58,63`, `swirl.go:68,106,140,170,205`); the cells they do not name are open below. Each step names the auras it reacts with and the gauge of aura each unit of the trigger consumes. A step meeting several auras consumes them together, by the larger one's gauge. What is left of the trigger after a step goes on to the next, so 2U of Hydro on a Burning target vaporizes the Burning and its Pyro, then blooms the Dendro under them with the unit left over. A reaction is recorded once per application, however many auras it meets.

| Applied | Its reactions, in order                                                                                                |
| :------ | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pyro    | Overloaded on Electro (1); Vaporize on Hydro (0.5); Melt on Cryo and Freeze (2); Burning on Dendro or Quicken          |
| Hydro   | Vaporize on Pyro and Burning (2); Frozen on Cryo (1); Bloom on Dendro and Quicken (0.5)                                |
| Electro | Aggravate on Quicken; Overloaded on Pyro and Burning (1); Superconduct on Cryo, then Freeze (1); Quicken on Dendro (1) |
| Cryo    | Superconduct on Electro (1); Melt on Pyro and Burning (0.5); Frozen on Hydro (1)                                       |
| Dendro  | Spread on Quicken; Quicken on Electro (1); Burning on Pyro or Burning; Bloom on Hydro (2)                              |
| Anemo   | Swirl on Electro, Pyro and Burning, Hydro, Cryo, then Freeze (0.5 each)                                                |
| Geo     | Shattered on Freeze; Crystallize on Electro, Pyro and Burning, Hydro, then Cryo (0.5 each)                             |

What is left once every step has run stays as an aura, if the element leaves one and no aura it consumes remains. A reaction with no coefficient consumes neither side, which is how auras come to coexist.

- **Electro-Charged** is no step: it is Electro and Hydro left on a target together. It ticks the moment they meet and each second after (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/electrocharged.go`:78-81,130-131,181), every tick taking 0.4U from each aura that has more than that to give. Once one decays away it ends, ticking once more if half a second has passed since the last.
- **Quicken** consumes Dendro and Electro at 1 and leaves the gauge it consumed as its own aura, lasting 5 seconds a unit plus 6 (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/catalyze.go`:85). Aggravate and Spread consume neither the Quicken nor their trigger, so the trigger goes on to its later steps and may lie beside the Quicken.
- **Burning** consumes nothing when it lights. It leaves a 2U Burning aura that never decays over the Pyro and Dendro beneath it, ticks every quarter second (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/burning.go:133`), and applies 1U of Pyro under Burning's internal cooldown. The Dendro under it drains at twice its own decay and the Quicken at the Burning fuel's rate (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/reactable.go`:367-373), 0.4U a second at least, and it goes out once neither is left. Pyro or Dendro on a burning target refreshes it without triggering it again, and Dendro then replaces the Dendro whatever its gauge.
- **Frozen** leaves twice the gauge it consumed as a Freeze, which decays at 0.4U a second and faster by 0.1 every second it holds, so its gauge G lasts 2√(5G + 4) − 4 seconds. Hydro and Cryo lie under it. Pyro and Electro meet only the Freeze over a Hydro, and what is left of them stays as no aura. A Geo hit shatters it, at no gauge too, and `applyBluntHit` drains 0.006U of it per point of poise damage before shattering what is left. A Shatter takes 8U.
- **Swirl** records the gauge it spreads its element at, (G − 0.04) × 1.25 + 1, where G is the Anemo's gauge if the aura takes all of it and the aura's gauge if not. 1U of Anemo on 0.8U of Hydro spreads 2.2U.
- **Crystallize** triggers once a second on a target at most.
- **Bloom** leaves a Dendro Core by its target, which bursts on its own or as Electro or Pyro meets it: Electro on a core is Hyperbloom and Pyro is Burgeon (`DendroCoreReactionTypeMap`). The world places and bursts the cores, as its reach below describes.

Each recorded reaction carries the element its damage is dealt in, none for Shattered's physical damage or for a reaction that deals none.

### Damage

`getDamage` is the game's general formula. The talent's multiplier of its stat, plus any flat bonus, a hit's own additive base damage and a catalyze's, is multiplied by one plus the damage bonus (the element's or the physical, the all-damage bonus, the normal and charged attack bonus on a normal or charged attack, the plunging attack bonus on a plunge, a hit's own, and an enemy's DMG taken) and by a crit's one plus critical damage. It is then cut by the enemy's defence against the attacker's level, (5 × attacker level + 500) / (enemy defence + 5 × attacker level + 500) before any reduction or ignoring, and by the enemy's resistance, less any RES a status on the enemy takes off it, its element's or its Physical RES, as Enduring Rock's Geo RES and the Jade Shield's every RES do. An enemy of level L has 5 × L + 500 defence, so a hit from its own level is halved. Last it is multiplied by an amplifying reaction's multiplier. `getResistanceMultiplier` adds half a negative resistance, takes off a resistance up to 75% (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/enemy/damage.go:86-88`), and leaves 1 / (4 × resistance + 1) past that.

- **Amplifying**: Vaporize and Melt multiply their hit by 2 where Hydro vaporizes or Pyro melts, and by 1.5 the other way round (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/vaporize.go`:26,33 and `melt.go`:24,30). Elemental mastery raises that by 2.78 × EM / (EM + 1400) (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/enemy/damage.go:109`), plus the triggering character's party reaction DMG Bonus, which sits inside that term as gcsim's `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/enemy/damage.go` reads it (`getAmplifyingMultiplier`, fed by `priceEnemyReactions`).
- **Catalyze**: Aggravate adds 1.15 and Spread 1.25 times the character's level multiplier to the hit's flat bonus (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/catalyze.go`:24,43), raised by 5 × EM / (EM + 1200) (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/combat/reaction.go:42`) plus the same party DMG Bonus inside the term, as gcsim's `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/combat/reaction.go` CalcCatalyzeDmg reads it (`getCatalyzeBonus`).
- **Transformative**: Overloaded, Superconduct, Electro-Charged, Swirl, Shattered, Bloom, Hyperbloom, Burgeon and Burning deal their own damage, from 0.25 to 3 times the level multiplier. That is raised by 16 × EM / (EM + 2000) (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/combat/reaction.go:38`) and cut by resistance alone, less what the enemy's statuses take off it, since it ignores defence and cannot crit (`getTransformativeDamage`). A reaction's damage on one enemy is limited by its own internal cooldown, below, and an Overloaded, a Superconduct, a Swirl and Electro-Charged reach the enemies round it, as the section after it describes.
- **Superconduct's shred**: a Superconduct gives its enemy a status taking 40% off its Physical RES for 12 seconds, so every physical hit on it until then reads the lower RES.

`CharacterLevelMultiplierMap` is the wiki's level multiplier for characters at every level to 90, then 95 and 100.
`EnemyLevelMultiplierMap` is the wiki's level multiplier for enemies and the environment, to 100. It agrees with the characters' table to level 57 and parts from it at 58, so a reaction an enemy triggers takes its own table through `getTransformativeDamage`'s last parameter.

The deployed team's [elemental resonance](../party/design.md) adds to a kit hit's CRIT Rate, read in `strikeEnemy` before the hit's own reactions: Shattering Ice's 15% against an enemy Frozen or affected by Cryo, so a hit's critical roll is `random() < CRIT Rate + 0.15` on that enemy. A kit's `getStrikeCriticalRateBonus` adds its per-strike CRIT Rate to the same roll through the hit's `criticalRateBonus`, and its `getStrikeCriticalDamageBonus` adds per-strike CRIT DMG to the hit's `criticalDamageBonus`. The roll is drawn from the world's one seeded random source, the stream the kit's other rolls read, so a session's rolls repeat. The other resonances' stat effects are summed into each member's attributes, and reach the damage formula through them. Enduring Rock's DMG and Geo RES, and Sprawling Greenery's timed Elemental Mastery, are read as a strike lands, as the [party](../party/design.md) page describes.

### Internal cooldown

A hit applies its element under an internal cooldown, kept per attacker, target and tag. `applyInternalCooldown` counts a hit into one and returns the share its group's sequence holds for it, of its gauge here. A hit once the reset interval has passed restarts the timer and the count and takes the first share; any other is the next hit of the group's sequence. `DEFAULT_INTERNAL_COOLDOWN_GROUP` is the standard 2.5 seconds (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/attacks/icd_groups.dm.go:75`) with every third hit applying (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/attacks/icd_groups.dm.go`:142), so Yoimiya's seven arrows melt on the first, fourth and seventh. `BURNING_INTERNAL_COOLDOWN_GROUP` applies once each two seconds (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/attacks/icd_groups.dm.go`:79,146). A tag gcsim gives a group of its own keeps that group in place of the standard one (`InternalCooldownTagGroupMap`), as Furina's Salon members' tags keep gcsim's one in two over 30 seconds.

A transformative reaction's damage is limited the same way, its sequence a share of the damage, kept on the enemy per source, reaction and element, so Swirls of each element count apart (`applyReactionInternalCooldown`). Swirl, Superconduct, Shattered, Bloom, Hyperbloom and Burgeon deal two instances each half second (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/attacks/icd_groups.dm.go:77-78`), Overloaded and Electro-Charged one, and Burning's damage has no limit (`TransformativeReactionInternalCooldownGroupMap`). A Swirl, Superconduct or Overloaded triggered within a tenth of a second (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/swirl.go`:77,115,149,180, `superconduct.go`:89, `overload.go`:44) of the last of its kind to land on the enemy does not land, whatever its source: it deals no damage and reaches nothing, so two characters swirling one element together deal one Swirl (`applySimultaneousReactionInternalCooldown`).

### Reach

A reaction that lands reaches every other standing enemy within its radius of the one it landed on, measured to the enemy's edge, once the strike that triggered it has landed on every enemy it reaches (`landReactionReach`). An Overloaded's and a Superconduct's explosion reach 3 metres and deal each enemy their damage, and a Superconduct's takes each one's Physical RES down as it does its own target's. An Overloaded's explosion is a blunt hit of 90 poise, gcsim's, on its own target and on each enemy it reaches: each takes the poise damage, and on an enemy it reaches it drains a Freeze and shatters what is left, as any blunt hit does (`TransformativeReactionPoiseDamageMap`). It strikes the ores within its 3 metres too ([gathering](../gathering/design.md)). A Swirl reaches 5 metres. It applies its element to each enemy at the gauge it spreads, and every Swirl but a Hydro one deals each its damage, which the reactions its element triggers there amplify or add to as the wiki's chain reactions do, so a Pyro Swirl on a Wet enemy is vaporized. The reactions it triggers deal their own damage, priced from the character that swirled, and an Overloaded or a Superconduct among them reaches on in turn. The damage on each enemy reached counts into the source's internal cooldown there, and the tenth of a second's limit is the triggering target's alone (`TransformativeReactionReachRadiusMap`). A deployed member's `getPartyReactionBonus` adds a flat base, a DMG Bonus, a CRIT Rate, a CRIT DMG and an Elevation to each reaction a member makes, as `getPartyStrikeBonus` does a strike (`getDeployedReactionBonuses`), a kit's own reactions included. The reaction it reads carries the element its damage is dealt in (`KitReaction.element`): the element a Swirl absorbed, a Lunar reaction's own hit's, Electro for Lunar-Charged, and none for Shattered's physical damage, as gcsim v2.47.2 keys Mona's Hydro Swirl bonus on the Swirl's element (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/mona/cons.go:75`) and Columbina's Constellation 6 on a reaction attack's element (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/columbina/cons.go:215`). It carries the enemy the reaction lands on too, so a reach reads the bonuses afresh for each enemy it reaches and a Lunar reaction for the enemy its hit strikes, as gcsim hands a reaction attack's event its target and Mona's Constellation 4 raises the Lunar CRIT Rate only on an enemy under her Omen or bubble (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/mona/cons.go:196`, `:206`). The flat base is added before the enemy's RES, the DMG Bonus with the reaction's own, and a Stellar Swirl takes the same. An amplifying or a catalyze reaction takes its DMG Bonus inside its Elemental Mastery term instead, as the Amplifying and Catalyze bullets above say. The Elevation is a multiplier of its own, apart from the Base DMG Bonus and the DMG Bonus, by one plus it, over a Lunar contributor's share and over a Stellar reaction's damage with its flat base, as gcsim v2.47.2 multiplies a reaction's damage and flat damage alike (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/combat/reaction.go:30`). Only Lunar and Stellar damage is elevated in the game, so a transformative reaction reads none. Kaveh's Constellation 4 is his own hook, which adds 60% to each Bloom he makes. A Lunar reaction reads it per contributor, each contributor's CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG and DMG Bonus from its own bundle (`priceReactionContributors`), and a strike's transformative reactions on its target read it too (`priceEnemyReactions`, which takes the kit context a strike carries).

Electro-Charged arcs from each tick to the one Wet enemy nearest it within 5 metres, the wiki's figure, dealing it Electro-Charged's damage and applying nothing, as the wiki has the arc consume none of its Hydro. The ticks are priced from the combatant whose Electro or Hydro last charged the enemy, as each application that charges or refreshes it takes it over in gcsim (`applyEnemyElement`). The enemies' fixed step leaves the reactions an enemy's elements bring on their own on the enemy (`elementalReactions`), and the step of the kit on the field strikes them: each Electro-Charged tick deals its damage, drops the enemy's energy to the team and arcs on, and a tick on an enemy no combatant charged deals nothing (`strikeElementalTicks`).

A Burning tick deals Burning's damage to its enemy and reaches every other standing enemy within a metre, gcsim's circle on its target, applying its Pyro to each under that enemy's own Burning internal cooldown (`applyBurningInternalCooldown`) and dealing each Burning's damage, which no internal cooldown limits. The ticks, and the reactions a tick's Pyro triggers on its own enemy, are priced from the combatant whose Pyro or Dendro last lit or fed the Burning, as gcsim prices Burning again from each application that feeds it (`applyEnemyElement`). A tick takes the same combatant's party reaction DMG Bonus for its Burning reaction that an amplifying reaction takes (`getDeployedReactionBonuses` on that combatant, then `getPartyTransformativeDamage`), so Crimson Witch's Burning bonus and every other party bonus on Burning read the one rule. A tick's Pyro that lands on a Dendro enemy beside it lights that one too, from the same combatant, so Burning spreads through a group standing close.

A Crystallize a kit's hit triggers drops a shard 23 frames on (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/crystallize.go`:113), half a metre out from the enemy's edge (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/template/crystallize/shard.go`:33) at a bearing drawn from the world's random source, holding the shield of its element the crystallizing character's level and Elemental Mastery price (`addCrystallizeShards`). The shard lies 15 seconds (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/template/crystallize/shard.go`:38), and once more than three lie on the ground (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/combat/gadget.go`:21) all but the newest three go within two seconds. The character on the field picks one up within 2 metres of it (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/template/crystallize/shard.go`:35), 31 frames after it drops (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/template/crystallize/shard.go`:40, `pickUpCrystallizeShards`), and the team takes its Crystallize shield for 15 seconds. The pickup goes on the outbox as a `CrystallizeShardPickedUp` naming that character and the shard's element, sent in the same step, as gcsim's shard adds its shield (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/template/crystallize/shard.go:71`) and the add emits `OnShielded` (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/player/shield/handler.go:81`), which Gorou's Constellation 2, Archaic Petra's four-piece and Navia's Crystal Shrapnel read for a Crystallize shield. The shield itself is cast through `castKitShield`, which tells the team's kits it stands as a `ShieldCast` owned by the character whose reaction left the shard, gcsim's owner of a shard's shield (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/crystallize.go:102`). That shield overwrites the last Crystallize shield and stands beside every character's own, and it is no character's own: it holds Enduring Rock up, but not a heal that wants its striker's own shield.

A Bloom on an enemy, struck by a kit's hit or reached by a Swirl's Hydro, leaves a Dendro Core among the team's effects, dropping where a shard does and standing half a second on, as gcsim drops it (`addDendroCores`, `getEnemyDropPoint`). Five seconds after it stands (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/template/dendrocore/dendrocore.go`:42, marked `??` there) it bursts, dealing Bloom's damage to every enemy within 5 metres of it, the wiki's radius, priced from the character whose Bloom left it as the team's buffs then leave that character, and only while that character stands in the deployed team and is not down (`burstKitDendroCores`). At most five cores stand, and each core past the newest five bursts as the newest stands. Each core names its kind, Bloom for the cores a Bloom leaves and Bountiful for the cores a Bloom leaves under Nilou's Golden Chalice's Bounty, which burst 0.4 seconds after they are made over 6.5 metres and which no strike reaches (`burstKitDendroCores`, `strikeKitDendroCores`). A burst's reaction is read against its core's kind, so the deployed team's `getPartyReactionBonus` hooks give a Bountiful Core Ascension 4's bonus on Nilou's Max HP and a Bloom none (`burstKitDendroCores`). A kit's hit whose Electro or Pyro reaches a standing core, as it would reach an enemy's capsule standing there, sets it off from the striker (`strikeKitDendroCores`): Pyro bursts it as Burgeon over 5 metres at the next step, and Electro sends it off as Hyperbloom, which a second on lands on the standing enemy nearest the core within 15 metres, gcsim's shot, dealing its damage to every enemy within a metre of that one. Each is a transformative reaction's damage of Dendro under its own internal cooldown, two instances every half second from each source, and each gives Sprawling Greenery's Elemental Mastery as it is triggered.

### Lunar reactions

A character whose kit holds a Moonsign Benediction turns some of the party's reactions Lunar (`KitMoonsignBenediction`). A strike reads the deployed team's Moonsign as it lands (`getPartyMoonsign`): its level, Nascent Gleam for one Moonsign character and Ascendant Gleam for two or more (`getMoonsignLevel`); the Lunar reactions its Benedictions name; and the Base DMG Bonus each Benediction gives its reactions from its holder's stats as the team's buffs leave them, two on one reaction adding up. The Base DMG Bonus is its own multiplier, apart from the reaction's DMG Bonus, as gcsim v2.47.2 prices it (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/combat/reaction.go:30`), and a Benediction is the one source of it gcsim holds for a Lunar reaction (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/ineffa/asc.go:102`, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/columbina/asc.go:37`, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lauma/asc.go:119`, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/flins/asc.go:81`), so a deployed member's party reaction bonus has none. A flat rise to a Lunar reaction's base, as Nefer's Elemental Mastery gives her Lunar-Bloom, is that bonus's flat base. Lauma's turns Bloom into Lunar-Bloom, and Ineffa's turns Electro-Charged into Lunar-Charged.

`applyElement` takes the Lunar reactions the Moonsign names, and a step one of them converts is recorded as its Lunar reaction, consuming the auras as the step it replaces does (`getLunarReactionType`). Lunar-Bloom deals no damage of its own, as the wiki has it. It leaves a Dendro Core as a Bloom does, whose burst is a Bloom's, gives Sprawling Greenery's 30 Elemental Mastery, and lets the party's Verdant Dew gather for 149 frames, a Dew in each 146 of them up to three (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/player/verdantdew.go:11-12`), which a Lunar-Bloom triggered again restarts (`startPartyLunarReactions`, `stepVerdantDew`). The Dew is the party's, kept across swaps, and a kit spends it (`spendDew`), its Verdant Dew first and then its Moonridge Dew.

Where the Moonsign turns Electro-Charged Lunar, each Electro or Hydro that leaves the two lying together, with no Freeze over them, triggers Lunar-Charged instead, and the two tick no Electro-Charged while they lie together so. Lunar-Charged forms the party's cloud for 5.5 seconds (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/lunarcharged.go`:69), or keeps the one standing for 5.5 seconds again (`startPartyLunarReactions`). A new cloud ticks 9 frames on (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/lunarcharged.go:66`) and every 6 after, each tick striking every Lunar-Charged enemy within 8 metres (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/lunarcharged.go`:246) of the character on the field, measured to its edge, once in 2 seconds on each (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/lunarcharged.go`:259, `strikeLunarChargedCloud`). An aura keeps each source's share of it, the share an enemy's own application leaves, which every reduction of the aura takes alike (`getAuraSourceIds`). Each member of the deployed team whose Electro or Hydro still stands in the enemy's auras contributes the Lunar formula's damage of Lunar-Charged at its own level and Elemental Mastery, with its own CRIT and DMG Bonus, which the party reaction bonuses of the kits read for that contributor raise (`getPartyReactionBonus`'s CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG and DMG Bonus). The hit deals the largest at 60%, the next at 30% and two more at 5%, a fifth adding nothing (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/lunarcharged.go`:22, `sumLunarContributions`), against the enemy's Electro RES alone, and takes 0.4 units from its Electro and its Hydro (`strikeLunarCharged`). Its energy drops to the team as the element of the member who last charged the enemy.

Where the Moonsign turns Crystallize Lunar, Geo meeting Hydro triggers Lunar-Crystallize instead, under none of Crystallize's cooldown and dropping no shard, consuming the Hydro as Crystallize does and keeping the sources whose share stood in it (`getLunarReactionType`). Each counts toward the party's next Moondrift Harmony, up to 6, and its trigger and those sources join the Harmony's contributors. While fewer than three Moondrifts stand within 20 metres of the character on the field, it leaves as many more as make three, each a metre out from the enemy and lasting 9 seconds (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/lunarcrystallize.go:21`). With three standing and three counted, at most once in 37 frames, it spends the three to resonate a Harmony: three hits on their way to the enemy, landing 13, 25 and 37 frames on (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/lunarcrystallize.go`:29), the Moondrifts near kept standing for 9 seconds again and the contributors cleared (`addMoondrifts`, `stepMoondrifts`). A kit's hit that carries a `lunarHit` is a direct Lunar hit, which `strikeEnemy` prices by the Lunar formula instead of the general one (`getDirectLunarDamage`). Its reaction's coefficient, 1 for Lunar-Bloom, 3 for Lunar-Charged (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/combat/reaction.go`:26) and 1.6 for Lunar-Crystallize (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/combat/reaction.go`:28), multiplies the talent's multiplier of the stat it scales on, raised by the Moonsign's Base DMG Bonus for its reaction, then by 6 × EM / (EM + 2000) (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/combat/reaction.go:30`, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/enemy/damage.go:223`) plus the reaction bonus (`getLunarDamage`). Its flat bonus is added after, the enemy's RES alone cuts it, and its own CRIT raises it by the striker's CRIT DMG and the hit's own CRIT DMG bonus. It ignores defence and takes no DMG Bonus of its striker's. The party reaction bonuses its Lunar reaction takes on that enemy reach it as they reach the reaction (`getDeployedReactionBonuses`): the DMG Bonus into the reaction bonus, the CRIT Rate into its roll, the CRIT DMG onto a CRIT hit, and the Elevation over the whole last, as gcsim v2.47.2 multiplies a direct hit by one plus its Elevation after its RES and CRIT (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/enemy/damage.go:256`).

At Ascendant Gleam, a skill or burst of a character outside the Moonsign lights Ascendant Gleam on that character for 20 seconds as it starts, replacing the one another lit (`lightAscendantGleam`). While it stands, every Lunar damage takes a reaction bonus from the lighter's own stats, by its element: 0.9% for every 100 ATK of an Electro, Pyro or Cryo character, 0.6% for every 1,000 Max HP of a Hydro one, 2.25% for every 100 Elemental Mastery of a Dendro or Anemo one, and 1% for every 100 DEF of a Geo one, at most 36% (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/simulation/setup.go`:458,462,464,466, `getAscendantGleamBonus`), its 20 seconds at `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/simulation/setup.go`:486.

### Stellar Glimmer reactions

Stellar-Conduct and Stellar Swirl are the two Stellar Glimmer reactions, and the deployed team's Stellar Jubilee passives enable them. Neither occurs without an enabler. Only Odette's kit carries a Stellar Jubilee passive as built (`packages/genshin-world/src/data/kits/odette.json`, the only kit record under `packages/genshin-world/src/data/kits/` that names one), and it enables both reactions for its party. The Cryo Traveler's kit has no Cryo passive yet, and no other kit carries one.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  STEP["A reaction step: a Cryo Swirl or a Superconduct"] --> APPLY{"applyElement: does the party enable its Stellar form?"}
  ENABLE["Deployed Stellar Jubilee passives, Odette's the only one built"] -->|"enables"| APPLY
  APPLY -->|"no"| ORDINARY["The ordinary Swirl or Superconduct"]
  APPLY -->|"Swirl"| SSW["Stellar Swirl: Cryo element, spreads no Cryo"]
  APPLY -->|"Superconduct"| SC["Stellar-Conduct: deals no damage of its own"]
  SSW --> ANEMO["strikeStellarReaction: Anemo instance, Glimmer formula"]
  SSW --> VORTEX["Odette's kit: a Stellar Vortex, one stack a swirl"]
  VORTEX -->|"third stack"| LV2["Lv. 2 vortex"]
  VORTEX -->|"sixth stack or expiry"| BLAST["detonateStellarVortex: Cryo at the stack's coefficient"]
  SC --> FIELD{"addPolestarField: is the body inside a standing field?"}
  FIELD -->|"yes"| REFRESH["That field's time set back to full"]
  FIELD -->|"no"| PLACE["A new Polestar Field at the body"]
  REFRESH --> TICK["Each tick: the recorded Cryo and Electro hits become the count"]

  RADIANCE -->|"Stellar-Conduct's coefficient"| PRICE
```

When an enabler holds, `applyElement` records a Cryo Swirl as a Stellar Swirl and a Superconduct as a Stellar-Conduct, consuming its auras as the step it replaces does. A Stellar Swirl keeps the Cryo of its step and spreads no Cryo beside it. A Stellar-Conduct keeps the Superconduct's element and step, and covers the Freeze Superconduct as well as the Cryo aura. With no enabler held, the step is recorded as the ordinary reaction it always was.

A Stellar Swirl's Anemo instance and a Stellar Vortex's explosion are Stellar Glimmer hits, dealt by `strikeStellarReaction`. Each is the Lunar formula over its reaction's coefficient (`STELLAR_SWIRL_ANEMO_COEFFICIENT` for the Swirl, and the vortex's by stack). Each contributor's damage is worked out alone with standard crit, and the four highest are weighted 0.6, 0.3, 0.05 and 0.05 (`LUNAR_CONTRIBUTION_WEIGHTS`, through `sumLunarContributions`). A Stellar-Conduct deals no damage of its own, so the Glimmer formula reaches it only through a direct hit.

A Stellar-Conduct leaves a Polestar Field of 15 metres in radius (`POLESTAR_FIELD_RADIUS`) for 7 seconds (`POLESTAR_FIELD_SECONDS`). `addPolestarField` runs on the reaction path for any enabler's Stellar-Conduct, so no kit places the field. If the body stands inside a standing field, that field's time is set back to full and no second one is made. Otherwise a new field is cast at the body, beside any that stand. A field ticks every 4 seconds (`POLESTAR_FIELD_TICK_SECONDS`). While it stands, each enemy inside has its Physical RES reduced by 40% (`POLESTAR_PHYSICAL_RES_REDUCTION`), refreshed each step by `shredPolestarEnemies`.

Inside the field, the Starlight Prism records each Cryo or Electro hit on an enemy, as that element's internal cooldown lets it through. Each field tick makes the recorded count the one the buffs read, and starts the count again. While the body stands inside, its Cryo and Electro DMG Bonus carries the count, and every deployed member is in Radiance: Stellar-Conduct carrying it. The Prism's coefficient and its DMG Bonus by count are the `STELLAR_CONDUCT_*` constants in `packages/genshin-world/src/services/combat/damage/constants.ts`, each cited to the wiki's Stellar-Conduct page, and are read by `getStellarConductCoefficient` and `getStellarConductElementalDamageBonus`. A second application inside gcsim's per-character stack cooldown is recorded, as that cooldown is not built.

Odette's kit places a Stellar Vortex on the Stellar Swirl its reaction triggers, for the seconds her record gives it. A further Stellar Swirl adds a stack without renewing the vortex's time. The third stack makes it Lv. 2, with a wider radius (`STELLAR_VORTEX_LEVEL_TWO_RADIUS`), and the sixth stack explodes it at once (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/stellarswirl_gadget.go`:15 and `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/stellarswirl.go`:20). A vortex that runs out explodes as it stands. Its explosion's coefficient is the Lv. 1 value at its first two stacks and the Lv. 2 value from the third, read by `getStellarVortexCoefficient`, with both values in `packages/genshin-world/src/services/combat/damage/constants.ts` as the wiki's Damage page gives them.

A kit's hit that names a `stellarReactionType` is a direct Stellar hit, priced by `getDirectStellarDamage`. It takes the Glimmer formula over its talent multiplier of its striker's ATK, raised by the Base DMG Bonus of the team's Stellar Jubilees, its elemental mastery and its Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG Bonus. The enemy's RES alone cuts it, with no Defence. Its coefficient is 1 for a Stellar Swirl, and for a Stellar-Conduct it is the Prism's by the count its striker's Radiance carries. No kit names a `stellarReactionType` yet, and no kit's Stellar Jubilee gives a Base DMG Bonus, so the direct path and that bonus are built and wait on the kits.

The reasons behind these choices:

- **The party's enabler gates the reactions, and no gadget does.** A Stellar Jubilee is a passive of its character and holds while the character is deployed, so the Stellar reactions are read off the deployed team (`getPartyStellarReactionTypes`), as the Moonsign's Lunar types are. The wiki's Stellar Linchpin page says each Stellar Linchpin user's Jubilee enables the reactions, and gcsim v2.47.2 sets both enable flags at Odette's init (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/odette/stellar.go`). A gadget a kit places is not the gate.
- **A Stellar reaction keeps its step's element and consumption.** gcsim's "queueStellarSwirl" deals the Anemo hit and creates the vortex with no spread call, so the Stellar Swirl spreads no Cryo. gcsim's frozen variant takes the same trigger as the Cryo aura's, so the Stellar-Conduct covers the Freeze Superconduct too.
- **A Stellar-Conduct deals no damage, and a direct Stellar hit does.** The wiki's Stellar-Conduct page says the reaction does no damage on its own, and gcsim's `queueStellarConduct` only emits the event and places or refreshes the field (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/stellarconduct.go:74`). The Stellar-Conduct is a different reaction type from the Superconduct, so it takes none of the Superconduct's own damage or reach.
- **The Glimmer formula is the Lunar one.** The wiki's Damage page gives the Lunar and Stellar Glimmer reactions one formula, so the Stellar damage reuses `getLunarReactionDamage` and `sumLunarContributions`. A Stellar Swirl or a vortex has one contributor, its striker or its caster, where gcsim tracks every character that contributed. That is the provisional part.
- **The Starlight Prism lasts 7 seconds, as the wiki gives it.** gcsim v2.47.2 sets the field at 6 seconds (`sscFieldDur`, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/stellarconduct_gadget.go:17`), so the two disagree. The wiki's figure is taken provisionally in `POLESTAR_FIELD_SECONDS`, and a recording would settle it.
- **A Stellar-Conduct refreshes the field the body is in, and places one beside the others otherwise.** gcsim's `queueStellarConduct` resets the field the player is within and places a new one where the player is within none (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/stellarconduct.go:74`). Placing it on the reaction path means any enabler's Stellar-Conduct gets the field, not only Odette's kit.
- **A Stellar Vortex's stacks follow the wiki, one a Stellar Swirl.** gcsim's "addSSwStack" adds more than one stack a swirl, which would level the vortex on the second swirl. The wiki's third swirl is taken, and a restack does not renew the vortex's lifetime, as gcsim leaves it. A recording would settle it.
- **Each member's Radiance carries the Prism's count, and a direct hit reads it from its striker.** gcsim tags each character with the count while the body stands in the field (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/reactable/stellarconduct_gadget.go:160`). A direct Stellar-Conduct hit therefore takes its coefficient from its striker's status, and needs no state of its own.

### Shields and energy

`absorbShieldDamage` takes damage on a shield. Each point of its health absorbs its absorption times one plus the character's shield strength: a Geo shield's 150% of any damage, an elemental shield's 250% of its own element, and 100% otherwise (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/player/shield/template.go`:51-54,62-65). What it cannot absorb passes to the character. `getCrystallizeShieldHealth` is the shard's shield: its base for the crystallizing character's level, raised by 4.44 × EM / (EM + 1400) (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/template/crystallize/shield.go`:31).

A normal or charged attack's hit that strikes an enemy has a chance to give its character 1 energy, which Energy Recharge does not raise. The chance starts at its weapon's base, a sword's 10% and every other's none (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/energy.go`:36,39-44), and rises with each such hit that gives none, by 5% for a sword and a bow, 10% for a claymore and a catalyst and 4% for a polearm, until one gives it and the chance falls back to the base (`WeaponTypeAttackEnergyChanceMap`). The roll is drawn from the world's random source, and the misses are counted on the kit's state, which a swap starts afresh (`rollKitAttackEnergy`). The strikes that struck an enemy roll once each, however many enemies they struck (`gainKitAttackEnergy`).

`getEnergyGain` is the energy a party member gains from a particle or an orb. A particle gives 3 of their own element, 1 of another and 2 of none, and an orb three times that. A member off the field takes 80%, 70% or 60% in a party of two, three or four, and their energy recharge multiplies it (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/player/character/energy.go`:54-66).

## Calling in

The rules hold no clock of their own past a target's state, so a caller drives them:

1. Each fixed step, `advanceElementalState` moves every target's state on and pushes the ticks it brings onto the caller's array, so a quiet step allocates nothing.
2. A hit runs its element's gauge through `applyInternalCooldown` with its tag's group, a blunt hit through `applyBluntHit` first, then `applyElement` with the share it applies.
3. Each reaction returned prices its damage through the damage functions, a transformative reaction's at the share `applyReactionInternalCooldown` leaves of it. A reaction that reaches past its target is landed on the targets round it by `landReactionReach`, and a Dendro Core's burst on the targets round the core.

The enemies' `damageEnemy` takes a hit's damage and poise damage, which is where these meet the enemies.

### The kit's hits

The character on the field runs its kit at the world's fixed step ([character controller](../character-controller/design.md)). Each step reads the frame's presses and the body's state, starts the action the kit allows, and turns the body to the enemy the action's area scores highest. Every hit that lands runs through the steps above on each enemy in its area, and every particle an enemy drops is handed to the party's energy.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  PRESS["A frame's presses, held for the step, and the attack held"] --> GATE{"Allowed? Movement state, cooldown, charges, energy, stamina"}
  GATE -->|"no"| IDLE["Nothing starts"]
  GATE -->|"yes"| ACTION["The kit's action: string, charged attack, plunge, skill or burst"]
  ACTION -->|"it starts"| AIM["The body turns to the enemy its area scores highest"]
  ACTION -->|"each hitmark, past the action's end too"| HIT["A hit lands on each enemy in its area"]
  HIT --> STRIKE["strikeEnemy: blunt, internal cooldown, element, reactions' limits, getDamage, damageEnemy"]
  STRIKE -->|"an Overloaded, Superconduct, Swirl or Electro-Charged that lands"| REACH["landReactionReach: the enemies round it"]
  REACH -->|"a reaction its Swirl triggers that reaches"| REACH
  TICKS["strikeElementalTicks: each enemy's ticks"] -->|"an arc or a Burning tick's Pyro"| REACH
  STRIKE -->|"a Bloom"| CORE["addDendroCores: a Dendro Core by the enemy"]
  HIT -->|"its Electro or Pyro on a standing core"| CORE
  CORE -->|"its seconds run out: burstKitDendroCores"| REACH
  STRIKE -->|"particles"| ENERGY["gainPartyEnergy: each standing member, up to its burst's cost"]
  STRIKE -->|"a normal or charged attack's that struck"| ATTACK_ENERGY["gainKitAttackEnergy: its weapon's chance of 1 energy"]
  ENEMY["An enemy's strike reaches the body"] --> HURT["strikePartyMember: getDamage, then the shields, then Max HP"]
  HURT -->|"the team all down"| RESPAWN["reviveParty at 35%, jumped to the nearest statue"]
```

The enemies' strikes go the other way: `strikePartyMember` prices an enemy's ATK through `getDamage` against the member on the field as the team's buffs leave it, takes it from the team's shields first, all at once, through `absorbKitShield`, and the world screen respawns the deployed team when all of it has fallen. It returns a `ShieldAbsorbed` event for each shield that took any of it, then one `PartyMemberStruck` for the strike itself, with the enemy, the member and whether a shield took any of it, which the world screen puts on the kit outbox. A strike a shield absorbed whole sends it too, as gcsim emits `OnPlayerHit` ahead of the shields (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/avatar/avatar.go:31`), so a kit counting the attacks a member takes reads it rather than `HealthChanged`, which has no striker and misses an absorbed strike. A live taunt in aggro range takes the strike through `damageKitTaunt`.

## Decisions

- **The Traveler's kit is the first, at talent level 1.** Its five strikes, charged attack and plunges are the wiki's figures for Lumine's Foreign Ironwind, held in her record: strikes of 44.5%, 43.4%, 53.0%, 58.3% and 70.8% of ATK with poise 40.5, 39.6, 48.6, 54 and 64.8; a charged attack of 55.9% and 72.2% for 20 stamina, poise 50.6 each; a plunge's collision of 63.9% with poise 25; and a low and a high plunge of 128% and 160%, poise 100 and 150, both blunt. The normal and charged attacks share the Normal Attack tag, and the plunges apply under none. Until the Traveler resonates with a statue, its skill and burst are placeholders shaped as the Anemo Traveler's: Palm Vortex's one hit of 176% on a 5 second cooldown, and Gust Surge's one hit of 80.8% for 60 energy on a 15 second cooldown.
- **The elementless Traveler deals physical damage with them.** Their gauges are set, but combat applies an element only for a character that has one, so the skill and burst are physical until a statue gives the Traveler its element.
- **Actions are a state machine on the fixed step.** Pressing the attack advances the string while the last strike's window is open, which closes at the frame the next attack may start (gcsim v2.47.2's frames, at 60 fps), and the string starts again 0.5 seconds after a strike ends unpressed, provisional. Holding past 0.3 seconds, also provisional, makes the string's next strike the charged attack once that strike ends, if 20 stamina is left to spend it (`startNextKitAction`). A press in the air high enough above the ground is a plunge, a low one when it lands from 2.4 metres or less and a high one from higher. `E` is the skill, on its cooldown, and `Q` the burst, at full energy, which it empties. The step reads the body's movement state, so no attack starts while climbing, swimming or gliding, and the skill and burst also start in the air, falling or jumping.
- **An attack aims as the game's does.** At each action's start the body turns to the enemy its targeting area scores highest: 0.7 × (1 − distance ÷ radius) + 0.3 × (1 − angle ÷ 180°), a fifth of it while the body stands more than 2 metres above the ground the enemies stand on, and a dead enemy never scored. The swords' normal and charged attacks target within 5 metres and 6 high, Palm Vortex within 15 and 10. With none in the area the body keeps its facing (`selectAttackTarget`). The enemy it turns to is the primary target until another action targets, which a hit no action targets is placed on, as gcsim's on-target hits are on its `PrimaryTarget` (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/combat/enemy.go:50`), and the step lets it go once it dies or leaves the world (`stepActiveKit`).
- **A hit reaches what its cylinder holds.** Each hit's area is a cylinder centred on the body's feet. It reaches an enemy within its radius plus the capsule's, within half its fan plus the capsule's angular half width as seen from the body, and overlapping in height (`checkIsInAttackArea`). Palm Vortex's area is the wiki's, 6 metres at 100 degrees and 2 high; the plunge's collision is 1 metre all round, as the wiki gives it; the rest are provisional until the attack clips and a recording measure them.
- **An action holds the body still.** While an action plays, the controller is given the frame's presses with no move, so the body stands where it is and only turns to its target. A jump or a dash the action does not allow ends it at once, ahead of the attack-cancel frame its `seconds` gives, since the kit does not model the earlier cancels yet.
- **A queued dash or jump waits for the action to end.** The kit records hold no dash or jump cancel frames, so the cancel windows are not built, and the movement starts at the action's end unless its start gate refuses it then, or the body stands in neither a ground state nor a dash, fall or jump state, which drops it, as a glide does.
- **A hit lands at its hitmark, even past its action's end.** gcsim queues each attack at its hitmark as its action plays (its attack queue), whatever the character does next, so an arrow in flight or a burst's later strikes land after the cancel frame an action's `seconds` gives. An action that runs its course hands the hits whose hitmarks fall past its seconds to `queueKitHits`, which casts them as a summon at the body running on from the action's own clock. Each lands at its hitmark from where the action was played, priced by the combatant that played it and dealt as its action's kind of attack, an infusion on that character infuses it as it lands, and a normal attack's first sends `NormalAttackLanded` as it lands. A later action or a swap leaves them landing, as gcsim's queue does. A jump, a dash or a fall that ends the action before its `seconds` drops them with the rest of its hits, and a drown or a jump clears them with the team's effects. An action's `seconds` is therefore its gcsim cancel frame, never stretched to its last hit, and a hit's hitmark is gcsim's, never pulled inside the action.
- **The plunge strikes on its way down and as it lands.** Every 0.3 seconds while it falls, provisional, its collision hits what stands in its area, and the landing is the low or high plunge by the drop from its start.
- **A hit goes through combat as built.** A kit's hit is priced by `getDamage` from the attacker's attributes at the talent's multiplier. Its order is blunt first, then the internal cooldown and the element, then the damage with the reactions' bonuses and own damage as `priceEnemyReactions` prices them, and last `damageEnemy` (`strikeEnemy`). A kit's `getStrikeAdditiveBaseDamageBonus` adds a flat amount into the hit's base damage that `getDamage` prices, and each per-strike hook reads the team's effects and the striker as it is priced, so a status can gate it and a buff on the striker counts. No kit computes damage of its own.
- **Each member's cooldowns run at the fixed step.** Every member of the deployed team's skill and burst cooldowns lower each step, off the field too, and stand still while a screen holds the world (`stepPartyCooldowns`).
- **Particles reach every member standing.** Each particle a struck enemy drops takes the striker's element, and gives each standing member of the deployed team `getEnergyGain`'s energy, more to a member of that element, up to its burst's cost. A member's element is its row of the roster's, none for the Traveler (`createCombatant`). A fallen member takes none (`gainPartyEnergy`).
- **An enemy's strike uses the same formula.** Its ATK times a provisional multiplier of 1, dealt in its kind's attack element and as physical damage by a kind with none, through `getDamage` with the member on the field's DEF and its RES to that element or its Physical RES, as its buffs leave them, its base 0%. Each element's RES is the attribute `ElementResistanceAttributeMap` names, which Defender's Will gives too (`computeEnemyStrikeDamage`). The least overflow the team's shields leave, over the member's Max HP, is the share the member takes (`strikePartyMember`).
- **A member's DMG Reduction lowers an enemy's strike's DMG Bonus.** The wiki's [DMG Reduction](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/DMG_Reduction) is an attribute of the target, subtracted from the incoming attack's DMG Bonus, its sources adding up, and past the whole it leaves the attack no damage. It is `Attribute.DamageReduction`, the game's `FIGHT_PROP_SUB_HURT` (gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/model/enums.pb.go:455`), which a kit gives a member as a buff, so Jean's Constellation 6, Xingqiu's Rain Swords and Beidou's Thunderbeast's Targe each buff the member they cut a strike on. An enemy's strike carries no DMG Bonus of its own, so its multiplier is 1 less the reduction, never below none (`computeEnemyStrikeDamage`). gcsim models none of it: its DamageReductionMod (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/player/character/mods.go:27`), whose amounts `DamageReduction` adds up (`:329`), is added and read by nothing, and its Jean marks Constellation 6 not implemented (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/jean/cons.go:63`).
- **A normal attack rolls energy once per hit, not once per enemy it strikes.** gcsim rolls for each enemy after a fifth of a second's pause, its own guess; a sword's swing through a group should show no more energy than through one enemy.
- **A Dendro Core is struck where an enemy's capsule on the core's ground point would be reached.** No source gives the size a hit finds a core at, so `strikeKitDendroCores` reaches a core as it reaches that capsule.
- **A Crystallize shard flies in from within 2 metres.** `CRYSTALLIZE_SHARD_PICKUP_RADIUS` is gcsim's 2-metre radius, as no source gives the reach the game draws a shard from.

## Notes

- **What is left of a trigger stays at its own gauge.** An aura left over after a reaction is taxed and decays as an attack of the gauge left would, since the sources give no rate for a leftover.
- **A Freeze over a Freeze keeps the larger.** Frozen triggered again over a Freeze keeps the larger gauge at the Freeze's growing rate, as any aura reapplied keeps its rate. A new Freeze starts at 0.4U a second.
- **No Frozen duration is extended.** Mona's Constellation 1 names a Frozen 15% longer, but the wiki's notes find it has no effect on Frozen's duration, contrary to its description ([Prophecy of Submersion](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Prophecy_of_Submersion)), and gcsim leaves it out as bugged (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/mona/cons.go:39`). No other source extends a Frozen, so the Frozen gauge's decay has no hook to lengthen it.
- **A Moondrift's place is gcsim's guess.** gcsim leaves the three a metre out from the enemy at even bearings and marks the place a guess; it matters only to whether three stand within 20 metres of the character on the field. The kit keeps the three even bearings at a metre: gcsim gives no measured placement, so the even spacing is the least-biased choice until a recording says otherwise. The roadmap's manual verification has the question.
- **Every source's share of an aura falls with the aura.** A source's share is kept as how far the aura stands above it, so the aura's decay and each reaction's consumption take every share alike, and a source contributes to Lunar-Charged while the aura holds more than the others laid above its share. gcsim keeps each source's durability apart; one rate for all of them is the simplification.
- **Burning's first tick is a quarter second after it lights.** The library places its first Pyro between a quarter second and 0.42 seconds after the text, and the rules take the earliest.
- **Electro-Charged's last tick is seen at the step.** An aura decaying out between two steps ends it at the step after, so a target advanced at the world's fixed step ends within a sixtieth of a second of the game's.
- **Electro-Charged arcs to the nearest Wet enemy.** The wiki names a single other Wet enemy within 5 metres, marking the radius to verify, and says nothing of which one is taken when several stand there, so the nearest is a provisional choice.
- **An attack's hit rolls once for energy, however many enemies it strikes.** The wiki has a hit roll as it hits an enemy and does not say what several enemies do; gcsim rolls for each enemy, with a fifth of a second after a pass in which none rolls, which its own source calls a guess. One roll a hit is the provisional choice, and a swap starting the misses afresh is gcsim's assumption.
- **A Dendro Core is reached as an enemy's capsule is, and drawn by nothing yet.** No source gives the size a hit finds a core at, so a kit's hit sets one off where it would strike an enemy standing on the core's ground point, provisionally. The core stands unseen until the world draws it.
- **Only a kit's own hit sets a Dendro Core off.** A Swirl's spread, a Burning tick's Pyro and an explosion leave the cores be, and a core's burst, Burgeon and Hyperbloom deal the 2% the wiki gives the party nothing yet.
- **A Crystallize shard is picked up within 2 metres, and drawn by nothing yet.** No source gives the reach the game draws a shard to the character from, so it is gcsim's shard radius, provisionally. The shard lies unseen until the world draws it.
- **The wiki's mastery scales are taken as written.** Its 2.78 and 4.44 are rounded, and its tables of the bonus they give read them as written.
