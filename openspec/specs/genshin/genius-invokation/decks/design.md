# Genius Invokation TCG decks

Each built deck's characters, their skills and their action cards has a module, and a duel plays them through the engine's public functions. This page holds how those modules are written and which decks are built. The rules they play under are on the [rules page](../rules/design.md), and the [card game](../README.md) index holds the data they are read from.

## How a card and a skill are played

- **A skill is its effect's name.** `Effect_Damage_<Name>_<n>` deals `n` damage of the element the name spells: `Physic` is Physical, and `Fire`, `Water`, `Ice`, `Electric`, `Wind`, `Rock` and `Grass` are the seven elements. The monsters' Electro is `Elec`, so the damage names map takes `Elec` beside `Electric` (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/constants.ts`, `GCG_EFFECT_DAMAGE_REGEX` and `GcgEffectDamageNameMap`). A character's own script is `Char_Skill_<id>`, and each one is a module under `packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/cards`. A card's own script is named by the card, and its module is keyed by the card's id.
- **The damage numbers of a character's script come from its wiki skill page.** The description's damage is a placeholder the game fills from the skill's configuration, which the dump's tables do not hold. Each module cites the wiki's value for its skill, and no test checks a damage against a description, since the description carries no number to check.
- **A card module's hooks are the card's behaviour, each optional.** Equipment is equipped to a character, a support takes a support zone's place, and an event is gone once played. A zone card's usages and rounds are the module's own, and a card with a limit is taken off the field once it runs out. A once-per-match effect the card does not spend keeps its count in the zone card's counter, since a usage would take the card off the field when it reaches zero (Dunyarzad's draw, `packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/cards/dunyarzad.ts`).
- **A card hears the turn's start, a declared round's end, and a summon's leaving.** A side handed its choice of action runs its field cards' `onBeforeAction` hooks first, and a side declaring its round's end runs their `onRoundEndDeclared` hooks before the declaration counts. A hook returns the damage its card deals from its side, and the damage seam bears it like any other. Each summon that leaves a side's field, whether the prune takes it off or a card's play does, gives that side's supports one `onSummonDisappears` each, through `notifyGcgSummonsDisappeared` (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/effects/notifyGcgSummonsDisappeared.ts`). The hooks' cards are `packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/cards/tenkoThunderbolts.ts` and `packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/cards/sesshouSakura.ts`, and the damage seam is `packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/runGcgSideDamageHook.ts`.
- **The action phase's hooks run over the cards listed when it begins.** A card a hook moves to the other side is not run again there, so Kid Kujirai creates its Omni Element die once a phase, for the side whose zone it sits in (its text's "you"), then moves to the opposing support zone (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/cards/kidKujirai.ts`).
- **A summon or combat status placed again is refreshed, or stacks up to its card's limit when its text says it can stack; a support never refreshes.** `placeGcgZoneCard` gives an existing zone card of the same id its module's starting usages and rounds, and pushes a new one only when none is there (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/effects/placeGcgZoneCard.ts`). A card that names a most usages stacks instead: placed again, its starting usages are added to the usages it has, up to that most, as Bake-Kurage, Sesshou Sakura and Superlative Superstrength do, and the reaction cards Burning Flame, Dendro Core and Catalyzing Field do too. A card that names a most rounds stacks its rounds the same way, as the General's War Banner does up to three. The game's card text says the first three stack, so the refresh rule for every other summon is unchanged. Talisman Spirit, Soumetsu's Frostflake, Shattering Ice and Yaksha's Mask keep one copy on the field that way. An onstage is a combat status, so it follows the same rule, and a second slot would make the card's own cap meaningless: Burning Flame's card text on the wiki reads "1 Usage. Max 2 stacks", and Changing Shifts and Chonghua Frost Field, placed again, refresh the one on the field. The copy already there keeps its counter, since Wagner's and Timaeus's counters are their own. A support is not refreshed: a second copy takes a slot of its own.
- **Every damage a side deals passes the field's bonuses in order.** Every field card's additive bonus applies first, then every doubling, so Illusory Bubble doubles after Inspiration Field's bonus. `modifyGcgDamageDealt` applies them, and `applyGcgDamage` runs it first on every damage, so a skill's hit, a card's damage on a skill, an End Phase DMG and a switch's DMG all bear it (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/effects/modifyGcgDamageDealt.ts`, `applyGcgDamage.ts`). A Swirl's spread and a piercing run it once for their event. A damage carries the skill it is dealt by, so a card can tell a Normal Attack from the rest: Jueyun Guoba's one DMG more is the next Normal Attack only. The Plunging Attack bonus is the skill's own hit's, so a spread, a piercing and a card's damage on the skill carry the skill but not the plunge.
- **A card's skill damage is returned, not dealt.** A card's `onSkillUsed` returns the damage it deals, and the skill run deals it. A card that deals damage itself would import the damage pipeline it is part of (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/effects/runGcgOnSkillUsed.ts`).
- **A skill follows the card path with its own costs.** It is paid, then its effect runs: a shared damage, dealt with the field's bonuses, then the character's script's own after-effects, such as Dawn's Pyro Infusion or Fantastic Voyage's Inspiration Field (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/runGcgSkillUse.ts`).
- **A skill whose only effect is a summon takes `createGcgSummonAfterDamage`.** Its `afterDamage` places the summon on its side's field once the damage is dealt, or refreshes it if it is there, so the skill names its summon and nothing else (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/effects/createGcgSummonAfterDamage.ts`). A skill whose `afterDamage` does more than place its summon keeps its own body.
- **A card in hand is placed by its kind.** Equipment is equipped to its target, a support takes the support zone, and an event is played and gone. Costs are paid, reduced by the field first, and then the card's module plays: its effect, then any skill it has used at once (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/playGcgCard.ts`).
- **The field holds what the cards say, up to their limits.** A dice cap of sixteen, four supports, and one equipment of each kind a character holds, as the game gives them. Timmie's Pigeon is gained at each end phase, the one round trigger the card's text does not name (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/cards/timmie.ts`).
- **The field's choices take the engine's fixed order.** Calx's Arts shifts one energy from each of at most two standing characters on standby, in their order. A weapon or artifact shift moves the first equipment that fits, and Quick Knit and Send Off take the summon their target names by its place on the field. A choice a duel screen can ask the player is left to that screen.
- **A card a skill creates hears the next skill, not its own.** The on-use hooks run over the cards on the field when the skill is used, so Pyronado does not deal its 2 Pyro DMG on the use that creates it.
- **A passive's hooks run after each skill its character uses.** Hide switches the next standing character in after the skill, through the passive's `afterSkillUsed` hook, which runs after the on-use hooks (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/cards/hide.ts`).
- **Passives give their statuses when the battle begins.** A passive's `getStartingStatuses` gives its statuses when the side is created, and Infused Stonehide gives two. Flowfire Edge converts the character's Physical DMG to Pyro for three usages, and Elemental Lifeform: Electro keeps Electro applied to its character, which a hit re-applies after it reacts, and takes no Electro DMG. The starting statuses are the cards' `getStartingStatuses` in `packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/cards/flowfireEdgeSkill.ts`, `electroCrystalCoreSkill.ts`, `fieryRebirthSkill.ts` and `kamisatoArtSenhoSkill.ts`.
- **Guaranteed dice are set at the roll.** Crimson Witch of Flames and Jade Chamber set their two starting dice when the dice are rolled, and a reroll may still throw them.
- **Flowing Flame's Searing Onslaught is used at once when it is equipped, without its cost.**

## Character and card decisions

- **A talent card is equipped and its skill used at once.** Naganohara Meteor Swarm, Streaming Surge and Transcendent Automaton each equip to their character while she is active, and the skill the card names is used at once, through one shared helper. Streaming Surge gives each summon of her side a usage when Tide and Torrent is used. Transcendent Automaton switches to the next standing character after Blustering Blade and to the previous one after Frosty Assault, the previous being the standing character before her in order.
- **Katheryne's fast switch is a card's hook.** The switch takes the fast action of a passive or of a support, so a switch consults the field's cards for `checkIsSwitchFast`, and Katheryne takes it once a round.
- **Oceanic Mimics are summoned by the kind fewest on the field (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/effects/summonGcgOceanicMimics.ts`).** The game chooses the kind at random, prioritising a kind the side does not hold. A duel's skill has no random source to draw on, so the summon takes the kind with the fewest copies, earliest kind first on a tie. Once a side holds all three kinds, a further Mimic refreshes the earliest kind's rather than doubling it. Rhodeia's Myriad Wilds summons two this way.
- **The Frog's one usage is kept until it is spent.** The Frog takes one DMG off each active-character hit, once, and its usage is kept while untouched, so it stays on the field until the end phase deals its two Hydro DMG and takes it off. Its spent state is its counter.
- **Food is one a character a round (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/effects/checkIsGcgFoodEatable.ts`).** Jueyun Guoba and Northern Smoked Chicken hold a food status for the round, and a character holding one takes no second food. Mondstadt Hash Brown keeps its own once-a-round gate, which is not a status. Minty Meat Rolls and Sweet Madame are food too, so their character eats no other food that round.
- **Minty Meat Rolls takes a Void die.** Its next three Normal Attacks cost one Unaligned die less, which its reduction takes from the Void line alone.
- **Two of the supports' values are the dump's descriptions.** Wangshu Inn heals the most injured standby character for two, and Iron Tongue Tian gives one Energy to a standing character without its maximum, active first, both from the card descriptions, at two usages each.
- **Dandelion Breeze heals the standing characters only**, since a defeated character takes no heal, as Signature Mix's heal does.
- **Niwabi Enshou's +1 is taken on the conversion.** Its card reads that its character's Normal Attacks deal one DMG more and convert their Physical DMG to Pyro. The engine's damage carries no skill to test for a Normal Attack, so the card adds one to each Physical damage it converts, which the decks' Normal Attacks are, and spends a usage on each.
- **Mona's Illusory Torrent is recorded when it applies.** The passive makes the first switch away from Mona in a round a fast action, and the switch records the passive as used for the round.
- **Midnight Phantasmagoria's piercing is a skill hook.** Its 2 Piercing DMG to each standby opposing character is the skill's `getStandbyPiercing`, dealt after the damage, so no card module imports the damage pipeline it sits in.
- **I Haven't Lost Yet! reads a defeat this round.** A side records a defeat when one of its characters falls, and the round's end clears the record, so the card plays once a round, and only after a defeat that round.
- **Cat-Claw Shield is a shield, not a card.** Icy Paws grants its one Shield point to the active character at once and leaves nothing on the field, as the card's text reads.
- **Tubby reads a location tag the slice writes.** Each card's slice entry carries `isLocation`, from the dump's `GCG_TAG_PLACE` tag, so the reduction is the card's own tag rather than a list kept beside it. Tubby takes two dice off the next location support a side plays, once a round.
- **Large Wind Spirit converts once, on the first Swirl its side makes.** Its damage is Anemo until a Swirl reaction on its side gives it the Swirled element, which its zone card keeps as its `element`. A second Swirl leaves it as it is, as the card's text allows.
- **A defeat a card answers restores its character.** Before a character falls, the cards attached to it may answer the defeat with the HP they name: Fiery Rebirth restores 4 HP once, and Electro Crystal Core restores 1 HP once, each spent by its answer. Only the characters standing before the hit are answered, and each answering card hears its answer through `onDefeatReplaced`.
- **A skill prepared for a side runs as its turn begins.** A skill's after-effect may prepare another skill for its user. The side's prepared skills run when the turn passes to it, before its before-action hooks, each for a preparer still standing; a skill prepared while they run waits for the following turn. The wiki's prepared skills are "executed automatically 1 turn after" the skill that prepared them (Heron Strike, Whirling Lashes), and a side that has declared its end keeps the turn with no new one begun (the Rules page, Combat Action and Declare Round End), so its prepared skills wait while the opponent has declared its end. A prepared skill is a use by its holder, so the on-use hooks of its field hear it (`runGcgOnSkillUsed`), and a Crowfeather Cover spends a usage on a prepared Elemental Skill, as the Rules page's Usage row states: "Each time the effect of such a card is triggered, 1 Usage will be consumed." A prepared skill is recorded as used this round, as a skill its character uses is, so the reads of used skills (Ellin's, Flowing Flame's, Searing Onslaught's) see it. This is provisional: no wiki page says whether a prepared skill counts as used. The phrase "Prepare Skill" has no page of its own, and the Rules, Ellin, Flowing Flame and Electro Hypostasis pages are silent, so the basis is the game's log, which calls it a skill use, not checked against the wiki here. The any-side hooks and passives still hear only the skills a character uses, pending a rule that says otherwise.
- **A card on one side may surcharge the other side's switch.** A card's `surchargeCost` adds dice to the opposing side's switch cost before the cost is taken, and hears the payment once it is made. Chains of Warding Thunder does so once a round (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/payGcgSubjectCost.ts`, `packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/cards/chainsOfWardingThunder.ts`).
- **A depleted shield is answered by the card attached to its character.** A hit that takes a character's shield to 0 calls each attached card's `onShieldDepleted`, and the piercing DMG it names reaches every opposing character: the active one through a hit, and those on standby through the standby piercing. Aegis of Abyssal Flame answers once with 1 DMG. A character the hit defeats has its cards discarded first, so the depletion answers only a character still standing.
- **A Technique is an action its character's equipment gives, and no skill.** Pyro Scorpion gives its character Burning Assault, which `useGcgSkill` finds on the equipment and pays as a skill is paid, the turn passing after it. The use is not recorded in `usedSkillIds`, so the rules that count a repeated skill, Ellin's once-a-Round reduction among them, never read it, and Sumeru City excludes it by its own check.
- **The DMG a character takes is heard wherever it lands.** Spirit of Omen's Power and Vourukasha's Glow hear the damage a standby character takes from a reaction's piercing or spread and from standby piercing, as well as the active character's hit, since every hurt of a character goes through the one primitive that tells its cards (the rules design's "A character hears the DMG it takes").
- **Shimenawa's Reminiscence reduces from any character's equipment.** Its game text names the side, so the artifact on a standby character still reduces the active character's Elemental Skill. The wiki's Equipment Card page reads "the character" instead, so the side reading is provisional, and the cards whose text names their holder reduce only while the holder is active (see the rules design).

## Words

- **The slice carries the text ids, and the words are the world's.** Each character and action card carries `nameTextId` and `descriptionTextId`, the dump's `nameTextMapHash` and `descTextMapHash` as numbers (`~/Esposter/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/GCGCharExcelConfigData.json`, a character row's `nameTextMapHash` and `descTextMapHash`). `pnpm -C scripts genshin:text gcg` publishes every name and description the slices name as `gcgText/<language>`, one record a language, which `GcgTextLoaderMap` fetches on demand (`scripts/src/services/genshinText/buildGcgText.ts`, `packages/genshin-world/src/services/gcg/GcgTextLoaderMap.ts`). A description is the card's full text, since the on-table text the dump also gives is missing from the text map for many cards.
- **A screen handles two things in the words.** A character's description id names no text in any language of the dump, so its entry is empty. Most descriptions keep the game's reference tokens (`$[K…]` for a keyword, a character, a skill or a card, and `{SPRITE_PRESET#…}` for an icon) for the screen to resolve.
