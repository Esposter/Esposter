# Hu Tao

Hu Tao's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createHuTaoKitBehaviour`, avatar 10000046: a six-strike string with its seventh hit, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, Guide to Afterlife's Paramita Papilio stance with its strikes and charged attack, and Spirit Soother's hit and heal. The heal is a share of her Max HP for each enemy the hit strikes, up to five a cast, read from proud skill group 4639 at indices 2 and 3, with the larger share taken at or under the half-HP threshold of index 6 (`ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`: 6.2625% and 8.35% of Max HP at level 1, and 0.5); the cached wiki's Spirit Soother page (6.26% and 8.35% at level 1, up to five triggers) and gcsim v2.47.2 (MIT) `hutao/burst.go` give the same two shares. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 4631, 4632 and 4639; the charged attack's stamina, the Paramita's seconds and the skill's cooldown, and the burst's cooldown and energy cost are read from the same groups (4631's index 8 is the charged attack's 25 stamina, the wiki's Secret Spear of Wangsheng `chargedStamina`; 4632's index 4 is the Paramita's 9 seconds, gcsim `skill.go` line 41; 4632's index 5 is the skill's 16-second cooldown, `skill.go` line 71; 4639's index 4 is the burst's 15 seconds, `burst.go` line 78, and its index 5 the 60 energy the wiki's Spirit Soother gives). The stance is a stance on her for its seconds, which infuses her normal attacks, charged attack and plunges with Pyro and raises her ATK by her ATK share of Max HP as a buff from the same seconds (4632's index 1, 3.841% at level 1, the wiki's Guide to Afterlife ATK Increase; gcsim `skill.go` lines 35 to 45). All three end as she leaves the field, as gcsim's exit deletes the Paramita's ATK, which its infusion reads (`hutao.go` lines 61 to 67). Guide to Afterlife's HP cost is built: 30% of her current HP, the share read from its skill group (4632's index 0, 0.3; gcsim `skill.go` line 56), paid through the party's HP as the stance starts and never taking her below 1 HP. Spirit Soother's hit is its half HP multiplier, the burst group's index 1, while her HP is at or under half of its Max HP, the threshold read at index 6, as the burst starts (379.09% at level 1, the wiki's Low HP Skill DMG; gcsim `burst.go` lines 24 to 31). Blood Blossom is built: the Paramita's charged attack puts it on each enemy it hits, and it ticks every 4 seconds for 9.5 seconds on that enemy, each tick a Pyro hit of 1U at the skill group's index 2 (64% at level 1; gcsim `skill.go` lines 107 and 111, a 240-frame interval and a 570-frame duration). Gcsim counts the Blood Blossom tick as the skill's one hit, and the record holds that hit as the named list `hits.bloodBlossom`, so the skill's own list is empty. Her Ascension 1 passive, Flutter By, is built: when the Paramita ends, each ally but her gains 12% CRIT Rate for 8 seconds (gcsim `asc.go` lines 9 to 27, a 480-frame buff). Her Ascension 4 passive, Sanguine Rouge, is built as a 33% Pyro DMG Bonus while her HP is at or under half, as the game text and gcsim v2.47.2 give it (`asc.go` lines 44 to 49). Constellation 1 is built: the charged attack's stamina multiplier is zero while a stance is on her, which the kit reads from its character (see Open). Constellation 2 is built: Blood Blossom's tick adds 10% of her Max HP as it stood when the effect was applied, which the status keeps as its `wielderMaxHealth`, and Spirit Soother applies Blood Blossom to each enemy its hit strikes, as the game's English text gives it (gcsim `skill.go` line 139 adds the same 10% of Max HP to each tick from the second constellation). Constellation 4 is built as each other deployed member's 12% CRIT Rate for 15 seconds when an enemy Blood Blossom marked is defeated (gcsim `cons.go` lines 70 to 90, a 900-frame buff). Constellation 6 is built as her 100% CRIT Rate for 10 seconds when a drain leaves her at a quarter of her HP or under, once a minute, and as a refusal of the fall that would take her to none, which leaves her at 1 HP, as gcsim v2.47.2 (MIT) `hutao/cons.go` revives her to 1 HP (the threshold at line 41, the 600-frame buff at line 55, the 3600-frame cooldown at line 62, and `SetHPByAmount(1)` at line 50): the refusal starts the minute's cooldown, and the CRIT Rate follows the `FallRefused` event. The refusal is asked only on the paths named in the kits README, so Xiao's and Kuki's drains and drownParty's leave her at none without it. The Paramita's strike reaches and its charged attack's frames are as gcsim gives them (`attack.go` and `charge.go`), and the plunges' frames are provisional, and their poise is gcsim's 150 high and 100 low (`plunge.go` lines 38 and 43).

## Open

- **The stance's ATK rise is capped in gcsim, and the kit has no cap.** gcsim takes the share of Max HP at most four times her base ATK (`skill.go` lines 35 to 38), while the kit reads the share alone. Whether the cap binds at her levels is not checked.
- **Constellation 1's zero stamina has no cached source.** gcsim has no stamina rule for her, and the cached Hu Tao wiki page names Crimson Bouquet without its wording, so the zero multiplier rests on the kit's own `getChargedAttackStaminaMultiplier` (`huTaoKit.ts`, line 150) until the constellation's text is cached.
