# Hu Tao

Hu Tao's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createHuTaoKitBehaviour`, avatar 10000046: a six-strike string with its seventh hit, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, Guide to Afterlife's Paramita Papilio stance with its strikes and charged attack, and Spirit Soother's hit and heal. The heal is a share of her Max HP for each enemy the hit strikes, up to five a cast, read from proud skill group 4639 at indices 2 and 3, with the larger share taken at or under the half-HP threshold of index 6; the wiki's Spirit Soother page and gcsim v2.47.2 (MIT) `hutao/burst.go` give the same two shares. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 4631, 4632 and 4639; the charged attack's stamina, the Paramita's seconds and the skill's cooldown, and the burst's cooldown and energy cost are read from the same groups. The stance is a stance on her for its seconds, which infuses her normal attacks, charged attack and plunges with Pyro and raises her ATK by her ATK share of Max HP as a buff from the same seconds. All three end as she leaves the field, as gcsim's exit deletes the Paramita's ATK, which its infusion reads. Guide to Afterlife's HP cost is built: 30% of her current HP, the share read from its skill group, paid through the party's HP as the stance starts and never taking her below 1 HP. Spirit Soother's hit is its half HP multiplier, the burst group's index 1, while her HP is at or under half of its Max HP, the threshold read at index 6, as the burst starts. Blood Blossom is built: the Paramita's charged attack puts it on each enemy it hits, and it ticks every 4 seconds for 9.5 seconds on that enemy, each tick a Pyro hit of 1U at the skill group's index 2. Her Ascension 1 passive, Flutter By, is built: when the Paramita ends, each ally but her gains 12% CRIT Rate for 8 seconds. Her Ascension 4 passive, Sanguine Rouge, is built as a 33% Pyro DMG Bonus while her HP is at or under half, as the game text and gcsim v2.47.2 give it. Constellation 1 is built: the charged attack's stamina multiplier is zero while a stance is on her, which the kit reads from its character. Constellation 2 is not built. Constellation 4 is built as each other deployed member's 12% CRIT Rate for 15 seconds when an enemy Blood Blossom marked is defeated. Constellation 6 is built as her 100% CRIT Rate for 10 seconds when a drain leaves her at a quarter of her HP or under, once a minute, and as a refusal of the fall that would take her to none, which leaves her at 1 HP, as gcsim v2.47.2 (MIT) `hutao/cons.go` revives her to 1 HP: the refusal starts the minute's cooldown, and the CRIT Rate follows the `FallRefused` event. The refusal is asked only on the paths named in the kits README, so Xiao's and Kuki's drains and drownParty's leave her at none without it. The Paramita's strike reaches and its charged attack's frames are as gcsim gives them, and the plunges' frames and the poise are provisional.
