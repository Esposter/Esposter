# Hu Tao

Hu Tao's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createHuTaoKitBehaviour`, avatar 10000046: a six-strike string with its seventh hit, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, Guide to Afterlife's Paramita Papilio stance with its strikes and charged attack, and Spirit Soother's hit. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 4631, 4632 and 4639; the charged attack's stamina, the Paramita's seconds and the skill's cooldown, and the burst's cooldown and energy cost are read from the same groups. The stance is a stance on her for its seconds, which infuses her normal attacks, charged attack and plunges with Pyro and raises her ATK by her ATK share of Max HP as a buff from the same seconds. All three end as she leaves the field, as gcsim's exit deletes the Paramita's ATK, which its infusion reads. Guide to Afterlife's HP cost is built: 30% of her current HP, the share read from its skill group, paid through the party's HP as the stance starts and never taking her below 1 HP. Spirit Soother's hit is its half HP multiplier, the burst group's index 1, while her HP is at or under half of its Max HP, the threshold read at index 6, as the burst starts. Blood Blossom is built: the Paramita's charged attack puts it on each enemy it hits, and it ticks every 4 seconds for 9.5 seconds on that enemy, each tick a Pyro hit of 1U at the skill group's index 2. Spirit Soother's heal under half HP and her Constellations 4 and 6 are not built. Her passives and other constellations are not built either. The Paramita's strike reaches and its charged attack's frames are as gcsim gives them, and the plunges' frames and the poise are provisional.
