## Purpose

Alyosha's kit as built: her four strikes, charged attack, plunges and Thunderbolt Strike's tap, with the timings and hitmarks provisional, and Hunter's Advance and the hold not built.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Skirmishing Spear's four strikes

Alyosha's four strikes SHALL land on provisional timings on the enemies within reach.

#### Scenario: Four strikes in sequence

- **WHEN** the player uses normal attack four times in a row
- **THEN** the four strikes land in order on provisional timings, the third with two hits

### Requirement: Alyosha's charged attack and plunges

Alyosha's charged attack and plunges SHALL land on the enemies within reach at provisional timings.

#### Scenario: Charged attack lands on the enemies in reach

- **WHEN** the player uses Alyosha's charged attack
- **THEN** its one hit lands on the enemies within reach at a provisional timing

#### Scenario: Plunge lands on the enemies in reach

- **WHEN** the player starts a plunge attack
- **THEN** the plunge lands on the enemies within reach at a provisional timing

### Requirement: Thunderbolt Strike's tap

Alyosha's skill tap SHALL deal its one Electro hit on the enemies within reach.

#### Scenario: Thunderbolt Strike's tap deals one hit

- **WHEN** the player taps Alyosha's skill
- **THEN** one Electro hit lands on the enemies within reach

### Requirement: Hunter's Advance not yet built

Alyosha's burst SHALL deal no hit until its field and Tugarin hits are built.

#### Scenario: Hunter's Advance deals no hit yet

- **WHEN** the player casts Alyosha's burst
- **THEN** no hit lands from the burst
