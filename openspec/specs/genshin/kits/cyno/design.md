# Cyno

Cyno's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createCynoKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000071: the Invoker's Spear with its four strikes (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Cyno_Talents.json`), charged attack, plunges and collision; Secret Rite: Chasmic Soulfarer; and Sacred Rite: Wolf's Swiftness, which sets Pactsworn Pathclearer on the team as a stance with its own five strikes, charged attack and skill, Mortuary Rite. His multipliers come from proud skill groups 7131, 7132 and 7139, read at level 1 (the game's `ProudSkillExcelConfigData` in `~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/`: group 7131 holds the charged attack's 25 stamina at index 6, group 7132 the skill's 7.5 seconds at index 3 and Mortuary Rite's 3 seconds at index 4, and group 7139 the stance's 100 Elemental Mastery at index 11, its 10 seconds at index 12, the burst's 20-second cooldown at index 13 and its 80 energy at index 14). gcsim's skill cooldowns are `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/cyno/skill.go` lines 18-19 (450 and 180 frames), and the burst's 712-frame status is `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/cyno/burst.go` line 35. The skill's cooldown is 7.5 seconds, the burst's cooldown 20 seconds and its cost 80 energy, and the charged attack's stamina 25 (the wiki's Cyno talent pages, `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Cyno_Talents.json`).

## Decisions

- **Pactsworn Pathclearer is a stance of the burst.** The burst sets a stance for its 10 seconds from the table plus the 112 frames gcsim adds to them, as gcsim's status is 712 frames (`burst.go` line 35, `NewBaseWithHitlag(burstKey, 712)`), and its normal attacks, charged attack and skill are Pactsworn's in place of the kit's. Each Mortuary Rite extends the stance by four seconds (the wiki's 4s duration bonus in `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Cyno_Talents.json`; `skill.go` line 143, `ExtendStatus(burstKey, 240)`), two times at most a burst, as gcsim extends its status. Leaving the field ends the stance and its Elemental Mastery (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/cyno/burst.go` lines 80 to 88).
- **Mortuary Rite is the stance's skill, and its cooldown is the factor of Secret Rite's.** While the stance holds, a skill press costs three seconds of cooldown rather than seven and a half, through `getSkillCooldownMultiplier` (group 7132's indexes 3 and 4, and the wiki's 7.5s and 3s Secret Rite CDs in the same wiki file). gcsim sets both cooldowns on each Mortuary Rite press (`skill.go` lines 159 to 162), so after the stance Secret Rite waits on the seven and a half seconds there. The kit holds one cooldown per character, so after the stance Secret Rite can be pressed sooner than in gcsim.
- **The burst's Elemental Mastery is a buff of 100 for the stance's seconds**, read from the table (group 7139's index 11, and `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/cyno/burst.go` line 33), and the Ascension 4 flat DMG reads it as the striker is priced.
- **Judication is a field that gives Endseer.** At Ascension 1 the burst adds a field round the body, which ticks at 328 frames and every 234 frames after, giving Endseer for 84 frames while the stance holds (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/cyno/burst.go` line 48, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/cyno/asc.go` lines 28 and 29). A Mortuary Rite cast under Endseer fires three Duststalker Bolts at its hitmark, each 1.0 times the table's talent multiplier, and raises Day of the Jackal by four stacks (`skill.go` lines 111, 116 and 127).
- **Judication's 35% is set by Mortuary Rite's press and read by its hit.** A press under Endseer sets a one-second status, as gcsim's `a1Buff` sets its 60-frame modifier (`asc.go` lines 34 and 37), and `getStrikeDamageBonus` adds 35% to the hit that press lands, that hit alone: the Duststalker Bolts and Secret Rite take none. gcsim's comment on `asc.go` names Secret Rite, but its code gates the buff on skill B, Mortuary Rite (`asc.go` lines 14 to 17 and line 41), which the kit follows.
- **Ascension 4 adds flat DMG from Elemental Mastery.** Pactsworn's normal attacks take 150% of it, and each Duststalker Bolt takes 250%, as gcsim's Ascension 4 passage reads the stat for a normal attack and for a bolt (`asc.go` line 74 gives the 1.5 times Elemental Mastery for the normal attacks, and line 84 the 2.5 times for a bolt).
- **Constellation 1's Normal Attack SPD of 20% is built from the burst, and each Judication restores its ten seconds.** It is a status for ten seconds (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/cyno/cons.go` lines 28 and 30) read by the own-kit attack-speed hook, so it lifts only Cyno's own normal attacks. A Mortuary Rite press under Endseer is the Judication trigger, as the wiki's Featherfall Judgment text gives it (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Cyno_Talents.json`), and it sets the status back to ten seconds while the status stands. gcsim's comment on `cyno/cons.go` names the same refresh, but its code does not make it.
- **Constellation 2 is an Electro DMG buff built from the strikes.** Each normal attack that strikes an enemy adds a stack, up to five, and the buff is 10% a stack for four seconds (`cons.go` lines 72 and 75). A 0.1-second internal cooldown lets one stack in a hit's step, as gcsim's does (`cons.go` lines 57-60).
- **Constellation 4 restores energy from reactions Cyno's own strikes trigger, while the stance holds.** Each such reaction gives every other member of the deployed team 3 energy (`cons.go` line 101), five times a burst. gcsim's code does not check the stance, though its comment places the effect in Pactsworn Pathclearer; the kit follows the comment (see the roadmap).
- **Constellation 6 is Day of the Jackal.** The burst and each Judication give stacks, up to eight, lasting eight seconds. While the stance holds, each normal attack that strikes an enemy spends one to fire a Duststalker Bolt, once every 0.4 seconds (`cons.go` lines 127, 136 and 155: the eight-second stacks, and the 0.4-second internal cooldown with its comment at line 136).
- **Provisional: each Duststalker Bolt is priced at 1.0 times the talent multiplier.** gcsim's code gives the bolts 1.0 (`cons.go` line 169, `Mult: 1.0`), but its comment on Judication says the bolts deal 50% of Cyno's ATK. The kit ships gcsim's code value, and the roadmap asks which one the game uses.
- **Provisional: the charged attack's stamina is the table's slot 6.** gcsim does not read that slot, so its identity is a best estimate, as Ayato's group holds the stamina there too. The roadmap does not ask about it, as the value comes from the table.

## Not built

- **Pactsworn Pathclearer's plunges are not built.** Its low, high and collision plunges are gcsim's Electro plunges with a wider reach, and a stance's actions carry no plunges, so its kit keeps the Invoker's plunges during the stance. The hook is plunges on `KitStanceActions`.
- **The stance's slide is not built.** gcsim extends the burst so a hit that lands after its end still counts (its burst-slide check). The stance ends on its seconds alone, so a late hit of the stance is cut.
- **Mortuary Rite's cooldown after the stance is approximate**, as the Decisions say. The kit has one cooldown per character, so the stance's skill cannot set a second one for Secret Rite.
