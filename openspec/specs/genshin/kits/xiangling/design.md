# Xiangling

Xiangling's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createXianglingKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000023: five strikes, the third of two hits and the fourth of four, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, Guoba Attack's Guoba, and Pyronado's three swings and its Pyronado. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 2331, 2332 and 2339, and match the wiki's Dough-Fu, Guoba Attack and Pyronado values to two decimal places; the charged attack's stamina, the skill's cooldown, the Pyronado's seconds and the burst's cooldown and cost are read from the same groups. Guoba is a summon and the Pyronado a summon that follows the body on the field, built on the kit framework's shared effects, with Crossfire's wider flame from Ascension 1, Beware, It's Super Hot!'s chili pepper from Ascension 4, and Crispy Outside, Tender Inside's Pyro RES, Oil Meets Fire's Implode, Slowbake's longer Pyronado and Condensed Pyronado's Pyro DMG Bonus from one, two, four and six constellations. Guoba's Pyro aura, its particles, Chef de Cuisine, Deepfry and Guoba Mad are not built.

## Decisions

- **Xiangling's strikes and charged attack are priced as boxes to their far corners.** gcsim spawns each strike's box, 1.2 or 1.6 metres wide and 3 or 3.3 long, on its near edge at the body, so each reaches the circle to its far corner, 3.06 to 3.4 metres. Her charged attack's lunge lands gcsim's circle of 0.8 metres on the primary target, so it reaches as far as her widest strike box. Its hit at 24 frames is gcsim's after a strike, and its 25 stamina is spent as it starts. Her plunges are gcsim's frames and reaches, counted from the plunge's action.
- **Guoba stands 1.3 metres ahead and breathes round itself.** gcsim places Guoba 1.3 metres ahead of where Xiangling faces, 13 frames into the skill, for 438 frames, and lands its four breaths at 126, 226, 326 and 426 frames from the cast; the wiki's notes give 2 seconds and then every 1.5 seconds, and the kit follows gcsim. Each breath is a fan of 60 degrees and 5 metres, 6 from Crossfire at Ascension 1, which gcsim turns to Guoba's closest enemy before it breathes. A summon cannot turn, so the fan is priced as its whole circle round Guoba, provisional until a summon faces its target. From one constellation each breath takes 15% off the Pyro RES of each enemy it strikes for 6 seconds.
- **The Pyronado follows the body on the field.** It is a summon whose body stands where the body on the field stands on each step, whoever that is, as the wiki's "The Pyronado will move with your character" gives it and gcsim's ticks round the player do. It ticks every 73 frames from 56 for the burst group's 10 seconds, nine times, or for 14 from Slowbake at four constellations, twelve times, each 1U of Pyro with no internal cooldown and the wiki's 30 poise, reaching 2.5 metres. The wiki's 0.6-second cooldown on each enemy and 1.2-second cycle are gcsim's one tick every 73 frames here, and its 7 or 8 hits on a small enemy are gcsim's nine.
- **Condensed Pyronado reaches Xiangling's own Pyronado.** From six constellations, a task runs as the Pyronado starts and gives every member of the deployed team a 15% Pyro DMG Bonus for the Pyronado's seconds, as gcsim's cons.go does. The wiki's note says the bonus does not raise the Pyronado that gave it, which snapshots her stats as it starts; a summon here is priced with the team's buffs as each hit lands, so her Pyronado reads it too.
- **Beware, It's Super Hot!'s chili pepper is a field the body on the field picks up.** From Ascension 4, Guoba leaves a field where it stood as its seconds end, for the wiki's 10 seconds, which ticks on each step the body on the field stands within a metre of it: the character on the field gains 10% of its base ATK as flat ATK for 10 seconds, from a source of its own so it stands beside Fantastic Voyage's ATK bonus, and the pepper is gone. The metre is provisional, as no source gives the pepper's reach, and gcsim leaves the pickup's delay to the user.
- **Oil Meets Fire's Implode is a summon cast by the fifth strike.** From two constellations, the fifth strike's start casts a summon where Xiangling stands, which lands 75% of her ATK as 1U of Pyro with the wiki's 8.8 poise 120 frames past the strike's hit, reaching what the strike reaches, since gcsim centres its 2-metre circle on the enemy struck, which an area does not hold. It lands on whichever enemies its reach holds then, even when the strike missed, as Razor's Soul Companion does. gcsim's one Implode on an enemy at a time is not kept, since a string's five strikes outlast its 2 seconds.

## Not built

- **Guoba's aura, its particles, Chef de Cuisine, Deepfry and Guoba Mad are not built.** Guoba puts a Pyro aura on itself for gcsim's 30 frames before each breath, which Sucrose's skill and Faruzan's Pressurized Collapse can Swirl and an Anemo absorption can take, but a summon holds no element here. Its particle on each breath is not built. Chef de Cuisine is cooking, not combat, and Deepfry and Guoba Mad raise talent levels, which the [talents](../../talents/design.md) page holds.
