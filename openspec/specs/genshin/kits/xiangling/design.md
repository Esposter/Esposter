# Xiangling

Xiangling's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createXianglingKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000023 (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/xianglingKit.ts`): five strikes, the third of two hits and the fourth of four, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, Guoba Attack's Guoba, and Pyronado's three swings and its Pyronado. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 2331, 2332 and 2339 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), and match the cached wiki's Dough-Fu, Guoba Attack and Pyronado values (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Xiangling_Talents.json`) at level 1, to the wiki's precision; the charged attack's 25 stamina (group 2331, index 6), the skill's 12-second cooldown (group 2332, index 1), the Pyronado's 10 seconds (group 2339, index 4), and the burst's 20-second cooldown and 80 energy (group 2339, indexes 5 and 6) are read from the same groups. Guoba is a summon and the Pyronado a summon that follows the body on the field, built on the kit framework's shared effects, with Crossfire's wider flame from Ascension 1, Beware, It's Super Hot!'s chili pepper from Ascension 4, and Crispy Outside, Tender Inside's Pyro RES, Oil Meets Fire's Implode, Slowbake's longer Pyronado and Condensed Pyronado's Pyro DMG Bonus from one, two, four and six constellations. Guoba's Pyro aura, its particles, Chef de Cuisine, Deepfry and Guoba Mad are not built.

## Decisions

- **Xiangling's strikes and charged attack are priced as boxes to their far corners.** gcsim spawns each strike's box, 1.2 or 1.6 metres wide and 3 or 3.3 long, on its near edge at the body (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/attack.go`, lines 18-24), so each reaches the circle to its far corner, 3.06 to 3.4 metres. Her charged attack's lunge lands gcsim's circle of 0.8 metres on the primary target (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/charge.go`, line 47), so it reaches as far as her widest strike box. Its hit at 24 frames is gcsim's after a strike (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/charge.go`, line 14), and its 25 stamina is spent as it starts. Her plunges are gcsim's frames and reaches, counted from the plunge's action. (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/xianglingKit.ts`)
- **Guoba stands 1.3 metres ahead and breathes round itself.** gcsim places Guoba 1.3 metres ahead of where Xiangling faces (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/guoba.go`, lines 30-37), 13 frames into the skill (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/skill.go`, line 59), for 438 frames, and lands its four breaths two seconds from the cast and then every 1.5 seconds, at 120, 210, 300 and 390 frames, as the wiki's Guoba Attack notes give them (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Xiangling_Talents.json`, "After 2s from skill activation, Guoba will breathe fire at opponents every 1.5s, 4 times"). gcsim breathes every 100 frames from 126 (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/guoba.go`, lines 47-53), and the kit does not follow it. Each breath is a fan of 60 degrees and 5 metres (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/guoba.go`, line 71; `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/xiangling.go`, line 24), 6 from Crossfire at Ascension 1 (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/asc.go`, lines 14-18), which gcsim turns to Guoba's closest enemy before it breathes. A summon cannot turn, so the fan is priced as its whole circle round Guoba, provisional until a summon faces its target. From one constellation each breath takes 15% off the Pyro RES of each enemy it strikes for 6 seconds (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/cons.go`, lines 14-25). (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/xianglingKit.ts`, and the breaths' frames in `packages/genshin-world/src/data/kits/xiangling.json`)
- **The Pyronado follows the body on the field.** It is a summon whose body stands where the body on the field stands on each step, whoever that is, as the wiki's "The Pyronado will move with your character" gives it (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Xiangling_Talents.json`), and gcsim's ticks round the player do. It ticks every 73 frames from 56 (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/burst.go`, lines 50 and 72) for the burst group's 10 seconds, nine times (gcsim's `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/burst.go` sets `maxDuration` to 600 at line 66), or for 14 from Slowbake at four constellations, twelve times (its `if c.Base.Cons >= 4` sets `maxDuration` to 840 at lines 67-68), each 1U of Pyro with no internal cooldown and the wiki's 30 poise (the Pyronado DMG row of the cached category), reaching 2.5 metres (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/burst.go`, line 78). The Pyronado wiki page's notes (in `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Xiangling_Talents.json`) give a 1.2-second cycle with the character still and a 0.6-second cooldown on each enemy, which are gcsim's one tick every 73 frames here, and its 7 or 8 hits on a small enemy are gcsim's nine. (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/xianglingKit.ts`)
- **Condensed Pyronado reaches Xiangling's own Pyronado.** From six constellations, a task runs as the Pyronado starts and gives every member of the deployed team a 15% Pyro DMG Bonus for the Pyronado's seconds, as gcsim's cons.go does (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/burst.go`, lines 84-85; `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/cons.go`, lines 66-80). The Condensed Pyronado wiki note (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Xiangling_Talents.json`) says the bonus does not raise the Pyronado that gave it, which snapshots her stats as it starts; a summon here is priced with the team's buffs as each hit lands, so her Pyronado reads it too. (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/xianglingKit.ts`)
- **Beware, It's Super Hot!'s chili pepper is a field the body on the field picks up.** From Ascension 4, Guoba leaves a field where it stood as its seconds end, for 10 seconds, which ticks on each step the body on the field stands within a metre of it: the character on the field gains 10% of its base ATK as flat ATK for 10 seconds (gcsim's `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/asc.go`, lines 22-40, an ATK percentage of 0.10 for 10 seconds, which the Beware, It's Super Hot! wiki page (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Xiangling_Talents.json`) gives as ATK up 10% for 10s), from a source of its own so it stands beside Fantastic Voyage's ATK bonus, and the pepper is gone. The metre is provisional, as no source gives the pepper's reach, and gcsim leaves the pickup's delay to the user (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/skill.go`, lines 39-45). (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/xianglingKit.ts`)
- **Oil Meets Fire's Implode is a summon cast by the fifth strike.** From two constellations, the fifth strike's start casts a summon where Xiangling stands, which lands 75% of her ATK as 1U of Pyro 120 frames past the strike's hit (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/attack.go`, line 51; `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/cons.go`, lines 36-62), with 8.8 poise that no cached Xiangling page gives, so it is open, reaching what the strike reaches, since gcsim centres its 2-metre circle on the enemy struck, which an area does not hold. It lands on whichever enemies its reach holds then, even when the strike missed, as Razor's Soul Companion does. gcsim's one Implode on an enemy at a time is not kept, since a string's five strikes outlast its 2 seconds. (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/xianglingKit.ts`)
- **Oil Meets Fire's Implode deals no kind of attack.** The Oil Meets Fire wiki page gives the explosion 75% of Xiangling's ATK as AoE Pyro DMG and names no kind, so the [framework](../README.md#how-a-kit-is-read) gives it no kind of attack, and its summon names no kind as it casts. (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/xianglingKit.ts`)

## Not built

- **Guoba's aura, its particles, Chef de Cuisine, Deepfry and Guoba Mad are not built.** Guoba puts a Pyro aura on itself for gcsim's 30 frames before each breath, which Sucrose's skill and Faruzan's Pressurized Collapse can Swirl and an Anemo absorption can take, but a summon holds no element here. Its particle on each breath is not built. Chef de Cuisine is cooking, not combat, and Deepfry and Guoba Mad raise talent levels, which the [talents](../../talents/design.md) page holds.
