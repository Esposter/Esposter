# Kachina

Kachina's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createKachinaKitBehaviour` (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/kachinaKit.ts`), avatar 10000100 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`): four normal attacks of one, two, one and one hits (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kachina_Talents.json`), a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, and the burst's one Geo hit. Her strikes', charged attack's and plunges' multipliers are read from proud skill group 10031 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), and match the wiki's talent pages to their own precision (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kachina_Talents.json`). Her cooldowns and the costs of her burst and charged attack come from the same tables: 20 seconds, 18 seconds, 70 energy and 25 stamina (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kachina_Talents.json`). The burst's multiplier is the wiki's, as a share of DEF (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kachina_Talents.json`).

gcsim has no folder for Kachina at v2.47.2, so no value comes from gcsim. Her record is built from the tables, the wiki and her overlay, and most of its frames and areas are provisional.

## Decisions

- **Frames come from the clips' durations, as totals only.** The clips carry no events, so no event is read as a hit or a cancel. Each action's frames are its clip's length at 60 a second: the four normal attacks are `Attack_01` to `Attack_04` (160, 186, 198 and 206 frames, from `~/Esposter/genshin-parity/extracted/timings/11290214/AnimationClip/`, each clip's `m_StopTime` at 60 a second), the charged attack "ExtraAttack_AS" (102), the low plunge "FallingAttack_Strike" (18), the high plunge "FallingAttack_Strike_As" (102), the skill "ElementalArt_Throw" (48) and the burst `ElementalBurst` (50). Which clip is which action is read from its name, not from a recording.
- **Hitmarks are Xiangling's, provisional.** The hit frames are taken from Xiangling's gcsim hitmarks (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/attack.go` line 16, the same polearm body type), not one strike to one strike: her first strike takes Xiangling's first (12), her second Xiangling's third, both hits (11 and 18), her third the first hit of Xiangling's fourth (5), and her fourth Xiangling's fifth (21). Her charged attack takes Xiangling's 24 (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/charge.go` line 14) and her plunges 45 and 46 (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/xiangling/plunge.go` lines 21 and 22, 42 and 43 plus 3). Her record tags these `NoGcsimUnit` and they are not Kachina's, so a recording of her strikes settles them.
- **Areas are Xiangling's circles, provisional.** Each strike's area is one of Xiangling's measured reaches, and the burst's and plunges' radii are Xiangling's too.
- **The burst's hit is Geo, scaled by DEF.** The wiki's Time to Get Serious! page gives her burst's Skill DMG as a share of her DEF, 380.57% at level 1 (`skilldmg_1`, `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kachina_Talents.json`), and proud skill group 10039's index 0 is 3.805672 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), which the kit reads with the scaling attribute set to Defense. Its poise is none as built: the wiki's Skill DMG poise row on the same page is 150 (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kachina_Talents.json`), but the table reader does not take that label (see the labels it takes, below), so the burst deals no poise until the roadmap settles it. Its gauge is unset: no source gives one.
- **The burst's energy and cooldown are the table's (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`, group 10039 indices 2 and 3).** Her burst is one hit at 18 frames of a 50-frame animation, and its field and its Turbo Twirly are not built.

- **Her values no source gives are `NoGcsimUnit`.** gcsim has no folder for Kachina, so her values no source gives take `NoGcsimUnit`, as the [kits README](../README.md#framework-decisions) states: her frames, areas, hit counts and scaling attribute. Her burst's multiplier is the dump's proud skill group 10039 at index 0, 3.805672 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), held in her overlay: the table reader takes only the normal attack, charged attack, plunge and cooldown labels, and a generic `Skill DMG` label reads for every burst and skill would move the wiki's poise and gauge values of other kits, through the label map the wiki reads by. Her burst's Geo element is no overlay value: the energy element rule takes the energy skill's `costElemType` through the element map, where no source gives one.

## Not built

- **The skill's Turbo Twirly is waiting on its record.** Its press summons Turbo Twirly, and its mounted and independent hits scale with DEF. The tables do not name those rows, and the wiki gives their levels without a table, so the kit's skill plays its 48-frame animation and deals no hit, and her skill's hits are not built.
- **Her Nightsoul's Blessing waits on Turbo Twirly's riding states, so it is not entered yet.** Her press enters the blessing with 60 points, which Turbo Twirly's actions spend, and it ends when they are gone. The wiki gives the spend as 0.923 a second mounted and not climbing, 1.2 climbing, 3.6 a climb jump, 10 a Perform Attack and 2 a dismount. Those states need the mounted Twirly, which the kit does not build, so entering the blessing now would hold points that never drain. gcsim v2.47.2 has no Kachina folder, so these figures are the wiki's, and the Nightsoul primitive takes them once the Twirly is built.
- **Her passives wait on their hooks.** Mountain Echoes, The Weight of Stone and Heart of Unity each read the Nightsoul state, the party's Nightsoul Bursts or DEF, which no kit reads yet.
