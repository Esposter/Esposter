# Lisa

Lisa's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createLisaKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000006: four strikes, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, Violet Arc's press and its hold, released by itself at 4 seconds, and Lightning Rose's thirty discharges. Its multipliers are read from proud skill groups 431, 432 and 439, agreeing with gcsim to six decimal places, and with the wiki's Lightning Touch, Violet Arc and Lightning Rose values to two decimal places. Lightning Rose is a summon, built as the [shared effects](../README.md).

## Decisions

- **Lightning Rose's activation deals no damage.** The wiki gives it 0U and 10 poise and no multiplier, so the activation is a poise-only hit, its poise cited from its Activation DMG row. gcsim gives it a multiplier of 0.1 that the table does not (`burst.go` lines 35 and 36), so it is left out.
- **Lightning Rose lasts the 1019 frames its discharges land in, from the cast.** gcsim queues the discharges from the cast, the first at 119 frames and one every 30 for 15 seconds (`burst.go` lines 60 to 64), so the last lands at 989 and the window closes at 119 plus 900. gcsim's status for the burst adds that same sum (`burst.go` lines 191 to 193, `Status.Add("lisaburst", 119+900)`) at the burst's hitmark, 56 frames into the cast, so it outlasts the window by 56 frames that carry no discharge.
- **Violet Arc's press cooldown is the wiki's 1 second, and its hold's is gcsim's 16 seconds.** The wiki's Violet Arc page gives the 1 second, and gcsim's hold sets 960 frames (`skill.go` line 167). The hold plays on its release, as Passion Overload's Charge Levels do, once it has been held 1.9 seconds, the wiki's time to charge in full (`Category_Lisa_Talents.json`, Violet Arc). That 1.9 is provisional, as no table gives the hold's minimum, and the roadmap's References sought list holds `lisa-violet-arc-hold.mkv` to measure it.
- **Conductive is a status that stacks on the enemy, and gcsim gives its stacks no duration.** Each press hit applies a stack, up to three (`skill.go` lines 90 to 92), and Lisa's charged attacks apply one from A1, as [gcsim's ascension file](https://github.com/genshinsim/gcsim/blob/v2.47.2/internal/characters/lisa/asc.go) gives them (lines 19 to 21); her normal attacks do not apply it, as `attack.go` sets no stack. Stacks are an `EnemyStatus` with `stacks` and `maxStacks`, which a stacking status adds to rather than restarts, and they never expire, which gcsim leaves open (its TODO, `skill.go` line 67; see Provisional constants).
- **Violet Arc's hold reads the stacks on each enemy it strikes.** A `stackedHit` sets the hold's multiplier and poise for each enemy by its Conductive stacks, 320% to 487% and poise 150 to 300 from the wiki (the level-1 hold rows: 320% with no stacks, 487.2% with three), and consumes the stacks (`skill.go` lines 177 to 191). The hold reaches 10.2 metres, gcsim's 10 metres with each enemy's 0.2 circle (`skill.go` lines 162 to 164).
- **Lightning Rose's Ascension 4 lowers the DEF of each enemy a discharge hits by 15% for 10 seconds.** The wiki's 4th Ascension Passive text and gcsim's `lisa/asc.go` (lines 26 to 38, `Value: -0.15` for 600 frames) give the same values. The reduction is a status's `defenseReduction`, which the enemy's DEF takes off where a hit reads it. gcsim applies it after the hit that carries it, and the framework applies a hit's own status before pricing that hit, so the first discharge of a cast is priced with the reduction where gcsim's is not; the later discharges match, as the status refreshes on each one.
- **Violet Arc's hold is released by itself at 4 seconds.** A kit's `elementalSkillMaximumHeldSeconds` caps the seconds a skill is held at its maximum, and the skill is released there, once: the hold level its seconds reach at that moment starts, and holding on past it starts nothing again. The wiki's Violet Arc page gives the maximum of 4 seconds (`Category_Lisa_Talents.json`).

## Provisional constants

- **Conductive stacks never expire, and gcsim does not say how long they last.** `skill.go` line 67 is a TODO asking that very question, so the no-expiry rule is the kit's own choice until a recording settles it.
- **The hold's 1.9-second charge is the wiki's, and no table gives its minimum.** It sets when the hold releases, as the Decision above says.
- **The order of a hit's own DEF status and its pricing is the framework's, not checked here.** The first discharge of a cast is priced as the Decision above describes, and only the framework's code settles that order.
