# Chevreuse

Chevreuse's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createChevreuseKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000090 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`): four normal attacks, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Chevreuse_Talents.json`), Short-Range Rapid Interdiction Fire's press and its hold, and Ring of Bursting Grenades with its eight shells (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/burst.go` lines 71 to 74). Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 9031, 9032 and 9039 (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/chevreuseKit.ts`) (the game's `~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), and at level 1 they match gcsim's tables in `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/zz_chevreuse.dm.go` (the charged attack at line 125, the press, hold and Overcharged Ball at 143, 161 and 179, Surging Blade at 233, the burst at 251) to the game's six decimals; the higher levels were not compared on this page. The cooldowns and the burst's energy cost come from the groups' labels (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/chevreuseKit.ts`), the wiki's Attribute Scaling gives `cd=15s` and `energycost=60` (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Chevreuse_Talents.json`), and gcsim sets the skill's and burst's 15-second cooldowns (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/skill.go` line 81, `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/burst.go` line 91). The charged attack's stamina is the wiki's `stamina=25` (Charged Attack Stamina Cost, `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Chevreuse_Talents.json`), which gcsim does not give. The hold releases a Pyro box of 3 by 7 metres (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/skill.go` line 134), and once an Overloaded reaction has happened, the next hold casts the Overcharged Ball instead, a Pyro circle of radius 5 on the primary target (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/skill.go` line 118) with 125 poise (the wiki's Overcharged Ball poise, `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Chevreuse_Talents.json`, and `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/skill.go` line 114), which spends the ball as it starts. Explosive Grenade's circle of radius 6 lands at 59 frames (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/burst.go` lines 57-59), and its eight shells explode from 83 to 119 frames after the cast (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/burst.go` lines 63-85: five groups of mines at delays 24 to 60 from the 59-frame hitmark), past its animation's end of 61 frames (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/burst.go` line 15), which they still land in.

## Decisions

- **The Overloaded reaction sets the ball going from any source.** gcsim's subscription (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/skill.go` lines 242-250) sets Chevreuse's overcharged flag on any Overloaded reaction on an enemy, whoever triggers it, so `onKitEvent` reads every reaction event of the team and sets a status on her that has no end (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/chevreuseKit.ts`). A hold that casts the ball takes it as a variant, and its start spends the status, so the next hold is a plain one.
- **Shells are priced round the primary target.** gcsim's shells are 60-degree arcs of radius 6 centred on the primary target's position as she casts (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/burst.go` lines 63 and 82: `burstInitialPos`, `info.NewCircle(burstInitialPos, 6, direction, 60)`), and the kit prices each as the circle of radius 6 round that target (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/chevreuseKit.ts`), which reaches 21 metres from her as the targeting area's 15 metres plus the 6. The arcs are not modelled, so the shells reach farther than the game's. The record tags each shell's 21-metre area `Unsourced`, and no source on disk gives the 15-metre targeting area, so the reach is open (see Not built).
- **A hit that lands after its action ends keeps gcsim's frame.** The burst's shells fall from 83 to 119 frames against an animation of 61 (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/burst.go` lines 15 and 74), so they are not stretched into it (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/chevreuseKit.ts`), and a hit past its action's end is left to the framework's own handling of late hitmarks.
- **The press and hold are priced at gcsim's frames.** The press's box is 2 by 6 metres, at 25 frames of a 31-frame animation (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/skill.go` lines 16, 36 and 73). The hold's box is 3 by 7 at 19 frames of a 26-frame animation (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/skill.go` lines 20, 43 and 134), measured from its release, since the framework starts a hold's action on release (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/chevreuseKit.ts`). gcsim anchors both boxes on the primary target with `NewBoxHitOnTarget(PrimaryTarget, {Y: -0.5}, w, h)`, not on her, so the half metre is from the target in the enemy's default direction (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/enemy/enemy.go` line 81) and not from behind her as this decision first read. How the kit places the boxes is not in the record's sidecar, so their placement is open (see Not built).
- **Provisional: the hold's threshold.** The seconds a press must be held to become the hold are not in any table, so it takes Zhongli's half second, as the question in the roadmap's Manual verification list asks.
- **Vertical Force Coordination is built as the Overcharged Ball's hold start.** From Ascension 4 the ball's firing gives each Pyro and Electro member of the deployed team 1% ATK for every 1,000 of Chevreuse's Max HP, up to 40%, for 30 seconds, as gcsim does (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/asc.go` lines 63-76: the ATK share at line 70, the 30 seconds at line 76, its members taken from every character in the party), with no range check on the members, as gcsim has none (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/chevreuseKit.ts`).

- **Constellation 4's waiver cuts the hold's cooldown to none.** Ring of Bursting Grenades sets a status of two shots and six seconds, as gcsim's `c4StatusKey` does (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/cons.go` lines 113-122). A hold that the status lets through spends one shot, and its start cuts the party member's skill cooldown by all it runs. A hold starts only once the skill is ready, so that cooldown is none before the hold's start, and the cut leaves none whatever multiplier the start applied. The overcharged hold is waived too, as gcsim's check sits after both shapes of the hold (the ball's circle at `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/skill.go` line 118 and the plain box at line 134, with the shot count at lines 138-141).

## Not built

- **Her skill's heal is waiting on a timed party heal.** Short-Range Rapid Interdiction Fire heals the active character every 2 seconds for 12 seconds from her Max HP and a flat amount (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/skill.go` lines 24, 197 and 205), which a kit has no hook for over time. It waits on that hook.
- **Surging Blade is waiting on a conditional follow-up hit.** Each press or hold that hits anything sets off a circle of radius 2 round the target after a delay, once per 10 seconds (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/skill.go` lines 173 and 189), and a hit that fires only when an earlier one landed, under the character's own cooldown, has no hook yet.
- **Her first ascension passive is waiting on the party-composition hook.** Coordinated Tactics applies only when the deployed team is all Pyro and Electro with at least one of each (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/asc.go` lines 13-33), which the kit cannot read as party composition yet, and it adds the Overloaded reaction's Pyro and Electro RES cut of 40% for 6 seconds to the enemies it hits (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/asc.go` lines 16-17 and 48-56). Her first constellation's energy on an Overloaded reaction by a member with that status waits on the same hook (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/cons.go` line 22). Her dash's stamina cut is not built.
- **Her constellations 1, 2 and 6 are not built.** The first's energy on an Overloaded reaction waits on the party-composition hook, the second's chain explosions after a hold (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/cons.go` lines 78-106, two circles of radius 3 at 1.5 metres either side of the target), the sixth's team heal and its stacked elemental damage bonus (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/cons.go` line 156, 8 seconds; `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chevreuse/skill.go` line 211) wait on the hooks above, and the constellations that raise talent levels are the [talents](../../talents/design.md) page's.
- **The 21-metre reach of the shells (open).** The 15-metre targeting area it is built from is not in gcsim (its shells are placed on the target with no reach from her) or in the wiki cache, and the record tags the 21 metres `Unsourced`.
- **The boxes' placement (open).** gcsim anchors the press and hold boxes on the primary target (see Decisions), and the record's sidecar does not say which anchor the kit prices, so the placement waits on the kit's reading being checked against a recording.
