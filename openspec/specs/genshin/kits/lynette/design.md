# Lynette

Lynette's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createLynetteKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000083 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`): four strikes (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lynette/attack.go` line 16), a charged attack of two slashes (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lynette/charge.go` line 16), a collision and two plunges (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lynette/plunge.go` lines 59, 109 and 158), Enigmatic Feint's press (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lynette/skill.go` line 63), and Magic Trick: Astonishing Shift's initial hit (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lynette/bogglecatbox.go` lines 41 to 52) with the Bogglecat Box beside it. Her strikes and plunges are physical, as her gcsim folder deals them (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lynette/attack.go` line 53, `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lynette/charge.go` line 38, `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lynette/plunge.go` line 87), and her skill and burst are Anemo (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Lynette_Talents.json`, for the skill; `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lynette/bogglecatbox.go` line 48, for the burst). Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 8331, 8332 and 8339 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`); the charged attack's stamina, the skill's cooldown, and the burst's cooldown and energy cost are read from the same groups (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/lynetteKit.ts`). The Bogglecat Box ticks eleven times, first 122 frames after it is placed and then every 59 frames, each tick reaching every enemy within six metres of the box, which stands 1.8 metres ahead of the body where the burst starts (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lynette/bogglecatbox.go` lines 28, 67 and 68; `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lynette/burst.go` lines 16 to 18).

Not built yet: Enigmatic Feint's hold, its Shadowsign and release strike, which need a hold whose hit lands at the held time; its aligned attack, the Surging Blade, which lands only off a ten-second cooldown; the Bogglecat Box's vivid shots, which absorb an enemy's Cryo, Pyro, Hydro or Electro and need an absorb check that a kit's field cannot make; the heal and HP drain on Enigmatic Feint, which read her own HP; her Ascension 4 and Constellation passives.

## Decisions

- **The Bogglecat Box's ticks are gcsim's, measured from its placing, and its poise is the wiki's 40.** gcsim gives the box 754 frames (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lynette/burst.go` line 13), its first tick at 136 frames from the cast and its interval of 59 (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lynette/burst.go` lines 17 and 18), and the box is placed 14 frames into the cast (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lynette/burst.go` line 16). The first tick is therefore 122 frames after the placing, and the ticks run every 59 frames from there to the box's end (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lynette/bogglecatbox.go` lines 67 and 68), eleven in all. The record marks the 122 and the 59 `Unsourced` (`frames.bogglecatFirstTick`, `frames.bogglecatTickInterval`), which is out of date; see Provisional constants. The tick's zero hitmark has no literal, as gcsim queues each tick at its own time (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lynette/bogglecatbox.go` line 68). Its multiplier is gcsim's, joined to proud skill group 8339 at index 1 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`, 0.512, as `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lynette/zz_lynette.dm.go` line 272 gives it), and its poise is the wiki's 40 (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Lynette_Talents.json`, Magic Trick).
- **Constellation 4's extra charge is a skill-charge bonus, and it applies while Enigmatic Feint's press and hold are not built.** It is a cooldown property of the skill, as Lan Yan's Constellation 6 is, so it applies through `getSkillChargeBonus` (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/lynetteKit.ts`, line 69); gcsim gives the skill two charges from Constellation 4 (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lynette/cons.go` lines 23 to 26).
