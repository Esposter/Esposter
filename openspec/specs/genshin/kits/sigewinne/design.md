# Sigewinne

Sigewinne's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createSigewinneKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000095: three strikes, the Fully-Charged Aimed Shot, a collision and two plunges, Bolstering Bubblebalm's five bubbles, and Super Saturated Syringing's six waves. Her strikes, shot and plunges read proud attack group 9531 (`~/Esposter/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), and her bubbles and waves are shares of her Max HP, priced as each hit's additive base damage on its striker, as Zhongli's Max HP share is.

## Decisions

- **Her hits land 10 frames after gcsim's hitmarks, for the projectile's travel.** gcsim's attack queue lands each projectile 10 frames after its hitmark, so her strikes land at 22, 24 and 48 frames and the shot at 96, as gcsim gives them (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sigewinne/attack.go:16` and `attack.go:40` for the strikes, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sigewinne/aimed.go:16` and `aimed.go:55` for the shot). The hosted record still holds the bare hitmarks, 12, 14, 38 and 86, which the kit takes as listed until the record is republished with the travel added. That republish is owed to the PC, and the roadmap's question on the travel stays open until it is done.
- **The press casts Bolstering Bubblebalm where she stands.** Its five bubbles are circles on the primary target, each with a 16-metre reach from her (the 15-metre targeting area plus the bubble's radius of 1), at 35 frames and then every 107 frames, each a share of her Max HP. Its animation is 41 frames (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sigewinne/skill.go:51`), its bubbles at 35 frames then every 107 (`skill.go:23`, `skill.go:30`), each of radius 1 (`skill.go:31`), and its cooldown 18 seconds (`skill.go:28`).
- **Super Saturated Syringing is six waves from her.** Each wave is a box 4 metres wide and 10 long, at 99 frames (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sigewinne/burst.go` line 20) and then every 25 frames (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sigewinne/burst.go` lines 159 to 166), through its 241-frame animation (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sigewinne/burst.go` lines 24 to 27), each a share of her Max HP. Its cooldown is 18 seconds (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sigewinne/burst.go:69`) on 70 energy, the wiki's Super Saturated Syringing energy cost (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Sigewinne_Talents.json`), and each wave is gcsim's box of 4 by 10 (`burst.go:141`).
- **The charged shot is the Fully-Charged Aimed Shot, the charge's first level.** It is gcsim's default, hitting at 86 frames of its 94 (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sigewinne/aimed.go` lines 16 and 28), in a box as her first strike is, and costs no stamina, as Ganyu's does not.
- **Plunges use the shared plunge reaches, at the indices their table labels name.** gcsim names no plunge group for her, so the collision, low and high plunges read attack group indices 3, 4 and 5. The table's labels settle them at every level: `Plunge DMG` reads parameter 4, and `Low/High Plunge DMG` reads parameters 5 and 6, low first, with values 0.568, 1.136 and 1.419 at level 1. They land at the start of their 0.4-second action.
- **Her strikes, shot and plunges take their poise from the wiki.** gcsim gives them none, so her strikes take 18.354, 17.816 and 27.311, her shot 20, her collision 10, and her high and low plunges 100 and 50, as the cached Targeted Treatment page gives them (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Sigewinne_Talents.json`).
- **Constellation 2 is a Hydro RES cut and a Bubbly Shield.** From Constellation 2, each enemy a bubble or a wave strikes loses 35% Hydro RES for 8 seconds through the hit's `enemyStatus`, as gcsim v2.47.2 (MIT) `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sigewinne/cons.go` lines 34 to 38 give it, and the press and the burst each cast a Hydro Bubbly Shield of 30% of her Max HP (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sigewinne/shield.go:18`, cast at `skill.go:100` and `burst.go:72`). gcsim removes the shield when the action's animation is removed, so the kit casts it for the action's seconds, which is the same span when the action plays out and ends early only when a cancel removes it in gcsim.
- **Semi-Strict Bedrest is her Ascension 1 Hydro DMG Bonus.** From Ascension 1, each press of Rebound Hydrotherapy gives her an 8% Hydro DMG Bonus for 18 seconds, as a buff on herself, as gcsim's a1Self gives it (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sigewinne/asc.go` lines 17 and 67 to 75).

## Not built

- **The Mini-Stration Bubble, the shot's second charge level,** is gcsim's hitmark 103 of 112 frames and a second hit, waiting on a charge level that carries a follow-up hit.
- **The bubbles' hold tiers,** which are gcsim's held press at 66 and 90 frames with their damage buffs, wait on a summon whose tier its cast chooses.
- **The bubbles' healing and the final bounce's heal,** which heal her party by a Max HP share and a flat amount, wait on the publish that carries their parameters into her record. The party healing hook exists (`KitHit.healParty`, as Barbara's kit uses it), so the gap is the record's data, not a framework hook. The builder reads them from her skill's labels as `bolsteringBubblebalmHealingShare`, `bolsteringBubblebalmHealingFlat` and `finalBounceHealingShare`. The dump's tables carry the final bounce at 0.5 at every level, the constant gcsim fixes. Each bounce's heal to the other members is `healKitOtherMembers`, already in place.
- **Surging Blade,** the second damage gcsim gives the press, waits on a summon's second timed hit.
- **Her Ascension 1 Convalescence and Ascension 4 healing bonus, her Constellations 1, 4 and 6, and the droplets her press and burst spawn,** are not built. Constellation 1 needs the bubbles' tier shrink, which waits on the hold tiers; Constellation 4 extends the burst past its hold, which the kit does not build; Constellation 6 is set off by the bubbles' heals, which wait on the record's parameters. Convalescence adds a flat base to her off-field members' Elemental Skill hits, which no kit reaches, since a character's module cannot touch another kit's hits. Ascension 4 raises the healing she performs, and a heal reads the healed member's HealingBonus rather than its healer's, so no hook raises her own heals.
