# Nahida

Nahida's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createNahidaKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000073: four strikes, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, and All Schemes to Know with its press and its full hold, which strike the enemies within their reach as Dendro and mark each enemy they strike with Seeds of Skandha. A reaction on a marked enemy sets off Tri-Karma Purification, which strikes every marked enemy. Illusory Heart's field is cast by her burst: its Pyro count gives Tri-Karma a damage bonus while the field holds her, its Electro count cuts Tri-Karma's interval, and its Hydro count extends the field. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 7331 (the charged attack), 7332 (the skill, and Tri-Karma's ATK and Elemental Mastery multipliers among its indices) and 7339 (the burst, and the Pyro, Electro and Hydro rows of Illusory Heart among its indices); the charged attack's stamina, the skill's cooldowns, and the burst's cooldown and energy cost are read from the same groups. Her hold is gcsim's full hold of 300 frames, and a shorter hold has no level of its own, so its release plays the press. Her Ascension passives (Illusory Heart's Elemental Mastery buff and the Tri-Karma bonus from Elemental Mastery beyond 200) and her constellations are not built. Bennett's timings and poise are borrowed for the plunges.

## Decisions

- **Seeds of Skandha is an enemy status, one per enemy, keyed by its id `nahida-seeds-of-skandha`.** It lasts 25 seconds and a new mark restarts it, as gcsim's status refresh does. Each enemy carries at most one of her marks, so there is no source field to key on: the id names her skill. Tri-Karma's targets are the enemies that carry the status.
- **The mark cap of eight is per cast, counted on a status of Nahida's.** gcsim resets its count at each cast of All Schemes to Know and counts every enemy the cast strikes, including one already marked, so a cast refreshes marks until it has marked eight. Here a status of hers, `nahida-seeds-of-skandha-marks`, carries the count as stacks, restarted by each cast; the press and the full hold's strikes add to it through `onStrike`, which runs for each enemy a hit strikes.
- **Tri-Karma fires from a reaction on a marked enemy, from any character on the team.** It reads `ReactionTriggered`, as gcsim subscribes to every reaction event of the enemy. `emitKitEvent` reaches the kit of every deployed character, on the field or off it, so the trigger does not wait on Nahida being active. A reaction on an enemy with no mark does nothing.
- **One kit clock gates Tri-Karma, a status of hers of 2.5 seconds cut by the Electro count.** The clock is a status, restarted at each trigger that passes the gate, so a reaction within the interval strikes nothing. gcsim recomputes the interval once a second; here it is read at each trigger, so only a trigger in the first second after the field's window opens or closes can take the other interval.
- **Tri-Karma strikes every marked enemy at once, through `queueKitPassiveHit` with each enemy as its target.** The hit is Nahida's own record hit, Dendro, at the skill's ATK multiplier, with `additiveBaseDamageBonus` set to Elemental Mastery times the skill's Elemental Mastery multiplier. That term reuses `elementalMasteryMultiplier`, which Alhaitham's kit already uses for the same flat-damage shape, moved to a shared module so the two kits do not carry twins.
- **The Pyro bonus is the Tri-Karma hit's `damageBonus`, set at each trigger.** It applies when the field's window is open and the Pyro count is at least one. gcsim applies it as an attack modifier that lasts a second from each in-field tick; the window here is the same second.
- **The first Tri-Karma strike of a trigger gives three Dendro particles, once every 7 seconds.** This follows gcsim's particle callback on the first target. The strike's 3-frame queue delay is not kept: a passive hit lands in the step it is raised, as `queueKitPassiveHit` documents.
- **Illusory Heart's field is cast by the burst at the burst's own timing.** The field has a radius of 20 metres, centred one metre ahead of the body, and lasts 15 seconds plus the Hydro count's extension. The field starts 66 frames after the cast, and its first tick comes 30 frames later, at 96 frames, then every 30 frames as gcsim ticks it. A tick the body stands inside opens a one-second window, which is when Tri-Karma takes the Pyro bonus and the Electro cut.
- **Element counts are read from the deployed team at each use, clamped to two.** gcsim counts them once at init, which matches a team that does not change in a run; the count is at most two, so a third member of an element does not raise it.
- **Plunges keep Bennett's timings and poise.** No plunge file exists in her gcsim folder, so the collision, low and high plunge in `nahidaKit.ts` use Bennett's timings (frames 36 and 38 for the low and high hits) and poise of 100 and 150, under her own multipliers. A different animation of hers would move them.
