# Rosaria

Rosaria's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createRosariaKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000045: a seven-hit string over five normal attacks, a charged attack, then a collision and two plunges, Ravaging Confession with its two hits and cooldown, and Rites of Termination with its slash, lance and field of ticks. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 4531, 4532 and 4539; the charged attack's stamina, the skill's cooldown, and the burst's seconds, cooldown and energy cost are read from the same groups. The field ticks every two seconds from the cast's body, each tick a circle that sits three metres ahead of the body, and Constellation 2 adds four seconds to it. Ravaging Confession's reach prices from the body, as Rosaria's teleport behind the target is not built, Ascension 4's Crit Rate share is built on the cast. From Ascension 1, Regina Probationum gives her 12% CRIT Rate for 5 seconds as Ravaging Confession's first hit strikes an enemy, taken as from behind, as gcsim assumes it lands so. Her Constellations 1, 4 and 6 are built as gcsim v2.47.2 (MIT) rosaria/cons.go gives them: a CRIT hit of hers on the field gives her 10% Normal ATK SPD and Normal Attack DMG for 4 seconds, the first CRIT hit of each Ravaging Confession cast returns 5 Energy, and each enemy Rites of Termination strikes loses 20% Physical RES for 10 seconds. Constellation 2 is the field's four seconds above; Constellations 3 and 5 raise talents, which the [constellations](../../constellations/design.md) page reads; her teleport is not built. The strikes' and the charged attack's poise takes the wiki's values, while the plunges' frames, and the charged attack's stamina's meaning, read by its place in the table, are provisional.
