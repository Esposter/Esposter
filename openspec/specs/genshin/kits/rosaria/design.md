# Rosaria

Rosaria's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createRosariaKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000045: a seven-hit string over five normal attacks, a charged attack, then a collision and two plunges, Ravaging Confession with its two hits and cooldown, and Rites of Termination with its slash, lance and field of ticks. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 4531, 4532 and 4539 (level 1 of group 4531 gives 0.5246 for the first strike, the wiki's 52.5%). The charged attack's stamina, group 4531's index 7 at 25, is the wiki's charged stamina, the skill's cooldown is group 4532's index 2 at 6 seconds, and the burst's cooldown and energy cost are group 4539's indices 4 and 5, at 15 seconds and 60 energy; its seconds, 8, are the game's tables (`parameters.ritesOfTerminationSeconds`) and group 4539's index 3 gives the same 8. The field ticks every two seconds from the cast's body, each tick reaching 9.5 metres round the body, which is gcsim's circle of radius 6.5 three metres ahead of it (`burst.go:75`) with the offset summed into its reach, as the record's area gives it. Constellation 2 adds four seconds to the field (`burst.go:68` to `burst.go:69`), and gcsim's 510 frames are the table's 8 seconds and a half second more (`burst.go:67`), which the kit keeps. Ravaging Confession's reach prices from the body, as Rosaria's teleport behind the target is not built, and Ascension 4's Crit Rate share is built on the cast. From Ascension 1, Regina Probationum gives her 12% CRIT Rate for 5 seconds as Ravaging Confession's first hit strikes an enemy, taken as from behind, as gcsim assumes it lands so. Her Constellations 1, 4 and 6 are built as gcsim v2.47.2 (MIT) rosaria/cons.go gives them: a CRIT hit of hers on the field gives her 10% Normal ATK SPD and Normal Attack DMG for 4 seconds, the first CRIT hit of each Ravaging Confession cast returns 5 Energy, and each enemy Rites of Termination strikes loses 20% Physical RES for 10 seconds. Constellations 3 and 5 raise talents, which the [constellations](../../constellations/design.md) page reads; her teleport is not built. The strikes', the charged attack's, the collision's and the plunges' poise takes the wiki's values, and the plunges' frames are provisional.

## Decisions

- **Ravaging Confession's first hit is the only one that raises her CRIT Rate, from behind.** gcsim queues the first hit as a box from her body (`skill.go:56`), and gives the skill its 6-second cooldown (`skill.go:93`). The Regina Probationum passive is the wiki's "When Rosaria strikes an opponent from behind", cached at `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Rosaria_Talents.json`, and gcsim's `makeA1CB` gives it on any first-hit strike with a TODO on the position (`asc.go:12`, `asc.go:14`). The kit does the same, since her teleport, which decides whether a hit lands from behind, is not built; gcsim's `a1CB` is set on hit 1 alone (`skill.go:46` to `skill.go:49`).
- **Ascension 4's share is the cast's, and each other member's.** Rites of Termination's cast gives every other member 15% of Rosaria's CRIT Rate, at most 15%, for 10 seconds (`asc.go:44` to `asc.go:49`, and the 600 frames at `asc.go:62`), and gcsim's `c.a4()` is called from the burst (`burst.go:103`), as the wiki's Shadow Samaritan gives it.
- **Constellation 1's bonus is a CRIT hit's, while she is on the field.** Her CRIT hit gives 10% Normal Attack DMG and 10% Normal ATK SPD for 4 seconds, as `makeC1CB` gives it with a 240-frame hitlag-aware modifier (`cons.go:14`, `cons.go:22` for the active check, `cons.go:25` for the CRIT check, `cons.go:35`).
- **Constellation 4's Energy is one per cast.** The first CRIT hit of each Ravaging Confession cast restores 5 Energy to her, as `makeC4CB` gives it through its `done` flag, set per cast (`cons.go:60`, `cons.go:79`, and `skill.go:52` for the cast's callback). The kit arms its status at the cast's start, lasting through the second hit, as the flag does.
- **Constellation 6's Physical RES cut is each enemy Rites of Termination strikes.** Each attack of the burst lowers each enemy it strikes by 20% of its Physical RES for 10 seconds, as `makeC6CB` gives it with a 600-frame status (`cons.go:84`, `cons.go:93` to `cons.go:96`), set on the burst's three kinds of hit (`burst.go:45`, `burst.go:80`, `burst.go:99`).

## Provisional constants

- **The plunges' frames are provisional.** `rosaria.json` carries no plunge entry, so the plunges' timings are not read from gcsim's `plunge.go`, whose hitmarks are 42 and 43 frames (`plunge.go:21`, `plunge.go:22`) and whose animations are 78 and 80 frames (`plunge.go:38`, `plunge.go:47`). Their poise is the wiki's, 100 and 150 for the low and high plunges and 25 for the collision, from the Spear of the Church page.
- **The tick's shape is open.** The kit centres each tick on the body and reaches 9.5 metres, where gcsim's tick is a circle of radius 6.5 three metres ahead of it. The record's area is gcsim's reach from the body, and no source settles which shape the game draws.
