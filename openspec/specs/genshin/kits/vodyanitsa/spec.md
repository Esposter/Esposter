## Purpose

Vodyanitsa's kit as built: her four Hydro strikes, charged attack, plunges, Rechitativ: Sonorous Dawn and Koda: Sink With Thee, with the timings and hitmarks provisional and the Song of Ages Past effect not built.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Psyshkhwe Arietta's four strikes

Vodyanitsa's four Hydro strikes SHALL land on provisional timings on the enemies within reach.

#### Scenario: Four strikes in sequence

- **WHEN** the player uses normal attack four times in a row
- **THEN** the four Hydro strikes land in order on provisional timings

### Requirement: Vodyanitsa's charged attack and plunges

Vodyanitsa's charged attack and plunges SHALL land on the enemies within reach at provisional timings.

#### Scenario: Charged attack lands on the enemies in reach

- **WHEN** the player uses Vodyanitsa's charged attack
- **THEN** its one Hydro hit lands on the enemies within reach at a provisional timing

#### Scenario: Plunge lands on the enemies in reach

- **WHEN** the player starts a plunge attack
- **THEN** the plunge lands on the enemies within reach at a provisional timing, its high impact Hydro

### Requirement: Rechitativ: Sonorous Dawn

Vodyanitsa's skill SHALL deal its one Hydro hit on the enemies within reach, scaled by her Max HP.

#### Scenario: Rechitativ: Sonorous Dawn deals one hit

- **WHEN** the player presses Vodyanitsa's skill
- **THEN** one Hydro hit lands on the enemies within reach, scaled by her Max HP

### Requirement: Koda: Sink With Thee

Vodyanitsa's burst SHALL deal its one Hydro hit on the enemies within reach.

#### Scenario: Koda: Sink With Thee deals one hit

- **WHEN** the player casts Vodyanitsa's burst
- **THEN** one Hydro hit lands on the enemies within reach
