# Yaoyao

Yaoyao's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createYaoyaoKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000077: four strikes, the third of two hits, a charged attack, then a collision and two plunges after it, her skill's cast and Moonjade Descent's initial hit. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 7731, 7732 and 7739; the charged attack's stamina, the skill's cooldown, and its cooldown and energy cost come from the same groups. The first strike is a box of 1.2 by 3 metres, the second and third fans of 270 degrees, and the fourth a circle of radius 2.2. Her skill's cast takes 52 frames and cools down for 15 seconds, and Moonjade Descent costs 80 energy with a 20-second cooldown.

Her skill spawns a Yuegui two metres ahead of her, 63 frames after the cast, which throws a White Jade Radish 29 frames after it spawns and then on its throw interval. Each radish lands 13 frames after its throw, where the throw picks its target: a random enemy within 8 metres of the Yuegui while the active character stands above 70% of her Max HP, and her own position otherwise, as gcsim's yuegui.go picks it. The radish's heal goes to the active character from her Max HP as thrown, and reaches her only while the body stands in the radish's two-metre circle as it lands, so the radish is a summon of its hit and a one-tick field of its heal. Moonjade Descent spawns its Yuegui behind her at 104, 162 and 221 frames, while the burst lasts, and each one's radishes heal the whole team from her Max HP as thrown. Each radish heal also applies Constellation 1's Dendro DMG Bonus and Ascension 4's heal chain. A radish's strike gives Constellation 2's energy while Moonjade Descent lasts, her casts give Constellation 4's Elemental Mastery, and the skill Yuegui's third and sixth radishes throw Constellation 6's Mega Radish. Her Ascension 1 passive is not built, as it reads the locomotion state the kit context does not carry. Her plunge timings are provisional.

## Decisions

- **Constellation 2 reads Moonjade Descent when a radish strikes, where gcsim reads it when the radish is thrown.** gcsim attaches the energy to a radish only when it is thrown during the burst, and the kit gives it at the strike while the burst lasts, so the two differ for a radish thrown and struck across the burst's edges.
- **Constellation 4's Elemental Mastery is one buff that each cast restarts.** It is the buff a cast's restart rule already merges by attribute and source, as gcsim's status is refreshed by the next cast, so a second cast does not stack a second buff.
- **The Mega Radish heals only while its four-metre circle reaches the body as it lands.** Its heal sits on the circle at the target, as gcsim's heal does, so it follows the radish heal's rule rather than healing from anywhere.
- **Constellation 1's stamina restore takes the party's pool, behind its own cooldown.** gcsim restores 15 stamina on each radish heal, on a five-second cooldown of Yaoyao's, which the kit keeps as a status on her, so the restore adds to the one pool the team's actions spend from through `restore`, as the kit context carries it.
- **Ascension 1 reads her locomotion from the kit context, on a chain of tasks.** gcsim reads the player's state at each 0.6-second tick of the burst, and the kit reads the locomotion state the step's input gives the context before its effects run, so a tick reads the state of its own step. Each tick queues the next while the burst lasts, so a swap, which ends the burst's seconds, ends the chain with it.
- **A swap off Yaoyao ends the Yuegui her burst spawned with the burst.** Her burst's status ends on the swap, so the kit removes the Yuegui it spawned with it, rather than letting it run out its own seconds with no radish to throw.
- **A radish's heal is priced from Yaoyao's Max HP when the radish is thrown, and reaches the active character.** The kit prices a summon's heal at its cast, as Bake-Kurage's heal is priced from its cast's Max HP in Kokomi's kit.
- **Yaoyao's fourth strike reaches 2.7 metres ahead of the body.** gcsim's code gives the strike a circle of radius 2.2 at an offset of 0.5, which the kit prices as 2.7; its config's 1.5 offset would reach 3.7.
- **Yaoyao's plunges land 0.4 seconds after they start.** her plunges take Ganyu's reaches and landing, as gcsim gives her plunges none.
