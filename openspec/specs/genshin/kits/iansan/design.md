# Iansan

Iansan's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createIansanKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000110: her three strikes and charged attack, a plunge collision with two plunges, and the hits of the skill's activation and the burst. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 11031, 11032 and 11039, matching gcsim's to four decimal places. Her skill cools for 16 seconds, her burst for 18 at 70 energy, and her charged attack costs 25 stamina.

## Decisions

- **Her strikes land at their hitmarks, with no travel.** gcsim queues each strike's damage at its hitmark, so the strikes land at 14, 7 and 22 frames in animations of 27, 31 and 55 frames. The first is a fan of 260 degrees and 1.75 metres centred 0.4 metres ahead of the body, the second a circle of 2 metres centred 0.5 metres ahead, and the third a box of 2.2 metres by 3 on its near edge 0.5 metres behind the body, with 59.896 blunt poise. They are Physical, so they carry no gauge.
- **Her second and third strikes' areas are `Unsourced`.** The second's 2.5 is its circle's 2 plus the 0.5 offset, and no line gives that sum. The third is a box, which has no constructor to cite. The published values stand. The first's fan matches gcsim's, so it carries no entry.
- **Her charged attack is a circle of 0.8 metres centred on the primary target at 21 frames, in an animation of 55.** It is Physical, with no poise, as gcsim gives it. The kit takes the normal charged attack only: the Swift Stormflight that replaces it while the Nightsoul state runs waits on that hook, below.
- **Her skill's activation hit is a circle of 0.8 metres centred on the primary target at 9 frames, in an animation of 137 frames.** It is Electro at one unit of gauge and has no poise, as gcsim gives it.
- **Her burst's hit is a circle of 5 metres round the body at 38 frames, with 180 blunt poise, in an animation of 45 frames.** It is Electro at one unit of gauge, as gcsim's 25 durability gives it.
- **Her plunges are provisional.** gcsim has no plunge file for Iansan, so they take the standard borrowed plunge ([how a kit is read](../README.md#how-a-kit-is-read)). A recording of them would settle it; the [roadmap](../../../../changes/genshin-roadmap/tasks.md) holds the question.

- **Constellation 1 counts the points her blessing drains.** From Constellation 1, each 6 points consumed restore her 15 energy and start an 18-second cooldown, as gcsim (MIT) `iansan/cons.go`'s `c1` does on each point reduction (`skill.go`'s `reduceNightsoulPoints`). The step raises each drain as a `NightsoulPointsConsumed` event, which the kit answers, and the count of 6 is read through `checkIsAmountReached`, since sixty steps of six a second sum a residue short of six.

- **The Electro Swift Stormflight plays in place of her charged attack while her blessing lasts.** gcsim (MIT) iansan/charge.go queues it as a single circle on the primary target, a 2.5-metre fan of 360 degrees offset 0.5 metres, at 25 frames in an animation of 33, with 120 blunt poise, Electro, Durability 25 and its multiplier from talent group 11031 index 4 (0.84194 at level 1, 1.6643 at level 10). The record's `chargedAttackVariant` holds it, its check is the Nightsoul Blessing, and the builder reads it as the variant's one hit from the queue call that fires it (`addGcsimChargedAttackVariantCandidates`), its frames, hitmark, element, gauge, poise and multiplier from gcsim. Its fan's area is the one value still `Unclassified`, as the reader does not parse a fan hitbox. Its ICD, hitlag halt and Nightsoul tag are not modelled.

- **Ascension 1's Precise Movement gives her 20% ATK for 15 seconds from the Stormflight's strike, at Ascension 1.** gcsim (MIT) iansan/asc.go's `makeA1CB` adds the modifier on the Stormflight's first enemy hit. Every strike of the Stormflight lands at its one hitmark, so the kit adds the status on each strike, and the status restarts rather than stacks. The status is `iansan-precise-movement`, and `getPassiveBonuses` reads it as an `AttackPercent` bonus of 0.2, the way a passive that a status gates reads its bonus. Her blessing's exit ends the status, by its points or by its seconds, as gcsim's `exitNightsoul` deletes `a1Key`.
- **Her burst's points land 40 frames after the press, and her restore runs for the burst's 12 seconds, as gcsim (MIT) iansan/burst.go and asc.go do.** The 15 points enter a 16-second blessing if she is in none, and generate onto the blessing if she is in one. The restore is a once-a-second `schedulePeriodicKitTask` that reads the `iansan-burst-restore` status the burst gives for those 12 seconds. The blessing's exit ends that status, as gcsim's `exitNightsoul` resets `burstSrc`, so a blessing she enters again does not resume the restore. From Ascension 1, while Precise Movement lasts, each tick gives a point, or 4 once after she gains or consumes points on the field; that arming holds for 2.8 seconds, and her own restore generations arm it again through the same callback, as gcsim's generate and consume callbacks do. The restore's share of the movement of the other members is not built, since no kit event carries movement.
- **Ascension 4's Warming Up heals the active character on her restore, once every 2.8 seconds, for 60% of her ATK.** A nearby party member's Nightsoul burst (`KitEventKind.NightsoulBurst`, from `triggerKitNightsoulBurst`) gives her the status for 10 seconds, as gcsim (MIT) iansan/asc.go's `a4Init` subscribes. `healWarmingUp` runs on each restore tick that gives points, as gcsim's `restorePointsTask` runs `a4Heal` when the tick's points reach 1, and it heals through `changeKitMemberHealth`, so the source's Healing Bonus applies as gcsim's `Bonus` does. Her ATK is her total with buffs and passives, Precise Movement's 20% among them, as gcsim's `TotalAtk` is. The restore's movement share does not heal, since no kit event carries it: that share is named unbuilt in the kit's header.

## Not built

- **The skill's press enters the Nightsoul Blessing; the rest waits on the hook.** The press enters the blessing with 54 points for 16 seconds, which drain six a second as gcsim's do. gcsim enters it three frames after the press, and the kit enters it at the press. Her burst's party ATK bonus is not built.
- **Constellations 2, 4 and 6 wait on the party ATK bonus, the Surging Force stacks and the Kinetic Energy Scale.** Ascension 1's +20% ATK and its points, and Ascension 4's Warming Up, are built (see the Decisions bullets above).
