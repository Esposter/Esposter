# Iansan

Iansan's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createIansanKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000110 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`): her three strikes and charged attack, a plunge collision with two plunges (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Iansan_Talents.json`), and the hits of the skill's activation and the burst. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 11031, 11032 and 11039, matching gcsim's to four decimal places (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/zz_iansan.dm.go` line 111; `~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`: 11031's index 4 is the Swift Stormflight's 84.194% at level 1, 11032's index 1 the skill's 54 points, and 11039's indices 6 and 7 the burst's 18 seconds and 70 energy). Her skill cools for 16 seconds (11032's index 2; gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/skill.go` line 64), her burst for 18 at 70 energy (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/burst.go` line 73), and her charged attack costs 25 stamina (11031's index 5; the wiki's Weighted Spike, `chargedattack&swiftstormflightstaminacost`, cached in `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Iansan_Talents.json`).

## Decisions

- **Her strikes land at their hitmarks, with no travel.** gcsim queues each strike's damage at its hitmark, so the strikes land at 14, 7 and 22 frames in animations of 27, 31 and 55 frames (gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/attack.go` lines 16 and 29 to 37). The first is a fan of 260 degrees and 1.75 metres centred 0.4 metres ahead of the body (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/config.yml` lines 37 to 42), the second a circle of 1.6 metres centred 0.5 metres ahead, reaching 2.1 metres from the body, and the third a box of 2.2 metres by 3 on its near edge 0.5 metres behind the body, with 59.896 blunt poise (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/attack.go` line 69). They are Physical, so they carry no gauge (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/attack.go` line 55).
- **Her second and third strikes' areas are gcsim's.** The second is gcsim's N2 circle, its radius 1.6 at an offset of 0.5, cited at `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/config.yml` lines 45 to 49, so 2.1. The third is gcsim's box, read as a circle of radius 2.7313 at `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/config.yml` lines 52 to 56, so it carries no entry: the box runs from 0.5 behind the body to 2.5 ahead with half-width 1.1, and its far corner at (1.1, 2.5) is 2.7313 from the body. The first's fan matches gcsim's, so it carries no entry.
- **Her charged attack is a circle of 0.8 metres centred on the primary target at 21 frames, in an animation of 55.** It is Physical, with the wiki's 120 poise, which gcsim does not give (gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/charge.go` lines 18 and 23, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/config.yml` line 64; the wiki's Weighted Spike charged-attack row, `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Iansan_Talents.json`). The kit takes the normal charged attack only: the Swift Stormflight that replaces it while the Nightsoul state runs waits on that hook, below.
- **Her skill's activation hit is a circle of 0.8 metres centred on the primary target at 9 frames, in an animation of 137 frames.** It is Electro at one unit of gauge and has the wiki's 120 poise, which gcsim does not give (gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/skill.go` lines 13, 20 and 39, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/config.yml` lines 75 to 77; the wiki's Thunderbolt Rush Skill DMG poise, pageid 365385, `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Iansan_Talents.json`).
- **Her burst's hit is a circle of 5 metres round the body at 38 frames, with 180 blunt poise, in an animation of 45 frames.** It is Electro at one unit of gauge, as gcsim's 25 durability gives it (gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/burst.go` lines 18, 27, 42 and 47, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/config.yml` line 88).
- **Her plunges are provisional.** gcsim has no plunge file for Iansan (the package has none), so they take the standard borrowed plunge ([how a kit is read](../README.md#how-a-kit-is-read)). A recording of them would settle it; the [roadmap](../../../../changes/genshin-roadmap/tasks.md) holds the question.

- **Constellation 1 counts the points her blessing drains.** From Constellation 1, each 6 points consumed restore her 15 energy and start an 18-second cooldown, as gcsim (MIT) `iansan/cons.go`'s `c1` does on each point reduction (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/cons.go` lines 25 to 31; `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/skill.go`'s `reduceNightsoulPoints`, line 102). The step raises each drain as a `NightsoulPointsConsumed` event, which the kit answers, and the count of 6 is read through `checkIsAmountReached`, since sixty steps of six a second sum a residue short of six.

- **Constellation 2's party ATK and Precise Movement on the burst are built from Ascension 1.** gcsim (MIT) `iansan/cons.go`'s `c2OnBurst` (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/cons.go` lines 62 to 68) gives Precise Movement on the burst, and its `c2Init` gives every other member 30% ATK while the status holds (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/cons.go` line 43), gated on it. The kit gives the burst and each strike the same status, and gives each other deployed member a buff of its seconds, which the blessing's exit removes with the status, as gcsim's exit deletes the key its mod is gated on. `c2Init` returns below Ascension 1, so the party buff and the burst's status are gated on it too.
- **Constellation 4's Surging Force and overflow are built.** gcsim (MIT) `iansan/cons.go`'s `c4Init` gives two stacks (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/cons.go` line 85) when another member's burst goes off while her restore runs and she is off the field, once per burst of hers, as `c4OnBurst` resets it. Each restore tick spends a stack for 4 points and adds half of the overflow the tick before (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/cons.go` lines 98 to 107), which `generateKitNightsoulPoints` now returns as the points it could not hold. The kit keeps that overflow as a status for the next tick to read, as gcsim keeps `pointsOverflow`.
- **Constellation 6's Kinetic Energy Scale and Extreme Force are built.** The burst's restore runs 3 seconds longer, as `c6BurstBonusDur` gives it (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/cons.go` line 124), and a restore tick that overflows her maximum gives the active character 25% DMG Bonus for 3 seconds, as `c6OnOverflow` does (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/cons.go` lines 116 and 134). gcsim's attack mod prices the character's attacks, and the kit's buff is on each damage bonus attribute, as Enduring Rock's is, so it prices every hit the character's damage is read from.
- **The Electro Swift Stormflight plays in place of her charged attack while her blessing lasts.** gcsim (MIT) iansan/charge.go queues it as a single circle on the primary target, a 2.5-metre fan of 360 degrees offset 0.5 metres (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/charge.go` line 99; the radius at `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/config.yml` lines 66 to 69), at 25 frames (`swiftHitmark`, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/charge.go` line 19) in an animation of 33 (`swiftFrames`, `charge.go` line 31), with 120 blunt poise (`charge.go` line 85), Electro, Durability 25 and its multiplier from talent group 11031 index 4 (0.84194 at level 1, 1.6643 at level 10, `~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`). The record's `chargedAttackVariant` holds it, its check is the Nightsoul Blessing, and the builder reads it as the variant's one hit from the queue call that fires it (`addGcsimChargedAttackVariantCandidates`), its frames, hitmark, element, gauge, poise and multiplier from gcsim. Its fan's area is the one value still `Unclassified`, as the reader does not parse a fan hitbox. Its ICD, hitlag halt and Nightsoul tag are not modelled.

- **Ascension 1's Precise Movement gives her 20% ATK for 15 seconds from the Stormflight's strike, at Ascension 1.** gcsim (MIT) iansan/asc.go's makeA1CB adds the modifier on the Stormflight's first enemy hit (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/asc.go` the 20% at line 24, the 15-second status at line 50). Every strike of the Stormflight lands at its one hitmark, so the kit adds the status on each strike, and the status restarts rather than stacks. Its internal cooldown is 2.8 seconds (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/asc.go` line 40). The status is `iansan-precise-movement`, and `getPassiveBonuses` reads it as an `AttackPercent` bonus of 0.2, the way a passive that a status gates reads its bonus. Her blessing's exit ends the status, by its points or by its seconds, as gcsim's `exitNightsoul` deletes `a1Key`.
- **Her burst's points land 40 frames after the press, and her restore runs for the burst's 12 seconds, as gcsim (MIT) iansan/burst.go and asc.go do.** The 15 points enter a 16-second blessing if she is in none (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/skill.go` line 89 gives the blessing's 16 seconds), and generate onto the blessing if she is in one. The points land at `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/burst.go` line 65 (40 frames), and the restore is a once-a-second `schedulePeriodicKitTask` (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/iansanKit.ts` line 223) that reads the `iansan-burst-restore` status the burst gives for those 12 seconds (`burst.go` line 70). The blessing's exit ends that status, as gcsim's `exitNightsoul` resets `burstSrc`, so a blessing she enters again does not resume the restore. From Ascension 1, while Precise Movement lasts, each tick gives a point, or 4 once after she gains or consumes points on the field (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/asc.go` line 84); that arming holds for 2.8 seconds, and her own restore generations arm it again through the same callback, as gcsim's generate and consume callbacks do. The restore's share of the movement of the other members is not built, since no kit event carries movement.
- **Ascension 4's Warming Up heals the active character on her restore, once every 2.8 seconds, for 60% of her ATK.** A nearby party member's Nightsoul burst (`KitEventKind.NightsoulBurst`, from `triggerKitNightsoulBurst`) gives her the status for 10 seconds (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/asc.go` line 95), as gcsim (MIT) iansan/asc.go's `a4Init` subscribes. `healWarmingUp` runs on each restore tick that gives points, as gcsim's restorePointsTask runs `a4Heal` when the tick's points reach 1, and it heals through `changeKitMemberHealth`, so the source's Healing Bonus applies as gcsim's `Bonus` does (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/asc.go` the 60% of `TotalAtk` at line 111, the 2.8-second interval at line 115). Her ATK is her total with buffs and passives, Precise Movement's 20% among them, as gcsim's TotalAtk is. The restore's movement share does not heal, since no kit event carries it: that share is named unbuilt in the kit's header.

## Not built

- **The skill's press enters the Nightsoul Blessing; the rest waits on the hook.** The press enters the blessing with 54 points (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/iansan.go` line 43) for 16 seconds, which drain six a second as gcsim's do (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/skill.go` line 89 for the entry, line 111 for the six points a second). gcsim enters it three frames after the press (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/iansan/skill.go` lines 60 to 62), and the kit enters it at the press. Her burst's party ATK bonus is not built.
