# Clorinde

Clorinde's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createClorindeKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000098: five normal attacks, the fourth of three boxes and the fifth of one circle, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, and Last Lightfall's five strikes. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 9831 and 9839, matching gcsim's to three decimal places, and the burst's cooldown and cost and the charged attack's 20 stamina come from them. Hunter's Vigil's press plays its 33-frame animation and starts its 16-second cooldown, with no hit of its own.

## Decisions

- **Each strike is priced at its gcsim reach.** The first three strikes are circles of radius 1.7, 1.9 and 2.1 centred 1.1 to 1.3 metres ahead, priced as the offset plus the radius, as Zhongli's are. The fourth strike is three boxes 2 metres wide, 1.3 metres ahead, and the fifth a circle of radius 2.5 at 1.4 metres. Each strike takes the wiki's poise, 56.6, 54, 35.8, 24.234 and 94.2 from the first to the fifth, as gcsim gives no poise for a normal attack.
- **The charged attack is a circle of radius 4.1 round her.** gcsim strikes it at 38 frames of a 68-frame animation as a Physical hit, with 20 stamina. Its poise is the wiki's 120, which gcsim does not give.
- **Last Lightfall keeps gcsim's five frames.** Its boxes land at 97, 103, 109, 115 and 121 frames, each a gauge of 1U under the Elemental Burst internal cooldown, inside its 128-frame animation.
- **The press has no hit.** gcsim's press only starts Night Vigil, so the kit's press plays its animation and starts its cooldown, and enters Night Vigil, built in the kit's behaviour below.
- **Her plunges are provisional.** Clorinde has no plunge in gcsim, so hers share the plunge reach, deal the wiki's poise of 150 high and 100 low, and land as their action starts; the roadmap's Manual verification list has the question.
- **Night Vigil is the press's stance, built as a behaviour.** Hunter's Vigil's press enters Night Vigil for `nightVigilSeconds`, ending on a swap. Each Swift Hunt step casts its own entry of the list her Bond of Life share picks, the piercing shot below a full share and the normal shot at one, the entry at the step's index, where her body stands. The piercing shot gains `swiftHuntBondOfLifeGain` as it strikes. Impale the Night casts its full variant at a full share, its no-share variant at zero within gcsim's `0.0000001` tolerance and its low variant between, from `skill.go`'s HP debt ratio. Each summon lands where her body stands rather than where gcsim's lunge carries her, which the kit does not model.
- **Night Vigil's frames and piercing box are cited where gcsim states them.** The Swift Hunt steps' frames are `Gcsim`-sourced in the overlay, cited at `attack.go` lines 58, 62 and 66: gcsim reads them as frames.AtkSpdAdjust(skillAttackFrames[n][action.InvalidAction], atkspd) at `attack.go` line 163, and the reader does not resolve a wrapped call, so each value is the one `InitNormalCancelSlice` gives that element on its own line (18, 17 and 20). Their count of three, and each shot's three hits, are `Unsourced` lengths: gcsim queues one attack for a step, so no queue call counts them, and `skillAttackHitmarks` at `attack.go` line 26 holds three entries. Impale the Night's low and no-share lunges are single hits, read with `readHit`, and its full lunge is a list of three. Impale the Night's 43 is the literal at `skill.go` line 39 that `skillDashFrames[action.InvalidAction]` reads, so it stays cited in the overlay. The piercing shot's box overstates its reach: a 2 m wide, 14 m long box is priced as a radius-14 circle, which covers ground the box never does; the rule stands until an area holds the box.
- **Impale the Night's three hits are seeded in the kit's overlay.** Each lunge is a named hit joined to gcsim's Bond of Life variable for no, low and full Bond of Life, so the record carries them once it is published. Two of their values are seeded rather than read, since the builder reads a queue call's hitbox only where it is written inline, not through a variable, and a loop's one queued call does not fill the full lunge's three-hit list.

## Not built

- **Impale the Night's low and full variants clear her Bond of Life and heal her by it.** gcsim's `skill.go` calls `skillHeal` at line 170 (low) and line 132 (full, queued skillHealFrame = 6 frames after the press, line 24). `skillHeal` at line 182 takes `amt := c.CurrentHPDebt() * bolMult` and heals her by it with `Bonus: c.Stat(attributes.Heal)`, so her Healing Bonus applies. The multipliers are `skillLungeLowBOLHeal = 1.04` and `skillLungeFullBOLHeal = 1.1` in [zz_clorinde.dm.go](https://github.com/genshinsim/gcsim/blob/v2.47.2/internal/characters/clorinde/zz_clorinde.dm.go) lines 34 and 35. The reader does not read those constants: the builder reads them as table parameters 5 and 7 of skill group 9832 (`Impale the Night Healing`, Tables), and both table values match gcsim's at levels 1 and 10. The build heals her through `changeKitMemberHealth`, so her Bond takes the heal before her HP does, as the core's ReceiveHeal does. Approximations: the build heals at the press rather than six frames on, and the no-share variant has no heal.
- **Her Bond of Life gain and Last Lightfall's scaling are not built.** Her Bond of Life rises as she attacks and falls as she heals, and Last Lightfall's damage and her Bond of Life gain scale with it. The Bond of Life hook and the wearer's HP share are both built, so the attack and heal changes that set her Bond of Life are what is left, and until they land her burst deals its base damage only.
- **Her Surging Blade waits on a conditional follow-up hit.** Her skill's arkhe is a follow-up hit gcsim fires under a 10-second cooldown on a condition of its own, and a conditional follow-up under a cooldown has no hook yet.
- **Her passives and constellations are not built.** The Nightvigil Shade and the Glimbright Shade that Night Vigil summons, and the constellations that work through Bond of Life wait on the same hooks. Constellation 6's crit buff after Hunter's Vigil is built on the skill's start. The constellations that raise talent levels are the [talents](../../talents/design.md) page's.
