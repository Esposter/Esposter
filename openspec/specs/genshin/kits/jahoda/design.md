# Jahoda

Jahoda's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createJahodaKitBehaviour`, avatar 10000124: three strikes, a fully charged shot, a collision and two plunges, Savvy Strategy's pursuit with its flask and drains, and Hidden Aces' first hit. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 12431, 12432 and 12439; the skill's cooldown, the burst's cooldown and its energy cost are read from the same groups, and the flask's fill is 20 at every level, gcsim's `flaskFillValue` at skill.go line 24.

## Decisions

- **Savvy Strategy's cooldown starts when she presses it, and gcsim's starts when the flask drains.** gcsim sets the cooldown at the drain plus the windup's 30 frames, which can be up to 334 frames after the press; the framework starts a skill's cooldown at its press. The 15 seconds from the table are shipped from the press, as the framework gives it. Settled by the roadmap's question on the cooldown.
- **Jahoda's flask hits, robot strikes and Meowball take the wiki's poise: 80 unfilled and 120 filled for the flask, 30 for the robots' strikes, and 35 for the Meowball, and her strikes, charged shot, burst and plunges take it too.** gcsim gives her strikes, charged shot, flask hits and plunges no poise, so their wiki rows stand: 14.5, 6.7 and 17.9 for the strikes, 20 for the charged shot, 120 for the burst, 10 for the collision, and 100 and 50 for the high and low plunges. The flask's and the Meowball's poise come from Savvy Strategy's wiki row, the robots' from Hidden Aces' (see her Meowball below).
- **Her plunges take the shared reach at Bennett's timings.** gcsim has no plunge file for Jahoda, so the collision, low and high plunges borrow the shared reach and land at once, as the framework gives a kit without one. Settled by the roadmap's question on the plunges.
- **The flask's absorb reads the first element in the framework's order, not in gcsim's.** gcsim checks the element the flask has already absorbed before the others; the framework's absorb check reads its own fixed order. The two agree while an enemy holds one element's aura; they differ only when an enemy holds two.
- **Constellation 6 is read at each full drain, to every Moonsign member.** gcsim applies its buffs when the flask drains full at Ascendant Gleam, to each character with a Moonsign, Jahoda included, for 20 seconds at 5% Crit Rate and 40% Crit DMG, each restarted by the drain that gives it. The drain reads the team as the press lands, so the buff follows the team it was drained for.
- **The second press's drain is a summon, not a hit of the press.** Whether the drain is a filled or an unfilled hit depends on the gauge when the press lands, which an action's own hit list cannot choose between. The summon is cast where she stands and lands 2 frames after the press when the gauge is full, or 4 frames after it when not, gcsim's frames.
- **Hidden Aces' heals run on a whole-world schedule, not a field's.** gcsim queues each heal tick on her clock, so the heals fall wherever the body stands: nine ticks from frame 12, every 87 frames, stopping at the burst's 790. `schedulePeriodicKitTask` runs them as a whole-world field with a tick interval, cut at its end. Each heal is priced by Jahoda as she stood at the cast, a flat amount and a share of her ATK from proud skill group 12439, and the lowest member gets the second heal too while she has over 70% of her health. Ascension 1's heal multiplier is not built, and neither is Ascension 4's Elemental Mastery buff.

- **Her robots check on the periodic task and strike once they absorb.** gcsim's two robots check their circle for an aura every 46 frames, from frame 6 and 41 frames apart, until one absorbs an element, then strike the three nearest enemies from 41 frames after it, every 140 frames, until the burst's 790. `startJahodaRobots` runs each robot's checks as a whole-world periodic task that a flag stops after the absorb, and a strike as one more task from the absorb's own seconds. They are cast only at Ascendant Gleam, as gcsim reads the Moonsign level at her cast. Ascension 1's extra Electro robot and Constellation 4's energy are not built.
- **Her Moonsign Benediction carries no lunar reaction types.** Rooftop Dash raises the party's Moonsign by one level and names no lunar reaction, so her record holds none. gcsim gives her only the Moonsign flag, and the wiki's page names the same team bonus without any lunar reactions to turn the team's reactions into.

## Not yet built

- **Her Meowball.** A full flask's drain at Ascendant Gleam starts ten Meowballs, the first 129 frames after the drain and each 116 frames after the one before, as gcsim's meowballTick does (`skill.go` lines 32-33, 266-270, 301-347). Each strikes the living enemy nearest her body with the absorbed element, reaching the enemies within 19 metres of her, 4 round the enemy it strikes (`skill.go` line 335). Each strike that reaches an enemy gives her 2 energy, at most once per 3.5 seconds (`skill.go` lines 129-140). The values are read from the record, which the next publish adds: `hitFields.meowball.multiplier` (index 4 of proud skill group 12432, the Meowball DMG row, the same values as [zz_jahoda.dm.go](https://github.com/genshinsim/gcsim/blob/v2.47.2/internal/characters/jahoda/zz_jahoda.dm.go) line 182 at all 15 levels), `frames.firstMeowballFirstHitmark` (129) and `frames.meowballHitmarkInterval` (116), named as gcsim's constants are so the builder reads them from `skill.go`, `areas.meowballCircle` (reach 19) and `hitFields.meowball`, whose poise, 35, and gauge, 1U, come from the wiki's Savvy Strategy row, which the wiki reader joins through that multiplier. The nearest enemy stands in for gcsim's default target, which the kit does not read. A press of Savvy Strategy while Meowballs run ends them: gcsim's Skill clears the Meowball status and zeroes the flask gauge (`skill.go` lines 77-81), and its tick stops on a stale source or an empty status (`skill.go` lines 303-307). The press removes the Meowball field, so the ticks stop there too. Her robots' strikes carry the internal cooldown gcsim gives them, a group of 15 seconds and four hits, under the `JahodaRobotStrike` tag.
- **Ascension 1 and Constellation 2 read the team and the robots.** Ascension 1 takes the party's most common element of Pyro, Hydro, Electro and Cryo, the first in gcsim's order on a tie: Pyro scales the robots' damage by 1.3, Hydro the heals by 1.2, Electro adds a robot, and Cryo shortens the robots' intervals by 0.9. At Ascendant Gleam, Constellation 2 gives the team's second most common element its Ascension 1 effect as well, the two adding up. Constellation 4 gives her 4 energy on each absorb. The constants are gcsim's, cited in `jahodaAscension.ts`. The absorb check's 46 frames scale to 41.4 where gcsim truncates 126 / 3 to 42, a 0.6 frame difference the record cannot carry.
- **Ascension 4 and Constellation 1.** Ascension 4 gives the active character 100 Elemental Mastery for six seconds on each heal above 70% HP, restarting that buff, as gcsim's `a4()` does on each heal tick. Constellation 1 bounces Meowball and is not built yet.
