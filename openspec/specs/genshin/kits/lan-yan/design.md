# Lan Yan

Lan Yan's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createLanYanKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000108 (`{data-root}/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`): her four strikes (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/attack.go:18`), a charged attack of three hits (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/charge.go:15`), a plunge collision and two plunges (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/plunge.go:21`), and the burst's three hits (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/burst.go:15`) with Constellation 4's party Elemental Mastery (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/cons.go:49`). Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 10831, 10832 and 10839 (`{data-root}/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), matching gcsim's to four decimal places at every talent level from 1 to 15 (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/zz_lanyan.dm.go`). Her skill's cooldown is 16 seconds, her burst's 15 with a cost of 60 energy, and her charged attack takes 50 stamina (`{data-root}/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarSkillExcelConfigData.json`, and `{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Lan_Yan_Talents.json` for the stamina).

## Decisions

- **Her strikes and charged attack are gcsim's shapes (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/attack.go:18` and `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/charge.go:15`), all Anemo at one unit of gauge.** The first strike is a box of 2.2 by 3 metres offset 0.2 metres back, the second a box of 2.3 by 3, each landing its two hits from the second and third strikes, the third a circle of 2.2 metres ahead by 0.3, and the fourth a fan of 240 degrees of 2.4 metres ahead by 0.5 (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/attack.go:22`, `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/attack.go:23`, `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/attack.go:24`), at 11, 17 and 37, 15 and 21, and 40 frames (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/attack.go:18`). Her charged attack is three hits of a circle 2.5 metres centred on the primary target at 42, 49 and 56 frames (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/charge.go:45`). Her strikes take the wiki's poise, 25.535 to 72.586 a hit (`{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Lan_Yan_Talents.json`, Black Pheasant Strides on Water, poises 1 to 6), as gcsim gives none.
- **Her plunges are gcsim's own, not borrowed.** gcsim has a plunge file for Lan Yan (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/plunge.go`), so her low plunge is a circle of 3 metres at 44 frames, her high plunge one of 3.5 metres at 46, and the collision one of 1.5 metres at 38 frames (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/plunge.go:161`), each in a 67-frame animation (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/plunge.go:32`). Her high plunge's 3.5 metres differs from Bennett's 5 in gcsim's `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/bennett/plunge.go:33`, so it is written here rather than borrowed; her low plunge's 3 metres matches his.
- **Her burst's three hits are circles of radius 6 on the primary target, at 30, 46 and 51 frames (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/burst.go:15`, `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/burst.go:45`).** Each is priced as the targeting area's radius plus its own (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/checkIsInAttackArea.ts`), and deals two units of Anemo gauge (the wiki's 2U in `{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Lan_Yan_Talents.json`), from gcsim's 50 durability (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/burst.go:35`), in a 100-frame animation (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/burst.go:19`).
- **Constellation 4 adds 60 Elemental Mastery to each member of the deployed team for 12 seconds as the burst is cast (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/cons.go:49`, `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/cons.go:52`).** It is a buff on each member under one source, so a second burst restarts it, as gcsim's party stat mod does (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/cons.go` keys the mod `lanyan-c4`, and `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/player/character/mods.go:157` and `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/modifier/modifier.go:86` overwrite a mod of the same key).
- **Constellation 6's extra charge is a skill-charge bonus, which applies while the press is not built.** The charge is a cooldown property of the skill, not an effect of its press, so it applies from Constellation 6 through `getSkillChargeBonus`; the press's effects wait on the shield and stance hooks. The base is one charge (`maxChargeNum` 1 on skill 11082 in `{data-root}/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarSkillExcelConfigData.json`), and gcsim gives two from Constellation 6 (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/lanyan.go:37`).

- **Ascension 4 adds 774% of her Elemental Mastery to each hit of Lustrous Moonrise, as flat damage.** It is built: gcsim adds it on each burst hit (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/burst.go:39`), the wiki's Skyfeather Evil-Subduing Charm gives 774% (`{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Lan_Yan_Talents.json`), and the kit adds the same share as an additive base damage bonus on each hit (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/lanYanKit.ts`).

## Not built

- **The Swallow-Wisp Pinion Dance press deals nothing.** It plays its 49-frame animation, which gcsim gives it when nothing is detected (80 when something is, `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/skill.go`), and spends its 16-second cooldown, but its detect hit, its Feathermoon Rings and its shield are not built. The rings are a stance, which replaces her normal attacks and skill for 66 frames from the detect (gcsim `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/skill.go`, "leapBackDuration"), and are not built yet. The shield is cast with the element the detected enemy carries, which the framework cannot read yet, and that wait is the shield's element hook.
- **Ascension 1 (the shield absorbs the element) and Ascension 4's share on the press and its rings wait on the press.** gcsim's Ascension 1 is the shield's absorb (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/asc.go`). The wiki's Skyfeather Evil-Subduing Charm gives the press 309% of her Elemental Mastery, and gcsim gives the rings the same flat share (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/lanyan/attack.go`, `Ascension >= 4`). Constellation 1 (the rings' extra hits) waits on it too, and Constellation 2 (the shield restored by a normal attack) waits on the shield hook, since it restores a shield the press casts.
