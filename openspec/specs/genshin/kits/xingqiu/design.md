# Xingqiu

Xingqiu's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createXingqiuKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000025: five strikes, the third and fifth of two hits each, a charged attack of two hits, a collision and two plunges, Guhua Sword: Fatal Rainscreen's two hits with its Rain Swords, and Guhua Sword: Raincutter's sword rain. His multipliers are read from proud skill groups 2531, 2532 and 2539, and match the wiki's Guhua Style, Guhua Sword: Fatal Rainscreen and Guhua Sword: Raincutter values to two decimal places; the charged attack's stamina, the Rain Swords' seconds, the skill's cooldown, Raincutter's seconds and the burst's cooldown and cost are read from the same groups. The Rain Swords are a summon that follows the body on the field, putting Hydro on what touches it, and Raincutter a summon that the normal attacks of whoever is on the field set off, through the kit framework's coordinated summons, with Hydropathic's heal from Ascension 1 and Blades Amidst Raindrops' Hydro DMG Bonus from Ascension 4, and The Scent Remained's fourth sword, Rainbow Upon the Azure Sky's longer Raincutter and Hydro RES cut, Evilsoother's stronger Fatal Rainscreen and Hence, Call Them My Own Verses' five-sword wave and energy from one, two, four and six constellations are built. The Rain Swords' DMG reduction, interruption resistance and shattering, the skill's Wet on Xingqiu and its particles, the burst's activation knockback, and Raincutter's end as Xingqiu falls are not built.

## Decisions

- **Xingqiu's areas are priced with their offsets, and his box's far corner.** gcsim centres his first and second strikes' circles of 1.5 metres 0.8 metres ahead, his third's 0.6 ahead and his fifth's second circle of 2 metres 0.8 ahead, so each reach is its offset plus its radius, 2.3, 2.1 and 2.8 metres, and Fatal Rainscreen's first circle of 3 metres 0.8 ahead reaches 3.8. It spawns his fourth strike's box and his fifth's first, 1 wide and 2 long, on their near edge at the body, so each is priced as the circle to its far corner, 2.06 metres, and Fatal Rainscreen's second box, 3.5 wide and 4.5 long from 1.5 metres behind, as the circle to its far corner 3 metres ahead, 3.47 metres. His charged attack's two slashes are gcsim's circles of 2.2 metres round his body at 8 and 20 frames after a strike, which every charged attack here follows, and its 20 stamina is spent as it starts.
- **Fatal Rainscreen's hits land from a summon.** The skill's action has no hits of its own: its start casts a summon where Xingqiu stands, which lands the two hits at gcsim's 12 and 31 frames, each 1U of Hydro with no internal cooldown and the wiki's 120 poise, so the start can choose their multipliers. From four constellations, Evilsoother multiplies both by 1.5 while his Raincutter stands as the skill starts, the Base DMG Multiplier the wiki's constellation page names and gcsim's skill.go applies, which a DMG Bonus buff would not equal. gcsim queues both hits as the skill starts, so they land whatever cancels it, as the summon's do.
- **The Rain Swords are a summon that follows the body on the field.** They put 1U of Hydro, with no damage, internal cooldown or poise, on what stands within 1.2 metres of whoever is on the field every 135 frames, the wiki's 2.25 seconds, from 44 frames past the skill or 19 past the burst, as gcsim's orbital.go does, for the skill group's 15 seconds or for Raincutter's. A recast stands them longer only when its seconds outlast what they have left, and their ticks keep the schedule they started on: the wiki's notes give a recast that prolongs them and a burst that holds them to its end, where gcsim's status would cut them back to a recast's 15 seconds inside an 18-second Raincutter, and the kit follows the wiki.
- **Hydropathic heals as the Rain Swords end.** From Ascension 1, a task runs as the Rain Swords' seconds end and heals the character on the field by 6% of Xingqiu's Max HP for each sword, 3, or 4 from The Scent Remained, read as he cast them. No strike shatters a sword here, so every sword heals at the end: in the game each heals once, as it shatters or as its seconds end, so the heal is the same and only its timing differs. A recast that stands them longer adds a task at the new end, and the old one heals nothing, as the wiki's note gives a prolonged sword no heal, since it neither shattered nor expired.
- **Raincutter is a coordinated summon.** It stands for the burst group's 15 seconds, 18 from Rainbow Upon the Azure Sky, and gcsim's 33 frames more, with no hits of its own, and the burst stands the Rain Swords for those 15 or 18 seconds. As whoever is on the field starts a normal attack, it casts a wave of swords where that character stands, landing 20 frames on, unless a wave was cast within the second, the wiki's cooldown. The waves run 2, 3, 2, 3 swords, or 2, 3, 5 from Hence, Call Them My Own Verses, each wave counting those standing since Raincutter began, as each stands until it ends. gcsim's watcher also casts a wave every second the normal attack state goes on, and delays its check 7 frames on Xingqiu's own strikes; neither is kept, as a kit sees an action's start and not that its string is still playing, so a long string casts its next wave on the first strike starting a second after the last. The charged attack and the plunges cast none, as gcsim watches the normal attack state alone, and Raincutter does not end as Xingqiu falls, as no effect reads a downed member.
- **Rainbow Upon the Azure Sky and Hence, Call Them My Own Verses act on the swords.** From two constellations each sword takes 15% off the Hydro RES of each enemy it strikes for 4 seconds, and from six a wave of five swords gives Xingqiu 3 energy up to his burst's cost through a one-tick field as its swords land, struck or not, as Razor's Electro Sigils are, since no hit writes the team's effects as it strikes.

## Provisional constants

- **Xingqiu's plunges are provisional.** His plunges take the index's no-file rule. The plunges' poise and blunt are the wiki's Guhua Style.
- **Each sword reaches 5 metres round where its wave was cast, provisionally.** gcsim drops each sword as a circle of 0.5 metres on the primary target, which an area does not hold, so a sword reaches the 5 metres an attack targets within, as Ayaka's charged attack does, and strikes each enemy there rather than the one the wiki's notes aim it at. Each is 1U of Hydro under the Elemental Burst internal cooldown at the wiki's 25 poise, priced as it lands rather than as the burst snapshots it, as the wiki's notes give it.

## Not built

- **The Rain Swords' DMG reduction, interruption resistance and shattering are not built.** An enemy's strike on the character on the field reaches the team's shields and its HP and reads no DMG reduction, so the skill group's 20% and its share of Xingqiu's Hydro DMG Bonus have nowhere to go, and nothing tells the kit a strike landed to shatter a sword. The interruption resistance has nothing to act on, and the skill's Wet on Xingqiu has no place to sit.
- **Xingqiu's particles, his other constellations, his utility passive and the burst's knockback are not built.** The skill's 5 particles are not built. Weaver of Verses and Embrace of Rain raise talent levels, which the [talents](../../talents/design.md) page holds, and Flash of Genius is crafting, not combat. Raincutter's activation knockback, 400 poise with no damage, is not built, as gcsim does not implement it and no source gives its frame.
