## Purpose

Lohen's kit as built: his five strikes, charged attack, plunges and six-hit burst, with his skill not built and his timings provisional.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Lohen's strikes, charged attack and plunges

Lohen's five strikes, charged attack and plunges SHALL land on provisional timings, hitting the enemies within reach.

#### Scenario: Five strikes in sequence

- **WHEN** the player uses normal attack five times in a row
- **THEN** the five strikes land in order on the enemies within reach

#### Scenario: Plunge lands on the enemies in reach

- **WHEN** the player starts a plunge attack
- **THEN** the plunge lands on its provisional timing on the enemies within reach

### Requirement: Lohen's six-hit burst

Lohen's elemental burst SHALL land six hits on the enemies within reach.

#### Scenario: Burst lands six hits

- **WHEN** the player casts Lohen's elemental burst
- **THEN** six hits land on the enemies within reach

### Requirement: Lohen's skill deals no hit yet

Lohen's elemental skill SHALL play its animation and deal no hit until its hits are built.

#### Scenario: Skill press deals no damage

- **WHEN** the player presses Lohen's elemental skill
- **THEN** its animation plays
- **AND** no damage is dealt
