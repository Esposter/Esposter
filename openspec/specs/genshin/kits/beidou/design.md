# Beidou

Beidou's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createBeidouKitBehaviour`, avatar 10000024: five strikes, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, Tidecaller's press with its shield, and Stormbreaker's hit with its Thunderbeast's Targe. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 2431, 2432 and 2439, and match the wiki's Oceanborne, Tidecaller and Stormbreaker values to two decimal places (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json` against `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Beidou_Talents.json`); the charged attack's stamina, the shield's share of her Max HP and its flat HP (group 2432's 0.144 and 1386.3678), the skill's cooldown, the Targe's seconds (group 2439's 15) and the burst's cooldown and cost are read from the same groups; group 2439 also holds the discharge's 0.96 at index 1 (the wiki's 96.0% Lightning DMG, in the same wiki file). The Targe is a summon that follows the body on the field, built atop the shared effects, with its lightning discharge beside Beidou's strikes and charged attack at most once a second (the wiki's Stormbreaker note, `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Beidou_Talents.json`), and Bane of Evil's Electro RES cut from six constellations (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/beidou/burst.go` lines 90 to 98). Tidecaller's hold and its bonus on hits taken, Retribution, Lightning Storm, the Targe's DMG reduction and its discharge from the other members' attacks, Sea Beast's Scourge, Upon the Turbulent Sea, the Thunder Arises and Stunning Revenge are not built.

## Decisions

- **Beidou's areas are priced with their offsets, and her box's far corner.** gcsim centres her first strike's 270-degree fan half a metre ahead and her third's circle and fourth's 270-degree fan a metre ahead, so each reach is its offset plus its radius, 2.5 and 3 metres (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/beidou/attack.go` lines 19 to 21). It spawns her second's box on its near edge at the body and her fifth's half a metre behind, and both reach 2.5 metres ahead, so each is priced as the circle to that far corner, 2.69 metres.
- **Tidecaller plays as its press, its shield standing until its hit.** gcsim models the skill as one press: an Electro shield of the group's 14.4% of her Max HP plus 1386 is set as it starts (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/beidou/skill.go` line 75) and stands until its hit at 23 frames (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/beidou/skill.go` line 22, line 77), which lands 2U of Electro with 100 poise (the wiki's Tidecaller Base DMG row, with its advanced properties in `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Beidou_Talents.json`), blunt, reaching 6 metres (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/beidou/skill.go` line 18). The hold is not built: a skill with holds starts on its release and nothing runs while it is held, so no shield could stand through the hold, and its bonus of 160% for each of up to two hits taken counts strikes landing on Beidou, which reach the team's shields and never the kit. Retribution, which grants the greatest bonus, and Lightning Storm, which follows it, wait on the hold. Its Electro on Beidou as it activates has no place to sit, and its particles are not built.
- **The Thunderbeast's Targe is a summon that follows the body on the field.** It stands for the burst group's 15 seconds from Stormbreaker's cast, as gcsim's burst status does (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/beidou/burst.go` line 54), where the body on the field stands, whoever that is, as the wiki's notes give it (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Beidou_Talents.json`). Its DMG reduction of 20% is not built, though an enemy's strike reads a buff of `Attribute.DamageReduction` on the member on the field, nor is its interruption resistance, which has nothing to act on. The Targe should end as Beidou falls, the wiki's Stormbreaker utility `Effects End Character Fallen` (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Beidou_Talents.json`), but it does not, as no effect reads a downed member.
- **The Targe's discharge is cast by Beidou's own strikes and charged attack.** While the Targe stands at a hit's hitmark, the start of each of her strikes and her charged attack casts a summon where she stands, which lands the burst group's 96% as 1U of Electro under the Elemental Burst internal cooldown with 50 poise (the wiki's Stormbreaker Lightning DMG row, in `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Beidou_Talents.json`), a frame past the hit (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/beidou/burst.go` line 143), and stands a second past it: a hit whose hitmark falls within that second casts none, the wiki's one discharge a second. gcsim chains it from the enemy struck to a random other within 8 metres (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/beidou/burst.go` line 163), three hits or five from Upon the Turbulent Sea, the Thunder Arises (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/beidou/burst.go` lines 155 to 160), which an area can neither pick nor count, so one discharge lands on each enemy its hit's reach holds, as Razor's Soul Companion does, and that constellation is not built. The wiki says a discharge arises only when the attack hits (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Beidou_Talents.json`), so a missed hit should give none. The wiki's discharge from every member's normal and charged attacks is not built either: the wiki sets it off as their hits land, and the strike loop where another member's hits land gives no kit a hook. It is priced as it lands, not as the burst snapshots it, as Condensed Pyronado's summon is.
- **Bane of Evil's cut lands from the Targe.** From six constellations, the Targe lands a hit of no damage, poise or element every 30 frames from 30 to its end (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/beidou/burst.go` line 91), which takes 15% off the Electro RES of each enemy within 5 metres of the body on the field (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/beidou/burst.go` line 93, line 98) for 90 frames (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/beidou/burst.go` line 96), as gcsim's burst gives it. As any hit does, a tick sets an idle enemy it reaches on the character. Its Radiance, which cuts Cryo RES and raises the Elemental Mastery of the character on the field while Stormbreaker stands, waits on Stellar-Conduct, a reaction the combat does not hold.

- **Ascension 1's Retribution is not built, and its counter window is a recording owed.** The passive grants the greatest DMG Bonus to a counterattack with Tidecaller at the precise moment Beidou is hit. Its counter needs Tidecaller's hold, which the kit does not build, and no source gives the window: gcsim's `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/beidou/asc.go` line 11 to line 12 leaves Ascension 1 unimplemented under the same "precise moment" TODO, and the wiki says only "the precise moment". The window has to be measured in frames, from the hit to the press that counters it, in a recording of the game, before the bonus's hit can be timed. Until then, the passive is not built and no window is guessed.

## Provisional constants

- **Beidou's charged attack and plunges are provisional.** gcsim models neither and the wiki gives no frames, so the charged attack's slashes are priced at half a second and a second, and the charge closes at a second and a fifth, draining the table's 40 stamina a second as it plays, and its reaches are gcsim's unimplemented 3.3 and 3.5 metres. Each plunge hits from its action's start to 0.4 seconds later, reaching 1, 3 and 5 metres. Their damage is the table's; the charged attack's poise is 60 and 120, the plunges' is the wiki's 200 and 150, and the collision's is the wiki's Oceanborne 35.

## Not built

- **Beidou's Constellations 1 and 2 and her utility passive are not built.** Sea Beast's Scourge's shield would be recast over by Tidecaller's, as a shield is keyed on its caster, and telling two shields of one caster apart is a change to the shield. Stormbreaker's arc lightning jumping to two more targets needs a chain the burst's hit does not make. Summoner of Storm and Crimson Tidewalker raise talent levels, which the [talents](../../talents/design.md) page holds, and Conqueror of Tides' swimming stamina is not combat.
- **Stunning Revenge, from Constellation 4, is built for HP lost to a strike, a drown or a wildlife's charge while Beidou is on the field** (gcsim's `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/beidou/cons.go` line 24, 10 seconds, and line 56, 20% of her ATK). A hit a shield takes whole sends no HP change, so it starts no status. Each normal attack hit that lands while the 10 seconds run gets an Electro hit of 20% of her ATK, which lands where that hit reaches, where gcsim hits the enemy it struck alone (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/beidou/cons.go` line 58). The wiki says it also applies to her charged attack, which is not built: gcsim leaves it out, and the hook is handed only normal attacks.
