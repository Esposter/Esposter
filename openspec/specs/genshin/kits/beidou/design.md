# Beidou

Beidou's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createBeidouKitBehaviour`, avatar 10000024: five strikes, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, Tidecaller's press with its shield, and Stormbreaker's hit with its Thunderbeast's Targe. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 2431, 2432 and 2439, and match the wiki's Oceanborne, Tidecaller and Stormbreaker values to two decimal places; the charged attack's stamina, the shield's share of her Max HP and its flat HP, the skill's cooldown, the Targe's seconds and the burst's cooldown and cost are read from the same groups. The Targe is a summon that follows the body on the field, built atop the shared effects, with its lightning discharge beside Beidou's strikes and charged attack at most once a second, and Bane of Evil's Electro RES cut from six constellations. Tidecaller's hold and its bonus on hits taken, Retribution, Lightning Storm, the Targe's DMG reduction and its discharge from the other members' attacks, Sea Beast's Scourge, Upon the Turbulent Sea, the Thunder Arises and Stunning Revenge are not built.

## Decisions

- **Beidou's areas are priced with their offsets, and her box's far corner.** gcsim centres her first strike's 270-degree fan half a metre ahead and her third's circle and fourth's 270-degree fan a metre ahead, so each reach is its offset plus its radius, 2.5 and 3 metres. It spawns her second's box on its near edge at the body and her fifth's half a metre behind, and both reach 2.5 metres ahead, so each is priced as the circle to that far corner, 2.69 metres.
- **Tidecaller plays as its press, its shield standing until its hit.** gcsim models the skill as one press: an Electro shield of the group's 14.4% of her Max HP plus 1386 is set as it starts and stands until its hit at 23 frames, which lands 2U of Electro with the wiki's 100 poise, blunt, reaching 6 metres. The hold is not built: a skill with holds starts on its release and nothing runs while it is held, so no shield could stand through the hold, and its bonus of 160% for each of up to two hits taken counts strikes landing on Beidou, which reach the team's shields and never the kit. Retribution, which grants the greatest bonus, and Lightning Storm, which follows it, wait on the hold. Its Electro on Beidou as it activates has no place to sit, and its particles are not built.
- **The Thunderbeast's Targe is a summon that follows the body on the field.** It stands for the burst group's 15 seconds from Stormbreaker's cast, as gcsim's burst status does, where the body on the field stands, whoever that is, as the wiki's notes give it. Its DMG reduction of 20% is not built, as an enemy's strike reads no DMG reduction, nor is its interruption resistance, which has nothing to act on. The Targe does not end as Beidou falls, as no effect reads a downed member.
- **The Targe's discharge is cast by Beidou's own strikes and charged attack.** While the Targe stands at a hit's hitmark, the start of each of her strikes and her charged attack casts a summon where she stands, which lands the burst group's 96% as 1U of Electro under the Elemental Burst internal cooldown with the wiki's 50 poise, a frame past the hit, and stands a second past it: a hit whose hitmark falls within that second casts none, the wiki's one discharge a second. gcsim chains it from the enemy struck to a random other within 8 metres, three hits or five from Upon the Turbulent Sea, the Thunder Arises, which an area can neither pick nor count, so one discharge lands on each enemy its hit's reach holds, even when the hit misses, as Razor's Soul Companion does, and that constellation is not built. The wiki's discharge from every member's normal and charged attacks is not built either: the wiki sets it off as their hits land, and the strike loop where another member's hits land gives no kit a hook. It is priced as it lands, not as the burst snapshots it, as Condensed Pyronado's summon is.
- **Bane of Evil's cut lands from the Targe.** From six constellations, the Targe lands a hit of no damage, poise or element every 30 frames from 30 to its end, which takes 15% off the Electro RES of each enemy within 5 metres of the body on the field for 90 frames, as gcsim's burst gives it. As any hit does, a tick sets an idle enemy it reaches on the character. Its Radiance, which cuts Cryo RES and raises the Elemental Mastery of the character on the field while Stormbreaker stands, waits on Stellar-Conduct, a reaction the combat does not hold.

## Provisional constants

- **Beidou's charged attack and plunges are provisional.** gcsim models neither and the wiki gives no frames, so the charged attack's slashes are priced at half a second and a second, and the charge closes at a second and a fifth, draining the table's 40 stamina a second as it plays, and its reaches are gcsim's unimplemented 3.3 and 3.5 metres. Each plunge hits from its action's start to 0.4 seconds later, reaching 1, 3 and 5 metres. Their damage is the table's and their poise the wiki's Oceanborne.

## Not built

- **Beidou's Constellations 1, 2 and 4 and her utility passive are not built.** Sea Beast's Scourge's shield would be recast over by Tidecaller's, as a shield is keyed on its caster, and telling two shields of one caster apart is a change to the shield. Stunning Revenge follows a strike landing on Beidou, which never reaches the kit. Summoner of Storm and Crimson Tidewalker raise talent levels, which the [talents](../../../../changes/genshin-talents/design.md) page holds, and Conqueror of Tides' swimming stamina is not combat.
