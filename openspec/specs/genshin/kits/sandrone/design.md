# Sandrone

Sandrone's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createSandroneKitBehaviour`, avatar 10000133 (`{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`). gcsim has no folder for her at v2.47.2, so her record follows [the kits index](../README.md), and her multipliers, energy and cooldowns come from the game's own tables (proud skill groups 13331, 13332 and 13339, `{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), with the wiki's Sandrone talent pages cached at `{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Sandrone_Talents.json`.

## Decisions

- **Her kit is named Sandrone, the dump's English name.** The kit's files take the kebab-cased English name, which the dump gives as Sandrone: avatar 10000133's name hash, 108893506, resolves to Sandrone in `{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/TextMap/TextMap_MediumEN.json`. The avatar's icon and model keep the name Marionette, as its icon `UI_AvatarIcon_MarionetteNew` in `{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json` shows.
- **Sandrone borrows Kinich's strikes, Aino's charged attack and skill, Chongyun's first three burst hits and Diluc's fourth, provisional.** Kinich's three claymore strikes take her three strikes, one hit each, as gcsim's Kinich queues one attack per strike (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/attack.go:102`). Her charged attack takes Aino's single hit, and her Prism Shot takes Aino's two hits, the skill's loop over two entries (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/aino/skill.go:46` to `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/aino/skill.go:52`). Her burst's Bombardment takes Chongyun's three hits at 50, 59 and 67 frames (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chongyun/burst.go:14`, `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chongyun/burst.go:43` to `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/chongyun/burst.go:48`), and its Convective Inhibition Ray takes Diluc's single hit (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/diluc/burst.go:107`, the burst at `burstHitmark` 100, `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/diluc/burst.go:16`). Her plunges and collision borrow Bennett's (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/bennett/plunge.go`).
- **Her charged attack reads its "Charged Attack Sweeping Fire DMG" label and drains no stamina, provisional.** Her table names no "Charged Attack DMG" and no stamina cost, so her overlay names the Sweeping Fire label and a stamina of 0, as Lauma's does. The label's per-level values are in the wiki's Formule Phenomenale: Self-Evident Proposition page, and group 13331's index 3 gives 0.43 at level 1, the wiki's 43.
- **Her plunges and collision borrow Bennett's hitmarks and areas, each a `BorrowedFromDonor` entry naming his path.** Each plunge's hit count is a `NoActionFile` entry, as she has no plunge file to count them, and her plunges' animation frames are read from her own clips, which is open: see [Provisional constants](#provisional-constants).
- **Her frames follow [the kits index](../README.md)'s rule for a character the tag predates.** Her strikes read `Attack_01` to `Attack_03`, her charged attack ExtraAttack_Start and ExtraAttack_Loop together, her skill ElementalArt_BS, her burst `ElementalBurst`, and her plunges FallingAttack_Strike and FallingAttack_Strike_AS. The record's frames, 145, 228 and 296 for the strikes, 128 for the charged attack, 66 for the skill and 92 for the burst, are those clips' lengths, which are open for want of a cached timings run.
- **Her strikes are Physical, and her skill and burst are Ice, the engine's name for Cryo.** The wiki names no element for her strikes. Her skill's Prism Shot and her burst deal Cryo DMG in the wiki's text (`{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Sandrone_Talents.json`), and `packages/genshin-world/src/models/Element.ts` names Cryo `Ice`. Her charged attack is open against the wiki's text: its Sweeping Fire and Condensed Beam deal "AoE Cryo DMG" (see [Provisional constants](#provisional-constants)).
- **Prism Shot's first hit keeps Aino's area.** It is far wider than her other hits, and stays provisional, since gcsim's Aino circle for the same hit has a radius of 1.2 on the target (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/aino/skill.go:17`), not the record's 16.2 metres.

## Not built

- **Power Overdrive is waiting on its record.** Her burst's Power Overdrive form and the hits it deals are not built; the wiki's text gives its state, when her Decoding Power is under 50, and its longer intervals.
- **Her Condensed Beam is not built.** Its charged attack label, "Charged Attack Condensed Beam DMG" in the wiki's advanced properties, is not read, so her charged attack deals Sweeping Fire alone.
- **Her Stellar-Conduct and Stellar Swirl hits are not built.** Her Prism Shot's and Condensed Beam's Stellar hits, and the burst's Stellar hits, are the wiki's Stellar-Conduct and Radiance: Stellar Glimmer text (`Light of Rationalisme`), and they are not in her record.

## Provisional constants

- **The frames are open until a timings run is cached.** Her clips are read with `genshin:assets timings` from the installed game, and no timings output is on disk to confirm the record's 145, 228, 296, 128, 66 and 92 frames, nor the plunges' FallingAttack clips.
- **Her charged attack's element is open.** The wiki's Self-Evident Proposition text gives its Sweeping Fire and Condensed Beam as AoE Cryo DMG, but the record names no element for it, so it reads the default Physical until a source or the record settles it.
- **Her charged attack's stamina of 0 is provisional.** No table or wiki text states a stamina cost for it, so the 0 stands as the overlay's value.
- **Prism Shot's 16.2-metre area is open.** No source gives the hit a radius of 16.2 metres; gcsim's Aino circle is 1.2 on the target (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/aino/skill.go:17`), and the wiki's cached text gives no area.
