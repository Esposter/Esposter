## Purpose

Sandrone's kit as built: her three strikes, charged attack, plunges, Prism Shot and the burst's four hits, with the timings and hitmarks provisional and Power Overdrive and the Stellar hits not built.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Sandrone's three strikes

Sandrone's three strikes SHALL land on provisional timings on the enemies within reach.

#### Scenario: Three strikes in sequence

- **WHEN** the player uses normal attack three times in a row
- **THEN** the three strikes land in order on provisional timings

### Requirement: Sandrone's charged attack and plunges

Sandrone's charged attack and plunges SHALL land on the enemies within reach at provisional timings.

#### Scenario: Charged attack lands on the enemies in reach

- **WHEN** the player uses Sandrone's charged attack
- **THEN** its one hit lands on the enemies within reach at a provisional timing

#### Scenario: Plunge lands on the enemies in reach

- **WHEN** the player starts a plunge attack
- **THEN** the plunge lands on the enemies within reach at a provisional timing

### Requirement: Prism Shot

Sandrone's skill SHALL deal its two Ice hits on the enemies within reach.

#### Scenario: Prism Shot deals two hits

- **WHEN** the player presses Sandrone's skill
- **THEN** two Ice hits land on the enemies within reach

### Requirement: Bombardment and the Convective Inhibition Ray

Sandrone's burst SHALL deal its four Ice hits, three Bombardment hits and the Convective Inhibition Ray, on the enemies within reach.

#### Scenario: Bombardment and the Convective Inhibition Ray deal four hits

- **WHEN** the player casts Sandrone's burst
- **THEN** four Ice hits land on the enemies within reach

### Requirement: Power Overdrive not yet built

Sandrone's Power Overdrive SHALL NOT take effect.

#### Scenario: Power Overdrive does nothing

- **WHEN** the player uses Sandrone's burst in Power Overdrive
- **THEN** no Power Overdrive hit lands
