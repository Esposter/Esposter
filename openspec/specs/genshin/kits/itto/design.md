# Itto

Itto's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createIttoKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000057: four strikes, the Saichimonji Slash as his charged attack, a collision and two plunges, Masatsu Zetsugi's hit with Ushi, and Royal Descent's ATK bonus per DEF with its Geo conversion and Normal ATK SPD. His multipliers are read from proud skill groups 5731, 5732 and 5739; the table indexes of his strikes, the Saichimonji Slash, the Arataki Kesagiri's slashes and the plunges, Ushi's share of his Max HP and its seconds, the skill's cooldown and the burst's cooldown and energy cost are read from the same groups. The conversion reaches his strikes, slashes and plunges with the reaches gcsim gives them under the burst.

Superlative Superstrength is a resource of up to 5 on the team's effects. His second strike gives 1 as it lands and his fourth 2, and as the Raging Oni King his first and third 1 each. Ushi gives 1 the first time Masatsu Zetsugi's hit strikes, 1 as it takes an enemy's strike, at most once every 2 seconds, and 1 as it leaves, its seconds run out, its health spent or a second Ushi sending it away. Ushi is a taunt on the enemy the skill turned to, holding all of Itto's Max HP for 6 seconds from the hit. While he holds stacks a stance turns his charged attack into the Arataki Kesagiri for them, at no stamina: a combo slash from the left and the right in turn for each stack but the last, then the final slash, with its poise from gcsim and its hitmarks, windups, boxes and circles unsourced. His passives and constellations are not built.

## Decisions

- **Itto's charged attack is the Saichimonji Slash while he holds no stacks.** gcsim's charged attack is the Saichimonji Slash only with no Superlative Superstrength, and the Arataki Kesagiri combo and final slashes with stacks, which the kit plays as a stance while he holds them.
- **The Arataki Kesagiri is one charged attack holding every slash its stacks buy, and spends them as it starts.** gcsim plays each slash as its own charged attack, spending a stack and choosing the next slash from the stacks then held, so a stack gained mid-combo lengthens it. The kit's charged attack starts only once a strike ends, so it cannot chain one into another: the stance holds the whole combo for the stacks at its start, and lasts as long as the combo plays so its slashes keep the burst's conversion and their kind of attack. A stack gained while it plays waits for the next charged attack.
- **Ushi is a taunt with no explosion, and gives its stack as an enemy's strike damages it.** Ushi flees without dealing damage, so it carries no explosion. Each strike it takes is a `TauntDamaged` event, which gives Itto a stack off a 2-second cooldown of his own, the talent's "only 1 stack can be gained in this way every 2s"; gcsim leaves that stack to a parameter that defaults to none, since its enemies do not strike.
- **Masatsu Zetsugi's gauge is 1U**, the Durability of 25 gcsim gives it.
- **Royal Descent ends as Itto leaves the field.** gcsim's `itto-exit` deletes the burst's ATK and ATK SPD on a swap, which its Geo conversion reads (`itto/burst.go`), so the burst's buff and infusion each end on a swap (`isEndedOnSwap`).
- **The Arataki Kesagiri's hitmarks and frames are not read from gcsim, and its areas are only partly read.** gcsim picks each slash's hitbox row and hitmark by the state the stacks leave, and subtracts a windup from the hitmark, so no line gives the record's value and they are Unsourced. Its radii sit in gcsim's hitbox table, which the area reader does not resolve yet, so the record marks them Unsourced although gcsim gives them; the [character kits proposal](../../../../changes/genshin-character-kits/design.md) holds that reader. Each slash's poise is cited at the line that sets it, and the multipliers and stamina wait for a reader that joins a slash to its talent row.
