# Bennett

Bennett's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createBennettKitBehaviour`, avatar 10000032 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`): five strikes, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Bennett_Talents.json`), Passion Overload's press and its two Charge Levels, and Fantastic Voyage's damage. Its multipliers are read from proud skill groups 3231, 3232 and 3239 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), and match the wiki's Strike of Fortune, Passion Overload and Fantastic Voyage values to two decimal places (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Bennett_Talents.json`). Fantastic Voyage's field, its heal, its ATK bonus and its constellations 1 and 6 are built, as the shared effects below, and A1's and A4's cooldown cuts (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/bennett/asc.go:8` and `:18`, and the wiki's Rekindle and Fearnaught pages), and A4's no-launch of the Level 2 hold (`asc.go:26`), are built with them. Its self infusion is left.

## Decisions

- **A field's tick reads the character on the field.** Fantastic Voyage's heal and ATK bonus go to the character standing in the circle, not to each member of the team, as gcsim applies them (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/bennett/burst.go:60`, `:108` and `:113`).
- **Fantastic Voyage infuses only at constellation 6.** Its tick infuses nobody below 6 (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/bennett/burst.go:128`, and the wiki's C6 note, `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Bennett_Talents.json`). Grand Expectation at 1 lifts the ATK bonus's HP threshold to any HP above zero and adds 20% of Bennett's base ATK to it (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/bennett/burst.go:89` and `:123`), so a character under 70% of its HP gets the bonus. At 6, the bonus's branch also gives the character on the field a 15% Pyro DMG Bonus whatever it wields (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/bennett/burst.go:95`, and the wiki's C6 note on bow and catalyst characters, `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Bennett_Talents.json`), and Pyro on its weapon if it wields a sword, claymore or polearm (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/bennett/burst.go:128` to `:142`, not the self aura at `burst.go:106`), for the buffs' 2.1 seconds (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/bennett/burst.go:20`, 126 frames). The wielders are gcsim's, and the wiki's [Fire Ventures With Me](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Fire_Ventures_With_Me) page, which is not cached, is open for them, see [not built](#not-built). The wielder is the combatant's `weaponType`, which the world's combat fills from the roster's table it already holds.
- **Fearnaught's no-launch is the Level 2 hold's shorter animation.** Inside Fantastic Voyage's field at Ascension 4, the Level 2 hold plays gcsim's 175 frames (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/bennett/skill.go:58`) in place of 343 (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/bennett/skill.go:50`), with the same two hits and explosion (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/bennett/skill.go:16` and `:179`), all of which land before it ends. The wiki's Fearnaught page gives the effect (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Bennett_Talents.json`), and gcsim the animation. Bennett's launch is not modelled, so only the animation changes.
- **Bennett's hold boxes are circles.** The 3 by 3 box of the hold's second hit (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/bennett/skill.go:17`, `{3, 3}`, drawn at `skill.go:161` to `:166`) is priced as the circle to its far corner, 2.12 metres, as the other boxes are.

- **Impasse Conqueror is a passive, read at the HP share the pricing passes.** At constellation 2 and up, Bennett's Energy Recharge is 30% while his HP is under 70% of his Max HP, and none from 70% on (gcsim's `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/bennett/cons.go:11` and `cons.go:17`). The kit's passive hook takes that share as the character stands, so the bonus follows his HP without a field or a timer. The wiki's constellation table would confirm the effect, but the cached Bennett page only calls that table as a template, so that check is open, see [Open](#open).

## Provisional constants

- **Passion Overload's Charge Levels' thresholds are provisional.** The hold that reaches Level 1 is 0.5 seconds and Level 2's is 1 second, and no table or wiki page gives them, so the roadmap's References sought list holds `bennett-charge-levels.mkv` to measure them. The hold's hits and explosion, their multipliers and poise, and the cooldowns of 7.5 and 10 seconds are the wiki's (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Bennett_Talents.json`) and gcsim's (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/bennett/skill.go:209` and `:216`).

## Not built

- **Bennett's self infusion is left.** "ApplySelfInfusion" puts a 1U Pyro aura on the character on the field each tick, [gcsim v2.47.2](https://github.com/genshinsim/gcsim/blob/v2.47.2/internal/characters/bennett/burst.go) (MIT), an elemental aura and not a weapon infusion. Its aura on Bennett is not built. The wiki's Fire Ventures With Me page, which is not cached, is said to apply the C6 infusion and bonus also when the field heals a character under 70%, where gcsim applies them on the ATK bonus alone (`burst.go:110`); that wiki reading is open, and the kit follows gcsim.
- **Unexpected Odyssey, constellation 4, is not built.** A Passion Overload Level 1 hold adds a follow-up attack at 135% of its second hit's DMG (`skill.go:187`), [gcsim v2.47.2](https://github.com/genshinsim/gcsim/blob/v2.47.2/internal/characters/bennett/skill.go) (MIT). The record holds no variant of that hold for it, and the hook that adds a hit, `getNormalAttackExtraHit`, runs only for a kit's normal attacks, so no module hook reaches the hold.
