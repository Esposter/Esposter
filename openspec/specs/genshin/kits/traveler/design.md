# Traveler

The Traveler's kit is her record, with `createTravelerKitBehaviour` adding to it only the Anemo set's Constellation 2 Energy Recharge passive (16%, gcsim's `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/traveler/common/anemo/cons.go` lines 11-22) and its Constellation 6 Anemo RES status (20% for 10 seconds, `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/traveler/common/anemo/cons.go` lines 24-35), and it is the first kit, at talent level 1. Its hitmarks and seconds are gcsim v2.47.2's frames (its Anemo attack, charge, skill and burst files), and the plunges' poise is the wiki's Foreign Ironwind's, the same 25, 100 and 150 in its Aether and Lumine blocks of the cached `{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Traveler_Talents.json`. gcsim sets no poise on the Anemo skill or burst (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/traveler/common/anemo/skill.go` and `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/traveler/common/anemo/burst.go` carry no poise line), so the skill hits with none and the burst takes the wiki's 13, its Gust Surge Tornado DMG row (`{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Traveler_Talents.json`). Its strikes' poise is not in that file, so each takes the wiki's value, as do the charged attack's and the plunge collision's. Its multipliers are read from the Anemo form's proud skill groups (`{data-root}/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), 731 for the attacks, 732 for Palm Vortex and 739 for Gust Surge. Those agree with the wiki's infobox values at level 1 (Foreign Ironwind's, Palm Vortex's and Gust Surge's, in `{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Traveler_Talents.json`), to the wiki's precision. The normal attacks match gcsim's `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/traveler/common/anemo/stats.go` `attack` array (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/traveler/common/anemo/stats.go`) to four decimal places, gcsim's own rounding of the last place aside, and the charged attack's and Palm Vortex's (`charge`, `skillInitialCutting`, `skillMaxCutting`, `skillInitialStorm` and `skillMaxStorm`, `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/traveler/common/anemo/stats.go` lines 96, 113, 150, 169, 203, 238 and 272) exactly, while Gust Surge's 739 matches none of gcsim's values, gcsim's `burstDot` giving 0.376 at level 1 (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/traveler/common/anemo/stats.go` line 306) against 739's 0.808 (the same dump). The wiki's 80.8% sides with 739 (`{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Traveler_Talents.json`), and the table is the latest version where gcsim copies an older one, so the record holds 739 as `DiffersFromSource` for that reason. The charged attack's second hit is 731's 0.7224 (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/traveler/common/anemo/stats.go` line 150, and the dump's group 731 at index 6), the female row of gcsim's `charge` array and the wiki's Lumine 72.2%, where the male row gives 0.60716 (the dump's group 730 at index 6, Aether's 60.7%), so the kit reads 731, Lumine's group, for it. A hit can name its own element, which strikes the enemy as that element whatever the character's own is. The Traveler's hits name none, so they deal the element of the Traveler's form, which is none until a statue gives one.

## Decisions

- **The Traveler's Anemo set** holds a skill of 5 seconds, one charge, and a burst of 15 seconds for 60 energy, which her record holds beside its hits (the skill's cooldown is gcsim's `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/traveler/common/anemo/skill.go` line 79, a single cooldown and so one charge, and the burst's is `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/traveler/common/anemo/burst.go` line 98; the wiki's Palm Vortex and Gust Surge infoboxes give 5 seconds, 15 seconds and 60 energy, `{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Traveler_Talents.json`, as the dump's groups 732 and 739 do, `{data-root}/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`).
- **The strikes' poise is open between the wiki's two blocks.** The Foreign Ironwind Aether block gives the strikes 41.4, 43.2, 53.1, 57.6 and 70.2 and the Lumine block 40.5, 39.6, 48.6, 54 and 64.8, and the page does not say which the record reads, though its multipliers are Lumine's. The roadmap's manual verification has the question.
- **Palm Vortex's initial multiplier is held `Unsourced`, though gcsim gives it.** The record's `skill.hits.0.multiplier` at 732's index 2 (`{data-root}/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`) is `Unsourced`, but gcsim's `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/traveler/common/anemo/skill.go` line 69 takes `skillInitialStorm` for it, 1.76 at level 1 (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/traveler/common/anemo/stats.go` line 238), the value the record holds, so the overlay's `Unsourced` cause is wrong as it stands.
