# Venti

Venti's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createVentiKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000022: six shots, the first and fourth of two arrows each, a fully charged aimed shot, a collision and two plunges, Skyward Sonnet's press and its hold, and Wind's Grand Ode's Stormeye. His multipliers are read from proud skill groups 2231, 2232 and 2239, and match the wiki's Divine Marksmanship, Skyward Sonnet and Wind's Grand Ode values to two decimal places; the hold's and the press's cooldowns, the Stormeye's seconds and the burst's cooldown and cost are read from the same groups. The press's Wind Domain and the Stormeye are summons, built over the kit framework's shared effects, with the Stormeye passive's energy from Ascension 4, Splitting Gales' arrows from one constellation, and Breeze of Reminiscence's and Storm of Defiance's Anemo RES from two and six. Elemental Absorption, the Stormeye's pull, the hold's rise and Embrace of Winds' upcurrent, Breeze of Reminiscence's Physical RES, Hurricane of Freedom, his other constellations and his Hexerei kit are not built.

## Decisions

- **Venti's arrows, shots and Wind Domain are priced round his body.** gcsim lands each arrow and shot at the primary target through a box a metre long and a tenth wide, and the press's circle of 3 metres on it, which an area does not hold, so each is priced round his body, as Amber's arrows and Mona's strikes are. gcsim's 10 frames of an arrow's flight are not added, as Amber's are not.
- **Skyward Sonnet's press ends at its earliest cancel, and its Wind Domain lands past it.** gcsim queues the press's hit 51 frames on, apart from its animation, which every next action cancels at 22 frames, so the press plays 22 frames and its hit, queued past the press's end, lands from where Venti stood as a skill hit. The hold's hit lands within its action at 74 frames, and the hold ends at gcsim's earliest cancel, its high plunge at 116 frames: the rise into the air is the controller's and is not built, so the full 289 frames would hold him still through a flight he does not take.
- **The Stormeye is a summon 5 metres ahead of where Venti faces.** gcsim places it there as the burst starts and lands its 20 ticks every 24 frames from 106, each 1U of Anemo with the wiki's 4 poise, reaching 4 metres round it. Its ticks and the fully charged shot are priced under the standard internal cooldown, though the wiki gives each a cooldown of 1 second over 3 hits, as the standard group is the only one a kit's hit is priced under. The Stormeye's pull of the enemies within 15 metres is not built.
- **The Stormeye passive gives its energy as the Stormeye's seconds end.** From Ascension 4, a task runs 94 frames plus the burst group's 8 seconds after the burst starts, gcsim's 574, and gives Venti 15 energy up to his burst's cost. A drown or a jump before then clears it with the team's effects.
- **Splitting Gales' arrows land with the shot from a summon.** From one constellation, the fully charged shot's start casts two more arrows from where Venti stands, each the wiki's 33% of the shot's multiplier as 1U of Anemo under the Charged Attack internal cooldown with 2 poise, landing at the shot's hitmark. gcsim gives each a third of it.
- **Breeze of Reminiscence and Storm of Defiance are enemy statuses on Anemo RES.** From two constellations each Skyward Sonnet hit takes 12% off the Anemo RES of each enemy it strikes for 10 seconds, and from six each Stormeye tick takes 20% for gcsim's 10 seconds, the two adding up. Breeze of Reminiscence's Physical RES is not built: a status lowers an element's RES, and a physical hit reads the enemy's own. Its further cut while an enemy is airborne waits on a launch, which no hit makes.

## Provisional constants

- **Skyward Sonnet's hold is reached at half a second, provisionally.** No table or wiki page gives a press's hold seconds, so the index's half second stands until the roadmap's References sought list's `venti-skyward-sonnet.mkv` measures it.
- **Venti's plunges are provisional.** gcsim models only the high plunge after a held Skyward Sonnet and counts its 58 frames from its start in the air, where this kit starts a plunge's action as it lands, so each plunge lands as its action starts and ends at 0.4 seconds. The high plunge reaches gcsim's 3.5 metres, and the collision and the low plunge Amber's 1 and 3. Their poise is the wiki's Divine Marksmanship.

## Not built

- **Elemental Absorption is not built.** The Stormeye absorbs the element of what it touches, as gcsim's absorb check finds an aura within its box, and then deals the burst group's Additional Elemental DMG as that element. A summon's step reads no enemy, a hit's callbacks see no aura, and the strike loop that sees a Swirl's element is the character component's, so nothing tells the Stormeye what it touched. The Stormeye passive's energy for the party members of the absorbed element and Storm of Defiance's cut to that element's RES wait on it.
- **Venti's other passives, his other constellations and his Hexerei kit are not built.** Embrace of Winds' upcurrent and Windrider's gliding stamina are the controller's movement, not combat. Hurricane of Freedom waits on a particle pickup reaching the team's effects, as Razor's Wolf's Instinct does, and Ode to Thousand Winds and Concerto dal Cielo raise talent levels, which the [talents](../../../../changes/genshin-talents/design.md) page holds. His Hexerei kit, its Windsunder Arrows and the Hexerei halves of his constellations, are not built.
