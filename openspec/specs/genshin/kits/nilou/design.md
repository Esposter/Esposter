# Nilou

Nilou's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createNilouKitBehaviour` (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/nilouKit.ts`), on avatar 10000070 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`): three strikes (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/attack.go` line 14), a charged attack of two hits (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/charge.go` line 16), a collision and two plunges, Dance of Haftkarsvar with its Pirouette and Lunar Prayer, and Dance of Abzendegi's hit. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 7031, 7032 and 7039 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), and her skill and burst strike for shares of her Max HP (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/skill.go` line 105; `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/burst.go` lines 40, 45 and 79), not her ATK, as gcsim's flat damage does. The skill's cooldown is 18 seconds, the burst's cooldown 18 seconds and its cost 70 energy, the charged attack's stamina 20 (group 7032's index 8 is the skill's 18 seconds, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/skill.go` line 121; group 7039's index 2 is the burst's 18 seconds, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/burst.go` line 52, and its index 3 is the 70 energy; group 7031's index 5 is the charged attack's 20 stamina, as the Dance of Samser page's `stamina=20` gives it, `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Nilou_Talents.json`).

## Decisions

- **Nilou's skill and burst are priced at a share of her Max HP.** gcsim sets its flat damage to a multiplier times her Max HP (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/skill.go` line 105), so each hit has no talent multiplier of its own and takes the share as its `additiveBaseDamageBonus`, read from its striker as it is priced, as Ayato's Shunsuiken hits take a share of his.
- **Pirouette is a stance of the sword dance, and its third step sets Lunar Prayer on the team.** Dance of Haftkarsvar's cast leaves a stance for ten seconds (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/skill.go` line 120) in which her normal attacks are the three sword dance steps (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/attack.go` lines 39 to 43). The third step's `onStart` adds a field that ends the stance 30 frames on, handing the team an eight-second Lunar Prayer stance of the same sword dance (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/skill.go` lines 49 and 166), as gcsim's Pirouette does once its third step has landed. A second cast is blocked by the cooldown the first cast set, so it does not restart the Pirouette, and the gap to gcsim's steps is named under Not built.
- **The sword dance's Luminous Illusion is the hit Constellation 1 finds.** Lunar Prayer's third step plays the illusion's record hit, and Pirouette's third step plays a copy of it that carries Constellation 4's strike. `getStrikeDamageBonus` finds the illusion in either stance and adds 65% to it alone, so no other strike of the kit takes it (gcsim's 65% is `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/cons.go` lines 14 and 18).
- **Constellation 4 is set on Pirouette's third step, once per attack.** The copy's strike gives Nilou 15 Elemental Energy and a status that raises Dance of Abzendegi's DMG by 50% for eight seconds, from the first enemy the hit strikes. The strike hook's `isFirstEnemyStruck` flag tells that enemy, and `strikeKitEnemies` sets it on the first enemy each strike lands on, so the kit reads no flag of its own. The burst bonus is read through `getStrikeDamageBonus`'s effects. gcsim (MIT) `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/cons.go` (lines 67 to 75: 15 energy and 50% for eight seconds) checks the stance as the step starts (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/cons.go` lines 85 to 91, and once per attack at lines 93 to 102), but the step's hit, at 35 frames (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/skill.go` line 19), lands after the 30-frame hand-over to Lunar Prayer (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/skill.go` line 49), so the check is the copy's placement and not a stance read at the hit. Lunar Prayer's third step plays the record hit, which has no strike of Constellation 4's, so it gives none.
- **Constellation 6 is a stat converted from her Max HP.** Each 1,000 of Max HP gives 0.6% CRIT Rate and 1.2% CRIT DMG, capped at 30% and 60% (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/cons.go` lines 106 to 107 and 132 to 133), as `getConversionBonuses` reads her buffed Max HP in the conversion phase, after her ordinary passives and buffs.
- **Golden Chalice's Bounty is set on the team as the third step's Lunar Prayer lands, and a Bloom under it leaves a Bountiful Core.** From Ascension 1, the field that hands the team Lunar Prayer also gives each deployed member the Bounty for 30 seconds when the team holds a Dendro member and no member that is neither Dendro nor Hydro. gcsim's `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/asc.go` reads the team once, at its start (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/asc.go` line 34, the 30 seconds; lines 70 to 73, the 100 Elemental Mastery for 10 seconds), where this kit reads it as the step lands. A Dendro hit on a member under the Bounty gives every deployed member 100 Elemental Mastery for 10 seconds (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/asc.go` lines 58 to 76), as gcsim gives the buff to each character in the team. A Bloom by a member under the Bounty leaves a Bountiful Core in place of its Dendro Core (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/asc.go` lines 39 to 52), which bursts 0.4 seconds after it is made over 6.5 metres (gcsim (MIT) `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/bountifulcore.go` lines 25 and 36), and which no strike reaches, as gcsim's Bountiful core cannot trigger Hyperbloom or Burgeon (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/asc.go` line 25), and a hit on it is handed to the core's event and removes nothing (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/bountifulcore.go` lines 59 to 61). gcsim's comment (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/asc.go` line 27) says the Bounty ends when the team stops being Dendro and Hydro, but its code (line 34) does not end it, so the Bounty runs its 30 seconds here, as the code does.
- **Ascension 4 raises every deployed member's Bountiful Core DMG by Nilou's Max HP, through `getPartyReactionBonus`.** The hook is read over the deployed team for each reaction a member makes, and for a reaction bursting from a Bountiful Core, Nilou's share, 9% for each 1,000 of her Max HP above 30,000 and at most 400% (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/asc.go` lines 83 to 88 and 122 to 126), is added as its DMG Bonus, so it reaches a Bountiful Core made by any member. gcsim's `asc.go` gives every character the same reaction bonus mod, on any Bloom attack tagged as Bountiful Core damage (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/asc.go` lines 90 to 101), so the team sum is gcsim's shape. The hook reads her Max HP with its buffs as the burst is priced, where gcsim refreshes the bonus every half second (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/asc.go` lines 110 and 129), and a Bloom's core is not Bountiful, so a Bloom takes none. The Bountiful Cores exist only under the Bounty, which gcsim's mod is added with, so no window of its own is built (gcsim (MIT) `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/asc.go`).
- **Leaving the field ends Pirouette and Lunar Prayer, and cancels a pending field.** Both stances end on a swap, and her kit's swap (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/nilouKit.ts`) ends the field that would have handed the team Lunar Prayer, as gcsim's exit from the field deletes both statuses (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/skill.go` lines 337 and 338).
- **Provisional: the plunges are borrowed.** gcsim has no plunge file for Nilou, so her plunges follow [how a kit is read](../README.md#how-a-kit-is-read) in reach and landing, with Zhongli's poise for the low and high plunge and the collision (Zhongli's record rows 12 to 14, 25, 100 and 150, and Nilou's own Low and High Plunge rows, 100 and 150, in `Category_Nilou_Talents.json`). The roadmap asks whether a recording of her plunge agrees.

## Not built

- **Her plunges' timings and reaches are borrowed, not sourced.** gcsim has no plunge file for Nilou, so the reach and landing follow the kit-reading rule until a recording of her own plunge gives them.
- **The Bountiful Core's self-hit is not built.** A Bountiful Core's burst also hits each party member within its circle for 2% of its flat damage (gcsim (MIT) `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/bountifulcore.go` lines 36 to 41), which the kit does not model. Its damage also shares its creator's Bloom internal cooldown, as `applyReactionInternalCooldown` keys a reaction's damage by source, reaction and element, where gcsim tags Bountiful damage with its own `BountifulCoreDamage`.
- **Constellation 2's RES cuts are built as enemy statuses.** A Hydro hit by a team member under Golden Chalice's Bounty, and a Bloom or Lunar-Bloom reaction, each lower the enemy's RES by 35% for 10 seconds (gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/cons.go` lines 45 to 61, gated on the striker's Bounty for both halves), read from the team's events and gated on Ascension 1 and Constellation 2. The Dendro half follows the talent's text, which does not gate it on the Bounty as gcsim does.
- **Lingering Aeon is not built.** Dance of Abzendegi's second hit, at 121 frames (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nilou/burst.go` line 19), lands on the enemy the first hit struck. `onStrike` gives the body and the team but not the enemy, so no hook can aim the later hit at it.
- **Tranquility Aura and the Pirouette's later steps are not built.** The Whirling Steps' second and third presses, and the Tranquility Aura that the third press sets off, need one step count shared by the dance and the steps, which gcsim keeps. A stance holds neither that count nor a skill chain, as `Kit.elementalSkillChain` belongs to the kit, so the stance's skill plays the first Whirling Steps alone.
- **A press of the skill inside Pirouette is blocked by the cooldown the cast set.** gcsim lets the Whirling Steps be pressed through the cooldown, while a stance's skill press is not exempted from one, so the steps are reachable only once Pirouette has run out. This waits on a hook that lets a stance's skill press ignore the cooldown.
