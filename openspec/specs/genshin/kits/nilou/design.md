# Nilou

Nilou's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createNilouKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000070: three strikes, a charged attack of two hits, a collision and two plunges, Dance of Haftkarsvar with its Pirouette and Lunar Prayer, and Dance of Abzendegi's hit. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 7031, 7032 and 7039, and her skill and burst strike for shares of her Max HP, not her ATK, as gcsim's flat damage does. The skill's cooldown is 18 seconds, the burst's cooldown 18 seconds and its cost 70 energy, the charged attack's stamina 20.

## Decisions

- **Nilou's skill and burst are priced at a share of her Max HP.** gcsim sets its flat damage to a multiplier times her Max HP, so each hit has no talent multiplier of its own and takes the share as its `additiveBaseDamageBonus`, read from its striker as it is priced, as Ayato's Shunsuiken hits take a share of his.
- **Pirouette is a stance of the sword dance, and its third step sets Lunar Prayer on the team.** Dance of Haftkarsvar's cast leaves a stance for ten seconds in which her normal attacks are the three sword dance steps. The third step's `onStart` adds a field that ends the stance 30 frames on, handing the team an eight-second Lunar Prayer stance of the same sword dance, as gcsim's Pirouette does once its third step has landed. A second cast restarts the Pirouette, as a stance of the same character does.
- **The sword dance's Luminous Illusion is the hit Constellation 1 finds.** The illusion's hit is one object shared by both stances, and `getStrikeDamageBonus` adds 65% to that hit alone, so no other strike of the kit takes it.
- **Constellation 6 is a stat converted from her Max HP.** Each 1,000 of Max HP gives 0.6% CRIT Rate and 1.2% CRIT DMG, capped at 30% and 60%, as `getConversionBonuses` reads her buffed Max HP in the conversion phase, after her ordinary passives and buffs.
- **Golden Chalice's Bounty is set on the team as the third step's Lunar Prayer lands, and a Bloom under it leaves a Bountiful Core.** From Ascension 1, the field that hands the team Lunar Prayer also gives each deployed member the Bounty for 30 seconds when the team holds a Dendro member and no member that is neither Dendro nor Hydro. gcsim's `asc.go` reads the team once, at its start, where this kit reads it as the step lands. A Dendro hit on a member under the Bounty gives every deployed member 100 Elemental Mastery for 10 seconds, as gcsim gives the buff to each character in the team. A Bloom by a member under the Bounty leaves a Bountiful Core in place of its Dendro Core, which bursts 0.4 seconds after it is made over 6.5 metres (gcsim (MIT) `nilou/bountifulcore.go`), and which no strike reaches, as gcsim's Bountiful core cannot trigger Hyperbloom or Burgeon. gcsim's comment says the Bounty ends when the team stops being Dendro and Hydro, but its code does not end it, so the Bounty runs its 30 seconds here, as the code does.
- **Ascension 4 raises every deployed member's Bountiful Core DMG by Nilou's Max HP, through `getPartyReactionBonus`.** The hook is read over the deployed team for each reaction a member makes, and for a reaction bursting from a Bountiful Core, Nilou's share, 9% for each 1,000 of her Max HP above 30,000 and at most 400%, is added as its DMG Bonus, so it reaches a Bountiful Core made by any member. gcsim's `asc.go` gives every character the same reaction bonus mod, on any Bloom attack tagged as Bountiful Core damage, so the team sum is gcsim's shape. The hook reads her Max HP with its buffs as the burst is priced, where gcsim refreshes the bonus every half second, and a Bloom's core is not Bountiful, so a Bloom takes none. The Bountiful Cores exist only under the Bounty, which gcsim's mod is added with, so no window of its own is built (gcsim (MIT) `nilou/asc.go`).
- **Leaving the field ends Pirouette and Lunar Prayer, and cancels a pending field.** Both stances end on a swap, and her kit's swap ends the field that would have handed the team Lunar Prayer, as gcsim's exit from the field deletes both statuses.
- **Provisional: the plunges are borrowed.** gcsim has no plunge file for Nilou, so her plunges follow [how a kit is read](../README.md#how-a-kit-is-read) in reach and landing, with Zhongli's poise for the low and high plunge and the collision. The roadmap asks whether a recording of her plunge agrees.

## Not built

- **The Bountiful Core's self-hit is not built.** A Bountiful Core's burst also hits each party member within its circle for 2% of its flat damage (gcsim (MIT) `nilou/bountifulcore.go`), which the kit does not model. Its damage also shares its creator's Bloom internal cooldown, as `applyReactionInternalCooldown` keys a reaction's damage by source, reaction and element, where gcsim tags Bountiful damage with its own `BountifulCoreDamage`.
- **Constellation 2's RES cuts are built as enemy statuses.** A Hydro hit by a team member under Golden Chalice's Bounty, and a Bloom or Lunar-Bloom reaction, each lower the enemy's RES by 35% for 10 seconds, read from the team's events and gated on Ascension 1 and Constellation 2. The Dendro half follows the talent's text, which does not gate it on the Bounty as gcsim does. Constellation 4, the energy of the third sword dance and the 50% burst DMG bonus for eight seconds, waits on `onStrike`, which does not say whether a hit is the first of its action, so the energy would be given on every enemy the hit strikes. `getStrikeDamageBonus` now takes the team's effects, so the burst's timed bonus no longer waits on a hook.
- **Lingering Aeon is not built.** Dance of Abzendegi's second hit, at 121 frames, lands on the enemy the first hit struck. `onStrike` gives the body and the team but not the enemy, so no hook can aim the later hit at it.
- **Tranquility Aura and the Pirouette's later steps are not built.** The Whirling Steps' second and third presses, and the Tranquility Aura that the third press sets off, need one step count shared by the dance and the steps, which gcsim keeps. A stance holds neither that count nor a skill chain, as `Kit.elementalSkillChain` belongs to the kit, so the stance's skill plays the first Whirling Steps alone.
- **A press of the skill inside Pirouette is blocked by the cooldown the cast set.** gcsim lets the Whirling Steps be pressed through the cooldown, while a stance's skill press is not exempted from one, so the steps are reachable only once Pirouette has run out. This waits on a hook that lets a stance's skill press ignore the cooldown.
