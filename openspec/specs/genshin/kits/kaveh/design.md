# Kaveh

Kaveh's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createKavehKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000081: four strikes, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, Artistic Ingenuity's Dendro circle of radius 5 round the body at 35 frames, and Painted Dome, a Dendro circle of radius 5 at 36 frames that sets a stance for its twelve seconds (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kaveh/skill.go` lines 43-44, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kaveh/burst.go` lines 18-19 and 47-48: radius 5 at hitmarks 35 and 36, a 720-frame stance). The stance widens each of his strikes' fans to gcsim's second set of radii (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kaveh/attack.go` lines 19 and 58-60), as gcsim's burst state does, and plays the rest of his kit as it stands. It ends as he leaves the field, as gcsim's exit deletes the burst's state (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kaveh/burst.go` lines 80-84). The stance starts with the burst's animation rather than at its hit (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kaveh/burst.go` line 76 sets the state, line 18 gives the 36-frame hitmark), a difference of 0.6 seconds that is not measured. His multipliers are read from proud skill groups 8131, 8132 and 8139 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), each level-1 row matching the wiki's scaling table (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kaveh_Talents.json`, for example `skilldmg_1=204` and `hit1_1=76.19`; see Sources); the skill's cooldown, and the burst's cooldown and energy cost are read from the same groups (the cooldowns as gcsim's `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kaveh/skill.go` line 51 and `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kaveh/burst.go` line 49 also give them, and the energy cost as the wiki's `energycost=80` gives it).

Gcsim gives Kaveh no charged attack, as it has no charge file. His charged attack takes the table's shape like Dori's: a spin lands at once and a final hit at the longest duration the table allows, both circles of radius 2 round the body, costing the stamina per second the table gives. The collision takes the wiki's 35 poise (the Schematic Setup page's Plunging Attack Collision row, `poise7 = 35` in `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kaveh_Talents.json`), since gcsim gives it none. The charged attack's radius of 2 is no source's, and gcsim gives it no frames, so its shape is open until a recording settles it; the wiki's Schematic Setup page gives its 5-second maximum duration and 40 stamina a second (`maxduration=5s` and `stamina=40/s` in `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kaveh_Talents.json`).

Not built yet: the Dendro Cores his skill and burst rupture, which strike the enemies on each core in reach (gcsim calls `ruptureDendroCores` at `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kaveh/skill.go` line 45 and `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kaveh/burst.go` line 53), as a rupture is a reaction on gadgets the kit does not yet hold; Painted Dome's Bloom DMG Bonus, which adds to each bloom reaction the team makes for its twelve seconds (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kaveh/burst.go` lines 53-68; the wiki's Painted Dome page gives it as 27.49% at level 1); and his Ascension passives and Constellation 6, which stays unbuilt until the Dendro-core rupture exists. Constellation 2's Normal Attack SPD is built, as Painted Dome's stance is on him (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kaveh/cons.go` lines 31-40, 15% for the burst's duration), and Constellation 4's Bloom DMG is built through the kit's `getPartyReactionBonus`, which reads it on each core's burst for the Bloom he makes (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kaveh/cons.go` lines 43-52, 60% on `AttackTagBloom`). Constellation 1's Dendro RES and Incoming Healing Bonus are a buff on Kaveh himself for three seconds after Artistic Ingenuity: gcsim models only the Incoming Healing Bonus, 25% for 180 frames on Kaveh alone (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kaveh/cons.go` lines 19-28), and the 50% Dendro RES is open, since the cached wiki holds no text for Sublime Salutations, the constellation's name on the Artistic Ingenuity page, and gcsim leaves the RES out.
