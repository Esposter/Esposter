# Heizou

Heizou's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createHeizouKitBehaviour`, avatar 10000059 (`genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`, named Shikanoin Heizou in `genshin-dump-backup-6.6/TextMap/TextMap_MediumEN.json`): five strikes with the fourth's three hits, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, Heartstopper Strike's press and its full hold as one hit, and Fudou Style Vacuum Slugger with its Windmuster Iris (`cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Shikanoin_Heizou_Talents.json`, the Fudou Style Martial Arts, Heartstopper Strike and Windmuster Kick pages). His multipliers are read from proud skill groups 5931, 5932 and 5939; the charged attack's table index and stamina, the Iris's multiplier, the skill's and burst's cooldowns and the burst's energy cost are read from the same groups (`genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`: 5931's index 8 is the charged attack's 25 stamina, 5932's index 3 the skill's 10 seconds, 5939's index 2 the burst's 12 seconds and index 3 its 40 energy, each matching the cached wiki, `cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Shikanoin_Heizou_Talents.json`). Every hit of his is Anemo, at gcsim's Durability: 2U from Heartstopper Strike (`skill.go`, Durability 50), none from the plunge collision (`plunge.go`, Durability 0) and 1U from the rest (Durability 25). The hold is gcsim's full charge, which releases one hit at three seconds: four stacks at 45 frames each (`skill.go`, `skillHoldDuration`).

The burst lands an aura check just before its hit, with no damage, element or poise (`burst.go`, lines 45 to 58: Physical at Durability 0 and Mult 0, and no poise). Each of the first four enemies it strikes in a cast is tagged, and one holding Pyro, Hydro, Electro or Cryo, the first of them in gcsim's priority, has a Windmuster Iris cast where it stands, which explodes 40 frames on in that element, 1U, round it (`burst.go` line 129, a 2.5-metre circle at 40 frames, and line 110, Durability 25). His Ascension 4 passive, Penetrative Reasoning, and Constellation 4's Energy, which each tag gives Heizou as it is made, are built (`cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/heizou/asc.go`, lines 52 to 54, for Penetrative Reasoning). His Ascension 1 passive and Constellations 1, 2 and 6 are not built, and neither are the full hold's Declension stacks and Conviction bonus: its one hit carries the stacks' total as one multiplier, and no stack is counted or read.

## Decisions

- **Heizou's hold is gcsim's full charge.** gcsim's Heartstopper hold charges its four Declension stacks over 180 frames (`skill.go` lines 31 to 36), so the kit's hold is one level at three seconds, and a shorter hold reads as the press. That reading is provisional: gcsim's hold is binary and gives no stack count for a release before three seconds, so the count a short hold gives is unsourced until a recording shows it. The full hold's one hit is the stacks' total as one multiplier, 568.8% at level 1: the skill's 227.52% plus four stacks of 56.88% plus the Conviction bonus of 113.76% (`genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`, group 5932's indices 0 to 2, gcsim `skill.go` lines 59 to 72, and the cached wiki's Heartstopper Strike scaling, `cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Shikanoin_Heizou_Talents.json`).
- **Poise is unbuilt, and the wiki's figures are its target.** Heizou's hits deal no poise as built, and `heizouKit.ts` and `shikanoin-heizou.json` carry none. The wiki's figures, held on the roadmap, are that target: his press and hold would take the wiki's Heartstopper Strike poise, which rises with the Declension stacks it holds: 150 at none, rising by 25 a stack to 250 at four (cached `cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Shikanoin_Heizou_Talents.json`, Heartstopper Strike). The stack count a press or hold carries is open (see Open). His burst would take the wiki's 100 on its hit and none on its aura check (Windmuster Kick); his strikes would take the wiki's 42.16, 41.46 and 57.44 for the first three, 16.63, 18.29 and 21.62 for the fourth's three hits, and 69.13 for the fifth; his charged attack the wiki's 120, his collision the wiki's 5, and his plunges the wiki's 100 high and 50 low (Fudou Style Martial Arts); and his Windmuster Iris would take the wiki's 25 (Windmuster Kick). gcsim gives no poise for any of them.
- **Windmuster Iris reads the aura from a hit of its own, ahead of the burst's Anemo.** gcsim queues a no-damage aura check on the burst's frame ahead of its Anemo hit, so the Swirl the hit triggers does not take the aura first (`burst.go` line 58 and line 76, both at the burst's 34-frame hitmark, `burst.go` line 23). The kit's burst holds the same check as its first hit, and the kit reads the aura of each enemy it strikes as the damage it took reaches it. The Iris is a summon on the enemy as the check found it, where gcsim's circle follows the enemy over the 40 frames.

- **Constellation 4's Energy is counted on the aura check's tags, not on the Irises.** gcsim calls its Energy from the check's callback, once for each enemy the check tags up to four (`burst.go` lines 27 to 39), before the Iris it may cast explodes 40 frames on, so the kit grants it on the tag the same way. The first tag gives 9 Energy and each of the three after it 1.5 (gcsim `cons.go`, `c4`, lines 39 to 46), capped at the character's burst cost by the shared `packages/genshin-world/src/services/party/gainPartyMemberEnergy.ts`, as the flat Energy grant of a passive does.

## Open

- **The stack count a press or hold carries.** The wiki's poise rises from 150 to 250 with the Declension stacks, and the kit counts none of the full hold's stacks, so the poise a press or full hold deals waits on that count being read.
- **Whether the built hits carry the wiki's poise.** The kit's code and tests read no poise, and `packages/genshin-world/src/data/kits/shikanoin-heizou.json` holds none, so the poise above is the wiki's, not checked against the generated data.
