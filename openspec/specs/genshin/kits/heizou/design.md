# Heizou

Heizou's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createHeizouKitBehaviour`, avatar 10000059: five strikes with the fourth's three hits, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, Heartstopper Strike's press and its full hold with the Declension stacks and Conviction bonus, and Fudou Style Vacuum Slugger with its Windmuster Iris. His multipliers are read from proud skill groups 5931, 5932 and 5939; the charged attack's table index and stamina, the Iris's multiplier, the skill's and burst's cooldowns and the burst's energy cost are read from the same groups. Every hit of his is Anemo, at gcsim's Durability: 2U from Heartstopper Strike, none from the plunge collision and 1U from the rest. The hold is gcsim's full charge, which releases at three seconds with four stacks.

The burst lands an aura check just before its hit, with no damage, element or poise. Each of the first four enemies it strikes in a cast is tagged, and one holding Pyro, Hydro, Electro or Cryo, the first of them in gcsim's priority, has a Windmuster Iris cast where it stands, which explodes 40 frames on in that element, 1U, round it. His Ascension 4 passive, Penetrative Reasoning, and Constellation 4's Energy, which each tag gives Heizou as it is made, are built. His Ascension 1 passive and Constellations 1, 2 and 6 are not built.

## Decisions

- **Heizou's hold is gcsim's full charge.** gcsim's Heartstopper hold charges its four Declension stacks over 180 frames, so the kit's hold is one level at three seconds, and a shorter hold reads as the press. That reading is provisional: gcsim's hold is binary and gives no stack count for a release before three seconds, so the count a short hold gives is unsourced until a recording shows it. His skill gives no poise, as neither gcsim nor the wiki gives one, and his burst takes the wiki's 100 on its hit and none on its aura check; his strikes take the wiki's 42.16, 41.46 and 57.44 for the first three, 16.63, 18.29 and 21.62 for the fourth's three hits, and 69.13 for the fifth, his charged attack the wiki's 120, his collision the wiki's 5, his plunges the wiki's 100 high and 50 low, and his Windmuster Iris the wiki's 25.
- **Windmuster Iris reads the aura from a hit of its own, ahead of the burst's Anemo.** gcsim queues a no-damage aura check on the burst's frame ahead of its Anemo hit, so the Swirl the hit triggers does not take the aura first. The kit's burst holds the same check as its first hit, and the kit reads the aura of each enemy it strikes as the damage it took reaches it. The Iris is a summon on the enemy as the check found it, where gcsim's circle follows the enemy over the 40 frames.

- **Constellation 4's Energy is counted on the aura check's tags, not on the Irises.** gcsim calls its Energy from the check's callback, once for each enemy the check tags up to four (`burst.go` lines 27 to 39), before the Iris it may cast explodes 40 frames on, so the kit grants it on the tag the same way. The first tag gives 9 Energy and each of the three after it 1.5, capped at the character's burst cost, as the flat Energy grant of a passive does.
