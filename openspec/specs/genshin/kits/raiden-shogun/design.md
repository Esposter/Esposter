# Raiden Shogun

Raiden Shogun's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createRaidenKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000052: five strikes, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, Eye of Stormy Judgment with its Eye and coordinated attacks, and Musou Shinsetsu with the Musou Isshin stance. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 5231, 5232 and 5239; the charged attacks' stamina, the skill's and the burst's cooldowns, the Eye's seconds, the burst's energy cost and the energy and seconds of Musou Isshin are read from the same groups. The skill's hit lands 51 frames on from a summon at the body, and 66 frames on the Eye reaches every member of the deployed team, so a member holding it who deals damage sets off a coordinated attack on the enemy struck, once every 0.9 seconds. Another member's burst gives her Resolve as it starts, the burst group's stacks per point of the energy that burst cost, up to 60, and the burst spends her Resolve stacks into Musou no Hitotachi and every Musou Isshin attack as it is cast, and takes Musou Isshin as a stance, whose Electro strikes and two-hit charged attack restore the whole team's energy once a second, five times a cast; leaving the field ends it. The Eye's Elemental Burst DMG Bonus raises her own burst hits, read as the burst is cast. Ascension 1's 2 Resolve stacks as the party collects particles or orbs, once every 3 seconds, Ascension 4's energy and Electro DMG Bonus from Energy Recharge, Constellation 1's 80% more Resolve from an Electro member's burst and 20% more from any other's, Constellation 4's ATK to the rest of the team as Musou Isshin ends, and Constellation 6's second off the others' burst cooldowns on the hits that restore energy are built. Resolve is held among the team's effects, so a drown or a jump clears it, where the game keeps it. Constellation 2's DEF ignored is not built, as no hit carries one to pricing, nor the Eye's bonus to another member's burst, as no kit reaches another's pricing, nor the Musou Isshin plunges, as a stance holds none, nor the skill's particles. Musou Isshin's hits are dealt as normal and charged attacks, where gcsim deals them as Elemental Burst.

## Decisions

- **The Raiden Shogun's strikes, charged attacks, skill, coordinated attack, burst and collision deal 0 poise.** Provisional, as gcsim gives none, so the kit sets each at 0.
