# Kujou Sara

Kujou Sara's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createKujouSaraKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000056 (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/kujouSaraKit.ts`): five strikes, a fully charged shot, a collision and two plunges (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/attack.go` line 19 sets five hits, `aimed.go` line 32 holds the fully charged shot, and `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kujou_Sara_Talents.json` lists the collision and the Low and High Plunge rows), Tengu Stormcall's Cover with the crowfeather a fully charged shot drops under it and its Ambush, Constellation 2's weaker crowfeather, and Subjugation: Koukou Sendou's Titanbreaker with its Stormclusters, four bouts of six ticks or six from Constellation 4 (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/burst.go` lines 74 to 78: six at Constellation 4, four below it). Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 5631, 5632 and 5639 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), each level-1 row matching the wiki's scaling tables (see Sources); the skill's 10-second cooldown and the ATK Bonus's six seconds (group 5632 indices 3 and 2), and the burst's 20-second cooldown and 80 energy (group 5639 indices 2 and 3), are read from the same groups. Her Electro hits give the character on the field an ATK Bonus of the table's share of her base ATK, for six seconds (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/skill.go` lines 83-100). The covered shot is built from Ascension 1, but its shorter charge is not, as a charged attack has one duration. Ascension 4's Decorum restores the party's Energy as the Ambush lands, and Constellation 6's mark gives the character the ATK Bonus reaches 60% CRIT DMG on its Electro DMG and its Lunar-Charged reactions for six seconds. Constellation 1 cuts a second off her skill's cooldown from each Tengu Juurai hit that strikes an enemy, once every three seconds, where the kit's cut omits two hits gcsim's cut takes (see Provisional constants).

## Decisions

- **Tengu Stormcall's ATK Bonus is read on the hit, to the character on the field.** A hit's context holds the party but not the field's combatant, so the bonus takes the active member's id from the party and prices its amount from Sara's own base ATK (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/kujouSaraKit.ts`, `addAtkBonus`, reading `getActiveCharacterId`). gcsim applies it through `attackBuff`, which applies only once the attack will land on an enemy (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/skill.go` lines 83-86, `AttackWillLand`), so the kit's `onStrike` gives it on the same hits. (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/kujouSaraKit.ts`)
- **The Cover is a status, and its crowfeather is the Ambush at the target.** The Cover lasts eighteen seconds (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/skill.go` line 39, `18*60`), and a fully charged shot under it from Ascension 1 spends it (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/aimed.go` lines 65-66 and 141) and sets out the Ambush at 150 frames: the shot's hit is at 50 frames, and the crowfeather explodes 90 frames later, plus 10 frames of travel (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/aimed.go` lines 121-138). The Ambush is priced round the target as the strikes' targeted reach, a 6-metre circle on the primary target (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/aimed.go` line 134). The plunges take the wiki's poise, 100 for the high and 50 for the low, and the crowfeather's Ambush takes the wiki's 100 poise and 1U gauge, as gcsim gives no poise (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/` has no `PoiseDMG`). The plunges' seconds are Ganyu's, see Provisional constants. (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/kujouSaraKit.ts`)
- **Decorum's Energy is 1.2 per 100% of Energy Recharge.** gcsim's comment on `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/asc.go` gives 0.012 Energy per 1% of Energy Recharge, on a 180-frame cooldown (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/asc.go` lines 14-19 and 30-31), so the 1.2 is for each 100%. The kit multiplies 1.2 by the striker's Energy Recharge as a fraction, where 1.0 is 100% (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/kujouSaraKit.ts` lines 42 and 83), which is the same figure. (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/kujouSaraKit.ts`)
- **Constellation 1's cut comes from the burst's first hit and the Cover's Ambush, and cools for three seconds.** gcsim's `c1()` cuts Sara's skill cooldown by 60 frames and starts a 180-frame cooldown on itself (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/cons.go` lines 21-29). It is called by the enemy-hit callback on the burst's initial hit and on each Stormcluster tick (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/burst.go` lines 50-56, passed at line 64 and line 91), and by `attackBuff` on each attack that calls it, which covers the Cover's Ambush (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/aimed.go` line 139) and Constellation 2's Ambush (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/skill.go` lines 57 and 83-120). The shots do not carry it, as gcsim does not call it from the fully charged shot's own hit, only from the Ambush it sets out. The kit's cut runs on the burst's first hit and on the Cover's Ambush (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/kujouSaraKit.ts` lines 54-56, 69-70 and `ambushHit`, lines 120-124), and the Stormclusters and Constellation 2's Ambush carry the ATK Bonus alone. (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/kujouSaraKit.ts`)
- **Constellation 6's mark is set where the ATK Bonus is, and its CRIT DMG is read by three hooks.** gcsim puts the mark on the character the attack reaches, in the same function as the ATK Bonus (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/skill.go` lines 83-120, `c.c6(active)` beside `c.c1()`), for 360 frames (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/cons.go` line 64). So it is added beside the ATK Bonus. Sara's own strikes read her own mark through her strike hook, the party's strikes read the striker's mark through the party strike hook, and Lunar-Charged reactions read the striker's mark through the party reaction hook (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/kujouSaraKit.ts` lines 97-117, `KUJOU_SARA_C6_MARK_ID`). gcsim's CRIT DMG is 60% on Electro hits (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/cons.go` lines 61-66) and on Lunar-Charged reactions (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/cons.go` lines 36 and 44-57). The reaction gatherer calls that hook for every deployed member, Sara's own kit included, so no member is skipped. (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/kujouSaraKit.ts`)

## Provisional constants

- **The plunges' seconds are Ganyu's, and the Mac measures both.** gcsim's `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/` folder has no plunge file (only `aimed`, `asc`, `attack`, `burst`, `cons`, `sara` and `skill`), so the plunges' timings and the collision's are borrowed from another kit. Provisional until the Mac measures both. Their poise is the cached wiki's, 50 for the low plunge and 100 for the high (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kujou_Sara_Talents.json`, Tengu Bowmanship's poise11 and poise12).
- **The Stormclusters and Constellation 2's Ambush do not cut the skill cooldown, where gcsim's cut them.** gcsim's Stormcluster ticks call `c1cb` (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/burst.go` line 91), and Constellation 2's Ambush calls `attackBuff` (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/skill.go` line 57), which calls `c1()` (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/skill.go` lines 83-120). The kit's `stormclusterTicks` and `constellationAmbushHit` carry the ATK Bonus alone. The spec's "a Tengu Juurai hit" covers both. Open against gcsim.

## Not built

- **Ascension 1's shorter charge is not built.** gcsim shortens the fully charged shot's charge by 60% while the Cover stands (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sara/aimed.go` lines 65-66, `skillActive` from Ascension 1), and the kit's charged attack has one duration, so the shot's charge is not shortened.
