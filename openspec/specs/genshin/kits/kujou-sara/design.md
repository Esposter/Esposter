# Kujou Sara

Kujou Sara's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createKujouSaraKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000056: five strikes, a fully charged shot, a collision and two plunges, Tengu Stormcall's Cover with the crowfeather a fully charged shot drops under it and its Ambush, Constellation 2's weaker crowfeather, and Subjugation: Koukou Sendou's Titanbreaker with its Stormclusters, four bouts of six ticks or six from Constellation 4. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 5631, 5632 and 5639; the skill's cooldown and the Ambush's seconds, and the burst's cooldown and energy cost, are read from the same groups. Her Electro hits give the character on the field an ATK Bonus of the table's share of her base ATK, for six seconds. The covered shot is built from Ascension 1, but its shorter charge is not, as a charged attack has one duration. Ascension 4's Decorum restores the party's Energy as the Ambush lands, and Constellation 6's mark gives the character the ATK Bonus reaches 60% CRIT DMG on its Electro DMG and its Lunar-Charged reactions for six seconds. Constellation 1 cuts a second off her skill's cooldown from each Tengu Juurai hit that strikes an enemy, once every three seconds.

## Decisions

- **Tengu Stormcall's ATK Bonus is read on the hit, to the character on the field.** A hit's context holds the party but not the field's combatant, so the bonus takes the active member's id from the party and prices its amount from Sara's own base ATK. gcsim applies it whenever the active character stands in the attack, so a strike on no enemy is where this differs.
- **The Cover is a status, and its crowfeather is the Ambush at the target.** The Cover lasts eighteen seconds, and a fully charged shot under it from Ascension 1 spends it and sets out the Ambush at 150 frames, priced round the target as the strikes' targeted reach. The plunges take the wiki's poise, 100 for the high and 50 for the low, and the crowfeather's Ambush takes the wiki's 100 poise and 1U gauge, as gcsim gives it no poise; the plunges' seconds are Ganyu's. Provisional: the Mac measures both.
- **Constellation 1's cut is read on the two hits gcsim's callbacks name, and cools for three seconds.** gcsim calls it from the burst's initial hit's callback, which proceeds only on an enemy, and from the Ambush once it hits, so each strike that hits an enemy and is one of those two cuts Sara's own skill cooldown by a second. The cut starts a three-second cooldown on her, which holds whichever of the two lands first. The Stormclusters and the shots do not carry it, as gcsim does not call it from them.
- **Constellation 6's mark is set where the ATK Bonus is, and its CRIT DMG is read by three hooks.** gcsim puts the mark on the character the attack reaches, in the same function as the ATK Bonus, so it is added beside the ATK Bonus. Sara's own strikes read her own mark through her strike hook, the party's strikes read the striker's mark through the party strike hook, and Lunar-Charged reactions read the striker's mark through the party reaction hook. The reaction gatherer calls that hook for every deployed member, Sara's own kit included, so no member is skipped.
