# Citlali

Citlali's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createCitlaliKitBehaviour`, avatar 10000107 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`): her three strikes, a charged attack, the hit of Obsidian Tzitzimitl, Ice Storm, and a collision, low and high plunge (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Citlali_Talents.json`). Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 10731, 10732 and 10739: the strikes, charged attack, stamina and plunges from the first, Obsidian Tzitzimitl and the skill's cooldown from the second, and Ice Storm, the burst's cooldown and its energy cost from the third. At level 1 the game's `ProudSkillExcelConfigData` (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`) gives the strikes 0.434072, 0.388136 and 0.537712 (index 0 to 2), the charged attack 0.992 (index 3), the stamina 50 (index 4), the plunges 0.568288, 1.136335 and 1.419344 (indices 5 to 7), the dump's 20 at 10732 index 3, which is not the skill's cooldown: that is 16 s (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/citlali/skill.go` line 58, `SetCD(action.ActionSkill, 16*60)`) and Frostfall Storm 0.17024 (10732 index 4), and the burst's cooldown 15 (10739 index 4) and energy cost 60 (index 5). The charged attack costs 50 stamina, and Ice Storm costs 60 energy (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Citlali_Talents.json`, the Edict of Entwined Splendor's energy cost).

## Not yet built

- **The Opal Shield is not built.** It is the shield the skill gives the party's active character at 37 frames (gcsim `skill.go` line 72, `QueueCharTask(c.addShield, 37)`), scaling with Elemental Mastery, and it waits on a timed shield the kit does not hold.
- **The Spirit Vessel skulls are not built.** Ice Storm summons a skull on each of the first three enemies within 7 of Citlali as it is cast (`burst.go` lines 79-84), and each lands at a random time between 87 and 117 frames after the burst's hit (`burst.go` lines 20, 27-28 and 87: 57 plus 30 plus a random 0 to 30 frames, added to the 118-frame hitmark on line 13). The start is given the world's context, so it can read the enemies and draw those times from its random source, but no skull is written yet, and the burst's hit is Ice Storm alone.
- **Citlali's Ascension 1 and 4 and Constellations 1, 4 and 6 are not built, and Constellation 2's share for the active member is not.** That share is 250 Elemental Mastery while the Opal Shield or the Blessing stands, and it waits on the Opal Shield, which is not built. Ascension 4's flat damage on the Frostfall Storm is among them, and each is named with its wait in `citlaliKit.ts`.

## Decisions

- **Constellation 2's own share is a passive bonus on her pricing.** Her 125 Elemental Mastery is gcsim's permanent mod on herself (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/citlali/cons.go` line 66, `buffSelf`), which `getPassiveBonuses` reads from her constellation count, so it needs no status. Gcsim gives the active member a further 250 while the Opal Shield or the Blessing stands (`cons.go` line 68, `buffOther`). That share is not built: it is a buff on another member gated on the field and on a condition that changes each step, which a passive bonus does not reach, and its Opal Shield half waits on the shield the kit does not hold.
- **The strikes land 10 frames after their hitmarks.** gcsim queues each strike with a 10-frame travel (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/citlali/attack.go` lines 40-42 and 69-70, and the charged attack's too, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/citlali/charge.go` lines 31-33), so each lands at its hitmark plus 10, as the bow kits and Ororon do.
- **Her strikes and charged attack take the wiki's poise.** gcsim gives them none (its citlali package has no `PoiseDMG`), so her strikes take 8.2, 7.3 and 10.1 and her charged attack 80, from the wiki's Advanced Properties (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Citlali_Talents.json`). A recording of the game's poise on them would settle it.
- **Her skill's press enters the Blessing with its points plus 24, for 20 seconds, and her burst gives 24 points.** gcsim queues the entry 18 frames after the press (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/citlali/skill.go` lines 61 and 70) and gives the burst's 24 at its delayed start, 115 frames in (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/citlali/burst.go` lines 70-73); the Blessing's points are capped at 100 (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/citlali/citlali.go` line 33); the kit applies both when each action starts (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/citlaliKit.ts`). The burst's 24 go through `generateKitNightsoulPoints`, so the generation event reaches the team's fields as gcsim's `GeneratePoints` raises it. With no Opal Fire state, the Blessing holds its points, as gcsim's does.
- **The Opal Fire is a field ticking each frame, drained and struck on gcsim's frames.** `tryEnterOpalFireState` (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/citlali/skill.go` 107–122) starts it in the Blessing at 50 points or more, unless it is on already, and it is entered again by each gain of points (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/citlaliKit.ts`). gcsim queues its drain 6 frames after it starts and its first Itzpapa hit 59 frames after (`itzpapaInterval`, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/citlali/skill.go` 14), so the field counts frames from the one it takes effect. It drains 0.8 points every six frames (`nightsoulPointReduceTask`, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/citlali/skill.go` 124–149) and ends at none. The Blessing holds on past that, as gcsim's `ConsumePoints` (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/template/nightsoul/nightsoul.go` 141–151) does not exit the Blessing at zero.
- **Itzpapa's Frostfall Storm strikes every enemy in its circle and applies its Cryo on every other hit.** Its circle is 6 metres round the player (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/citlali/skill.go` 173), so the kit reads the circle round the body at each storm. Its Cryo gauge sits under the Frostfall group's cooldown, restarted 1.5 seconds after a hit started it, and applies on the first of each two hits (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/attacks/icd_groups.dm.go` 95 and 162). Its damage sequence holds one (line 229), so every storm deals its damage. The circle's height of 2 is the kit's own, since gcsim's circle takes a radius and a fan angle and no height (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/info/circle.go` line 17).
- **The Frostfall Storm's multiplier is proud group 10732's at index 4, 0.17024 at level 1 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`).** The builder reads the hit's element and internal cooldown tag from `itzpapaHitTask`. Its hitmark is 0, as gcsim queues each storm (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/citlali/skill.go` line 173, `QueueAttack(…, 0, 0, …)`), and the 59 frames are the storm's interval (`itzpapaInterval`, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/citlali/skill.go` lines 14 and 175), not its hitmark. Its area is a circle whose centre is a `.Pos()` call, which the builder cannot cite, so it carries the Unclassified cause until the builder reads it, as Layla's Shooting Star does. The record needs a publish of `10000107`, which names `10000074` beside it because Layla's record is owed too.
- **Her plunges are the proud group's 5, 6 and 7** (group 10731's indices 5 to 7 at level 1, as above). With no gcsim plunge file to time them (the citlali package holds none), the collision, low and high plunge land at the start of their action with a zero animation, as the Ororon kit's do (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/citlaliKit.ts`). A recording of her plunges landing at once would settle it; a later landing would need its frames.
