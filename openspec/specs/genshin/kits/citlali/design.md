# Citlali

Citlali's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createCitlaliKitBehaviour`, avatar 10000107: her three strikes, a charged attack, the hit of Obsidian Tzitzimitl, Ice Storm, and a collision, low and high plunge. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 10731, 10732 and 10739: the strikes, charged attack, stamina and plunges from the first, Obsidian Tzitzimitl and the skill's cooldown from the second, and Ice Storm, the burst's cooldown and its energy cost from the third. The charged attack costs 50 stamina, and Ice Storm costs 60 energy.

## Not yet built

- **The burst's 24 points come at the press, not 115 frames after it.** gcsim queues them 115 frames after the cast (`burst.go` line 72), and the kit gives them at the press. That stands until the burst's timing is settled. Points gained with no Blessing are also spent by the shared engine on its next step (`stepKitNightsoul`, the points with no blessing left), where gcsim's `GeneratePoints` keeps them (`nightsoul.go` line 129, no blessing check). A burst cast outside the Blessing and then a skill press therefore gives the skill 24 points here and 48 in gcsim. That is the engine's to settle, not Citlali's.
- **Citlali's Constellations 1, 2, 4 and 6 are not built.** Each is named with its wait in `citlaliKit.ts`. Constellation 2 also cuts the Hydro and Pyro RES by 0.4, not 0.2, and waits with it.

## Decisions

- **Ascension 4 gives points on each Nightsoul burst on the team, and flat damage from Elemental Mastery.** gcsim's `a4` subscribes to `OnNightsoulBurst` for the whole team (`asc.go` 57 to 64), so the kit answers the team's `NightsoulBurst` event whichever member is on the field. Its flat damage (`a4Dmg`, `asc.go` 66 to 76) is 12 times Elemental Mastery on Ice Storm and 0.9 times it on each Frostfall Storm, each priced by the hit's `additiveBaseDamageBonus` from its striker's Elemental Mastery.
- **Ascension 1 answers a Melt or Frozen during the Blessing with sixteen points and a RES cut, from anyone on the team.** gcsim's `a1Hook` subscribes to `OnMelt` and `OnFrozen` for any enemy (`asc.go` lines 15 to 21) and does nothing outside the Blessing (line 28). It gives the points once each eight seconds, on the status `citlali-a1-ns-icd` (lines 32 to 38), and lowers the struck enemy's Hydro and Pyro RES by 0.2 for twelve seconds (lines 40 to 54). The kit's `onKitEvent` answers each `ReactionTriggered` event whose reactions include a Melt or Frozen, whoever on the team struck, and keeps the eight-second timer as a status on Citlali and the RES cut as an enemy status under one id, so a second Melt restarts the cut. gcsim counts the twelve seconds with hitlag (`NewBaseWithHitlag`). The kit's enemy statuses count plain seconds, since neither the kit nor the enemy services model hitlag, so that gap is the engine's to close.
- **The Opal Shield comes 37 frames after the press, with the Elemental Mastery Citlali holds then.** gcsim's `addShield` (`skill.go` line 72) queues it 37 frames after the press, and `shield.go` lines 13 to 28 give it a Cryo health of its share of Elemental Mastery plus its flat health, for 20 seconds. Those are proud group 10732's indexes 1, 2 and 3. The record holds them as references to that group, which `createKitRecordResolver` resolves at the skill's talent level (`readCharacterKit`'s `talentLevels`, level 1 by default): at level 1 they are 5.76 times Elemental Mastery and 1386.7 flat, matching gcsim's `shieldEM` and `shieldFlat` at level 1, and at level 15 they grow to 13.68 and 4380.0. The kit schedules it with `scheduleKitTask` and reads Citlali's Elemental Mastery from the team's combatants when it comes, as gcsim reads her stat then.
- **Ice Storm summons a Spirit Vessel skull on each of the first three enemies within 7 metres, and each lands 205 to 235 frames after the press.** `burst.go` queues each skull on the first three enemies its 7-metre circle reaches at the press, in the order the world lists them, and draws its delay there: the Ice Storm's hitmark, 118 frames read from the built burst hit, plus the record's 57 frames plus 30 to 60 frames drawn from the world's random source (`burst.go` lines 27 to 29 and 79 to 88). When the skull lands, Citlali gains three points, and it strikes each living enemy within 3.5 metres of its enemy, the circle centred where that enemy stands then, since gcsim reads `enemy.Pos()` when its task runs and not at the press. Its multiplier is proud group 10739's index 1, its element Cryo, and its cooldown tag `CitlaliSpiritVessel`, which has no group of its own. It is queued as a passive hit, which carries no attack tag, where gcsim's skull is an elemental burst. The 7-metre reach and the three-enemy limit are gcsim's literals, not record values.
- **The strikes land 10 frames after their hitmarks.** gcsim queues each strike with a 10-frame travel, so each lands at its hitmark plus 10, as the bow kits and Ororon do.
- **Her strikes and charged attack give no poise.** gcsim gives them none, so `citlaliKit.ts` stands them at 0, as Ororon's do. A recording of the game's poise on them would settle it.
- **Her skill's press enters the Blessing with its points plus 24, for 20 seconds, and her burst gives 24 points.** gcsim queues the entry 18 frames after the press (`skill.go`) and gives the burst's 24 115 frames after its press (`burst.go`); the kit applies the entry when the skill starts and the burst's 24 at the press, a gap named under Not yet built. The burst's 24 go through `generateKitNightsoulPoints`, so the generation event reaches the team's fields as gcsim's `GeneratePoints` raises it. With no Opal Fire state, the Blessing holds its points, as gcsim's does.
- **The Opal Fire is a field ticking each frame, drained and struck on gcsim's frames.** `tryEnterOpalFireState` (`skill.go` 107–122) starts it in the Blessing at 50 points or more, unless it is on already, and it is entered again by each gain of points. gcsim queues its drain 6 frames after it starts and its first Itzpapa hit 59 frames after (`itzpapaInterval`, `skill.go` 14), so the field counts frames from the one it takes effect. It drains 0.8 points every six frames (`nightsoulPointReduceTask`, 124–149) and ends at none. The Blessing holds on past that, as gcsim's `ConsumePoints` (`nightsoul.go` 141–151) does not exit the Blessing at zero. It also ends with the Blessing, in the step the Blessing runs out, as gcsim's `exitNightsoul` ends it (`citlali/skill.go` 93–98): the step runs the Blessing's seconds down before its fields tick, so the field sees them at none. A Blessing ended by a swap is seen on the field's next tick.
- **Itzpapa's Frostfall Storm strikes every enemy in its circle and applies its Cryo on every other hit.** Its circle is 6 metres round the player (`skill.go` 173), so the kit reads the circle round the body at each storm. Its Cryo gauge sits under the Frostfall group's cooldown, restarted 1.5 seconds after a hit started it, and applies on the first of each two hits (`icd_groups.dm.go` 95 and 162). Its damage sequence holds one (line 229), so every storm deals its damage. The circle's height of 2 is the kit's own, since gcsim's circle has none.
- **The Frostfall Storm's multiplier is proud group 10732's at index 4, 0.17024 at level 1.** The builder does not read `itzpapaHitTask` yet, so the hit's other values carry the Unclassified cause until it does, as Layla's Shooting Star does. The record needs a publish of `10000107`, which names `10000074` beside it because Layla's record is owed too.
- **Her plunges are the proud group's 5, 6 and 7.** With no gcsim plunge file to time them, the collision, low and high plunge land at the start of their action with a zero animation, as the Ororon kit's do. A recording of her plunges landing at once would settle it; a later landing would need its frames.
