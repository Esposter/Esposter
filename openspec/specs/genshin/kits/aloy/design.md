# Aloy

Aloy's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createAloyKitBehaviour`, avatar 10000062 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`, named Aloy in `~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/TextMap/TextMap_MediumEN.json`): four strikes, a fully charged shot, a collision and two plunges (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Aloy_Talents.json`, Rapid Fire), and Freeze Bomb (the same file, Frozen Wilds) with its six Chillwater Bomblets, as the same page's Gameplay Notes give them (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Aloy_Talents.json`), each of which adds a coil stack, four of them giving Cryo infusion for Rushing Ice's ten seconds (the same file, Frozen Wilds). Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 6231, 6232 and 6239 (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/aloyKit.ts`); the skill's cooldown, and the burst's cooldown and energy cost, are read from the same groups. Freeze Bomb's circle, the bomblets' and Prophecies of Dawn's are priced by [the hitbox offset rule](../README.md) (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/aloyKit.ts`). Her coil stacks are not cleared 30 seconds after she leaves the field, since a kit's status holds no field state. Her Ascension 1 and Ascension 4 passives are built as Rushing Ice's ATK and Cryo DMG Bonus (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/aloy/asc.go`, lines 9 and 31 to 32). Her constellations are not built, and the reason the kit's code gives is open, see [not built](#not-built). Her Coil and Rushing Ice normal attack bonuses are not built, as a normal attack's damage bonus has no hook in the kit yet (gcsim's `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/aloy/skill.go:161` to `:163` gives the Rushing Ice bonus). Her poise is the wiki's (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Aloy_Talents.json`).

## Decisions

- **Aloy's strikes take the wiki's 7.65 for each hit of the first, 14.7 for the second, 18 for the third and 22.38 for the fourth, her fully charged shot the wiki's 20, her burst the wiki's 200, her collision the wiki's 10 and her plunges the wiki's 100 high and 50 low; Freeze Bomb's own hit takes the wiki's 120, and its Chillwater Bomblets the wiki's 50 each (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Aloy_Talents.json`).** The poise is the Rapid Fire page's rows for the strikes, the fully charged shot, the collision and the plunges, the Prophecies of Dawn page's for the burst, and the Frozen Wilds page's for Freeze Bomb and its bomblets. The multipliers are the dump's groups at level 1 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), which match the wiki's (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Aloy_Talents.json`): group 6231 at 0.2112, 0.2376, 0.4312, 0.528 and 0.65648 for the strikes and 1.24 for the shot, group 6232 at 1.776 and 0.4 for Freeze Bomb and its bomblets, and group 6239 at 3.592 for the burst. Her plunge reaches and 0.4-second landing are the stand-ins other kits use.
- **Each Chillwater Bomblet adds one coil stack however many enemies it strikes, and Freeze Bomb and each bomblet give 1U of Cryo (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Aloy_Talents.json`, the Frozen Wilds gauge rows).** gcsim runs its coil callback for each enemy a bomblet strikes, but the callback's 6-frame internal cooldown, `coilICDExpiry` set at `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/aloy/skill.go:143` and checked at `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/aloy/skill.go:135`, holds back every enemy after the first at that frame, so a bomblet counts one stack. `packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/aloyKit.ts` counts each bomblet's landing once, and reads 1U as Ganyu's Cryo hits do: the Frozen Wilds page gives 1U on the Freeze Bomb and Bomblet rows, and gcsim gives the bomb no gauge.
- **Freeze Bomb's hit count is `DiffersFromSource`, and hits 3 to 6 are held.** The seven hits are one bomb and the six bomblets the wiki's Gameplay Notes give (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Aloy_Talents.json`, Frozen Wilds). The bomblets' loop at `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/aloy/skill.go:96` runs to "bomblets", defaulted to 2 at `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/aloy/skill.go:52`, and no reader counts a loop's hits, so only the first two bomblets' hitmarks are read, from `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/aloy/skill.go:101`, "skillRelease+travel+delay+((i+1)*6)" evaluated per bomblet. Hits 3 to 6 are held as `DiffersFromSource` overlay values at the frames that same line gives for bomblet i, counted from zero, 20 + 5 + (i+1)×6: 43, 49, 55 and 61 frames, each with the bomblets' circle and multiplier. The count of seven stays hand-set for the same reason.
- **Aloy's Ascension 1 and 4 passives run from the start of Rushing Ice.** From Ascension 1 Rushing Ice raises the ATK of the deployed team by 8%, and Aloy's own by 8% more, for its ten seconds (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/aloy/asc.go:9`'s comment, with the buff at `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/aloy/asc.go:22` on Rushing Ice's `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/aloy/skill.go:30` duration of 600 frames). From Ascension 4 her Cryo DMG Bonus rises 3.5% each second, as gcsim's stacks do (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/aloy/asc.go:46`), to 35% at the tenth (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/aloy/asc.go:43` to `:44`); each step restarts one buff that ends with Rushing Ice.

## Not built

- **The constellations are not built, and the reason is open.** The kit's code says the game cannot activate them, but the dump's talents `621` to `626` carry no parameters, and the cached wiki holds no constellation text, so no source confirms the reason.
- **The Coil and Rushing Ice normal attack bonuses are not built.** gcsim's `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/aloy/skill.go:161` to `:163` gives the Rushing Ice bonus at the skill's level, and the kit has no normal attack damage bonus to hold it.
