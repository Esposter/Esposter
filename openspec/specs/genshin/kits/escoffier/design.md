# Escoffier

Escoffier's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createEscoffierKitBehaviour`, avatar 10000112 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`): her three normal attacks, a charged attack, and a low, high and collision plunge, with Low-Temperature Cooking as her skill, Scoring Cuts as her burst, and Rehab Diet from Ascension 1 (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Escoffier_Talents.json`). Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 11231, 11232 and 11239 of the dump (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), the skill's press, tick, Surging Blade and interval among them and the burst's hit and heal (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/escoffier/burst.go:35`). The charged attack costs 25 stamina (the wiki's stamina 25, `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Escoffier_Talents.json`, and the dump's 11231 at index 5), the skill cools for 15 seconds (the wiki's press CD 15s, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/escoffier/skill.go:26`), and the burst costs 60 energy, cooling for 15 seconds (the dump's 11239 at indices 4 and 3, and the wiki's CD 15s and energy cost 60, `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Escoffier_Talents.json`).

## Not yet built

A combatant holds the deployed team's elements, which a status and a hit read as the team stands. The framework holds no timed effect on another member's hit, so the parts that depend on one wait on the hooks named below.

- **Constellation 1 is built.** Her skill and burst each open a 15-second window on an Ascension 4 Escoffier while the deployed team holds only Cryo and Hydro members, as gcsim (MIT, main) `escoffier/cons.go`'s `c1` does (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/escoffier/cons.go:15`, `:49`); the cached wiki gives Constellation 1 only its title, Pre-Dinner Dance for Your Taste Buds, so its wording is not checked. In the window each deployed member's Cryo strikes get 60% Crit DMG: her own through `getStrikeCriticalDamageBonus`, a teammate's through `getPartyStrikeBonus`, read beside the element's crit damage as each hit is priced. Two divergences from gcsim: the team is checked on the cast, where gcsim checks it once at init; and the stellar reactions are not built.
- **Constellation 2 waits on the party-hit hook.** Cold Dishes adds 240% of her ATK to each Cryo normal, charged, plunge, Elemental Skill or Burst hit of another member while Frosty Parfait holds, for five hits (`cons.go:18`, `:110`, `:127`). A hit reading another member's buff is not built.
- **Constellation 4 waits on the seeded-roll hook.** Its six extra Rehab Diet ticks and their 1.0-share heal roll on the character's Crit Rate, which the framework does not roll (`cons.go:20-21`, `asc.go:26`).
- **Constellation 6 waits on the party-hit hook.** Special-Grade Frozen Parfait strikes the target once every half second (`cons.go:28`, `:198`), when the active character's normal, charged or plunge hit lands while Frosty Parfait holds. A hit reading the skill's status on another member is not built.
- **Arkhe waits on the Arkhe hook.** The Surging Blade's Arkhe application is gcsim's TODO (`skill.go:75`), so the blade deals its damage and applies no Arkhe, as gcsim gives it.
- **Her skill's cooldown delay waits on the cooldown-delay hook.** gcsim starts the 15 seconds 22 frames after the press (`skill.go:95`). The framework starts every skill's cooldown with the skill, so the cooldown here runs 22 frames early.

## Decisions

- **The plunges are the airborne ones.** gcsim plays her plunges as airborne moves, so the kit takes their frames: the low plunge at 41 frames of a 76-frame animation, the high at 43 of 77, and the collision at 35 (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/escoffier/plunge.go:21-23`, `:38`).
- **The strikes, the charged attack and the collision take the wiki's poise, which the kit as built does not carry.** gcsim gives none to a normal attack, the charged attack or the collision (no `PoiseDMG` in `attack.go` or `charge.go`, and none in the collision at `plunge.go`), and the wiki's Kitchen Skills rows give 47.95, 44.27 and 34.11 for the normal attacks, 120 for the charged attack and 25 for the collision. The record carries no poise for any of them, so the poise is open.
- **The Surging Blade's interval is checked at its 83rd frame.** gcsim's task checks the 10-second interval 83 frames after the press, and queues the blade and starts the interval there only if it is not running (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/escoffier/skill.go` lines 18, 25 and 69 to 93). The kit schedules that check 83 frames after the press, starts the interval there, and lands the blade with its hitmark at 0 seconds. The record has no blade hitmark, so the kit sets it: gcsim's queued attack has zero delay (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/escoffier/skill.go` lines 89 and 90), so the blade hits at 0 seconds.
- **Scoring Cuts' heal is one tick of a field.** gcsim heals at frame 97 (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/escoffier/burst.go:15`) with no hit of its own, which the framework does not place, so the burst sets a field whose one tick heals the active character, as Diona's Signature Mix heals, and a second tick is kept past its end.
- **Rehab Diet is a field from the burst.** gcsim starts it from the burst on Ascension 1 or above, nine ticks from 157 frames every 58.5 frames (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/escoffier/asc.go:11-15`, nine at `asc.go:13`), each healing the active character for 1.3824 of her ATK, so the kit starts the same field from the burst's start.
- **Ascension 4's RES shred is set on each hit from the team as it stands.** gcsim takes 0, 5, 10, 15 or 55% off the enemy's Cryo and Hydro RES (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/escoffier/asc.go:19`) for 0 to 4 Hydro or Cryo members the team holds, herself included, for 12 seconds (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/escoffier/asc.go:16`), on each enemy her skill and burst hit (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/escoffierKit.ts`). Each hit's status reads its combatant's `deployedElements`, and the burst's hit is set through the burst behaviour's `hits`.
- **Frosty Parfait's ticks are a summon.** Its 21 ticks (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/escoffier/skill.go:19`) land on the primary target 5 frames after each tick (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/escoffier/skill.go:42-46`), as hits of a summon placed where she cast it, each priced as the target's reach and a circle of radius 1.5 (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/escoffier/skill.go:137`), the way the framework prices a hit on a target.
