# Kinich

Kinich's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createKinichKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000101 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`): his three strikes (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/attack.go` line 18) outside the Nightsoul Blessing, a charged attack of three thrusts (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/charge.go` line 16), a plunge collision and two plunges, the burst's hit with the Ajaw's Dragon Breath, and his skill's blessing with its two Loop Shots and blind spot. His multipliers are read from proud skill groups 10131, 10132 and 10139 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), matching gcsim's to four decimal places (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/zz_kinich.dm.go` lines 38, 145, 163 and 199 hold 0.98986, 1.862889, 0.5728 and 1.34, among them). Skill cooldown 18 seconds (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/skill.go` line 17); burst cooldown 18 seconds (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/burst.go` line 60) with an energy cost of 70 (the wiki's Hail to the Almighty Dragonlord page, `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kinich_Talents.json`); charged attack stamina 50 (the Nightsun Style page's `stamina=50`, `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kinich_Talents.json`).

## Decisions

- **His strikes and charged attack are boxes centred on the body, as gcsim has them.** Each strike is a box offset along the body's line by -0.7, -1 and 0 metres, 3 by 3, 2 by 3.9 and 2.2 by 4.3 metres (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/attack.go` lines 22-23, 95-102), and each is blunt with gcsim's poise of 131, 117 and 160 (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/attack.go` lines 24 and 85; the Nightsun Style page's blunt rows, `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kinich_Talents.json`, and poise 131, 117 and 160 agree). The charged attack's three thrusts are boxes of 3 by 5 metres offset 0.45 metres, at 71, 95 and 119 frames, each of 40 poise (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/charge.go` lines 16, 35 and 45-52).
- **The burst's first hit is a circle of radius 4 on the primary target, at 161 frames.** It is priced as the targeting reach plus its own radius, and deals Dendro at one unit of gauge, as gcsim's burst does (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/burst.go` lines 21, 53 and 57; the wiki's Hail to the Almighty Dragonlord page, `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kinich_Talents.json`, gives the Skill DMG's gauge as 1U).
- **The Ajaw's six lasers are drawn as the burst is cast.** The burst's start casts the Ajaw, a summon at the body dealt as Elemental Burst DMG (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/burst.go` line 87), whose Dragon Breath lasers land from 253 frames, each next 145 or 150 frames on, the two drawn evenly from the world's seeded random source. gcsim draws each interval as the laser before it fires (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/burst.go` lines 14, 22 and 101, `Rand.Intn(2)`), and stops at the sixth (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/burst.go` line 80, step 7); drawing them at the cast deals the same lasers on the same odds. Each is a box of 1 by 10 metres on the primary target (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/burst.go` line 99), priced as the targeting area's radius plus the circle to the box's far corner, of 1U Dendro (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/burst.go` lines 89 and 92, and the wiki's gauge of 1U, `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kinich_Talents.json`) under the burst's internal cooldown, as gcsim gives them, with the wiki's 35 poise (the Hail to the Almighty Dragonlord page's Dragon Breath DMG row, `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kinich_Talents.json`).
- **His plunges are provisional.** gcsim has no plunge file for Kinich, so his plunges borrow the kit constants' reach, each hits as its action starts, and the seconds of 0.4 are a placeholder. The collision's 35 poise is the wiki's row on his talent page, `Plunging Attack Collision` (the Nightsun Style page), and the plunges' 200 high and 150 low are the wiki's `High Plunge` and `Low Plunge` rows, their timings borrowed from gcsim's `bennett/plunge.go` (hitmarks 36 and 38 frames, line 21 onwards). Each is listed on the [roadmap](../../../../changes/genshin-roadmap/tasks.md) as a yes-or-no question for a recording.

- **The blessing's Loop Shots are a stance, and they give the blessing its points.** The skill's press enters the blessing with a stance of two actions, each two hits on the circle of radius 0.5 round the player, at hitmarks 30 and 38 frames for the first and 31 and 38 for the second, each 53 frames long, as gcsim (MIT) `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/attack.go` gives them (hitmarks at line 29, animations at lines 57-65, the circle at line 131). Each action gains three points as it starts, as `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/skill.go`'s loopShotGenerateNSPoints does (lines 172-177). The stance ends when the blessing does or Kinich swaps out (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/kinichKit.ts`). A blessing has no normal-attack variant in the kit, so the stance is the route. Its values come from gcsim through `addGcsimLoopShotCandidates`, as Xilonen's Blade Roller does; its animation and circle are literals gcsim writes inside calls rather than as constants, so they are cited by line.
- **The blind spot is placed from the press's target and the body's position as the press starts.** gcsim places it a quarter turn either side of Kinich's bearing from the enemy he is facing, on a side drawn from the random source (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/skill.go` lines 189-192, a 90-degree turn either way), and gives four points when he stands in its thirty-five-degree window (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/attack.go` lines 38 and 153). It appears 30 frames after the press, as entering the blessing takes the second of `blindSpotAppearanceDelays` (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/skill.go` lines 32 and 82). The kit does not model gcsim's virtual angle, so the blind spot is placed from the enemy the press turns the body to within the skill's targeting area, and from the body's real position. Its circle is the chord of the window at that distance, it appears 30 frames after the press, and it ticks each frame after that until Kinich's body stands in it, once. Only Kinich's own body fills it, so a character who swapped in gives nothing (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/attack.go` lines 150 to 155). With no enemy in reach at the press, no blind spot is placed.

## Not built

- **The press inside the blessing waits on the hook.** The press (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/skill.go` lines 74 to 83, which queue no hit) starts the blessing with no points, which gain two a second to 20 for ten and a sixth seconds, as gcsim (MIT) sets them (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/skill.go` lines 77 and 179-187: one point every 30 frames; `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kinich/kinich.go` line 42 the maximum of 20), and his Loop Shots replace his normal attacks in it. A press in the blessing is the Scalespiker Cannon, which waits on the hook, so it does nothing.
- **The Scalespiker Cannon's spending waits on the Nightsoul hook.** The Cannon spends all of the points, and the cooldown bypass and points gate it needs wait on the hook, along with the points his Ascension 1 and 4 read.
- **Kinich's Ascension 1 (Desolation's points), Ascension 4 (Hunter's Experience) and Constellations 2 and 4 wait on the Nightsoul hook.** Each of them reads the Nightsoul Blessing, its points or the Desolation status a Scalespiker Cannon leaves on an enemy. Constellation 1 instead needs a movement speed attribute, which no attribute carries (gcsim's `cons.go` line 25 comment says its Movement SPD is not implemented), and Cannon CRIT DMG (gcsim's `cons.go` line 39 gives it as `CD`), which the kit does not build. Constellation 6 needs its bounce hit, which the kit does not build.
