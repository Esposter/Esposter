# Kinich

Kinich's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createKinichKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000101: his three strikes outside the Nightsoul Blessing, a charged attack of three thrusts, a plunge collision and two plunges, and the burst's hit with the Ajaw's Dragon Breath. His multipliers are read from proud skill groups 10131, 10132 and 10139, matching gcsim's to four decimal places. Skill cooldown 18 seconds; burst cooldown 18 seconds with an energy cost of 70; charged attack stamina 50.

## Decisions

- **His strikes and charged attack are boxes centred on the body, as gcsim has them.** Each strike is a box offset along the body's line by -0.7, -1 and 0 metres, 3 by 3, 2 by 3.9 and 2.2 by 4.3 metres, and each is blunt with gcsim's poise of 131, 117 and 160. The charged attack's three thrusts are boxes of 3 by 5 metres offset 0.45 metres, at 71, 95 and 119 frames, each of 40 poise.
- **The burst's first hit is a circle of radius 4 on the primary target, at 161 frames.** It is priced as the targeting reach plus its own radius, and deals Dendro at one unit of gauge, as gcsim's burst does.
- **The Ajaw's six lasers are drawn as the burst is cast.** The burst's start casts the Ajaw, a summon at the body dealt as Elemental Burst DMG, whose Dragon Breath lasers land from 253 frames, each next 145 or 150 frames on, the two drawn evenly from the world's seeded random source. gcsim draws each interval as the laser before it fires, and stops at the sixth; drawing them at the cast deals the same lasers on the same odds. Each is a box of 1 by 10 metres on the primary target, priced as the targeting area's radius plus the circle to the box's far corner, of 1U Dendro under the burst's internal cooldown and no poise, which gcsim does not give.
- **His plunges are provisional.** gcsim has no plunge file for Kinich, so his plunges borrow the kit constants' reach, each hits as its action starts, and the seconds of 0.4 are a placeholder. The collision's 25 poise and the plunges' 100 and 150 are the wiki's standard for a plunge, not measured for Kinich. Each is listed on the [roadmap](../../../../changes/genshin-roadmap/tasks.md) as a yes-or-no question for a recording.

## Not built

- **The Nightsoul Blessing waits on the Nightsoul hook.** The skill's press enters the blessing, in which his Loop Shot and Scalespiker Cannon replace the skill and his normal attacks, and his charged attack and plunges are unavailable. The blessing is a stance (`KitStance`), whose hits would each name gcsim's attack tag, but it runs on Nightsoul points the framework does not hold yet, so the skill's press is a placeholder that deals nothing and spends its 18-second cooldown. Until the hook lands, his normal attacks are the three strikes above outside the blessing only.
- **The Nightsoul points wait on the same hook.** They are generated by his Loop Shots and his blessing's time, spent by the Scalespiker Cannon, and read by his Ascension 1 and 4 and his constellations, so each of them waits too.
- **Kinich's Ascension 1 (Desolation's points), Ascension 4 (Hunter's Experience) and Constellations 2, 4 and 6 wait on the Nightsoul hook.** Each of them reads the Nightsoul Blessing, its points or the Desolation status a Scalespiker Cannon leaves on an enemy. Constellation 1 instead needs a movement speed attribute, which no attribute carries, so it waits on that.
