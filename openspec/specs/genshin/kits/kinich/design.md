# Kinich

Kinich's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createKinichKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000101: his three strikes outside the Nightsoul Blessing, a charged attack of three thrusts, a plunge collision and two plunges, the burst's hit with the Ajaw's Dragon Breath, and his skill's blessing with its two Loop Shots and blind spot. His multipliers are read from proud skill groups 10131, 10132 and 10139, matching gcsim's to four decimal places. Skill cooldown 18 seconds; burst cooldown 18 seconds with an energy cost of 70; charged attack stamina 50.

## Decisions

- **His strikes and charged attack are boxes centred on the body, as gcsim has them.** Each strike is a box offset along the body's line by -0.7, -1 and 0 metres, 3 by 3, 2 by 3.9 and 2.2 by 4.3 metres, and each is blunt with gcsim's poise of 131, 117 and 160. The charged attack's three thrusts are boxes of 3 by 5 metres offset 0.45 metres, at 71, 95 and 119 frames, each of 40 poise.
- **The burst's first hit is a circle of radius 4 on the primary target, at 161 frames.** It is priced as the targeting reach plus its own radius, and deals Dendro at one unit of gauge, as gcsim's burst does.
- **The Ajaw's six lasers are drawn as the burst is cast.** The burst's start casts the Ajaw, a summon at the body dealt as Elemental Burst DMG, whose Dragon Breath lasers land from 253 frames, each next 145 or 150 frames on, the two drawn evenly from the world's seeded random source. gcsim draws each interval as the laser before it fires, and stops at the sixth; drawing them at the cast deals the same lasers on the same odds. Each is a box of 1 by 10 metres on the primary target, priced as the targeting area's radius plus the circle to the box's far corner, of 1U Dendro under the burst's internal cooldown and no poise, which gcsim does not give.
- **His plunges are provisional.** gcsim has no plunge file for Kinich, so his plunges borrow the kit constants' reach, each hits as its action starts, and the seconds of 0.4 are a placeholder. The collision's 25 poise and the plunges' 100 and 150 are the wiki's standard for a plunge, not measured for Kinich. Each is listed on the [roadmap](../../../../changes/genshin-roadmap/tasks.md) as a yes-or-no question for a recording.

- **The blessing's Loop Shots are a stance, and they give the blessing its points.** The skill's press enters the blessing with a stance of two actions, each two hits on the circle of radius 0.5 round the player, at hitmarks 30 and 38 frames for the first and 31 and 38 for the second, each 53 frames long, as gcsim (MIT) kinich/attack.go gives them. Each action gains three points as it starts, as kinich/skill.go's loopShotGenerateNSPoints does. The stance ends when the blessing does or Kinich swaps out. A blessing has no normal-attack variant in the kit, so the stance is the route. Its values come from gcsim through `addGcsimLoopShotCandidates`, as Xilonen's Blade Roller does; its animation and circle are literals gcsim writes inside calls rather than as constants, so they are cited by line.
- **The blind spot is placed from the press's target and the body's position as the press starts.** gcsim places it a quarter turn either side of Kinich's bearing from the enemy he is facing, on a side drawn from the random source, and gives four points when he stands in its thirty-five-degree window (kinich/attack.go lines 38 and 153). The kit does not model gcsim's virtual angle, so the blind spot is placed from the enemy the press turns the body to within the skill's targeting area, and from the body's real position. Its circle is the chord of the window at that distance, it appears 30 frames after the press, and it ticks each frame after that until Kinich's body stands in it, once. Only Kinich's own body fills it, so a character who swapped in gives nothing. With no enemy in reach at the press, no blind spot is placed.

- **The Scalespiker Cannon is the blessing's skill, and sets no cooldown of its own.** The stance replaces the skill with the Cannon, which starts only with all twenty points, its `canStart` gate, and spends them all at its thirty-sixth frame, a frame after its tap releases, so the points read as Kinich's through the windup, as gcsim (MIT) kinich/skill.go's `Skill` and `ScalespikerCannon` do (lines 20, 33, 52 to 58 and 95 to 160). The skill's cooldown neither holds the press nor takes one from it, as the blessing's `isSkillCooldownWaived` and a zero cooldown multiplier give. Its tap's hit lands at its forty-eighth frame, thirty-five frames to the release, one to the spend and twelve of travel, on the circle of radius three round the primary target, and its blind spot appears at its forty-first frame. Its Dendro strike is at one unit of gauge, and it shares the Scalespiker group's internal cooldown, of 1.2 seconds, which gates its gauge to the first of every four hits.

## Not built

- **The held Cannon waits on a hold record in the stance.** A tap releases at thirty-five frames, and a held press at seventeen, then the hold, as gcsim's Scalespiker Cannon does (skill.go lines 33 and 116 to 120). A stance holds no hold record, so only the tap is built.
- **Kinich's Ascension 1 (Desolation's points), Ascension 4 (Hunter's Experience) and Constellations 2, 4 and 6 wait on the Nightsoul hook.** Each of them reads the Nightsoul Blessing, its points or the Desolation status a Scalespiker Cannon leaves on an enemy, and Constellation 2 also widens the Cannon's circle. Constellation 1 instead needs a movement speed attribute, which no attribute carries, so it waits on that.
