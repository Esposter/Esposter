# Gaming

Gaming's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createGamingKitBehaviour` (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/gamingKit.ts`), avatar 10000092 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`): four strikes (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/attack.go:24`), a charged attack, a collision and two plunges (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/plunge.go:68`, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/plunge.go:75`, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/plunge.go:118`), Bestial Ascent, whose cast strikes nothing of its own, and Suanni's Gilded Dance's one strike. His multipliers are read from proud skill groups 9231, 9232 and 9239, its cooldowns and the burst's energy cost among them (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`: group 9232's index 2 is Bestial Ascent's 6-second cooldown, and group 9239's indices 3 and 4 are the burst's 15-second cooldown and 60 energy). Ascension 4's Incoming Healing Bonus under half HP is built, and the other Ascension passives and Constellations 1, 4 and 6 are not. Constellation 2 is built: its ATK buff lasts five seconds from each overflow of his own healing (gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/cons.go:42`, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/cons.go:47`, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/cons.go:56`). Bestial Ascent's Charmed Cloudstrider plunge, which is the skill's whole damage, is not built either (gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/skill.go` queues no hit).

## Not yet built

- **Bestial Ascent's Charmed Cloudstrider plunge.** The skill's damage is the special plunge that follows it, with its radius, its particle and its poise, and Constellation 6's radius and crit. It waits on a plunge variant the skill arms, as `elementalBurstVariant` arms a burst: a kit has no plunge variant yet. Its hit lands at 32 frames, its poise is 150 (wiki Bestial Ascent, Advanced Properties), its particle has a 3-second cooldown (wiki Bestial Ascent, Gameplay Notes; gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/plunge.go`, `particleICD`), and its radius is 4 metres, or 6 from Constellation 6, which also adds 20% CRIT Rate and 40% CRIT DMG (gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/plunge.go` `specialPlunge`, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/cons.go` `c6`).
- **Suanni's Man Chai and the burst's status.** The status lasts twelve seconds (gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/burst.go`, `burstDuration`; wiki Suanni's Gilded Dance, Wushou Stance Duration 12s). The Man Chai it sends out resets Bestial Ascent's cooldown when it links back up with him, and the special plunge also sends one out while the status lasts and he has over half his HP (gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/burst.go` `queueManChai`, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/plunge.go`). Constellation 1 heals on that link, for 15% of his HP (gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/cons.go` `c1`). It waits on a kit status that a plunge's landing reads, and a cooldown reset on that status.
- **The heals and the Max HP drain.** Suanni's Gilded Dance heals 30% of Max HP at 36 frames (gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/burst.go`; wiki Suanni's Gilded Dance, Skill Healing 30% Max HP), Ascension 1 regains 1.5% of Max HP every 0.2 seconds for 0.8 seconds (gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/asc.go`, `a1Heal`), and the special plunge drains 15% of Max HP, to no less than 10% (gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/plunge.go`, `hpDrainThreshold`). The burst's heal needs nothing more than a one-tick field healing him through `changeKitMemberHealth`, while Ascension 1's regeneration and the drain follow the special plunge's hit and wait on it.
- **The passives.** Ascension 4's DMG bonus on the plunge (20% at half HP or more, gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/asc.go` `a4`), Constellation 4's energy from the plunge (2 energy on a 0.2-second cooldown, gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/cons.go` `makeC4CB`) and Constellation 6's crit (gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/cons.go` `c6`) wait on the passive hook, which reads the combatant and the HP share the party holds.

## Decisions

- **The charged attack is provisional.** gcsim has none for Gaming, since its charge is not implemented: `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/config.yml` names a `charge` action with no hit, and the package has no charge file. So it takes the timing of Ayato's claymore charge, the strike at 24 frames of 55 (gcsim `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/ayato/charge.go`, `chargeHitmark` and `chargeFrames`), and the table's index 5 (113.09% of ATK) and 6 (40 stamina), which group 9231 gives as 1.1309 and 40 and the wiki's Stellar Rend page gives as Charged Attack Final DMG 113.09, until a recording of the attack settles them. Its reach is not Ayato's, whose charge hits a 0.8-metre circle on the primary target, so the reach is open (see Open). Its one hit is the final slash at index 5, where index 4 is the spin's cyclic damage (group 9231 index 4 is 0.62522, the wiki's Charged Attack Cyclic DMG 62.52%), which the kit gives no hit of its own.
- **Bestial Ascent strikes nothing of its own.** gcsim's skill has no hit (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/skill.go`): its damage is the special plunge that follows it, which is not built, so the skill's animation (64 frames) and its 6-second cooldown, starting 4 frames in (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/skill.go:11`, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/skill.go:12`, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/skill.go:16`), are the kit's and its hits are none.
- **The burst's strike is one circle of radius 5 round the body**, at 60 frames, as gcsim queues it (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/burst.go:19`, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/burst.go:70`, a circle of radius 5). The burst's status, its Man Chai and its heal are not built, so the burst's cooldown and energy cost are the whole of its state.

- **Air of Prosperity's Incoming Healing Bonus is a passive bonus from Ascension 4, while he has under half his HP.** gcsim gives it as 20% of Incoming Healing Bonus below half HP (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/asc.go:54`, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/gaming/asc.go:59`). It reads the HP share the party holds through the passive hook (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/gamingKit.ts`). Its other clause, 20% more Plunging Attack DMG at half HP or more, waits on the plunge variant.
