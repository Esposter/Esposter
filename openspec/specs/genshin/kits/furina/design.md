# Furina

Furina's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createFurinaKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000089 (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/furinaKit.ts`): four strikes, a charged attack that turns her Arkhe, a collision and two plunges, Salon Solitaire and Let the People Rejoice. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 8931, 8932 and 8939 of the dump (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`). The charged attack's stamina is 8931's index 5 (20), the Arkhe hits' cooldowns are 8931's indices 10 and 11 (6 seconds each, the six seconds of `internal/characters/furina/attack.go:52`), the skill's cooldown is 8932's index 10 (20 seconds, the wiki's CD 20s), and the burst's cooldown and energy cost are 8939's indices 6 and 7 (15 seconds and 60, `internal/characters/furina/burst.go:178`; the wiki's CD 15s and energy cost 60). (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/furinaKit.ts`)

She starts in Ousia, a status she holds for as long as she is not in Pneuma. Each strike that lands opens her Arkhe's own hit, Spiritbreath Thorn in Ousia or Surging Blade in Pneuma, a normal attack's Hydro hit with no gauge (`internal/characters/furina/attack.go:65` for the Hydro element, `:66` for the zero Durability) that strikes 30 frames on (`internal/characters/furina/attack.go:83`), once within each one's six-second cooldown (`internal/characters/furina/attack.go:52`). The charged attack's circle strikes 33 frames in, or 22 frames in after a normal attack, as its 11-frame windup is taken then (`internal/characters/furina/charge.go:16`, `:32`, `:51-57`), and her Arkhe turns a frame after the strike is queued (`internal/characters/furina/charge.go:75`). While Salon Solitaire lasts, the other Arkhe's summons take over from the ones out, for the seconds it has left, as `internal/characters/furina/charge.go:55-70` queues the other Arkhe's summon on the switch while the skill status holds, and `internal/characters/furina/skill.go:183-190` stops the old summon's ticks by their source. (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/furinaKit.ts`)

Salon Solitaire in Ousia strikes a bubble round her at 18 frames (`internal/characters/furina/skill.go:26`, `:121`) and brings out Gentilhomme Usher, Surintendante Chevalmarin and Mademoiselle Crabaletta with it, each on gcsim's mean schedule (`internal/characters/furina/skill.go:34-45`; the random spread round the mean is not taken, below). As each attacks the primary target (`internal/characters/furina/skill.go:211`, `:247`, `:283`), it drains every member of the deployed team above half HP (`internal/characters/furina/skill.go:341`) by its share of that member's Max HP through `changeKitMemberHealth` (`internal/characters/furina/skill.go:351-356`), and its hit, a share of Furina's Max HP, lands on a circle round that enemy once its attack has travelled, a tenth harder for each member it drained (`internal/characters/furina/skill.go:198`). Chevalmarin's and Usher's hits count under internal cooldown tags of their own (`internal/characters/furina/skill.go:204`, `:239`), gcsim's Salon group of one in two over 30 seconds (`pkg/core/attacks/icd_groups.dm.go:107`, `:174`), and Crabaletta's under none (`internal/characters/furina/skill.go:275`). In Pneuma the skill brings out the Singer of Many Waters 10 frames in, which heals the character on the field by a flat amount and a share of her Max HP every two seconds (`internal/characters/furina/skill.go:98`, `:155-160`, `:49`). The skill lasts 30 seconds from its summons, as the skill table has it (8932's index 1; gcsim has 1736 frames, about 28.9 seconds, `internal/characters/furina/skill.go:21`), and a new one ends the summons of the one before (`internal/characters/furina/skill.go:123`). (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/furinaKit.ts`)

Let the People Rejoice strikes a circle round her at 98 frames (`internal/characters/furina/burst.go:21`, `:176`), a share of her Max HP, and its Fanfare starts at 95 frames (`internal/characters/furina/burst.go:174`) for 18 seconds, as the burst table has it (8939's index 1); gcsim's 18.2 seconds (`internal/characters/furina/burst.go:22`) is not taken. It ends the one before. Each change to a member's HP while it lasts, every `HealthChanged` event, gives a point of Fanfare for each 1% of that member's HP changed (8939's index 2), up to 300 (index 3). The first lands 6 frames on and opens a 35-frame window a frame later, and those within the window land as it closes, gcsim's estimate of the game's debounce (`internal/characters/furina/burst.go:25-26`, `:53-72`). Each point gives every member of the deployed team 0.07% DMG Bonus to every element and Physical, and 0.01% Incoming Healing Bonus (8939's indices 4 and 5, `~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), for the seconds the Fanfare has left. (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/furinaKit.ts`)

Ascension 1's Endless Waltz starts when another character's heal runs past full HP on the character on the field: every member of the deployed team regenerates 2% of their own Max HP every two seconds while the last such heal is under four seconds old (`internal/characters/furina/asc.go:24-45`, `:62`). Ascension 4 raises the Salon members' DMG by 0.7% for each 1,000 of her Max HP, up to 28%, and cuts the Singer's interval by 0.4% for each 1,000, up to 16% (`internal/characters/furina/asc.go:95-96`). Constellation 1 starts the Fanfare at 150 and raises its most by 100, Constellation 2 gives 250% more Fanfare from each change, and Constellation 4 gives her 4 energy as a Salon member's hit lands or the Singer heals, once each five seconds (`internal/characters/furina/burst.go:171`, `:79`, `:42`; `internal/characters/furina/cons.go:43-51`; `internal/characters/furina/skill.go:317`).
