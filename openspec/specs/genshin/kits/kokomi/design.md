# Kokomi

Kokomi's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createKokomiKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000054 (`~/Esposter/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`): three strikes, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, Bake-Kurage, and Nereid's Ascension with Ceremonial Garment (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Sangonomiya_Kokomi_Talents.json`). Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 5431, 5432 and 5439 (`~/Esposter/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), each level-1 row matching the wiki's scaling tables (see Sources). Bake-Kurage is a summon of its seven ripples and a field that heals the active character from her Max HP while it stands within their circle, for 12.5 seconds in gcsim (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kokomi/skill.go:31`), where the dump's group 5432 index 3 gives 12 (see Open) on a cooldown of 20 (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kokomi/skill.go:43`; the seven ticks at `skill.go:35`). Nereid's Ascension's hit deals its share of her Max HP (group 5439's index 0, 10.42%), and the garment it starts is a stance for ten seconds (group 5439's index 5, and `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kokomi/burst.go:57`): her normal attacks, charged attack and Bake-Kurage gain their Max HP share to damage, and her normal and charged attacks heal the party by a share of her Max HP and a flat amount (group 5439's indices 1 and 2, 0.81% and 77.04). The garment's heal rolls on each enemy an attack strikes, which gcsim does once per attack, and her strikes are priced on the primary target's reach: the strikes' 5-metre targeting plus the circle's radius, 5.7 for a strike and 8.5 for the charged attack, in the garment as out of it, where gcsim widens both while it stands. Her passives are built: Flawless Strategy, Tamakushi Casket and Song of Pearls (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Sangonomiya_Kokomi_Talents.json`). Constellation 4's garment speed and energy are built (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kokomi/burst.go:68` to `:76`, and `cons.go:48` to `:63`), and Constellations 1, 2 and 6 are not. The plunges' timings, the burst's energy cost (the table's 70, where gcsim consumes 57) and the open points above are listed under Provisional constants.

## Decisions

- **Kokomi's Ceremonial Garment is a stance that Nereid's Ascension starts as it is cast.** gcsim starts the garment in the burst itself (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kokomi/burst.go:57`, `Status.Add(burstKey, 10*60)`), so it stands from the cast, as the kit's own comment says she is robed as it is cast (`kokomiKit.ts` line 168). Its actions replace the kit's while it lasts, so the Max HP bonuses and the heals sit on the garment's own actions, and the kit's base actions carry neither. A swap ends the garment, whose status gcsim deletes (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kokomi/burst.go:164`).
- **The garment's normal attack and charged attack Max HP bonuses are the wiki's rows.** Normal Attack DMG Bonus at 4.84% and Charged Attack DMG Bonus at 6.78% of Max HP, which are proud group 5439 indices 3 and 4 (`~/Esposter/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), and the wiki's Nereid's Ascension rows `normalattackdmgbonus_1` and `chargedattackdmgbonus_1` (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Sangonomiya_Kokomi_Talents.json`). gcsim's `attack.go` and `charge.go` read no Max HP term, so no gcsim line gives them (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kokomi/`).
- **Bake-Kurage's garment bonus of 7.1% of Max HP is the wiki's.** It is proud group 5439 index 8 (0.070963, `~/Esposter/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`) and the wiki's `bake-kuragedmgbonus_1` (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Sangonomiya_Kokomi_Talents.json`), read as `garmentSkillMaxHealthBonus`. gcsim's `skill.go` reads no garment status, so no gcsim line gives it (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kokomi/skill.go`).
- **Kokomi's plunges' damage is the wiki's.** Their Low and High rows are 113.63 and 141.93% and the collision's 56.83%, which are proud group 5431 indices 5 to 7 (`~/Esposter/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`) and the wiki's `plungelow_1`, `plungehigh_1` and `plunge_1` (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Sangonomiya_Kokomi_Talents.json`). gcsim's kokomi folder has no plunge file (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kokomi/`), so their timing is not from gcsim and is listed under Provisional constants.
- **Kokomi's Constellation 4 energy is a garment normal attack's hit, on a 0.2-second cooldown on her.** Each garment normal attack that hits an enemy restores 0.8 energy to the striker, from the hit's own `onStrike` and not a stance check (`kokomiKit.ts` lines 51-63 and 152-156). gcsim's C4 callback (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kokomi/cons.go` lines 48-63) gives the same 0.8 on a 0.2-second cooldown during the burst, but with no attack-tag check, and it is attached to the charged attack as well as the normal attacks (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kokomi/charge.go:61`). The kit gives none on the charged attack, which is listed under Provisional constants.
- **Kokomi's Constellation 4 speed reads any stance she holds, and her garment is her only one.** The check is `findKitStance` on her effects (`kokomiKit.ts` lines 175-181), which for her is the garment; a second stance would need the check to name the garment. gcsim gives the same 10% Normal Attack SPD for ten seconds from the burst (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kokomi/burst.go` lines 68-76).

## Provisional constants

- **The plunges land at half a second.** gcsim's kokomi folder has no plunge file, so the half-second landing and the plunges' reaches are the kit's own choice, borrowed from the other kits. Open until a plunge source is read.
- **The burst costs 70 energy, where gcsim consumes 57.** The table's 70 is proud group 5439 index 7 and the wiki's `energycost=70`; gcsim's `burst.go` line 80 is `ConsumeEnergy(57)`. The kit follows the table. Open until the two agree.
- **The garment's heal rolls on each enemy, where gcsim heals once per attack.** The kit's garment heal is rolled on every enemy a strike hits (`kokomiKit.ts` line 79, `healPartyChance: () => 1` on each hit). gcsim's burst heal callback has a `done` flag set once per attack event (`burst.go` lines 113-127, attached to each attack at `attack.go` line 68 and to each charged attack at `charge.go` line 60). Open until the heal is once per attack.
- **Constellation 4's energy is not given on the charged attack.** gcsim's C4 callback is attached to the charged attack (`charge.go` line 61) and checks no attack tag (`cons.go` lines 48-63), so it gives 0.8 energy on a charged hit during the burst. The kit gives none on the charged attack, as its spec says. Open against gcsim.
- **The garment's reach is the base reach, where gcsim widens it.** The kit's garment actions keep the base hits' reach (`kokomiKit.ts` lines 152-164), so the strike is 5.7 metres and the charged attack 8.5 in and out of the garment. gcsim's `attack.go` lines 57-59 widen the strike's circle to 1.2 metres and `charge.go` lines 50-52 the charged attack's to 4 metres while the burst status stands, which is 6.2 and 9 metres. Open against gcsim.
- **Tamakushi Casket's refresh lands at the cast, where gcsim queues it 46 frames later.** The kit refreshes Bake-Kurage from the burst's start (`kokomiKit.ts` lines 168-170), while gcsim's burst queues `a1` at 46 frames (`Tasks.Add(c.a1, 46)`), when the summon's status must still stand (`asc.go` lines 26-36). The two differ only when Bake-Kurage ends within those 46 frames. Open against gcsim.

## Not built

- **Constellations 1, 2 and 6 are not built.** Constellation 1 needs the effect the garment's final combo attack unleashes, which the kit does not build (`kokomiKit.ts` lines 107-110, and `cons.go` lines 12-41 for gcsim's version). Constellation 2 needs her Healing Bonus for characters at 50% HP or less, which her heals do not read (`kokomiKit.ts` line 108, `burst.go` lines 130-135 for gcsim's version). Constellation 6 needs the Hydro DMG Bonus her heals grant for a short time, which the kit does not build (`kokomiKit.ts` line 110, `cons.go` lines 70-85).
