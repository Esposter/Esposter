# Varesa

Varesa's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createVaresaKitBehaviour`, avatar 10000111: her three strikes, a charged attack, Rush as her skill with two charges (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/varesa/varesa.go` line 35), Flying Kick as her burst, and a low, high and collision plunge, all as she plays them outside her Nightsoul Blessing. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 11131, 11132 and 11139 (`~/Esposter/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`). Her charged attack costs 50 stamina (group 11131, index 4, and the wiki's `stamina=50` in `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Varesa_Talents.json`), Rush cools for 9 seconds (group 11132, index 4, and gcsim's `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/varesa/skill.go` line 77), and Flying Kick costs 70 energy (group 11139, index 3, and gcsim's energy maximum at `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/varesa/varesa.go` line 30) with an 18-second cooldown (group 11139, index 2, and `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/varesa/burst.go` line 82).

## Not yet built

Her Fiery Passion forms, the apex state that opens Volcanic Kablam and the Follow-Up Strike wait on a skill variant and a Rush follow-up hook the kit lacks. Her Nightsoul points and the timed Blessing a full set enters are built (see Decisions).

- **Flying Kick's area is its circle on the target.** Its circle of radius 7.5 sits a metre ahead of the target (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/varesa/burst.go` line 76) and is priced at the targeting area's 15 metres plus both, 23.5 metres from her, as the one queue call firing its multiplier's variable gives it (`burst.go`, line 76).
- **The Fiery Passion forms need a skill variant.** Inside the blessing Rush is Fiery Passion Rush, a different multiplier and a 2-frame hitmark (`skill.go`, lines 19 and 64-68), and Flying Kick is Fiery Passion Flying Kick (`burst.go`, lines 68-71). The burst's form fits the kit's burst variant, which the kit can hold; Rush's needs a skill variant, which the record has no slot for.
- **Her plunge chain's apex state waits on the apex hook.** gcsim's plunges set an apex state (`plunge.go`, line 191), which opens Volcanic Kablam, the short burst that follows a plunge (`burst.go`, lines 44-45 and 92-125). The plunges built here give the Nightsoul points but not the apex state, so Volcanic Kablam is not built.
- **The Follow-Up Strike waits on a Rush follow-up hook.** After Rush, her charged attack lands at 11 frames (`charge.go`, line 21) and costs no stamina (`varesa.go`, lines 55-59), from a status Rush sets (`skill.go`, line 90). A kit holds no charged attack variant to swap in, so the charged attack built here is the one without the follow-up.
- **Her Ascension 1 and 4 and Constellations 1, 2, 4 and 6 wait on the apex and follow-up hooks.** Ascension 1 adds a share of her ATK to each plunge while a status holds, Ascension 4 stacks ATK on each Nightsoul burst, and the constellations read the apex state, Kablam or her plunges' energy, so none is built (Ascension 1 at `asc.go`, lines 34-46; Ascension 4 at lines 58-76; the constellations at `cons.go`, lines 14-106).

## Decisions

- **Her Nightsoul points gain on each action's start, and a full set enters the Blessing for 15 seconds.** Her skill's press gives 20 points, her high and low plunges 25 each, and her burst fills them to 40; a full set outside the Blessing enters it with those points, which gcsim's generatePlungeNightsoul does (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/varesa/varesa.go` lines 87 to 92), with the burst's fill at `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/varesa/burst.go` lines 63 to 66. Her skill's gain only adds points, as gcsim's does (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/varesa/skill.go` line 89). gcsim lands each plunge's points at its hitmark (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/varesa/plunge.go` line 189 for the charged high), and the kit applies them when the plunge starts, which the kit's plunge hooks give.
- **The strikes are gcsim's outside the blessing.** The first and second strikes are boxes and the third a circle, each centred as gcsim centres it (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/varesa/attack.go` lines 19 to 21 and 85 to 111), with a box priced as its far corner as the framework prices a box.
- **Her charged attack keeps its own internal cooldown.** gcsim gives it the tag `VaresaCombatCycle` (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/varesa/charge.go` line 61), so it counts apart from her normal attacks, as the framework's tags keep a character's normal attacks, skill and burst apart.
- **Strikes and plunges take the wiki's poise, and the charged attack takes gcsim's 80.** gcsim gives Varesa's normal attacks and plunges none, so each takes the wiki's value: 8.8, 7.5 and 10.6 for her three strikes, 5 for her collision, 100 for a high plunge and 50 for a low one (the By the Horns rows of the cached `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Varesa_Talents.json`); the charged attack's 80 is gcsim's own (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/varesa/charge.go` line 60), which that wiki row gives too.
- **The plunges are the airborne ones.** gcsim plays a high plunge from a charged attack at 37 frames and from the air at 38, and the low plunge at 37 from the air, so the kit takes the airborne frames for both: the high at 38 frames of a 77-frame animation, and the low at 37 of 75 (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/varesa/plunge.go` lines 35 to 36 and 81 to 91). The collision is at 31 frames, which gcsim sets 6 frames before the charged high's 37 (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/varesa/plunge.go` lines 29 and 31), so it lands 7 frames before the airborne high's 38 the kit plays.
- **Rush's box is gcsim's, marked provisional.** gcsim queues Rush as a box 0.1 metres wide and 2 metres long centred on the primary target and marks it "TODO: approximated" (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/varesa/skill.go` line 82). The kit ships it as gcsim gives it, priced as the target's reach plus the box's far corner. The roadmap's manual verification has the question.
- **The Fiery Passion Flying Kick's area is gcsim's at radius 23.5.** gcsim's kick is a circle of radius 7.5 a metre ahead of the primary target (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/varesa/burst.go` line 76, `info.Point{Y: 1}`), and the target-centred convention stores that as 15 plus the offset and the radius, the 15 being `SKILL_TARGETING_AREA`'s (`services/kit/constants.ts`), so 23.5, the same as the Flying Kick's. The reader's burst talent-variable join reads that circle at line 76, so the overlay holds no value for it.
- **Rush's particles are not built.** Its hit gives particles in gcsim (`skill.go`, line 116), which the framework does not yet produce, so Rush's energy is not generated, as the kit page's shared gap records.
