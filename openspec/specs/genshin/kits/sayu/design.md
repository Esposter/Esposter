# Sayu

Sayu's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createSayuKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000053 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`): four strikes, a charged attack of two hits, a collision and two plunges, Fuufuu Windwheel's press, its short hold and its roll, and Yoohoo Art: Mujina Flurry with Muji-Muji Daruma. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 5331, 5332 and 5339 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), and agree with the cached wiki's scaling (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Sayu_Talents.json`): group 5331's index 0 gives 0.7224 for the first strike (the wiki's 72.2%), group 5332's indices 0 to 4 the Windwheel and kicks (36%, 16.8%, 158.4%, 217.6% and 76.2% at level 1), and group 5339's 0 to 5 the burst's Skill Activation DMG and healing and the Daruma's DMG and healing (117%, 577 and 92.2% ATK, 52%, 500 and 79.9% ATK). The press is a Windwheel DoT and a kick (`skill.go` lines 22 and 24). The short hold is a DoT and a kick at 48 frames (`skill.go:27`). The roll is a level for each whole second held up to 10, with a DoT tick every half second (`skill.go:222`), a kick at its end (`skill.go:255`), and a cooldown that grows by half a second per second held (`skill.go:260`). Mujina Flurry's initial hit (`burst.go` line 41) is followed by a field that heals the active character by its flat amount and ATK share at the cast, and by a summon of six Daruma ticks (`burst.go` lines 75 to 76), each striking what is in reach and healing the active character while it is at 70% HP or under (`burst.go` line 83). Her Ascension 1 and Ascension 4 are built as passives: a Swirl she triggers while on the field heals the active character (`asc.go` line 35), and Muji-Muji Daruma's ticks reach 3.5 metres (`asc.go` line 68). Constellations 1, 4 and 6 are built, and Constellation 2 is not: it needs the time the roll spends rolling for the roll's kick, which is not built, as the kit has no absorption check to read the element it takes. The charged attack and the plunges are provisional, and each Daruma tick strikes and heals together, where gcsim strikes only when no heal is due.

## Decisions

- **Sayu's roll is one hold level for each whole second held.** The hold a release plays is the last level whose seconds it reaches, so each level carries its own ticks, kick, animation and cooldown. gcsim's roll takes its frames exactly, so a release part way through a second plays the level below it. The roll's 10-second cap is the wiki's duration of 10 seconds for Fuuin Dash (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Sayu_Talents.json`).
- **Sayu's charged attack and plunges are read from the table.** gcsim gives the charged attack no hitbox (its `attack.go` has no charged attack, and its strikes end at four, `attack.go` line 16), so its two hits take the table's charged slots, group 5331's indices 5 and 6, the wiki's 62.5% cyclic and 113% final (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Sayu_Talents.json`), with its 40 stamina a second (index 7, `~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`, the wiki's "40.0/s"). The charged attack's two hits land at half a second and a second, which is provisional, see [Provisional constants](#provisional-constants). Her plunges have no gcsim file, so their timing is provisional too.
- **Constellation 6's Elemental Mastery is read at the cast.** gcsim v2.47.2 (MIT) sayu/burst.go prices each tick's flat DMG and heal from Sayu's Elemental Mastery as it stood at the cast (`burst.go:61` to `burst.go:68`), its TODO at `burst.go:66` asking only whether the game snapshots it. The tick's DMG adds her ATK times 0.2% of Elemental Mastery, at most 400% of ATK (`burst.go:67`), through the hit's flat base damage, and its heal adds 3 of Elemental Mastery, at most 6000, to the tick's heal (`burst.go:68`), as the wiki's Sleep O'Clock gives it (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Sayu_Talents.json`).
- **Constellation 1 heals at any HP.** gcsim's Daruma heals at any HP from Constellation 1 (`burst.go:81` to `burst.go:83`) and strikes whatever is in reach, which the kit's ticks already do, so only the 70% limit on the heal is lifted. The wiki's Multi-Task no Jutsu names the constellation (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Sayu_Talents.json`).
- **Constellation 4's Energy is a Swirl's trigger on the field.** A reaction event whose striker is Sayu, while she is the active character, gives her 1.2 Energy once every 2 seconds (`asc.go` lines 35 and 38), under a cooldown status, as gcsim gives it in `asc.go:35` to `asc.go:38`, which the Ascension 1 passive sits under, so it needs Ascension 1 as gcsim's does. Stellar Swirl is not built, as the reaction set has no such reaction.

## Provisional constants

- **The short hold begins at half a second.** No table or wiki page gives a hold's seconds, so it starts at Venti's 0.5 seconds, as Kazuha's hold does, until a recording settles it.
- **The charged attack's two hits land at half a second and a second.** gcsim models no charged attack, and the wiki gives its cyclic and final damage and stamina but no frames, so the timings are the table's provisional slots.
- **The plunges land at half a second.** gcsim has no plunge file for Sayu, and no cached source gives her plunge frames.
- **Each Daruma tick strikes and heals together.** The wiki's text gives one action per tick: a strike while every nearby character is above 70% HP, and a heal of the active character with the lowest HP when one is at 70% or under (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Sayu_Talents.json`). gcsim strikes only when no heal is due (`burst.go:83`). The kit does both, which neither source gives, so it is open.
- **Constellation 4's name is open.** Its wiki note is not in the cached Sayu page, which names constellations 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6 only, so the name "Skiving: New and Improved" in the kit's comment is not sourced here.

## Not built

- **Constellation 2 is not built.** The wiki's Egress Prep raises the Fuufuu Whirlwind Kick's DMG from 3.3% to 66%, depending on how long Sayu spends rolling. The roll's time spent rolling for the kick's DMG, and the roll's elemental kick, which takes the element a roll absorbs, are not built, as the kit has no absorption check to read the element.
