# Xiao

Xiao's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createXiaoKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000026: six strikes, the first and fourth of two hits each, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, Lemniscatic Wind Cycling's dash, and Bane of All Evil. His multipliers are read from proud skill groups 2631, 2632 and 2639, and match the wiki's Whirlwind Thrust, Lemniscatic Wind Cycling and Bane of All Evil values to two decimal places; the charged attack's stamina, the skill's cooldown, and the burst's DMG Bonus, drain, duration, cooldown and cost are read from the same groups. The dash lands from a summon, and Bane of All Evil is an infusion that converts his attacks to Anemo, each carrying the burst's DMG Bonus at its converted poise and reach, beside fields that drain his current HP each second and end the burst as he leaves the field, built over the kit framework's shared effects, with Tamer of Demons' Anemo DMG Bonus from Ascension 1 and Heaven Fall's stacking dash bonus from Ascension 4, on the skill's two charges. The burst's jump, interruption resistance and priority over another infusion, the dash's particles, Gravity Defier and his constellations are not built.

## Decisions

- **Bane of All Evil is an infusion that converts Xiao's attacks and carries its DMG Bonus.** The burst's action has no hits: as it starts, an Anemo infusion that converts his attacks stands for the burst group's 15 seconds past gcsim's 57-frame start, and each hit it infuses carries the group's 58.45% as its own DMG Bonus. The bonus is the Normal, Charged and Plunging Attacks' alone, so a buff to his Anemo DMG Bonus would raise his dash too. gcsim converts the attacks' element as it snapshots them rather than through a weapon infusion; the wiki names the effect one no other infusion overrides. Each converted hit takes the wiki's converted poise, half as much again, and gcsim's reach under the burst, up to 0.4 metres further on a strike and a metre on a plunge. The burst's 18-second cooldown and 70 energy are the group's.
- **Bane of All Evil ends as Xiao leaves the field, and drains his current HP while it stands.** The infusion and Tamer of Demons' bonus end with the swap that takes him off the field, as gcsim deletes the burst's status on a swap, and the drain stops with the infusion. Another field takes the group's 3% of his current HP every second from 60 frames past the burst's start, fourteen times, the last a second before the burst ends, as gcsim's burst.go does, so the drain never downs him. The drain and the bonus each act only while the infusion stands, so neither outlasts a swap within the step it happens.
- **Tamer of Demons is an Anemo DMG Bonus.** From Ascension 1, a field ticks every 3 seconds from the burst's start and restarts a buff of 5% for each tick so far, up to 25% at 12 seconds, standing as long as the infusion, as gcsim's asc.go counts it. The passive raises all of Xiao's DMG, all of which is Anemo under the burst, so the buff is on his Anemo DMG Bonus.
- **Lemniscatic Wind Cycling's dash lands from a summon.** The skill's action has no hits: its start casts a summon where Xiao stands, which lands the dash at gcsim's 4 frames as 1U of Anemo with the wiki's 100 poise, and the skill plays gcsim's 37 frames. gcsim lands the dash as a circle of 0.8 metres on the primary target, which an area does not hold, so it reaches 5 metres round him, provisionally, and the dash's own movement is the controller's, which a kit does not move. The wiki's internal cooldown of 0.1 seconds never holds a dash back, so it carries none.
- **Heaven Fall's stacks are the dashes standing.** From Ascension 4, a dash's summon stands for gcsim's 15 frames and 420 more, the life of the stack it gives, and each dash reads its stacks off those standing, up to 3, carrying 15% for each as its own DMG Bonus, and restarts them, as a new stack restarts the effect. gcsim gains a stack a quarter of a second after its dash, where this kit restarts the standing ones as the dash starts, so a stack lapsing within that quarter second lives on here. On the skill's two charges a second dash may fall within the first's 7 seconds, and reads the first's stack as it starts.
- **Xiao's charged attack carries no internal cooldown.** The wiki tags it Charged Attack with a 0.5-second timer, which never holds back a thrust that follows a strike and plays 45 frames, so it carries none; gcsim's count of a converted thrust under the Normal Attack cooldown is not followed. It is gcsim's fan of 200 degrees and 3 metres round the body at 16 frames, 3.2 metres converted, and its 25 stamina is spent as it starts.
- **Xiao's areas are priced with their offsets, at gcsim's fan angles.** gcsim centres each strike's circle up to 1.1 metres ahead of the body or 0.1 behind it and opens it to a fan of 150 to 360 degrees, so each reach is its offset plus its radius at that angle, 1.7 to 3.1 metres and 1.9 to 3.5 converted, and spawns the first strike's second hit and the fifth strike as boxes on their near edge at the body, each priced as the circle to its far corner. His plunges are gcsim's under the burst, the only ones it models, both landing at 46 frames and ending at 62 and 66, counted from the plunge's action, and reaching 1, 3 and 5 metres, 4 and 6 converted.
- **Constellation 4's DEF Bonus is a passive bonus read from his HP share.** `getPassiveBonuses` receives the health share the step gives, as Bennett's Constellation 2 does, so the 100% DEF Bonus holds while his HP is under half and lapses above it.

## Not built

- **Xiao's third charge, his jump and his Constellations 2 and 6 are not built.** Lemniscatic Wind Cycling holds its 2 charges, as the skill set, the wiki and gcsim's xiao.go give them, but Dissolution Eon: Destroyer of Worlds' third is not built, as a kit's charges are one count no constellation reads. Bane of All Evil's higher jump is the controller's, which reads no kit, so a high plunge still needs a drop over 2.4 metres; its interruption resistance has nothing to act on, and its infusion is the first on him in the team's list rather than one no other overrides, so Fire Ventures With Me's Pyro, cast before it, would win. The dash's 3 particles are not built, his plunges' immunity to fall damage has nothing to spare him from, as the controller deals none, and Transcension: Gravity Defier's climbing stamina is not combat. Annihilation Eon: Blossom of Kaleidos waits on a drop's energy reading a bonus, Transcension: Extinction of Suffering on a DEF Bonus the pricing derives DEF from again, as a passive bonus now reads his HP but `getBuffedCombatant` adds a DEF Bonus to its attribute total alone, and Conqueror of Evil: Guardian Yaksha on a hit telling the kit how many enemies a plunge struck. Conqueror of Evil: Wrath Deity and Evolution Eon: Origin of Ignorance raise talent levels, which the [talents](../../../../changes/genshin-talents/design.md) page holds.
- **Constellation 1's extra charge is a skill charge.** Lemniscatic Wind Cycling's second charge before its cooldown is a charge count, so Constellation 1 adds one through `getSkillChargeBonus`, as Ganyu's Constellation 2 does.
