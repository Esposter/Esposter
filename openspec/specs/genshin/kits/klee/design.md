# Klee

Klee's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createKleeKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000029: three strikes, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, all Pyro catalyst bombs, Jumpy Dumpty's three bounces and its mines, and Sparks 'n' Splash's sparks. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 2931, 2932 and 2939, and match the wiki's Kaboom!, Jumpy Dumpty and Sparks 'n' Splash values to two decimal places; the charged attack's stamina, the skill's cooldown, and the burst's cooldown, cost and seconds are read from the same groups. Every bomb, bounce and mine lands from a summon cast as its action starts, and the skill holds its two charges, each thrown on its own and back one at a time on the skill's cooldown. Sparks 'n' Splash is a summon that follows the body on the field and ends as she leaves it, setting off Sparkly Explosion where she left from four constellations, and Blazing Delight gives the team its Pyro DMG Bonus from six. Pounding Surprise's Explosive Sparks, Sparkling Burst's energy, the sparks a wave adds by chance, the mines' contact and the six more of them, the skill's particles, the first strike's lag after a skill, a dash or a burst, Chained Reactions, Explosive Frags, Blazing Delight's energy and her Hexerei kit are not built.

## Decisions

- **Klee's bombs are summons priced round her.** Each strike, the charged attack and the skill cast their hits from a summon as they start, priced as she stood then. gcsim centres every bomb on the primary target, so each reaches round the body the strikes' 5-metre targeting plus its radius, through `createTargetedHitArea` as Ningguang's gems do: 6 metres for the first two strikes, 6.5 for the third and for a spark, 8 for the charged attack, 9 for a bounce and 7 for a mine. Each strike's bomb lands 10 frames past gcsim's throw, its default travel, at 26, 33 and 47 frames, and the strikes end at gcsim's 34, 41 and 77.
- **Klee's charged attack is the one after a first or second strike.** gcsim skips 14 frames of the charge's windup after the first or second strike and none after the third, and an attack held from rest reaches the charge after the first, so the kit's one charged attack skips them: its bomb lands at 72 frames, gcsim's 76 less the 14 plus the travel, and it ends at 99. After the third strike gcsim's bomb lands 14 frames later, which the kit's one charged attack does not play. Its 50 stamina is the table's.
- **Jumpy Dumpty's three bounces all land, and two of its mines at gcsim's default.** The skill's summon lands the bounces at gcsim's 71, 111 and 140 frames, each 1U of Pyro and the third, the bomb's explosion, 2U, as Elemental Gauge Theory's character data and gcsim give them where the skill's own page gives 2U throughout, all blunt with the wiki's 40 poise. gcsim lands only the first bounce on one enemy by default, as the bomb bounces on past it, and the kit places no bounce, so each reaches round the body. The wiki's 8 mines go off on contact or after a short time, which no source measures, so two land at 240 frames, provisionally. The table's 15 seconds of the mines is not read, and neither the six more mines, their contact nor the first throw's mines going off as a second's are made is built.
- **Sparks 'n' Splash follows Klee and ends as she leaves the field.** The burst casts a summon that follows the body on the field, standing gcsim's 146 frames and the group's 10 seconds, whose six waves land at gcsim's 186 to 718 frames, each a spark and two more 12 and 24 frames on, 1U of Pyro under the Elemental Burst internal cooldown with the wiki's 25 poise. The sparks strike the enemies near her, so they follow her and reach 6.5 metres round her body, and they are priced as she cast the burst, where gcsim snapshots 100 frames in. gcsim adds a fourth spark at a 30% chance and a fifth at 50% each wave, where the wiki counts 4, and a summon's hits read no random source, so only the three certain sparks are built. The summon ends on a swap (`isEndedOnSwap`), landing nothing from the swap's step, as gcsim's waves check the burst's status that its swap handler deletes.
- **Sparkly Explosion goes off where Klee left.** From four constellations, her kit hears the swap that ended the burst and casts the explosion from the burst's summon, which stood with the body on the field until the swap: 555% of her ATK as 2U of Pyro with no internal cooldown and the wiki's 120 poise, reaching gcsim's 5 metres round it a frame later, priced as she cast the burst.
- **Blazing Delight's bonus is a task.** From six constellations, the burst's start sets a task that runs on the next step and gives each member of the deployed team 10% Pyro DMG Bonus for 25 seconds, gcsim's 1500 frames, which the next burst restarts. Its 3 energy every 3 seconds to the other members is not built: a task holds no member's kit, so it cannot cap the energy at that member's burst cost.
- **Klee's plunges are gcsim's from Xianyun's lift.** gcsim models them only from Xianyun's lift, so the low plunge lands at 47 frames and the high at 48, ending at 67 and 71, counted from the plunge's action, and reaching 3 and 3.5 metres round the body and the collision 1.5. Their gauges and poise are the wiki's Kaboom!'s: Pyro at 1U, 50 and 100 poise, and the collision at 0U with 5.

## Not built

- **Klee's Explosive Spark, from Ascension 1, and Sparkling Burst, from Ascension 4, are built.** Each hit of her normal attacks and Jumpy Dumpty rolls it at a 50% chance, at most once each 5 seconds, and her next charged attack spends it for no stamina and 50% more Pyro DMG. From Ascension 4, Sparkling Burst gives each member of the deployed team 2 energy, unraised by Energy Recharge, when her charged attack's bomb lands a CRIT hit, once in 0.6 seconds, as gcsim gives it; the hit is told apart by being that bomb. Her other constellations and her Hexerei kit are not built. Chained Reactions' sparks come at a chance that rises with each miss, rolled the same way and not built yet; their 120% of a spark is the burst group's index 3, which nothing reads yet. Explosive Frags waits on an enemy status lowering DEF, which none does. Exquisite Compound and Nova Burst raise talent levels, which the [talents](../../talents/design.md) page holds. The 9 frames gcsim adds to the first strike after a skill, a dash or a burst are not built, as a strike reads nothing of what came before it, nor are the skill's 4 particles. The Hexerei rework of her passive and constellations is not built.
