# Yanfei

Yanfei's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createYanfeiKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000048 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`): three strikes (`attack.go` line 17), a charged attack that spends her Scarlet Seals, a collision and two plunges, Signed Edict and Done Deal. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 4831, 4832 and 4839 (`ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`); the charged attack's stamina (group 4831's 50, the wiki's `stamina`) and its per-seal values, the skill's cooldown, and the burst's seconds, cooldown and energy cost are read from the same groups. The seals are a status for each of four slots, which a gain restarts together for 10 seconds (`attack.go` line 60, `skill.go` line 44, 600 frames), so they run out together, as gcsim's do. A normal attack's hit on the field, Signed Edict and Done Deal set her seals (`attack.go` lines 45 to 62, `skill.go` lines 40 to 46, `burst.go` lines 101 to 109), and Done Deal adds one each second for its 15 seconds (`burst.go` lines 35 and 85). Each seal the charged attack spends widens its circle, raises its multiplier and cuts its stamina, and Ascension 1 gives 5% Pyro DMG Bonus per seal for 6 seconds (`asc.go` lines 16 to 27). Constellation 1 cuts the stamina more (`yanfei.go` lines 36 to 38), and Constellation 6 holds a fourth seal (`yanfei.go` line 33). Done Deal's DMG Bonus goes to the charged attack while it stands (`burst.go` lines 35 to 41). From Ascension 4, Blazing Eye answers the first CRIT hit each charged attack lands while she is on the field with 80% of her ATK as 1U of Pyro, Charged Attack DMG, in a circle of radius 3.5 round the enemy struck, 10 frames on (`asc.go` lines 33 to 68); gcsim gives it no poise, so it deals none. The poise of the plunges and the collision takes the wiki's values (50 and 100 for the low and high plunges, 5 for the collision, `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Yanfei_Talents.json`), and the charged attack lands at 63 frames from every start (`charge.go` line 18).

## Decisions

- **Yanfei's seals are four statuses, one a slot, and her charged attack reads them as it starts.** A kit status of one id restarts rather than stacks, so each seal has its own slot id and a gain restarts the held slots together. The charged attack's hit is cast as a summon at her body as the seals are spent, as Ganyu's Bloom is, so its circle is priced round the body at the charge's radius plus the targeting area. gcsim's circle sits on the primary target, at the radius its seal count gives (`charge.go` lines 15 and 58 to 64).
- **Yanfei's charged attack lands at 63 frames from a standing start.** gcsim adds a 16-frame windup to a start from a run, which the kit does not read, so every start lands at 63 frames (`charge.go` lines 18 and 53 to 56).
- **Constellation 4's shield is a Pyro shield cast with Done Deal.** From four constellations, Done Deal's burst casts a shield of Pyro element at 45% of Yanfei's Max HP for 15 seconds (gcsim's `cons.go` lines 42 to 56). Its Pyro absorption of 250% is gcsim's comment at `cons.go` lines 39 to 41, the wiki's Supreme Amnesty page not being in the cache, and the combat shield gives its own element that absorption, so the shield needs no absorption rule of its own.
- **Constellation 2's CRIT Rate reads the enemy's health.** gcsim gives 20% CRIT Rate to her charged attacks against an enemy under half HP (`cons.go` lines 15 to 35). The below-half check reads its health as Ayato's Constellation 1 does, and no kit event is needed, the hook taking the enemy as [the kits README](../README.md#shared-effects) states.
