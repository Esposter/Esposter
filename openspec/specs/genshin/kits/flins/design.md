# Flins

Flins's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createFlinsKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000120: his five strikes, a charged attack, a plunge collision and two plunges, Manifest Flame's press, which starts the stance, and Cometh the Night's initial and final hits. His multipliers are read from proud skill groups 12031, 12032 and 12039 of the dump (`~/Esposter/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), matching gcsim's `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/flins/zz_flins.dm.go` to six decimal places where checked (`:35`, `:141` and `:355`). From the same groups come the charged attack's stamina (12031's index 6, 25), the burst's energy cost (12039's index 3, 80, the wiki's 80 in `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Flins_Talents.json`) and the skill's cooldown (12032's index 9, 16 seconds, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/flins/skill.go:84`). The burst's cooldown is the game's 20 seconds, the table's (11205, index 4 of 12039) and the wiki's, not gcsim's 15 (`burst.go:91`), as the decisions below give.

## Decisions

- **Flins's strikes are physical, with no travel, and land on their hitmarks.** Each is queued by gcsim at its hitmark (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/flins/attack.go:16`, `:86`), so the hits land at 11, 11, 16, 20, 29 and 29 frames, with the fourth strike's two at 20 and 29 and the fifth's at 29, in animations of 31, 31, 45, 67 and 68 frames (`attack.go:28-44`). The first two are boxes of 1.3 by 3.2 and 2 by 3.8 metres, and the rest circles of 2.8 metres (`attack.go:19`), each offset as gcsim gives it, and physical (`attack.go:62`).
- **His charged attack is a box of 4 by 6 metres from the body, at 28 frames in an animation of 60** (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/flins/charge.go:14`, `:17`, `:47-52`). It is physical (`charge.go:40`), and costs 25 stamina, read from 12031's index 6 (`~/Esposter/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`) after the charged attack's multiplier at index 5, as the other kits' charged attacks read theirs.
- **Manifest Flame's press plays no hit of its own, and starts the stance.** gcsim queues Arcane Light as a zero-damage Electro attack at 19 frames (`skill.go:70-77`), so the press builds no hit, and the stance it starts lasts the 10 seconds 12032's index 8 gives (`skill.go:79`). During the stance his strikes and charged attack are Electro, at the skill's multipliers (`attack.go:106-112`, `charge.go:72-78`), and the strikes keep their hitmarks and reaches. Swapping him out ends the stance, which gcsim's exit from the field deletes (`skill.go:45-56`).
- **The stance keeps the kit's own press and burst, as Raiden's Musou Isshin does** (a precedent this pass did not read, open). Northland Spearstorm, which the stance's skill would be, waits on the hook below, so the press stands in for it until then. Pressing it inside the stance restarts the stance, where gcsim fires Spearstorm (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/flins/flins.go:54`), so that one press differs until Spearstorm is built.
- **Cometh the Night's initial hit lands at 93 frames, and its final at 164, in an animation of 130** (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/flins/burst.go:21`, `:28`). Both are circles of 7 metres round the body (`burst.go:61`), Electro. The initial carries one unit of gauge (`burst.go:58`), while gcsim's middle and final hits carry none, as the wiki's final-phase 0U gives (`burst.go:62-88`). gcsim queues the final 71 frames after the initial (`burst.go:22`, `:88-89`: 17 + 9 + 9 + 9 + 27), which is 164 frames in all, past the animation, as the framework allows. The middle hits follow at 17, 26, 35 and 44 frames after the initial's 93 (`burst.go:18`, `:82`).
- **The burst's cooldown is the game's 20 seconds, and gcsim's 15 is not taken.** The game's skill table gives Cometh the Night (id 11205, index 4 of 12039) a cooldown of 20 seconds, as the wiki does (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Flins_Talents.json`), where gcsim sets 15 (`burst.go:91`). The record carries no burst cooldown, so the value the built kit runs is open; the spec states the game's 20.
- **His plunges are provisional.** No gcsim plunge file exists for Flins (his folder has no `plunge.go`), so his timing is Bennett's, gcsim's `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/bennett/plunge.go`, on the shared plunge reach and his own table's multipliers. His poise is the plunge rows of his Pocztowy Demonspear page, cached: 25 for the collision, 100 for the low plunge and 150 for the high. The question sits on the [roadmap](../../../../changes/genshin-roadmap/tasks.md).

- **Whispering Flame, from Ascension 4, is a passive bonus to his Elemental Mastery of 8% of his total ATK, to at most 160** (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Flins_Talents.json`, and `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/flins/asc.go:49-60`, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/flins/cons.go:145-150`). From Constellation 4 gcsim gives 10%, to at most 220 (`cons.go:145-150`), which the kit does not read, open, as its own comment says. It is read through the kit's passive hook from his ATK as it stands, and the bonus is flat, as the attribute total takes it.

## Not built

- **Northland Spearstorm waits on a stance's own skill cooldown.** Inside the stance, the skill is a box of 4 by 6 metres at 23 frames, Electro, on the table's skill multiplier at index 6 (`skill.go:106-107`, `:23`; the dump's 12032 index 6, 1.784). It has a cooldown of 6 seconds of its own (12032's index 7; `skill.go:108`, `cons.go:39-43`), which a stance's action cannot hold yet, so the kit keeps the press in its place until the framework can give a stance's skill its own cooldown.
- **Thunderous Symphony waits on the Lunar-Charged hook.** For six seconds after Spearstorm (`skill.go:121`), the burst is a Lunar-Charged hit and an extra one at moonsign level 2 (`burst.go:45`, `:118-120`). Both hits are Lunar-Charged, which the kit does not price yet, so the burst stays Cometh the Night.
- **Cometh the Night's middle hits are built by moonsign.** Two land always, and two more at Ascendant Gleam (`burst.go:76-78`), which gcsim reads when its initial hit lands; the kit's landing condition reads the level as each hit lands, and the team's composition is assumed not to change within the burst, open. Its middle hits are Lunar-Charged in gcsim, but the builder sources that reaction for the first middle hit alone, so the other hits' reaction waits on the reader's Gcsim candidate, read from gcsim's `AttackTagDirectLunarCharged` tag (`burst.go:67`).
- **Constellation 1's Energy is built, and the rest of its effects wait.** It gives 8 Energy once every 5.5 seconds on a Lunar-Charged reaction (`cons.go:26-35`), and its shorter Spearstorm cooldown, 4 seconds against 6 (`cons.go:39-43`), waits with Spearstorm. His Ascension 1 passive is not checked here, open. Constellation 2 reads Northland Spearstorm (`cons.go:50-60`), and Constellation 6 the Lunar-Charged reactions' damage (`cons.go:168-186`).
