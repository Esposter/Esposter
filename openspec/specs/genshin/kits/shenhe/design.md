# Shenhe

Shenhe's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createShenheKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000063: five strikes, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Shenhe_Talents.json`), Spring Spirit Summoning's press and its hold, and Divine Maiden's Deliverance with its initial hit, the field's DoT ticks, the Ascension 1 Cryo DMG Bonus for an active character inside it, and the RES Decrease its DoT gives. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 6331, 6332 and 6339 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`); the charged attack's stamina, the press's and hold's cooldowns, and the burst's energy cost and cooldown are read from the same groups. The field stands 2 metres ahead of the body (gcsim's `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/shenhe/burst.go` line 48 for `burstArea`, its centre at line 49), and each DoT tick's two hits are summoned there (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/shenhe/burst.go` lines 77 and 82 to 86). Spring Spirit Summoning's press and hold give the other party members her Icy Quill, whose ATK-based Cryo DMG her kit's party strike hook prices and whose triggers each Cryo hit spends. Constellations 4 and 6 are built on the quill, and her other passives sit outside the kit. The hold's threshold is provisional.

## Decisions

- **Shenhe's strikes, charged attack, plunges, skill and DoT ticks take the wiki's poise.** The strikes take the wiki's 46.08, 43.44, 56.31, 27.88 and 73.6 per strike, with 27.88 on each of the fourth strike's two hits, and the charged attack the wiki's 120 (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Shenhe_Talents.json`). The plunge collision's 25 is the wiki's too, and the plunges take the wiki's 150 high and 100 low. Spring Spirit Summoning's press takes the wiki's 100 and its hold 200, and Divine Maiden's Deliverance's DoT ticks 50 each. Her plunges' reaches and 0.4 seconds are the other kits' stand-ins.
- **Spring Spirit Summoning's hold begins at 0.5 seconds.** Provisional: gcsim reads the hold from the input alone, as it does for Eula and Diona.
- **Divine Maiden's Deliverance's RES Decrease holds between its DoT ticks.** `shenheKit.ts` holds the decrease for one tick interval from each tick, the 117 frames the record reads, where gcsim adds a 36-frame shred every 18 frames (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/shenhe/burst.go` lines 90 to 91, and 113 to 119).
- **Constellation 1's extra use is a skill charge.** Constellation 1 adds one through `getSkillChargeBonus` to Spring Spirit Summoning, as the [kits README](../README.md#how-a-kit-is-read) gives.
- **The Icy Quill is held by the other party members, not by Shenhe.** Her kit's party strike hook never prices her own strikes, and the quill's "nearby party members" is read as the rest of the deployed team. The hook's ATK is Shenhe's buffed ATK, read through the team's combatant map the strike loop now hands the hook.
- **Each Cryo hit a quill holder strikes from an attack kind spends a trigger, one per enemy it reaches.** Strikes are priced and their events sent per enemy, so a strike reaching several enemies spends one trigger for each. The enemy hit events carry the strike's attack tag, and only the five kinds the quill names count, so a reaction or a field's tick neither adds the bonus nor spends a trigger. Constellation 6 exempts a Normal or Charged Attack trigger from the quota while still giving its bonus (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/shenhe/skill.go` lines 189 to 191). Constellation 4's Skyfrost Mantra gains a stack per trigger, and Spring Spirit Summoning consumes the stacks as it starts into 5% DMG per stack on its hits (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Shenhe_Talents.json`, Insight; `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/shenhe/cons.go` lines 25 to 29). The empowerment's window is the press action's length, flagged: the wiki gives none, and a hold's own length is not read.
- **The quill's seconds and triggers do not change with the skill's level.** Its ratio is read at the start level, 45.656% of ATK (group 6332's index 2 at level 1, which reaches 108.433% at level 15, `~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`).
- **Constellation 2 extends the field by six seconds, and gives the active character Cryo CRIT DMG in it.** gcsim's burst lasts six seconds longer at two constellations (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/shenhe/burst.go` lines 57 to 62, and `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Shenhe_Talents.json`, Centered Spirit), so the kit adds six to the field's seconds where it is cast, and its ticks stay as they are. gcsim's `c2` gives the active character 15% CRIT DMG (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/shenhe/shenhe.go` line 50) on her Cryo attacks while she stands in the field (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/shenhe/cons.go` lines 13 to 23), so each tick adds an element CRIT DMG effect for the tick's interval, which the strikes of that element by that character price in as CRIT DMG. The effect belongs to the active character, not to Shenhe, and the next tick restarts it rather than stacking it.
