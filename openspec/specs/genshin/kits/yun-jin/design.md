# Yun Jin

Yun Jin's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createYunjinKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000064: five strikes, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, and Opening Flourish's tap and its two charge levels, each with a shield of a share of her Max HP and a flat share, and Cliffbreaker's Banner's initial hit. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 6431, 6432 and 6439; the charged attack's stamina, the skill's cooldown, and the burst's cooldown and energy cost are read from the same groups. The tap and both charges scale with her DEF, which the kit prices as their flat base damage, and each shield lasts until its hit, as gcsim expires it. Cliffbreaker's Banner's DMG Increase on the party's normal attacks is not built: the party strike hook reads Yun Jin's DEF for another member's normal attack, so the increase waits only on the banner's own state, a status per member for 12 seconds and 30 hits, which the burst does not set yet. Her Ascension 1 counter window is built, a hit taken during her hold opening a window that its release reads, her Ascension 4 passive is blocked on the hooks named under Decisions, Constellations 1, 2 and 6 are not built, and Constellation 4's DEF is built. The charge levels' thresholds are provisional.

## Decisions

- **Yun Jin's strikes, charged attack, plunges and Opening Flourish's hits deal 0 poise.** Provisional, as gcsim gives none, so `yunjinKit.ts` sets each at 0; her plunges' reaches and 0.4 seconds are the other kits' stand-ins.
- **Opening Flourish's tap and Yun Jin's Geo burst give 1U of Geo gauge each.** Provisional, as gcsim gives no gauge, so `yunjinKit.ts` reads 1U as the other Cryo and Geo hits do.
- **Opening Flourish's charge levels start at half a second and a second.** Provisional, as gcsim takes the levels from the input alone, so `yunjinKit.ts` uses the thresholds Bennett's Charge Levels do.
- **Constellation 4's DEF is her own reaction's buff.** A reaction event whose striker is Yun Jin, and whose reactions include Crystallize or Lunar-Crystallize, gives her 20% DEF for 12 seconds under its own source, so a second reaction restarts it. A reaction a party member triggers gives her nothing, as the text names her own reactions.
- **Yun Jin's Ascension 1 is a counter window on her held skill, and her Ascension 4 passive is not built, as the hooks it waits on are missing.** Ascension 1, True to Oneself, opens at phase 1 (proud skill group 6421). gcsim v2.47.2 leaves it unimplemented (`internal/characters/yunjin/asc.go`), and its TODO describes a press of Opening Flourish at the moment she is attacked, which plays the Level 2 Charged form. The kit builds the window the owner ruled instead: a hit on her while her skill is held opens a status on her for 0.5 seconds, and a release while it stands plays the top hold level however short the hold was. `openKitCounterWindow` opens it on a strike that costs her HP with no kit as the source, or a shield taking a strike for her, and `Kit.jumpsSkillHoldToTop` reads it in `startNextKitAction`. A release after the window closes plays the level its held seconds reach. The 0.5 seconds are Unsourced and provisional, as gcsim gives the window no length. Two readings differ from gcsim's: its perfect counter plays the tap's 13-frame hitmark at the Level 2 form, where this kit plays the top level's own 93-frame hitmark; and a completed charge, which Candace's Aegis uses, would also release at the top level, but a charge stays complete until its release, so it cannot end the window, which is why the window is a hook. A release shorter than Level 1's half second inside the window jumps to the top level as well, which a variant on each hold would miss. Confirm the length and both readings with the owner.
- **Yun Jin's Ascension 4 passive is not built, as the hooks it waits on are missing.** Ascension 4, Breaking Conventions, opens at phase 4 (proud skill group 6422) and adds to the banner's Normal Attack bonus a share of Yun Jin's DEF for each Elemental Type the party holds: 2.5%, 5% and 7.5% for one to three types, and 11.5% for four (gcsim (MIT) `internal/characters/yunjin/asc.go`). The banner adds DEF times its own share, plus this one, as flat damage to each party member's normal attack for 30 hits within 12 seconds (`internal/characters/yunjin/burst.go`), so the passive has no base to add to until the banner's state is built. The party strike hook reads her DEF from the member it is given, and it can gate on `strike.attackTag` so a charged attack takes none, but it is given no deployed combatants, so the team's Elemental Types cannot be counted in it: `getDeployedStrikeBonuses` holds them and does not pass them on. The share is flat base damage, so it belongs in the hook's `additiveBaseDamageBonus` and not its DMG Bonus. A party buff would raise her charged attacks as well, as `Attribute.NormalAndChargedAttackDamageBonus` is the only attribute a normal attack reads.
