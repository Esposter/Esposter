# Yun Jin

Yun Jin's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createYunjinKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000064: five strikes, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, and Opening Flourish's tap and its two charge levels, each with a shield of a share of her Max HP and a flat share, and Cliffbreaker's Banner's initial hit. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 6431, 6432 and 6439; the charged attack's stamina, the skill's cooldown, and the burst's cooldown and energy cost are read from the same groups. The tap and both charges scale with her DEF, which the kit prices as their flat base damage, and each shield lasts until its hit, as gcsim expires it. Cliffbreaker's Banner's DMG Increase on the party's normal attacks is not built, as it scales with Yun Jin's DEF, which the pricing of a party member's hit does not read. Her Ascension 1 and 4 passives are blocked on the hooks named under Decisions, Constellations 1, 2 and 6 are not built, and Constellation 4's DEF is built. The charge levels' thresholds are provisional.

## Decisions

- **Yun Jin's strikes, charged attack, plunges and Opening Flourish's hits deal 0 poise.** Provisional, as gcsim gives none, so `yunjinKit.ts` sets each at 0; her plunges' reaches and 0.4 seconds are the other kits' stand-ins.
- **Opening Flourish's tap and Yun Jin's Geo burst give 1U of Geo gauge each.** Provisional, as gcsim gives no gauge, so `yunjinKit.ts` reads 1U as the other Cryo and Geo hits do.
- **Opening Flourish's charge levels start at half a second and a second.** Provisional, as gcsim takes the levels from the input alone, so `yunjinKit.ts` uses the thresholds Bennett's Charge Levels do.
- **Constellation 4's DEF is her own reaction's buff.** A reaction event whose striker is Yun Jin, and whose reactions include Crystallize or Lunar-Crystallize, gives her 20% DEF for 12 seconds under its own source, so a second reaction restarts it. A reaction a party member triggers gives her nothing, as the text names her own reactions.
- **Yun Jin's Ascension 1 and 4 passives are not built, as the hooks they wait on are missing.** Ascension 1, True to Oneself, opens at phase 1 (proud skill group 6421) and makes a tap of Opening Flourish at the moment she is attacked play its Level 2 Charged form. gcsim v2.47.2 leaves it unimplemented (`internal/characters/yunjin/asc.go`), and the game dump holds no window for it. `startNextKitAction.ts` plays a tap as `kit.elementalSkill` with no variant, as a hold's variant applies only to a release that reaches that hold's minimum seconds, so no hook takes a tap's form. Ascension 4, Breaking Conventions, opens at phase 4 (proud skill group 6422) and raises the banner's Normal Attack bonus by a share of Yun Jin's DEF. That bonus is not built: gcsim adds it to each party member's normal attack as flat damage, for 30 hits within 12 seconds (`internal/characters/yunjin/burst.go`), and no hook prices another member's normal attack from her DEF, since the striker's own kit alone takes the additive and DMG Bonus hooks. A party buff's normal attack bonus would also raise her charged attacks, as `Attribute.NormalAndChargedAttackDamageBonus` is the only attribute a normal attack reads.
