# Yun Jin

Yun Jin's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createYunjinKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000064: five strikes, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, and Opening Flourish's tap and its two charge levels, each with a shield of a share of her Max HP and a flat share, and Cliffbreaker's Banner's initial hit. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 6431, 6432 and 6439; the charged attack's stamina, the skill's cooldown, and the burst's cooldown and energy cost are read from the same groups. The tap and both charges scale with her DEF, which the kit prices as their flat base damage, and each shield lasts until its hit, as gcsim expires it. Cliffbreaker's Banner's DMG Increase is party-wide, as gcsim gives it: the burst sets the banner for 12 seconds from its hit with 30 stacks, each enemy a normal attack of any member damages spends one, and while it stands each normal attack of a deployed member adds 0.3216 of Yun Jin's DEF as flat base damage, with Ascension 4's share of the team's Elemental Types on top. Her own normal attacks take it through her strike hook, and a teammate's through the party strike hook. Her Ascension 1 counter window is built, a hit taken during her hold opening a window that its release reads, her Ascension 4 passive is built on it, Constellation 1's Opening Flourish cooldown cut and Constellation 4's DEF are built, and Constellation 2's party Normal Attack DMG Bonus is built from the banner's hit, as a party-wide buff for 12 seconds (gcsim (MIT) `yunjin/cons.go`). Constellation 1 is a factor of 0.82 on the skill's cooldown, which gcsim's 443 frames round up to. Constellation 6 is not built: gcsim (MIT) `yunjin/cons.go` gates its Normal ATK SPD on each member's own banner stacks, which the kit's single pool of stacks cannot tell apart. The charge levels' thresholds are provisional.

## Decisions

- **Yun Jin's plunges, strikes, charged attack and Opening Flourish all take the wiki's poise.** gcsim gives the plunges none, so the high and low plunges take the wiki's 150 and 100; the strikes take 23.46 to 62.66 a hit, the charged attack 120, and Opening Flourish 100, 200 and 300, since gcsim gives them none; her plunges' reaches and 0.4 seconds are provisional, the other kits' stand-ins.
- **Opening Flourish's tap and Yun Jin's Geo burst give 1U of Geo gauge each.** Provisional, as gcsim gives no gauge, so `yunjinKit.ts` reads 1U as the other Cryo and Geo hits do.
- **Opening Flourish's charge levels start at half a second and a second.** Provisional: gcsim takes the levels from the input alone and gives no seconds for them, and the wiki page for Opening Flourish is unreachable, so `yunjinKit.ts` takes the 0.5 and 1-second thresholds Bennett's Charge Levels use. A recording or the wiki page settles them.
- **Constellation 4's DEF is her own reaction's buff.** A reaction event whose striker is Yun Jin, and whose reactions include Crystallize or Lunar-Crystallize, gives her 20% DEF for 12 seconds under its own source, so a second reaction restarts it. A reaction a party member triggers gives her nothing, as the text names her own reactions.
- **Yun Jin's Ascension 1 is a counter window on her held skill, and her Ascension 4 share is added to the banner.** Ascension 1, True to Oneself, opens at phase 1 (proud skill group 6421). gcsim v2.47.2 leaves it unimplemented (`internal/characters/yunjin/asc.go`), and its TODO describes a press of Opening Flourish at the moment she is attacked, which plays the Level 2 Charged form. The kit builds the window the owner ruled instead: a hit on her while her skill is held opens a status on her for 0.5 seconds, and a release while it stands plays the top hold level however short the hold was. `openKitCounterWindow` opens it on a strike that costs her HP with no kit as the source, or a shield taking a strike for her, and `Kit.jumpsSkillHoldToTop` reads it in `startNextKitAction`. A release after the window closes plays the level its held seconds reach. The 0.5 seconds are Unsourced and provisional, as gcsim gives the window no length. Two readings differ from gcsim's: its perfect counter plays the tap's 13-frame hitmark at the Level 2 form, where this kit plays the top level's own 93-frame hitmark; and a completed charge, which Candace's Aegis uses, would also release at the top level, but a charge stays complete until its release, so it cannot end the window, which is why the window is a hook. A release shorter than Level 1's half second inside the window jumps to the top level as well, which a variant on each hold would miss. Confirm the length and both readings with the owner.
- **Cliffbreaker's Banner's increase is party-wide, as gcsim gives it.** gcsim (MIT) `internal/characters/yunjin/burst.go` gives it to every party member's normal attacks, with the DEF share taken from Yun Jin. Her own normal attacks take it through her strike hook, `getStrikeAdditiveBaseDamageBonus`, and a teammate's through her party strike hook, `getPartyStrikeBonus`, which the deployed team reads for each strike of another member, priced from her buffed DEF. The kit keeps one pool of 30 stacks on the banner, spent by each enemy any member's normal attack damages, where gcsim gives each member its own 30; the per-member pools are not built.
- **The banner is set as the burst starts, its seconds counted from the burst's hit.** gcsim sets the banner on the burst's hitmark, 35 frames in, and its status runs 720 frames from there (`internal/characters/yunjin/burst.go`). The kit's status carries the hitmark's seconds plus 12, so it ends 12 seconds after the hit, and nothing reads it before that hit, as gcsim's burst gives no normal attack until 57 frames.
- **Yun Jin's Ascension 4 adds the team's Elemental Types' share to the banner's increase.** Ascension 4, Breaking Conventions, opens at phase 4 (proud skill group 6422) and adds to the banner's increase a share of Yun Jin's DEF for each Elemental Type the deployed team holds: 2.5%, 5% and 7.5% for one to three types, and 11.5% for four (gcsim (MIT) `internal/characters/yunjin/asc.go`). It is added to the banner's 0.3216, so it reaches a normal attack only while the banner stands, on the team's members as on hers. The count is each deployed member's element from `getDeployedCombatants`, read through the team's combatants by id that the strike hooks are handed as their last argument, so her own strike hook and the party hook read the same team.
