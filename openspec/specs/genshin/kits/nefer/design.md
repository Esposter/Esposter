# Nefer

Nefer's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createNeferKitBehaviour`, avatar 10000122. gcsim has no folder for Nefer at v2.47.2, so Nefer's record follows [the kits index](../README.md).

## Decisions

- **Nefer is a catalyst.** The installed game's avatar table gives her `weaponType` as `WEAPON_CATALYST` (avatar 10000122, `AvatarImage_Forward_Nefer`, in `~/Esposter/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`), so her donors are catalyst kits, each taken where its shape matches.
- **Nefer borrows Nahida's hitmarks and areas for her normal attacks, charged attack and skill, provisional.** Her overlay's `BorrowedFromDonor` entries name Nahida (10000073) for these, and gcsim's Nahida charged attack, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nahida/charge.go` lines 14 and 17, give the charged attack's hitmark and frames. Her plunges and collision are said to borrow Bennett's hitmarks and areas, as `NoActionFile`, but her overlay holds no plunge or collision entry to show it (open below).
- **Her burst borrows Nahida's charged attack area, and its hitmarks are that attack's hitmark fraction spread over the burst, Unsourced.** Keqing's burst was the donor before, but her Starward Sword strikes "nearby opponents" around herself (the cached `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Keqing_Talents.json`), while Nefer's Sacred Vow deals damage to "opponents ahead" (the cached `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Nefer_Talents.json`), so the shapes differ. Nahida's burst records no hit, since Illusory Heart is a field: the wiki's Illusory Heart text says the Shrine of Maya field is unleashed (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Nahida_Talents.json`), and gcsim's burst queues no attack (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nahida/burst.go`); its area is `packages/genshin-world/src/data/kits/nahida.json`'s `areas.illusoryHeart`, so the closest forward strike is her charged attack, Akara: an AoE in front, 69 frames, one hit at a 65-frame hitmark (gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/nahida/charge.go` lines 14 and 17). Each of the burst's four hits takes that area. Its hitmarks 23, 45, 68 and 90 are 65 of 69 frames applied to the burst's 96 frames and split into quarters (`packages/genshin-world/src/data/kits/nefer.json`, `Unsourced`; the 96 frames are the clip's length, read in Not built).
- **Her Phantasm Performance is a stance whose charged attack borrows Kokomi's.** Kokomi's ceremonial garment is the closest catalyst stance charged attack: 76 frames, one hit at a 48-frame hitmark (gcsim `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kokomi/charge.go` lines 15 and 18), and a target-centred radius of 8.5 in her built record (`packages/genshin-world/src/data/kits/sangonomiya-kokomi.json`, `stances.ceremonialGarment.chargedAttack.hits.0.area`). Phantasm Performance has seven hits against Kokomi's one, so its action takes Kokomi's 76 frames, and each hit's hitmark is Kokomi's 48 spread evenly over the action (7, 14, 21, 27, 34, 41 and 48), `Unsourced` in `packages/genshin-world/src/data/kits/nefer.json`. Every hit takes Kokomi's area. Poise and the cooldown tag come from each hit's wiki row (the Nefer hits are tagged `Charged Attack`, `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Nefer_Talents.json`), and targeting from the stance rule.
- **The shade hits are Lunar-Bloom, and keep Grass.** Hits 4 to 6 are the shades' by the wiki join: their rows are untagged (`icdtag4 = none` and `icdtag5 = none`, against `icdtag2 = Charged Attack` for Nefer's, in `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Nefer_Talents.json`), and the gauge split, 0 for the shades against 1 for Nefer, is not in `packages/genshin-world/src/data/kits/nefer.json` (open below). Their `lunarReactionType` is `LunarBloom`, `Unsourced`, from the wiki's text that "DMG dealt by the shades is considered Lunar-Bloom DMG" (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Nefer_Talents.json`). Their element stays Grass, as a hit record requires one. Hits 0 to 3 are Nefer's, and Grass.
- **Nefer's skill reads its "Skill DMG" label for two hits, and her burst its "1-Hit DMG" and "2-Hit DMG" labels for four** (the Senet page's one `Skill DMG` row and the Sacred Vow page's `1-Hit DMG` and `2-Hit DMG` rows, `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Nefer_Talents.json`).

## Not built

- **Her Shadow Dance starts with two Verdant Dew, and each Phantasm Performance spends one, the third ending the state.** The wiki's prose gives two initial charges ("Two initial charges", Senet page, in `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Nefer_Talents.json`), and its table gives the charges as 3 (`phantasmperformancecharges=3`, in the same file), which is the most Verdant Dew the state holds, so the module starts it at two and caps it at the record's 3. The wiki also gives that a performance needs at least one Verdant Dew ("if you have at least 1 Verdant Dew"), and that the first shade summon after each performance consumes one ("1 Verdant Dew will be consumed the first time shades are summoned after every Phantasm Performance"). The stance spends it as the performance starts, which approximates that summon's timing. A Phantasm Performance starts only with a Verdant Dew held, its action's start gate refusing it otherwise. The gain amounts are Unsourced and not built: the wiki gives the Slither's additional provision its rate, one Verdant Dew every 2.5 seconds for 2.5 seconds after a Lunar-Bloom, up to three, and an Elemental Mastery strengthening of 10% per 100 above 500, up to 50%, but not how many Verdant Dew a Shadow Dance Slither itself grants, nor the Lunar-Bloom gain of the fourth passive. The owed recording is that base amount as the game plays it. The burst does not use charges, since the wiki's burst spends Veils of Falsehood ("consume all Veils of Falsehood", in `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Nefer_Talents.json`), not Verdant Dew.
- **The Phantasm Performance measurement is owed.** Its action frames, hitmarks and areas are spread or borrowed until her official showcase is measured; the coordinator adds that measurement to the recordings list.
- **Nefer's burst, charged attack and normal attack frames are her clips' lengths.** Each clip's `m_StopTime` at 60 a second gives them: the burst's 96 frames (`Ani_Avatar_Lady_Catalyst_Nefer_ElementalBurst.json`), her charged attack's 190 (`Ani_Avatar_Lady_Catalyst_Nefer_ExtraAttack_AS.json`), and her normal attacks' 246, 254, 220 and 320 (`Ani_Avatar_Lady_Catalyst_Nefer_Attack_01.json` to `_04.json`), all in `~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-parity/extracted/timings/07411097/AnimationClip/`, as the [kits index](../README.md) reads Nefer's clips. `packages/genshin-world/src/data/kits/nefer.json` still tags them `Unsourced`.
- **The plunges and collision have no record entry.** Her overlay holds no plunge or collision entry, so the Bennett borrow the decisions name is not shown in the repo. It waits on her built plunge record being read.
- **The shades' gauge split is not in the record.** Hits 4 to 6 are said to carry gauge 0 against Nefer's 1, but `packages/genshin-world/src/data/kits/nefer.json` has no gauge entries and the wiki rows carry none, so it waits on the built record showing the split.
