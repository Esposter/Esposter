## Purpose

Nefer's kit as built: her four strikes, charged attack, plunges, Shadow Dance's skill and burst, with the timings and hitmarks provisional and her Phantasm Performance not built.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Nefer's strikes, charged attack and plunges

Nefer's four strikes, charged attack and plunges SHALL land on provisional timings on the enemies within reach.

#### Scenario: Four strikes in sequence

- **WHEN** the player uses normal attack four times in a row
- **THEN** the four strikes land in order on provisional timings

#### Scenario: Plunge lands on the enemies in reach

- **WHEN** the player starts a plunge attack
- **THEN** the plunge lands on the enemies within reach at a provisional timing

### Requirement: Shadow Dance's skill

Nefer's skill SHALL deal its two hits on the enemies within reach.

#### Scenario: Skill deals two hits

- **WHEN** the player presses Nefer's skill
- **THEN** two hits land on the enemies within reach

### Requirement: Shadow Dance's burst

Nefer's burst SHALL deal its four hits, one-hit and two-hit, on the enemies within reach.

#### Scenario: Burst deals four hits

- **WHEN** the player casts Nefer's burst
- **THEN** four hits land on the enemies within reach

### Requirement: Phantasm Performance not yet built

Nefer's Phantasm Performance SHALL NOT take effect.

#### Scenario: Phantasm Performance does nothing

- **WHEN** the player uses Nefer's skill or burst during Shadow Dance
- **THEN** no Phantasm Performance hit lands
