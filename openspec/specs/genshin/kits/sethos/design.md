# Sethos

Sethos's kit is built from its record alone, on avatar 10000097 (`{data-root}/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`), and `createSethosKitBehaviour` (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/sethosKit.ts`) holds no behaviour of its own beyond Constellation 1's CRIT Rate: three strikes, the second of two hits, a fully-charged Aimed Shot as his charged attack, a collision and two plunges, and Ancient Rite: Thunderous Roar of Sand. His multipliers are read from proud skill groups 9731, 9732 and 9739 (`{data-root}/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), matching gcsim's to three decimal places (group 9731's index 4 is 0.4386, gcsim's `aim[0]` at `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/zz_sethos.dm.go` line 114, and index 5 is 1.24, its `fullaim[0]` at line 132; group 9732's index 0 is 1.156, its `skill[0]` at line 186; index 6 of group 9731 is 1.4, `shadowpierceAtk[0]` at line 150, used at `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/aimed.go` line 147). His skill's cooldown, his burst's cooldown and cost, and the charged attack's lack of a stamina cost come from those groups or from gcsim: group 9739 gives the burst 15 seconds and 60 energy at indices 2 and 3 (`{data-root}/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), the charged attack's lack of a stamina cost is row 10974's "costStamina" of 0 (`{data-root}/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarSkillExcelConfigData.json`), and gcsim's skill cooldown is 8 seconds (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/skill.go` line 78). The skill strikes an Electro circle of radius 4.5 round him (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/skill.go` lines 70 and 75) at 13 frames (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/skill.go` line 76) of a 38-frame animation (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/skill.go` line 18). The fully-charged Aimed Shot lands at 74 frames of an 83-frame animation (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/aimed.go` lines 18 and 34), and its Electro gauge is 1U (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/aimed.go` line 82, for Electro), as the wiki's Fully-Charged Aimed Shot gives it (`{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Sethos_Talents.json`, the Royal Reed Archery page's advanced properties).

## Decisions

- **The fully-charged Aimed Shot is the charged attack.** Sethos has one aimed shot in the kit's slot, so the fully-charged shot takes it, as Ganyu's does, and it is marked aimed (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/build/createKitRecordResolver.ts`) so the body turns to the camera's aim while it is held (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/stepActiveKit.ts` line 133). gcsim's hold level 1 is that shot, at 74 frames, and its level 2 is the Shadowpiercing Shot, at 368 frames (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/aimed.go` line 18).
- **Hitmarks and animations are gcsim's.** His strikes' hitmarks are 10 frames for the first, 12 and 15 for the second's two hits, and 39 for the third (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/attack.go` line 18), with their walk-cancel frames at 19, 35 and 69 (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/attack.go` lines 24, 28 and 32). Their poise is the wiki's 18.879 for the first, 8.791 for each hit of the second and 25.809 for the third, as gcsim gives none (the Royal Reed Archery page's advanced properties, `{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Sethos_Talents.json`).
- **His plunges are provisional.** Sethos has no gcsim plunge file (his folder has none); his plunges use the shared reach and land as their action starts, with the wiki's poise, 50 for the Low Plunge and 100 for the High Plunge, and the collision's 150 (the same page), and the Manual verification list on the roadmap asks about them.

## Not built

- **Shadowpiercing Shot is waiting on a hold of the aimed shot.** Holding the aim to its full length fires a piercing shot that reaches 15 units from the player (`shadowpierce`'s comment, `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/aimed.go` line 167) with 1.4 ATK (group 9731's index 6, `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/aimed.go` line 147), and the Scorching Sandshade's Elemental Mastery share on the wiki's The Sand King's Boon, which the kit's charged attack has no second hold level for. It waits on a hook for the aimed shot's hold levels.
- **The plain Aimed Shot is not built.** A short, uncharged shot deals Physical damage at 16 frames (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/aimed.go` lines 18 and 93), and the kit has no slot for it beside the fully-charged one. It waits on the same hold-level hook as Shadowpiercing Shot.
- **Secret Rite's Dusk Bolts are not built.** For 8 seconds his burst makes his normal attacks Dusk Bolts, Electro with a flat Elemental Mastery bonus and a piercing reach, and forbids aiming (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/burst.go` line 17, the wiki's Secret Rite: Twilight Shadowpiercer). A stance holds normal attacks and a charged attack, and Dusk Bolts have no charged attack to stand in for, so the buff waits on a stance without one. The burst deals no damage itself, so it is built as its animation, cost and cooldown (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/burst.go` lines 13, 20 and 21).
- **Ancient Rite's energy refund on a reaction is not built.** A reaction that his own skill's damage triggers refunds 12 energy (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/skill.go` line 47, `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/zz_sethos.dm.go` line 36, `skillEnergyRegen = 12`, with the reaction events at `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/skill.go` lines 52 and 56), and the kit's reaction event does not say which action struck (`packages/genshin-world/src/models/kit/ActiveKitControls.ts` line 27, `triggerReaction(enemy, reactions)`), so it waits on a hook that names the striking action.
- **His ascension passives and Constellations 2, 4 and 6 are not built.** Constellation 1's 15% CRIT Rate is built as a charged-attack CRIT hook, and its source is open: gcsim's `cons.go` gives the 15% to the Shadowpiercing Shot, by its ability name and tag (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/cons.go` lines 32 to 50), not to the fully-charged shot the kit's charged attack is; see [Provisional constants](#provisional-constants). Ascension 1's shorter charge, which spends energy to skip part of the aim (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/asc.go` lines 12 to 27), waits on the same hold-level hook as Shadowpiercing Shot. The fourth ascension's flat bonus to Shadowpiercing Shot waits on it too (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/asc.go` line 39 gates it, and the bonus is at `aimed.go` lines 156 and 157 of the same folder). Constellation 2 needs the Electro DMG Bonus stacks on his conditions (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/cons.go` lines 55 to 64), Constellation 4 needs Shadowpiercing Shot and Dusk Bolt hits on two opponents (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/cons.go` lines 98 to 122, called from `attack.go` line 83 and `aimed.go` line 182 of the same folder), and Constellation 6 needs the Black Kite's Enigma Elemental Energy return (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/sethos/cons.go` lines 125 to 146, given the tallied energy at `aimed.go` lines 127 and 183 of the same folder). The constellations that raise talent levels are the [talents](../../talents/design.md) page's.

## Provisional constants

- **Constellation 1's CRIT Rate is open against its source.** gcsim's `cons.go` adds 15% CRIT Rate to attacks whose tag is extra and whose ability is the Shadowpiercing Shot. The kit gives it to the charged attack, the fully-charged Aimed Shot, which the wiki lists as a different shot from the Shadowpiercing Shot (its own 1U and 20 poise against the Shadowpiercing Shot's 2U and 150). No cached source gives the fully-charged shot the bonus, so the kit's reading stays open until one does.
