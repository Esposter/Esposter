# Kirara

Kirara's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createKiraraKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000061: four strikes, a charged attack of three hits, a collision and two plunges (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kirara_Talents.json`), Tail-Flicking Flying Kick with its shield and Dendro self-infusion (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kirara_Talents.json`), its short hold and its holds of one to ten seconds with Urgent Neko Parcel's cat ticks and Flipclaw Strike's kick, and Secret Art: Surprise Dispatch's box with six Cat Grass Cardamoms (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kirara_Talents.json`), two detonating at 180 frames (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kirara/burst.go` lines 57 and 92 to 93) and four at expiry (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kirara/burst.go` line 107, `80+12*60`). Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 6131, 6132 and 6139, each level-1 row matching the wiki's scaling table (see Sources; `~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`); the press's cooldown, the holds' cooldowns, the longest hold, the charged attack's stamina, and the burst's mines come from the same groups. Her passives and constellations sit outside the kit, and the short hold's 8.2-second cooldown (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kirara/skill.go` line 181) and the mines' six count (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kirara/burst.go` line 43) are gcsim's own values. Her attacks deal no poise yet, the wiki's values waiting on a recording (the first decision), while the plunges' seconds and reaches, and the short hold's minimum, are provisional.

## Decisions

- **Kirara's record carries no poise, so her hits deal none in the build, though the wiki gives each hit its row: her strikes 33.25 to 76.72, her charged attack 40 a hit, her burst 200, her high and low plunges 150 and 100, the two Cat Grass Cardamoms that detonate at 180 frames 25 each, and her skill 100 on its press and 25 and 200 on its short hold.** gcsim gives none for the strikes, charged attack, burst and plunges (gcsim's kirara folder sets no `PoiseDMG`), and the record none either, so the wiki's rows below are the values the [roadmap](../../../../changes/genshin-roadmap/tasks.md) asks a recording to test (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kirara_Talents.json`): the Boxcutter page's Normal Attack rows (50.1, 48.51, 33.25, 33.25 and 76.72) and Charged Attack rows (40 each), the Secret Art: Surprise Dispatch page's Skill DMG (200) and Cat Grass Cardamom Explosion (25), the Meow-teor Kick page's press (100), and its Urgent Neko Parcel (25) and Flipclaw Strike (200) rows, which the short hold's two hits take (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kirara/skill.go` line 23 and the kit's hit at 48 frames), and the Boxcutter page's Low Plunge (100) and High Plunge (150). The reaches and 0.4 seconds of her plunges are the kit's own placeholders, which no donor sets, and stay provisional.
- **Kirara's Constellation 6 buffs the whole deployed party, since the kit has no nearby check.** The constellation says nearby members, but the kit has no distance to read, so `buffKitTeam` gives every deployed member the buff, as the team buffs of the other kits do. gcsim gives the same, to every character on the field (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kirara/cons.go` lines 66 to 76, 12% for 15 seconds, called from `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kirara/burst.go` line 110 and `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/kirara/skill.go` lines 126 and 177).
