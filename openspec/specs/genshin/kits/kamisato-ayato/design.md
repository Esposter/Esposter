# Kamisato Ayato

Ayato's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createAyatoKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000066 (`~/Esposter/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`, named Kamisato Ayato in `~/Esposter/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/TextMap/TextMap_MediumEN.json`): five strikes, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, Kamisato Art: Kyouka with its water illusion's hit and Soukai Kanka's stance of three Shunsuiken strikes, and Kamisato Art: Suiyuu with its field, which follows the body for eighteen seconds and strikes on each of its ticks (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kamisato_Ayato_Talents.json`). His multipliers are read from proud skill groups 6631, 6632 and 6639 (`~/Esposter/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`); the charged attack's stamina, the skill's cooldown, and the burst's seconds, cooldown and energy cost are read from the same groups. Each Shunsuiken strike adds the table's share of his Max HP per Namisen stack to its DMG (0.56 per cent at level 1, the wiki's Kyouka page's Namisen row, `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kamisato_Ayato_Talents.json`, and the table's index 4 at group 6632, `~/Esposter/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`, which `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/ayato/ayato.go` line 89 multiplies by the stacks), and each enemy it strikes adds a stack, up to four or five (`ayato.go` lines 36-39). Ascension 1, Ascension 4's energy off the field and Constellations 1, 2 and 6 are built (the wiki's Mine Wo Matoishi Kiyotaki and Michiyuku Hagetsu pages, `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kamisato_Ayato_Talents.json`; gcsim's `asc.go` lines 10 and 21 for Ascension 1 and `cons.go` lines 18, 38 and 72 for Constellations 1, 2 and 6), the energy by a field that gives it each second Ayato is off the field, as gcsim does. Constellation 4's team attack speed, Constellation 2's Max HP at three stacks and Suiyuu's normal attack DMG bonus are not, as the kit prices no attack speed, a passive reads the combatant alone, and no kit step reads a normal attack's DMG Bonus from the team's effects. The illusion's delay of 35 frames is provisional, and each burst tick strikes every enemy within 12.5 metres of the body, not one random enemy, which disagrees with its source (see Provisional constants).

## Decisions

- **Ayato's Namisen stacks are a status that each Shunsuiken strike reads through the team's effects as it prices.** A hit's `additiveBaseDamageBonus` receives the striker alone, so the stance's strikes close over the owner of the team's effects that Kyouka's cast writes into, and read the stacks there. Each enemy a strike hits adds a stack through `onStrike`, and the status has no end, which a swap ends, as gcsim clears the stacks when he leaves the field (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/ayato/skill.go` line 95, `c.stacks = 0`); the stance ends on the same swap. Each Shunsuiken hit is one `skillStacks` call (`attack.go` line 120). Soukai Kanka replaces his normal attacks only, as a stance's charged attack and plunges are the kit's own: gcsim refuses both in the skill state (`charge.go` line 27, `plunge.go` lines 62 and 117), which a stance cannot express, and the wiki's Kyouka page lists both as disabled (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Kamisato_Ayato_Talents.json`, its Charged Attack Disabled and Plunging Attack Disabled rows).
- **Kamisato Art: Kyouka's water illusion explodes 35 frames after the cast.** gcsim takes the illusion's least delay, which a player's parameter may only raise (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/ayato/skill.go` line 23, `max(p["illusion_delay"], 35)`), so the record takes 35 frames, about 0.58 seconds. The wiki's Kyouka page has the illusion explode once opponents are nearby or when its 6-second duration ends, so the 35 frames is gcsim's fixed time, not a measured one.

## Provisional constants

- **The illusion's 35 frames is provisional.** gcsim's `max(p["illusion_delay"], 35)` is a floor, and the wiki's Kyouka page has the illusion explode on proximity as well as at its 6 seconds, so the 35 frames holds until a recording measures the explosion.
- **The burst tick's target is open.** The kit strikes every enemy within 12.5 metres of the body on each tick. gcsim's `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/ayato/burst.go` lines 53-67 pick one random enemy in its 10-metre area per tick, and the wiki's Suiyuu page says each Bloomwater Blade targets a random opponent that the last two or three blades have not, so the kit's reading disagrees with both. The 12.5 metres is the 10-metre area plus the 2.5-metre blade's reach (`burst.go` lines 46 and 67).
