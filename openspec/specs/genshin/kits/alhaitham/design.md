## Decisions

- **The Mirrors are a status for their count and a field for their decay.** A status holds the Mirrors as its stacks, and a field, unbounded and ticking every 233 frames, takes one away each tick until none is left. A new run's first Mirror or an overflow past three moves that one field back to its first tick, as gcsim's decay chain is superseded by the one that starts after it (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/alhaitham/skill.go:134` to `:169`, with the three-Mirror cap at `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/alhaitham/skill.go:150`). The field never restarts as an effect, so it is moved rather than added (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/alhaithamKit.ts`).
- **The Dendro infusion is held while the Mirrors are.** It is the infusion of his normal attacks, charged attack and plunges, which the status and the infusion are both set to end on a swap or on the burst (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/alhaithamKit.ts`, `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/alhaitham/alhaitham.go:59` to `:67`).
- **A Projection Attack triggers on each hit that strikes an enemy.** A normal attack, charged attack or plunge's `onStrike` runs it, and Ascension 1 makes a Mirror first from a charged attack or plunge, as gcsim's A1 callback does (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/alhaitham/asc.go:15`), so the count the projection reads includes it (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/alhaithamKit.ts`). Its own 1.6-second cooldown is a status, as gcsim's projection ICD of 96 frames gives it (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/alhaitham/skill.go:281`), and its hits carry the elemental skill's internal cooldown tag, as gcsim's art tag does (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/alhaitham/skill.go:248`), since the tag set has no tag of its own for it.
- **The burst's and the projection's flat damage reads the striker's Elemental Mastery.** It is the hit's `additiveBaseDamageBonus`, and Ascension 4's DMG bonus per Elemental Mastery is `getStrikeDamageBonus` on the burst's and the projection's hits, which are the hits carrying the burst's and the elemental skill's tags (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/alhaithamKit.ts`, `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/alhaitham/burst.go:36`, `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/alhaitham/asc.go:50`).

- **The values gcsim does not state are `Unsourced`, each for its reason.** The second to fourth normal attacks' areas are boxes, which the reader gives from their own constructor at `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/alhaitham/attack.go:80`; a citation must name the row its radius reads, `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/alhaitham/attack.go:22`, and from that row the reader reads the circle at `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/alhaitham/attack.go:72`, so no citation matches its value. The Particular Field's hit count is "4+2*mirrorCount" in its loop at `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/alhaitham/burst.go:40`, so its hit count, its per-hit hitmarks and the Projection Attacks' hit counts are the Mirrors' count. The hits of the Particular Field take the wiki's 35 poise (the Particular Field page's Single-Instance row, `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Alhaitham_Talents.json`), and the Projection Attacks' hits take the wiki's 50 (the Universality page's Projection Attack rows, `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Alhaitham_Talents.json`), as gcsim gives neither group any. The universality delays' count is its two casts (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/alhaitham/skill.go:67`, `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/alhaitham/skill.go:97`), and the hold's cooldown is not stated by a line. The skill's gauge is the wiki's 1U on the Universality page's Rush Attack row (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Alhaitham_Talents.json`), as gcsim's rush hit states no gauge (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/alhaitham/skill.go:71` to `:80`). The Elemental Mastery shares are talent-table indices, not lines (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/alhaithamKit.ts`). Ascension 4's and Constellation 4's coefficients are scaled by a stat or a count on their line (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/alhaitham/asc.go:50`, `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/alhaitham/cons.go:63`, `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/alhaitham/cons.go:84`).
