# Alhaitham

Alhaitham's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createAlhaithamKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000078: five strikes, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, Universality: An Elaboration on Form with its tap and its hold, and Particular Field: Fetters of Phenomena with its hits. His multipliers are read from proud skill groups 7831, 7832 and 7839; the charged attack's stamina, the skill's cooldown, and the burst's cooldown and energy cost are read from the same groups. The skill and burst make Chisel-Light Mirrors, up to three, which his normal attacks, charged attack and plunges deal Dendro while he holds any and which trigger a Projection Attack that strikes once per Mirror. The Mirrors decay one every 233 frames, and a swap clears them. Ascension 1 and 4 and Constellations 1, 2, 4 and 6 are built, Constellation 1 cutting his skill's running cooldown by a Projection Attack's hit. The low plunge after his hold is not built, as the kit has no plunge variant to set. The hold's half-second threshold is provisional, and the Projection Attacks take the left set of their two hitmark sets where gcsim picks at random, one or two frames apart.

## Decisions

- **The Mirrors are a status for their count and a field for their decay.** A status holds the Mirrors as its stacks, and a field, unbounded and ticking every 233 frames, takes one away each tick until none is left. A new run's first Mirror or an overflow past three moves that one field back to its first tick, as gcsim's decay chain is superseded by the one that starts after it. The field never restarts as an effect, so it is moved rather than added.
- **The Dendro infusion is held while the Mirrors are.** It is the infusion of his normal attacks, charged attack and plunges, which the status and the infusion are both set to end on a swap or on the burst.
- **A Projection Attack triggers on each hit that strikes an enemy.** A normal attack, charged attack or plunge's `onStrike` runs it, and Ascension 1 makes a Mirror first from a charged attack or plunge, as gcsim's A1 callback does, so the count the projection reads includes it. Its own 1.6-second cooldown is a status, and its hits carry the elemental skill's internal cooldown tag, as gcsim's art tag does, since the tag set has no tag of its own for it.
- **The burst's and the projection's flat damage reads the striker's Elemental Mastery.** It is the hit's `additiveBaseDamageBonus`, and Ascension 4's DMG bonus per Elemental Mastery is `getStrikeDamageBonus` on the burst's and the projection's hits, which are the hits carrying the burst's and the elemental skill's tags.

- **The values gcsim does not state are `Unsourced`, each for its reason.** The One Mirror's area, the normal attacks' second to fourth areas and the Projection Attack's shape are boxes, which the area reader does not give as a circle. The tap's and the hold's areas are centred on the primary target with an offset, which it has no shape for. The Particular Field's hit count is "4+2*mirrorCount" in its loop at `burst.go:40`, so its hit count, its per-hit hitmarks and the Projection Attacks' hit counts are the Mirrors' count. The hits of the Projection Attacks and the Particular Field carry no poise in gcsim. The universality delays' count is its two casts, and the skill's gauge and the hold's cooldown are not stated by a line. The Elemental Mastery shares are talent-table indices, not lines. Ascension 4's and Constellation 4's coefficients are scaled by a stat or a count on their line. The skill's element "Grass" is the name of "attributes.Dendro", which the reader does not join to a record's element name.

## Not built

- **The low plunge after his hold.** gcsim plays a plunge of 38 frames with its animation at 70, reading the skill's hold as the last action. The kit has one low plunge, and no plunge variant that the skill's hold sets. It waits on a plunge variant of the skill's hold, as the burst's `elementalBurstVariant` is set.
