# Amber

Amber's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createAmberKitBehaviour`, avatar 10000021 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`): five arrows, a fully charged aimed shot, a collision and two plunges, Explosive Puppet's Baron Bunny and Fiery Rain (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Amber_Talents.json`). Its multipliers are read from proud skill groups 2131, 2132 and 2139 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), and match the wiki's Sharpshooter, Explosive Puppet and Fiery Rain values at level 1: group 2131's 0.3612, 0.4644, 0.473 and 0.5934 are the wiki's 36.12, 46.44, 47.3 and 59.34, and group 2132's 0.4136 is the Explosive Puppet's 41.4%. Baron Bunny is a taunt, built as the shared effects below, and Fiery Rain is a summon of eighteen arrows, with Ascension 1's CRIT Rate and AoE.

## Decisions

- **Amber's fully charged aimed shot is the bow's charged attack.** Only the fully charged shot deals Pyro (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/amber/aimed.go:77`), under the internal cooldown tag `ExtraAttack` (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/amber/aimed.go:74`), so the unlit Aimed Shot and its weak-point hits are not built. A bow's aimed shot (`isAimed`, `packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/stepActiveKit.ts`) turns the body to the camera's yaw while the aim binding, `R` on a keyboard, is held, and to its enemy by the wiki's score otherwise. The kit's hits are drawn from the turned body, so the shot lands along the aim.
- **Fiery Rain's arrows are priced at twice the burst radius.** Each arrow lands at random within a radius of 2 round the burst's centre (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/amber/amber.go:26`) and is itself a circle of that radius (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/amber/burst.go:47`), so a summon's arrow is priced at 4 round the body. gcsim states the radius once, at `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/amber/amber.go:26`, and scales it at Ascension (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/amber/asc.go:29`), so the record leaves the radius and the arrows' areas `Unsourced`: the field is written twice in the package, and a random landing point has no place to cite. The eighteen arrows are gcsim's three loops of five, three and ten (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/amber/burst.go:41` to `:69`). Ascension 1, Every Arrow Finds Its Target, adds 10% CRIT Rate (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/amber/asc.go:18`), priced on the summon's combatant (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/amberKit.ts`), and widens the radius by 30% (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/amber/asc.go:29`). The [character kits proposal](../../../../changes/genshin-character-kits/design.md) settles the arrow area at 4, which the record holds once it is republished, and Ascension 1's widening is built in `packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/amberKit.ts` as her passive, scaling the base arrows' areas by 30%.
- **Baron Bunny lands where Amber stands.** The throw is not built, so it is priced round Amber's body (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/amberKit.ts`), and the throw's distance is open, see [not built](#not-built). Its HP is 41.36% of Amber's Max HP, from group 2132's index 0 (`0.4136`, `~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`), which the wiki's Explosive Puppet page gives as 41.4% at level 1 (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Amber_Talents.json`).

## Not built

- **Constellation 6's Movement SPD is open, and its ten seconds stand over gcsim's fifteen.** gcsim's `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/amber/burst.go:71` to `:80` gives only the ATK share, 15% for 900 frames, to every member. The dump's talent `216` holds 0.15, 0.15 and 10 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarTalentExcelConfigData.json`), which fits the ten seconds, but its two 0.15s do not say which is the Movement SPD, so that share has no source. The game data is first, so the ten seconds stand.
- **The throw's distance is open.** The cached wiki's Explosive Puppet page says only that a longer hold throws the bunny further, so the 1.4 metres the design once cited has no source.
- **Amber's A4 is left.** Its ATK bonus after a weak-point hit now has a hit to follow: the strike gives the Ruin machines' weak points to an aimed hit (provisional, see the kits README). The bonus itself is not built, so it stays left.
- **Amber's Constellation 2 is left.** Its manual detonation needs a charged attack that reports the bunny it lands on: `ChargedAttackLanded` names no target, and `damageKitTaunt` has no caller, so no shot reaches the bunny.
