# Gorou

Gorou's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createGorouKitBehaviour`, avatar 10000055: four strikes, a fully charged shot, a collision and two plunges, Inuzaka All-Round Defense with its General's War Banner, and Juuga with its field that follows the body and its Crystal Collapse. His multipliers are read from proud skill groups 5531, 5532 and 5539; the skill's cooldown and the banner's seconds, and the burst's seconds, cooldown and energy cost, are read from the same groups. The banner's flat DEF and Crunch's 15% Geo DMG Bonus reach the active character on each tick for two seconds, Crunch from a team of three Geo characters, and Juuga's ticks do the same from its field. From Ascension 1 Juuga adds 25% DEF to the party for twelve seconds, and from Ascension 4 the skill and burst add a share of his DEF to their base damage. Constellations 1, 4 and 6 are built: Constellation 1 reads the Geo hits in the field's circle from the enemy hit events, Constellation 4 heals the active character from a field's own ticks, and Constellation 6 gives the party's Geo strikes CRIT DMG through the element CRIT effect. Constellation 2 needs a crystal shard.

## Decisions

- **Juuga's field follows the body, so its centre is the body's ground point on each step.** The field moves with the active character, as gcsim's field does, and its radius is unbounded, so its buffs reach the character on the field wherever it goes. A following field takes the same step as a following summon, and no other field moves.
- **Crystal Collapse is one following summon with its six ticks set out at the cast.** A field's tick has no body to strike from, and a summon that follows the body holds every tick, which ends where the field does, 9 seconds from the hit, as gcsim's collapse stops there.
- **Constellation 1 reads the Geo hits in the banner's or Juuga's circle as enemy hit events, once every 10 seconds.** gcsim (`gorou/cons.go`) cuts Inuzaka All-Round Defense's cooldown by 2 seconds for a Geo hit by another character inside the General's War Banner's or General's Glory's area, with an internal cooldown of 10 seconds. Both circles are 8 metres, and Juuga's field is unbounded, so the circle is checked from its centre here rather than read from the field's radius. The cut is `changeKitSkillCooldown`, and the cooldown is a status on Gorou.
- **Constellation 6's CRIT DMG is an element CRIT effect on every party member's Geo, restarted by each cast.** gcsim (`gorou/cons.go`) gives every character a Geo attack modifier for 12 seconds, at 10%, 20% or 40% for one, two or three Geo characters on the team at the cast. The effect's source names it, so the next cast restarts each member's effect rather than stacking a second, and the team's Geo count is `countGeoCharacters`, which the Crunch count reads too.
- **Constellation 4's heal is a non-stacked field keyed by its id, so a new burst replaces the one standing.** gcsim (`gorou/burst.go`) queues the heal 1.5 seconds after the burst's hit and reruns it every 1.5 seconds while General's Glory stands, healing the active character by half of Gorou's DEF at the cast, after Ascension 1's buff, at his live Healing Bonus, and only when two Geo characters are on the team at the cast. The field lasts as long as Juuga's, so its ticks stop where the buffs stop, and the id's restart replaces the chain as gcsim's `qFieldSrc` does. The heal is `changeKitMemberHealth`, which the kit's other heals use too.
