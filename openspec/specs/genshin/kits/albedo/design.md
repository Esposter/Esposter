# Albedo

Albedo's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createAlbedoKitBehaviour`, avatar 10000038: five strikes, a charged attack of two hits, a collision and two plunges, Abiogenesis: Solar Isotoma with its hit and the Transient Blossoms it sets off, and Rite of Progeniture: Tectonic Tide with its Fatal Blossoms. His multipliers are read from proud skill groups 3831, 3832 and 3839; the charged attack's stamina, the field's seconds and cooldown, and the burst's cooldown and energy cost are read from the same groups. A Transient Blossom's tick is priced by his DEF, and the Fatal Blossoms come only while the field stands. Constellation 1 is built: each Transient Blossom regenerates 1.2 Energy for Albedo, and his Solar Isotoma raises his DEF by 50% for 20 seconds. Both passives are built: from Ascension 1, Calcite Might makes a Transient Blossom deal 25% more DMG to an enemy under half its HP, read as the blossom strikes, and from Ascension 4 the team's Elemental Mastery rises under Tectonic Tide. Constellation 2 is built: each Transient Blossom gives Albedo a Fatal Reckoning stack, which Tectonic Tide adds to each Fatal Blossom but not to its own first hit. Constellation 4's plunging attack bonus is built, as a buff on the active member alone on the Plunging Attack bonus attribute, checked on gcsim's 0.3-second clock. Constellation 6 is not built, as it reads the shield a Crystallize gives a member at a strike, which the kit does not hold.

## Decisions

- **Transient Blossoms trigger on a hit inside the Solar Isotoma, once every 2 seconds.** gcsim's field strikes on damage in its area behind one 2-second internal cooldown, and here the hit's enemy must stand within the field's radius of 10, the tick landing at that enemy a frame later. The tick's multiple is of DEF, so its hit takes no ATK, and the Fatal Blossoms come with the burst while the field stands.
- **Fatal Blossoms take their hitmarks from one cited array, and their area is `Unsourced`.** gcsim queues the seven blossoms in a loop of "maxBlossoms" at line 96, `fatalBlossomHitmark+i*5`, inside the burst's queue with `burstHitmark` as its delay, so the overlay cites `hits.fatalBlossoms.0-6.hitmarkFrames` as one array of 145 to 175 by element. Their area is a circle of 3 on the blossom's own centre, which the hitbox reader does not resolve, and their element, gauge, cooldown tag, blunt flag and multiplier are shared by the run and `Unsourced` with the same reason.
- **Fatal Reckoning is flat damage on each Fatal Blossom, not on the burst's first hit.** gcsim adds 30% of Albedo's DEF per stack as flat damage to the burst's first hit and to the blossoms, once at the burst's start (`burst.go`, `cons.go`), and the record reads that DEF with her buffs on, as her blossoms are priced. The record plays the burst's first hit with no per-cast override, so only the blossoms carry the bonus, and the first hit's share is the open part of Constellation 2.

- **Constellation 4's plunge bonus is the active member's buff on an attribute, read at pricing, on gcsim's 0.3-second clock.** gcsim's `cons.go` raises the Plunging Attack DMG of the active party member while it stands in the standing Solar Isotoma's field, for a second, and checks the field every 0.3 seconds from the skill's hitmark, when the Solar Isotoma is made. Albedo's kit starts the check's status at the press, for the hitmark plus 0.3 seconds, so the first check runs 0.3 seconds after the Solar Isotoma's spawn, and each check starts a 0.3-second status of its own. A check that finds the field standing refreshes a buff of 30% on `Attribute.PlungingAttackDamageBonus` for the active member alone, which `strikeEnemy` adds to a plunge's damage bonus, so a teammate's plunge is not raised. A Solar Isotoma whose seconds have run out is not standing, though the step has not yet dropped it, so neither a check nor the burst's Fatal Blossoms take it.
