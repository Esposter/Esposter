# Mika

Mika's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createMikaKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000080 (`{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`): five strikes, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges (`{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Mika_Talents.json`), Starfrost Swirl's press, which is a Cryo box of 2.5 by 10 metres 0.4 metres behind the body (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/mika/skill.go:89`), and its held form, Rimestar Flare, a Cryo hit on the primary target (the wiki's Hold text in `{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Mika_Talents.json`) that the held skill releases after half a second (the first Open item below). His burst, Skyfeather Song, heals every party member once at its 51st frame (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/mika/burst.go:15`, which gcsim says depends on ping; the heal at `burst.go` line 33 has `Target: -1`, which `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/pkg/core/info/heal.go` line 5 says heals all characters, as the wiki's Skyfeather Song text does for all nearby party members). His multipliers are read from proud skill groups 8031, 8032 and 8039 (`{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`); the charged attack's stamina (group 8031's index 7, 25.0, the wiki's Charged Attack stamina of 25), the skill's 15-second cooldown, and the burst's 18-second cooldown and 70 energy cost are read from the same groups (rows 10802, the skill, and 10805, the burst, in `{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarSkillExcelConfigData.json`, cdTime 15.0 and 18.0; group 8039's index 7, 70.0; the wiki's CD 15s and 18s, energy cost 70, `{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Mika_Talents.json`).

Not built yet: Rimestar Shard, which strikes three random enemies within 10 metres of the body after the flare (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/mika/skill.go` lines 179 to 183), as it needs a strike on three chosen enemies rather than all in reach; Eagleplume, the party's heal on each member's normal attack (the wiki's Skyfeather Song text, `{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Mika_Talents.json`), which waits on a reaction to another member's attacks; the ATK SPD bonus Starfrost Swirl gives the team (the wiki's Starfrost Swirl text, same file), as the attributes do not yet hold attack speed; and his Ascension and Constellation passives.

Gcsim gives Mika's plunges no file, so the design takes Bennett's timings and reaches (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/bennett/plunge.go`), and the wiki's poise: 25 for the collision, and 100 and 150 for the low and high plunges (`{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Mika_Talents.json`, rows 8 to 10). `packages/genshin-world/src/data/kits/mika.json` carries no plunge keys yet. The flare's hold threshold is the half second gcsim does not give (its hold is a parameter, `{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/mika/skill.go` line 51), and his strikes and charged attack take the wiki's poise rows: 40.245, 38.606 and 50.701 for the first three strikes, 25.68 for each hit of the fourth, 65.93 for the fifth and 120 for the charged attack (rows 1 to 7, `{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Mika_Talents.json`). Each is provisional until a recording settles it.

## Provisional constants

- **The initial heal's parameters are `Unsourced` in the record.** `parameters.initialHealFlatHealth` and `parameters.initialHealMaxHealthShare` read group 8039's indices 0 and 1, but gcsim's burst arrays give the same heal (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/mika/burst.go` lines 28 to 36, with the arrays at `zz_mika.dm.go` lines 232 and 250, whose first entries, 1172.0355 and 0.12168, are group 8039's indices 0 and 1 at level 1), so the causes should name gcsim.
- **The heal's 51st frame depends on ping.** gcsim's `initialHeal` is marked so (`{data-root}/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/mika/burst.go` line 15), so 51 is a measured value, not a fixed one.
