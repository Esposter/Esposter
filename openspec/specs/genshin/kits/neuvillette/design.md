# Neuvillette

Neuvillette's kit is built from its record alone, on avatar 10000087 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/AvatarExcelConfigData.json`), and `createNeuvilletteKitBehaviour` holds no behaviour of its own: three hydro strikes, a collision and two plunges, O Tears, I Shall Repay with its Spiritbreath Thorn, and O Tides, I Have Returned with its first hit and its two Waterfalls, and the burst's three ticks of Sourcewater Droplets (`~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Neuvillette_Talents.json`). His multipliers are read from proud skill groups 8731, 8732 and 8739 (`~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`); the skill's cooldown, and the burst's cooldown and energy cost are read from the same groups (the skill's 12 seconds is group 8732's index 3, as gcsim's `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/neuvillette/skill.go:94` sets it; the burst's 18 seconds is group 8739's index 2, `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/neuvillette/burst.go:140`, and its 70 energy is index 3, the wiki's `energycost=70`). His skill and burst hits deal their share of Max HP as a flat base damage, as gcsim gives them (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/neuvillette/skill.go:53`, `burst.go:54`, `:65`), so each takes the table's share times the wearer's Max HP; Spiritbreath Thorn alone is a share of ATK, Hydro with no gauge, landing 60 frames after the skill is pressed, 18 frames past its 42-frame animation (`skill.go:60`, `:89`, `:92`), as the second Waterfall lands at 154 frames, 19 past the burst's 135-frame animation (`burst.go:18`, `:32`).
Provisional: gcsim gives Neuvillette no plunge file (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/neuvillette/` has `dash.go`, `jump.go` and `walk.go`, but no `plunge.go`), so his plunges take Bennett's timings and reaches, with his own multipliers from the table's plunge entries; their poise is the wiki's, 100 high and 50 low (`As Water Seeks Equilibrium` rows 9 and 10 in `Category_Neuvillette_Talents.json`). Equitable Judgment's eight waves are the record's named hits `judgement`, their box priced as a circle of its length (`BoxAsCircle`) and their poise the wiki's 25 (rows 5 and 6, `~/Esposter/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Neuvillette_Talents.json`); the charge's own record holds no hits and no stamina, as `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/neuvillette/charge.go` spends none.

Built as well: Equitable Judgment, held, its length decided as it runs (`KitAction.decideLength`) from the Sourcewater Droplets its legal evaluation picks up (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/neuvillette/charge.go:23`, `:25`, `:96`, `:139`, with the 51-frame end lag at `:41`), with its waves at the frames `~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/neuvillette/charge.go:208-210` gives them, its HP drains (`charge.go:133-134`) and a 16% heal per droplet picked up (`charge.go:310`). Not built: the waves' internal cooldown, which the enum has no tag for; the end lag of the action the charge is cancelled into, the default of 51 frames used; Spiritbreath Thorn's aligned 10-second cooldown, which the skill's own 12 always outlast; his Ascension 1, which raises Equitable Judgment by the reactions it counts; and Constellations 1, 2 and 6. Ascension 4's Hydro DMG Bonus, read from his HP share (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/neuvillette/asc.go` lines 111 to 121), is built as a passive bonus. Constellation 4 is built: each heal that restores him on the field spawns a Sourcewater Droplet 8 metres ahead, 1.3 to 3 metres from that point, once every 4 seconds, as gcsim's `cons.go` gives it (`~/Esposter/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/neuvillette/cons.go:47-61`, `:63-72`).
