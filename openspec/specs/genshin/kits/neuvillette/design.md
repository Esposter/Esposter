# Neuvillette

Neuvillette's kit is built from its record alone, on avatar 10000087, and `createNeuvilletteKitBehaviour` holds no behaviour of its own: three hydro strikes, a collision and two plunges, O Tears, I Shall Repay with its Spiritbreath Thorn, and O Tides, I Have Returned with its first hit and its two Waterfalls, and the burst's three ticks of Sourcewater Droplets. His multipliers are read from proud skill groups 8731, 8732 and 8739; the skill's cooldown, and the burst's cooldown and energy cost are read from the same groups. His skill and burst hits deal their share of Max HP as a flat base damage, as gcsim gives them, so each takes the table's share times the wearer's Max HP; Spiritbreath Thorn alone is a share of ATK, Hydro with no gauge, landing at 60 frames past the skill's 42-frame animation, as the second Waterfall lands at 154 past the burst's 135.

Provisional: gcsim gives Neuvillette no plunge file, so his plunges take Bennett's timings, reaches and poise, with his own multipliers from the table's plunge entries. Equitable Judgment's eight waves are the record's named hits `judgement`, their box priced as a circle of its length (`BoxAsCircle`) and their poise absent from gcsim (`NoPoiseSource`); the charge's own record holds no hits and no stamina, as the judgement spends none.

Built as well: Equitable Judgment, held, its length decided as it runs (`KitAction.decideLength`) from the Sourcewater Droplets its legal evaluation picks up, with its waves, its HP drains and a 16% heal per droplet picked up. Not built: the waves' internal cooldown, which the enum has no tag for; the end lag of the action the charge is cancelled into, the default of 51 frames used; Spiritbreath Thorn's aligned 10-second cooldown, which the skill's own 12 always outlast; his Ascension 1, which raises Equitable Judgment by the reactions it counts; and his constellations. Ascension 4's Hydro DMG Bonus, read from his HP share, is built as a passive bonus.
