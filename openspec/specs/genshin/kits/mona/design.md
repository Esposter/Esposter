# Mona

Mona's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createMonaKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000041: four strikes, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, all Hydro catalyst hits, Mirror Reflection of Doom and Stellaris Phantasm's bubble. Its multipliers are read from proud skill groups 4131, 4132 and 4139, and match the wiki's Ripple of Fate, Mirror Reflection of Doom and Stellaris Phantasm values to two decimal places. Mirror Reflection is a summon, built as the [shared effects](../README.md); the bubble, its explosion and its Omen are built. A1's phantom and A4's Hydro bonus are built too, and Constellation 2's follow-up is built as a Charged Attack's hit after a normal attack. Constellation 1's Frozen duration and Hexerei's post-burst follow-up are not built.

## Decisions

- **Mona's strikes are priced round her body.** gcsim centres them on the primary target (`attack.go` lines 16 to 17 and 50 to 54), which an area does not hold yet. Her plunges' reach is provisional: gcsim has no plunge file for her and `mona.json` carries no plunge keys, so the reach is the kit's own until a recording settles it. Their poise is the wiki's 100 high and 50 low (`Category_Mona_Talents.json`, rows 7 and 8).
- **Mona's A1 phantom is cast from a sprint.** `onSprint` spends each 2 seconds of the sprint on a phantom, cast where Mona stands, which explodes at its end for half of Mirror Reflection's explosion, 0.664 at level 1, half of the explosion's 1.328 at group 4132's index 1 (`asc.go` lines 27 to 58, the 5-metre explosion at line 58). gcsim casts one only when an opponent is within 15 metres at each 2-second check (`asc.go` lines 36 to 42 and 63, rechecked from `dash.go` line 50), and the kit makes no such check. The two differ when an opponent comes within 5 metres of a phantom that gcsim would not have cast, which is open (see Provisional constants).
- **Mona's A4 is a passive bonus read as a hit is priced.** `getPassiveBonuses` adds 20% of her Energy Recharge to her Hydro DMG Bonus in `getBuffedCombatant`, for every hit she prices, her summons' included.
- **Stellaris Phantasm's Omen runs from the burst.** Each enemy its cast strikes is held in a bubble for the burst group's first value, 8 seconds (`burst.go` line 55, 481 frames, group 4139's index 0). A hit with poise damage bursts the bubble, as its seconds running out do (`burst.go` lines 137 to 151 and 77, and the wiki's Stellaris Phantasm notes for the poise rule), and the Omen goes on from then: 4 seconds from the burst group's index 3 (`burst.go` line 159, `zz_mona.dm.go`'s `omenDuration`), and the 42% DMG bonus from index 9 (`zz_mona.dm.go`'s `dmgBonus`), which raises the damage of every hit on the enemy. The `checkdm` run matched both arrays to group 4139's indices 3 and 9. The wiki's exception, that a Frozen enemy takes no poise damage and keeps its bubble, is not modelled (see Provisional constants).
- **Stellaris Phantasm's explosion lands on the one enemy its bubble burst on, on the step after the burst.** A burst deals 2U of Hydro (`burst.go` line 173) with 200 poise (the wiki's Illusory Bubble Explosion DMG row, `Category_Mona_Talents.json`), at the explosion's multiplier from the burst group's index 1, 442.4% at level 1 (`burst.go` line 174). gcsim fires it as a single-target hit one frame after the burst (`burst.go` lines 175 to 176), and applies the bubble to each enemy within 10 metres of the player (`burst.go` line 59), so the explosions land on the enemies the bubble caught and no others. The kit's bubble does the same: its explosion strikes the enemy alone, from where it stands (`stepKitBubble`), and the Omen goes on at the burst without waiting for it (`burstKitBubble`). A single-target call gives no area, so the record's `burst.hits.0.area` keeps the bubble's 10 metres as `DiffersFromSource` (the [kits README](../README.md) states the rule). The explosion's own record area, also 10, is `Unsourced` and stays open.
- **Constellation 6's sprint stacks are kit state on the Charged Attack.** Each second of sprint on the field adds a stack, up to three, to a buff of 60% DMG that the Charged Attack's damage hook reads. A sprint's first step starts a one-second clock, and each second it runs out while she still sprints adds the stack, which refreshes the buff's eight seconds. A sprint status runs out 0.1 seconds after the sprint ends (the kit's own grace, see Provisional constants), so the next sprint restarts the clock without adding a stack; the stacks the buff still holds stay, as gcsim's do. The Charged Attack's last hit resets the buff, after it has struck (`cons.go` line 301). The buff lasts 8 seconds (line 240), gives 60% per stack (line 249), caps at three stacks (line 396), and its one-second ticker is queued at line 361. Her Hexerei's share of Constellation 6 is not built.
- **Constellation 2's follow-up is one Charged Attack hit at the normal attack's hitmark.** Each normal attack's last hit has a 20% chance, drawn from the world's seeded source, to be followed by her Charged Attack's hit, at most once in 5 seconds, as gcsim's `cons.go` gives it (line 120 for the 20% roll and line 127 for the 5-second status). gcsim plays a whole Charged Attack 53 frames after the normal attack's hit (line 129), and the kit's one hit lands with that hit instead, as an extra hit does, so its damage is the Charged Attack's and its timing is 53 frames early. Her Hexerei post-burst follow-up and the Elemental Mastery share her Charged Attack gives are not built, as Hexerei is not modelled.
- **Constellation 1's bonus is a status on the striker that each hit on the Omen sets.** Mona's `onKitEvent` reads each `EnemyHit` on an enemy carrying the Omen and sets an 8-second status on the striker's character id, and `getPartyReactionBonus` reads it for the reacting member. The hit's reactions are priced before its `EnemyHit` event, so the hit that sets it is left out, as gcsim leaves it out (`cons.go` lines 59 and 61 for the 8 seconds and 15%, lines 66 to 68 for the 160% share of an off-field member). The bonus reads a Swirl only when the element it absorbed is Hydro, which the `KitReaction` carries, so an Anemo or a Pyro Swirl gets none.
- **Constellation 4 reads the Omen or the bubble on the enemy in each strike and Lunar reaction hook.** `getPartyStrikeBonus` gives every other member's strike 15% CRIT Rate, and Mona's own strikes read `getStrikeCriticalRateBonus` and `getStrikeCriticalDamageBonus`, as a member's own kit is left out of the party hook. Her Lunar-Charged and Lunar-Crystallize reactions read it in `getPartyReactionBonus`, which carries the enemy the reaction lands on, so the same CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG reach them. gcsim's Constellation 4 reads the Omen or the bubble at `cons.go` lines 186, 206, 277 and 334, its CRIT Rate at lines 161 to 169, its Hexerei CRIT DMG at lines 180 to 181, and the Lunar reactions at lines 194 to 228. The Hexerei count, two or more members, is the framework's and is not checked here.

## Provisional constants

- **Her plunge reach is the kit's own.** gcsim has no plunge file for Mona and `mona.json` has no plunge keys, so the reach stays provisional until a recording settles it.
- **The explosion's 2U and 200 poise are `Unsourced` in the record.** `mona.json` marks `burst.bubble.explosion.gauge` and `poiseDamage` `Unsourced`, though gcsim gives the 2U (`burst.go` line 173) and the wiki gives the 200, so their causes should name those sources. The record is data, and this pass does not change it.
- **The A1 phantom's 15-metre check is not made.** gcsim casts a phantom at each 2-second check only while an opponent is within 15 metres (`asc.go` line 40); the kit casts one at each 2 seconds of sprint regardless. The two differ when an opponent comes within 5 metres of a phantom gcsim would not have cast. Open: no source or recording here settles whether the game makes the check.
- **The 0.1-second sprint grace is the kit's own.** gcsim checks for a sprint at each one-second tick with no grace (`cons.go` lines 376 to 396). Open for the same reason.
- **Constellation 1's Frozen duration is open.** The wiki's talent note says it lengthens Frozen by 15%, and gcsim marks it a bugged TODO (`cons.go` line 39). No recording here settles which is right.

## Not yet built

- **Constellation 1's Frozen duration, 15% longer, is not built.** The wiki's talent note says it lengthens Frozen, but gcsim marks it a bugged TODO and leaves it out, so no hook extends a Frozen; the [combat](../../combat/design.md) page's Frozen duration is the gauge formula (line 62). Which is right is open (see Provisional constants).
