# Mona

Mona's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createMonaKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000041: four strikes, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, all Hydro catalyst hits, Mirror Reflection of Doom and Stellaris Phantasm's bubble. Its multipliers are read from proud skill groups 4131, 4132 and 4139, and match the wiki's Ripple of Fate, Mirror Reflection of Doom and Stellaris Phantasm values to two decimal places. Mirror Reflection is a summon, built as the [shared effects](../README.md); the bubble, its explosion and its Omen are built. A1's phantom and A4's Hydro bonus are built too, and Constellation 2's follow-up is built as a Charged Attack's hit after a normal attack. Constellation 1's Frozen duration and Hexerei's post-burst follow-up are not built.

## Decisions

- **Mona's strikes are priced round her body.** gcsim centres them on the primary target, which an area does not hold yet. Her plunges' reach is provisional.
- **Mona's A1 phantom is cast from a sprint.** `onSprint` spends each 2 seconds of the sprint on a phantom, cast where Mona stands, which explodes at its end for half of Mirror Reflection's explosion. The wiki's check for an opponent within 15 metres is not made: with none in the phantom's 5-metre explosion it deals nothing, so the check changes no damage.
- **Mona's A4 is a passive bonus read as a hit is priced.** `getPassiveBonuses` adds 20% of her Energy Recharge to her Hydro DMG Bonus in `getBuffedCombatant`, for every hit she prices, her summons' included.
- **Stellaris Phantasm's Omen runs from the burst.** Each enemy its cast strikes is held in a bubble for the burst group's first value, 8 seconds. A hit with poise damage bursts the bubble, as its seconds running out do, and the Omen goes on from then: 4 seconds from the burst group's index 3, and the 42% DMG bonus from index 9, which raises the damage of every hit on the enemy. The wiki's exception, that a Frozen enemy takes no poise damage and keeps its bubble, is not modelled, as the enemies' poise does not model it for any hit.
- **Stellaris Phantasm's explosion lands on each enemy the bubble caught, and its area is the bubble's 10 metres.** A burst deals 2U of Hydro with the wiki's 200 poise, at the explosion's multiplier from the burst group's index 1, 442.4% at level 1. gcsim fires it at each enemy as a single-target hit (`burst.go:176`), which alone would reach the targeting area's 15 metres. The bubble decides which enemies it lands on: gcsim applies the bubble to each enemy within 10 metres of the player (`burst.go:59`), so the explosion lands on those and no others, and its area is that circle's 10 metres. A single-target call cannot say which enemies the bubble caught, so the overlay keeps the bubble's 10 as `DiffersFromSource`. It lands on the step after the burst, as a taunt's does, and the Omen does not wait for it.
- **Constellation 6's sprint stacks are kit state on the Charged Attack.** Each second of sprint on the field adds a stack, up to three, to a buff of 60% DMG that the Charged Attack's damage hook reads. A sprint's first step starts a one-second clock, and each second it runs out while she still sprints adds the stack, which refreshes the buff's eight seconds. A sprint status runs out a moment after the sprint ends, so the next sprint restarts the clock without adding a stack; the stacks the buff still holds stay, as gcsim's do. The Charged Attack's last hit resets the buff, after it has struck. Her Hexerei's share of Constellation 6 is not built.
- **Constellation 2's follow-up is one Charged Attack hit at the normal attack's hitmark.** Each normal attack's last hit has a 20% chance, drawn from the world's seeded source, to be followed by her Charged Attack's hit, at most once in 5 seconds, as gcsim's `cons.go` gives it. gcsim plays a whole Charged Attack 53 frames after the normal attack's hit, and the kit's one hit lands with that hit instead, as an extra hit does, so its damage is the Charged Attack's and its timing is 53 frames early. Her Hexerei post-burst follow-up and the Elemental Mastery share her Charged Attack gives are not built, as Hexerei is not modelled.
- **Constellation 1's bonus is a status on the striker that each hit on the Omen sets.** Mona's `onKitEvent` reads each `EnemyHit` on an enemy carrying the Omen and sets an 8-second status on the striker's character id, and `getPartyReactionBonus` reads it for the reacting member. The hit's reactions are priced before its `EnemyHit` event, so the hit that sets it is left out, as gcsim leaves it out. The bonus reads a Swirl only when the element it absorbed is Hydro, which the `KitReaction` carries, so an Anemo or a Pyro Swirl gets none.
- **Constellation 4 reads the Omen or the bubble on the enemy in each strike and Lunar reaction hook.** `getPartyStrikeBonus` gives every other member's strike 15% CRIT Rate, and Mona's own strikes read `getStrikeCriticalRateBonus` and `getStrikeCriticalDamageBonus`, as a member's own kit is left out of the party hook. Her Lunar-Charged and Lunar-Crystallize reactions read it in `getPartyReactionBonus`, which carries the enemy the reaction lands on, so the same CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG reach them. Hexerei is counted from the party's members, two or more.

## Not yet built

- **Constellation 1's Frozen duration, 15% longer, has no effect, as it has none in the game.** The wiki's notes find it does not change Frozen's duration, and gcsim leaves it out as bugged, so no hook extends a Frozen, as the [combat](../../combat/design.md) page records.
