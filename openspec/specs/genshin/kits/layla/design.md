# Layla

Layla's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createLaylaKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000074: three strikes, a charged attack of two slashes, a collision and two plunges, Nights of Formal Focus with its Cryo hit and the shield it sets on the team, and Starlight Slug's field, which strikes from the body's place every 1.5 seconds for twelve seconds. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 7431, 7432 and 7439; the charged attack's stamina, the skill's cooldown and shield, and the burst's cooldown and energy cost are read from the same groups. The burst's strike is its share of her Max HP as flat damage, which her talent multiplier does not scale. Starlight Slug's tick reaches every enemy within 13.5 metres of the body, where gcsim picks the closest one, which overstates a tick against several enemies. Her Night Stars are built, with the Shooting Stars they fire, and so are her Ascension 4 and Constellation 2 passives, Constellation 4's Dawn Star, and Constellation 6's 40% DMG and shorter Night Stars tick. Her Ascension 1 and Constellation 1's team shields are not built. Her plunges take Bennett's timings and the wiki's poise.

## Decisions

- **Starlight Slug's ticks are the field's strikes, so `burst.hits` is empty.** gcsim queues eight ticks across the burst's twelve seconds, each striking from the field, so the record reads them as `hits.starlightSlugStrike` and keeps `burst.hits` empty, where gcsim counts eight.
- **Layla's plunges take Bennett's timings and the wiki's poise.** gcsim has no plunge file for her, so her record lands the collision, low and high plunge at Bennett's frames, 36 for the low hit and 38 for the high, with the wiki's 100 and 150 poise on the low and high and its 25 on the collision, and her own multipliers. Her animation would move them if it showed otherwise.
- **Constellation 6 is a DMG Bonus on the hit and a shorter tick.** Starlight Slug's ticks and each Shooting Star carry 40% DMG Bonus on their own hits, where gcsim's attack mod names the burst's attacks and the Shooting Stars, and the Night Stars' tick is 1.2 seconds, the 1.5 seconds scaled by gcsim's 0.8, from the tick's field.
- **Constellation 1's Curtain absorbs 20% more, as its shield's health is scaled.** The shield the skill sets is the one it sets for Layla; the team shields the same constellation gives members not under a Curtain need the Cryo effectiveness a shield does not carry, so they wait.
- **The Shooting Star hit is recorded from gcsim's `shield.go`, and it fires once four Night Stars are built.** Its travel is 35, 33, 30 and 28 frames for four, three, two and one Night Stars (`frames.shootingStarTravels`), and its hitmark is the four-star 35. Its circle is 0.8 m, centred on the target (`TargetCentred`), dealt as Ice (gcsim's Cryo) with one unit of gauge and multiplier group 7432 index 1 (starDmg, 0.1472 at level 1 to 0.26496 at level 10). Its element, gauge and multiplier are read from gcsim through the named hit of starDmg, joined by that multiplier, wherever the queue call sits in the character's folder. Its hitmark is the one value still `Unclassified`, because gcsim's delay for it is the travel of the stars left, which the reader cannot evaluate. The kit builds the Night Stars as gcsim's `shield.go` has them: the shield's tick adds one every 1.5 seconds, a skill adds two and a burst strike one while the shield is up, each under its internal cooldown of 0.3 and 0.5 seconds, and the stars cap at four. Four stars fire one Shooting Star each, the first 0.6 seconds after the fourth star or as soon as an enemy is within 10 metres after that, then one every 0.45 seconds, each at the nearest living enemy in reach, or when none is in reach, at the one the search or the star before it aimed at, as gcsim's `shootStars` keeps its last target. Ascension 4 adds 1.5% of her Max HP as flat damage to each shot, and Constellation 2 gives her 1 Energy on a shot's first hit.

- **Constellation 4's Dawn Star is granted by the first Shooting Star and priced by the kit's strike hooks.** gcsim's `shootStars` (`shield.go`, lines 112 to 117) gives `layla-c4` for three seconds to every party member when the first Shooting Star fires, and its `c4` (`cons.go`, lines 18 to 38) adds 5% of Layla's Max HP as flat damage to each Normal or Charged Attack a holder lands on an enemy, removing the status 0.05 seconds later. The kit prices her own strikes through its own strike hook and each member's through its party strike hook, both off her Max HP, and removes a holder's status 0.05 seconds after its `EnemyHit`, so a hit on several enemies is priced on each as gcsim's `OnEnemyHit` prices it.
