# Ningguang

Ningguang's kit is its record and one behaviour module, `createNingguangKitBehaviour`, on avatar 10000027: two strikes in turn, each of two gems, a charged attack, a collision and two plunges, Jade Screen's hit and its screen, and Starshatter's six gems. Her multipliers are read from proud skill groups 2731, 2732 and 2739, and match the wiki's Sparkling Scatter, Jade Screen and Starshatter values to two decimal places; the charged attack's stamina, the skill's cooldown, and the burst's cooldown and cost are read from the same groups. Every gem lands from a summon, and each strike whose gems strike an enemy gives her a Star Jade up to three, a field that ends as she leaves the field, which her charged attack fires beside its own gem. The screen is a summon standing 3 metres ahead of her for 30 seconds, which lands the skill's hit round it, and Starshatter shatters it into six gems more, priced as she stood when she cast it. Backup Plan's free charged attack from Ascension 1 is built, and Piercing Fragments' wider gems, Shock Effect's cooldown reset and Grandeur Be the Seven Stars' seven Star Jades from one, two and six constellations. Strategic Reserve, the screen's endurance and its blocking of projectiles, the skill's particles, Exquisite be the Jade, Outshining All Beneath, and the seven jades' expiry are not built.

## Decisions

- **Ningguang's strikes are Left and Twirl in turn, and her charged attack is the Left charge.** gcsim draws each strike at random from the two of Left, Right and Twirl the last was not, and the framework plays a kit's strikes in their order, so the string plays Left and Twirl in turn, one such draw, from Left, gcsim's strike after a dash. After either, gcsim plays the faster Left charge, so the kit's one charged attack is exact after every strike. It skips 15 frames of its windup after a strike, so its gem lands at 30 frames, its Star Jades at 40, or 35 for seven, and it ends at 37. Each strike's two gems land 10 frames past gcsim's hitmarks, its default travel, at 39 and 37 frames, and the strikes end at 61 and 66.
- **Ningguang's gems land from summons, priced round her body.** Each strike, the charged attack, the skill and the burst casts its hits from a summon as it starts, priced as she stood then, as gcsim snapshots an attack as it queues it, and landing whatever cancels the action, as gcsim's queued attacks do. gcsim centres every gem on the primary target, which an area does not hold, and that target lies within the strikes' 5-metre targeting, so a gem reaches those 5 metres plus its radius: 5.5 for a strike's gem, a Star Jade and a Starshatter gem, 6.5 for the charged attack's, and 8.5 for a strike's gem from Piercing Fragments, from one constellation. Jade Screen's hit is gcsim's circle of 5 metres round the screen itself.
- **A Star Jade is a field gained as a strike's gem strikes.** The first of a strike's two gems to strike an enemy gives Ningguang a Star Jade unless she holds 3, as the wiki and gcsim give it on the gem's hit, and gcsim's one guarded callback over both gems gives one a strike: a field with no edge that ends on the swap's kit event once she has left the field, as gcsim clears the jades on a swap. Her Star Jades are the fields of hers that tick each step. A hit gives its striker something back through its `onStrike`, run for each enemy it strikes, so a strike whose gems miss gives none. A charged attack fires a shot for every jade she holds beside its own gem, and spends them.
- **Jade Screen is a summon, and Starshatter shatters it into gems priced as she stood when she cast it.** The skill's start shatters a screen she has standing, as only one stands, and casts a summon 3 metres ahead of where she faces for gcsim's 1800 frames, which lands the skill's hit round it at 17 frames, 1U of Geo with the wiki's 133.2 poise. Starshatter's start casts its six gems at gcsim's 62 to 124 frames, and shatters a standing screen into six gems more at 154 frames, gcsim's 30 past the last, which gcsim marks as still to measure, from a summon priced by the screen's caster, as gcsim snapshots them with the skill. gcsim checks only that a screen stands, not that it is the wiki's "nearby", and so does the kit. Each gem is the wiki's 1U where gcsim gives 2U.
- **Shock Effect is a field that resets the skill's cooldown and stands as its own gate.** From two constellations, a shatter by a recast or by Starshatter casts a one-tick field, on the next step. A running cooldown is reset there and the field stands 6 seconds, gcsim's 360 frames, while no shatter casts another; with none running it ends at once, as gcsim marks no reset then. It is the field of hers that ticks once, as a Star Jade ticks each step.
- **Grandeur Be the Seven Stars replaces Ningguang's Star Jades with seven.** From six constellations, Starshatter's start sets her jades at 7, which her strikes add none to, and a charged attack fires all seven at gcsim's earlier hitmark for them. The wiki's notes have the seven expire over a time no source measures, and gcsim keeps them, so they stand until fired or until she leaves the field.

## Provisional constants

- **Ningguang's plunges are provisional.** Her plunges take the index's no-file rule. The plunges' gauges and poise are the wiki's Sparkling Scatter's: Geo at 1U with 50 and 100 poise, and the collision Geo at 0U with 5.

## Not built

- **Strategic Reserve, the screen's endurance and Exquisite be the Jade, Outshining All Beneath are not built.** Strategic Reserve's 12% Geo DMG Bonus goes to a character entering the screen's box, whose size no source measures, and gcsim gives it on a dash while a screen stands, which no field's tick reads. The screen's endurance, the skill group's 50.1% of her Max HP, and its blocking of projectiles have nothing to act on, as enemies here strike the character on the field or a taunt and fire no projectile, so the screen stands its 30 seconds unless shattered. Exquisite be the Jade's 10% Elemental RES for the characters near the screen reaches no strike, as an enemy's is Physical, and gcsim does not model it. The skill's 3 or 4 particles are not built; Majesty Be the Array of Stars and Invincible Be the Jade Screen raise talent levels, which the [talents](../../talents/design.md) page holds, and Trove of Marvelous Treasures is not combat.
