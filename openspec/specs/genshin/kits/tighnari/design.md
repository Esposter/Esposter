# Tighnari

His multipliers are read from proud skill groups 6931, 6932 and 6939 (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/kit/characters/tighnariKit.ts`, `~/Esposter.noindex/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ProudSkillExcelConfigData.json`); Vijnana-Phala Mine's cooldown and Tanglevine Shaft's cooldown and energy cost are read from the same groups. Not yet built: Vijnana Suffusion's Wreath Arrow, which turns the fully charged shot into a shot of its own while the suffusion lasts, since the kit offers no charged variant yet (gcsim's `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/tighnari/aimed.go`, lines 46-53); its Ascension 1 passive (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/tighnari/asc.go`, lines 11-24); and Constellations 2 and 6 (Constellation 2 at `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/tighnari/cons.go`, lines 28-47, and Constellation 6 at `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/tighnari/aimed.go`, lines 126-128 and 193-216). ## Decisions

- **Each action ends at its gcsim cancel, and its arrows land on their own.** Tanglevine Shaft's six hits and its secondary's six land from 112 to 175 frames, past the 118-frame animation; the strikes' arrows land past their cancels, and the fully charged arrow at 96 frames, past its 94 (gcsim's strike frames at `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/tighnari/attack.go`, lines 24-27 and 47-61, the charged shot's at `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/tighnari/aimed.go`, lines 31 and 84-95, and the Shaft's at `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/tighnari/burst.go`, lines 17-18 and 49-56). Each is the action's own hit, queued past its end as gcsim queues it, so a later action or a swap does not stop it.
- **Vijnana-Phala Mine's area is a circle of radius 6 round the primary target** (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/tighnari/skill.go`, line 48), priced as the skill's targeting reach plus the circle, as the Celestial Shower's field is.
- **Tighnari's strikes take the wiki's 16.5 and 15.9 for the first two, 9.75 for each of the third's two hits, and 25.5 for the fourth, his fully charged shot takes the wiki's 20, Vijnana-Phala Mine the wiki's 50, and Tanglevine Shaft's six hits and its secondary's six the wiki's 30 each.** gcsim sets no poise on the arrows, the mine or the Shaft (no poise line in the character's folder), so the record takes the wiki's row for each, the secondary's from its own row, Secondary Tanglevine Shaft DMG, which gives 30 as well (the Advanced Properties of the cached `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/genshin-wiki/Category_Tighnari_Talents.json` pages Khanda Barrier-Buster, Vijnana-Phala Mine and Fashioner's Tanglevine Shaft).
- **Constellation 1's CRIT Rate reads the Charged Attack tag.** It raises every strike tagged Charged Attack by 15% (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/tighnari/tighnari.go` line 31 gates it at Constellation 1), as the Ascension 4 damage bonus reads the same tag. gcsim's Constellation 1 (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/tighnari/cons.go`, lines 14-25) raises the Extra tag its charged shot carries (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/tighnari/aimed.go`, line 66).
- **Constellation 4 buffs the team as the burst starts.** gcsim gives every character on the roster 60 Elemental Mastery for 8 seconds as the burst is cast (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/tighnari/cons.go`, lines 52-69; the gate is `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/tighnari/tighnari.go` line 34), and the kit gives every deployed member the same. gcsim raises that to 120 when the burst triggers Burning, Bloom, Lunar-Bloom, Quicken or Spread, refreshing the 8 seconds (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/tighnari/cons.go`, lines 71-100; its TODO at line 50 names four of the five, and its code subscribes to all five, which the kit holds). The kit reads the reaction event's attack tag (`ReactionTriggered` carries the triggering hit's `KitHit.attackTag`) and the burst's reactions, and raises each member's buff from the same source to 120 through `buffKitTeam`, as gcsim's Constellation 4 sets each member's mod to 120 under the same key (`tighnari-c4`, `~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/tighnari/cons.go` lines 62 and 88).
- **The plunge collision's 25 poise is provisional.** The record's 25 stands as published: gcsim has no plunge file for Tighnari, and the wiki's Khanda Barrier-Buster advanced properties give no plunge row, so no source gives it. The roadmap's manual verification has the question.
- **The twelve Shaft hits are a count the record holds as `DiffersFromSource`, which this page does not explain.** gcsim's two loops of six (`~/Esposter.noindex/cache/gcsim-v2.47.2/internal/characters/tighnari/burst.go`, lines 49-56) and the wiki's six Shafts and six secondary waves both give twelve, so the cause stays open until the kit's module names what differs.
