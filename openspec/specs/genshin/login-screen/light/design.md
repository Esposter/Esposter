# Login light

The light on the login scene's stone and haze: the stone is lit as the game's deferred pass lights it, the cloud sea's haze hides the stone under the walkway, and the computer's frames take screen-space occlusion and shadows. Each hour's light is solved over the game's own exports, and the light comes from the sun or the moon the [sky](../sky/design.md) sets.

## How it works

- **The stone is lit as the game's deferred pass lights it.** The game's stone shader only writes its G-buffer, and `Hidden/Internal-DeferredShading` lights it ([game data formats](../../game-data-formats/design.md)). Each family's stone is therefore its albedo, plus a glow along its edges as the program writes it: its material's colour at its strength, by one less the facing ratio raised to its power, drawn only where its length reaches the material's emission range (`createStoneGlowNode`, `packages/genshin-engine/src/nodes/createStoneGlowNode.ts`). The glow's strength is an eighth of the material's for the door and the towers, the share the frame shows (`GLOW_SCALES` in `fitLoginStone`, `scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/fit/fitLoginStone.ts`). The walkway and the bridges draw no glow, their fitted strength being none.
- **The stone's light is the deferred pass's own shading.** `StoneLightingModel` (`packages/genshin-engine/src/models/nodes/StoneLightingModel.ts`) lights the stone with the sun's colour through a toon ramp read at a half plus half the face's facing to the sun, scaled by the sun's visibility there raised to a fifth. The sky's light is second-order spherical harmonics over the world normal. A fitted light fades with height over tens of metres (`STONE_HEIGHT_FALLOFF`, `packages/genshin-engine/src/nodes/constants.ts`), standing in for the light the reflection pass's clustered probes cast stepping up the towers, which the page does not sample. The stone also darkens with height at each hour's rate, up to 20 metres (`computeStoneDarkening`, `packages/genshin-engine/src/nodes/computeStoneDarkening.ts`, `STONE_DARKENING_TOP` in `packages/genshin-engine/src/nodes/constants.ts`); the night darkens at 0.14 a metre, as the game's high stone shows its light falling away.
- **One set of uniforms carries the light to every stone.** Every hour writes its values into one set of shared uniforms (`loginStoneLight`, `packages/genshin-world/src/services/login/scene/loginStoneLight.ts`), read from the `login/stoneLight` record (`packages/genshin-world/src/generated/gameDataLock.json`). The ramp holds the sun's colour, so the sun contributes its direction and its shadow only.
- **The stone's light is solved in scene colour.** Each hour's ramp, harmonics and height fall-off are solved by `genshin:parity calibrate` over the exports' interior pixels, under the frame's occlusion and each hour's haze (`scripts/src/services/genshinParity/commands/calibrateCommand.ts`, `scripts/src/services/genshinParity/witness/readStoneLightReading.ts`). The solve averages texels that do not line up in bins, so the fit is of the stone's light and not of its texel positions ([sky](../sky/design.md)).
- **No outline is drawn.** The game draws no outline round its stone, so the scene draws none (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/Login/Scene/Index.vue`).
- **The stone's specular waits on the highlight.** The pass's highlight is not drawn yet, so the stone's smoothness and specular colour wait on it ([roadmap](../../../../changes/genshin-roadmap/tasks.md)).
- **Each stone material marks itself in the haze.** Every stone material writes a mask beside its colour in the scene pass. Where the mask is set, the haze blends toward the stone light's own haze colours, away from the sun and toward it, at the same opacity and scatter as the cloud sea's (`createHeightFogNode`, `packages/genshin-engine/src/post/createHeightFogNode.ts`). Those colours are solved with each hour's light by `genshin:parity calibrate` and written beside it. The day's comes out dimmer and bluer than the cloud sea's with no glow toward the sun; the dusk's is warmer and redder; the night's is close to the sea's.
- **The cloud sea's haze hides the stone under the walkway.** The haze rises off the cloud sea far under the walkway and thins up past it, each hour at its own density and falloff, solved over that hour's frames at once under a light left free in each part's bins (`genshin:parity calibrate --haze`). The dawn's and the night's haze is a wall that hides all the stone under it, its top about three metres below the walkway at night and six at dawn, and nothing above. Under it the far towers stand darker against their albedo rather than paler, and the stone a few metres under the walkway, where the cloud sea's billows stand in front of it, turns wholly to the haze's colour. The day's and the dusk's settle on no such haze: the dusk's hides no more than about three quarters of its far towers, and the day's thins slowest, with no most opacity, since a most opacity under all scored the phone's door frame worse.
- **The computer's frames take screen-space occlusion, the phone's none.** The game's stone carries an occlusion strength, and its pipeline darkens creases and the feet of walls on screen. The day's, the dusk's and the night's frames are drawn with occlusion reaching 8 metres (`LoginOcclusionRadiusMap`, `packages/genshin-world/src/services/login/scene/LoginOcclusionRadiusMap.ts`, `LOGIN_OCCLUSION_RADIUS` in `packages/genshin-world/src/services/login/scene/constants.ts`), and each of their lights is solved under it through the witness's occlusion target. The dawn draws none: under occlusion it scored worse than without, and a reach of 4 metres scored the dawn and the day worse again. The phone's client draws no occlusion either: the screen takes the client's `qualityTier`, and the phone's frame is drawn at the medium tier, which draws none (`packages/genshin-engine/src/renderer/QualityTierSettingsMap.ts`).
- **The moon and the sun cast shadows across the walkway.** Their shadows fall from a shadow camera over the walkway ahead of the camera (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/Login/Scene/Index.vue`). The night's moon reads its shadows through a deeper bias, at 0.8 strength and filtered over two texels (`LoginShadowBiasMap`, `packages/genshin-world/src/services/login/scene/LoginShadowBiasMap.ts`; `LoginShadowIntensityMap`, `packages/genshin-world/src/services/login/scene/LoginShadowIntensityMap.ts`; `LoginShadowRadiusMap`, `packages/genshin-world/src/services/login/scene/LoginShadowRadiusMap.ts`; `LOGIN_NIGHT_SHADOW_INTENSITY` and `LOGIN_NIGHT_SHADOW_RADIUS` in `packages/genshin-world/src/services/login/scene/constants.ts`). At the light's own bias the walkway's carved relief casts speckled shadow edges the session's reference does not show; these settings bring the scene's edge count near the reference's, with the moon's direction unchanged (`genshin:parity shadows login-door-session`).
- **The door casts no shadow.** See [the door](../door/design.md).
- **The post chain has no god rays.** Lit in the light's colour, god rays washed the whole sky. Blanked to black, they darkened the sky and every part behind lit air. Each hour's light and sky were measured without them, so the chain has none (`packages/genshin-engine/src/post/createPostPipeline.ts`).
