# Shops

The shops whose goods are built (`packages/genshin-world/src/generated/gameDataLock.json`, its `shops/` keys) are Paimon's Bargains, which sells the two Fates for the Masterless currencies and rotates its characters and weapons, the Mondstadt grocery, which sells general goods for Mora, the Good Hunter's food shop, the Mondstadt vendors, the Fishing Associations, the Realm Depot, the Souvenir Shops, and the Card Shop, which is priced in Lucky Coins. Their goods are the game's own rows in the shop table, published as one record per shop to the shops dataset of the [hosted game data](../hosted-game-data/design.md) (`scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/shops/buildShopGoodSlice.ts`). The rule over them is built: a good is offered from its Adventure Rank within its dates, a buy limit is counted from the purchases made since its refresh, and a purchase takes the price from the wallet and gives the item's currency back (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/shop/buyShopGood.ts`). The Shop and Paimon's Bargains screens buy through `buyShopGoodCount` (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/shop/buyShopGoodCount.ts`).

## How it works

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  OFFER["A good of a built shop"] --> RANK{"Adventure Rank at its rank, within its dates?"}
  RANK -->|no| REFUSE["Refused"]
  RANK -->|yes| LIMIT{"Under its buy limit since its refresh? A limit of zero is none"}
  LIMIT -->|no| REFUSE
  LIMIT -->|yes| PRICE{"The wallet and bag hold every cost of it?"}
  PRICE -->|no| REFUSE
  PRICE -->|yes| BUY["Costs taken, the good's item given, the purchase kept with its moment"]
```

## Decisions

- **Paimon's Bargains' goods are those filed under 1001.** Its shop row, 102, is the Paimon shop and refreshes monthly, but holds no goods (`{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ShopExcelConfigData.json`, `{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ShopGoodsExcelConfigData.json`); the goods filed under 1001 are the table's monthly goods, Fates among them (`scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/shops/checkIsPaimonsBargainsFateGood.ts`), bar the one filed under shop type 1031 (goodsId 316006, `{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ShopGoodsExcelConfigData.json`).
- **The Mondstadt grocery is read as shop 1004.** The table numbers its shops, and no dump file names those numbers. The number is read from the table's order: shop 1002 takes item 305 and 1009 takes 307, the Sigils of Mondstadt's and Liyue's Souvenir Shops, so the numbering runs city by city and 1004 is Mondstadt's grocery (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/shop/constants.ts`, which maps 1004 to `shops/mondstadtGeneralGoods`). The table types shop 1004 as an activity shop, which the reading does not settle (see Open). Liyue's grocery, 1008, sells the same three items at the same Mora prices, which is the cross-check (`{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ShopGoodsExcelConfigData.json`: shops 1004 and 1008 both file items 100075, 100076 and 100077). The wiki's Blanche (Mondstadt) page (`{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Page_Blanche_(Mondstadt).json` lines 25 to 32) lists the same eight goods at the table's prices, Salt 60 to Wheat 100, with a stock of 100 each a day, which is the table's buy limit.
- **A good priced in Mora is priced at the table's Mora cost.** The grocery's goods carry their price in the table's Mora field rather than as an item, so the price is the Mora cost, taken as Mora's item id, `MORA_ITEM_ID`, which the wallet holds as Mora (`scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/shops/constants.ts`, `MORA_ITEM_ID`; `scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/shops/getShopGoodCosts.ts`).
- **A buy limit of zero is no limit.** The table gives the Starglitter-priced Fates no buyLimit field (rows 102003 and 102004 of `{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ShopGoodsExcelConfigData.json`), which reads as zero, and the Stardust-priced ones a buyLimit of five a month (rows 102501 and 102502).
- **Each daily refresh follows the [reset clock](../reset-clock/design.md).** The monthly refresh is the first of the month (`MONTHLY_RESET_DAY_OF_MONTH`) at that same reset, so a purchase made before the reset on the first belongs to the month before (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/time/constants.ts`, `GAME_DAY_START_HOUR` and `GAME_TIME_ZONE`; `packages/genshin-world/src/services/shop/computeShopRefreshTime.ts`). A good that never refreshes counts its purchases from the epoch (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/shop/constants.ts`, `SHOP_NEVER_REFRESHED_AT`).
- **Starglitter is item 221 and Stardust item 222, the Fates 223 and 224 (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/shop/ShopItemCurrencyMap.ts`; `{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/MaterialExcelConfigData.json` names 221 to 224 as Masterless Starglitter, Masterless Stardust, Intertwined Fate and Acquaint Fate).** The table's Fates are bought at five of 221 with no limit and at seventy-five of 222 with five a month (rows 102003, 102004, 102501 and 102502 of that dump; every shop-1001 row reads a "refreshParam" of 1), which matches the proposal's Starglitter for Fates and Stardust for Fates with monthly limits; the pairing of the names to the ids awaits a recording's check ([References sought](../../../changes/genshin-roadmap/tasks.md#references-sought)).
- **The Primogem Fates are the inventory's.** A Fate bought for 160 Primogems is not in the slice (`scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/shops/checkIsPaimonsBargainsFateGood.ts`, which takes no Primogems; `{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ShopGoodsExcelConfigData.json` files such Fates), since selling for Primogems is the inventory proposal's.
- **The grocery's goods wait on the bag (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/shop/buyShopGood.ts`).** They give ingredients the wallet does not hold, so the rule refuses them until the bag holds those items; the rule itself needs no change then.
- **The rotation's characters and weapons are sold by Paimon's Bargains' screen, through their own purchase rules (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/shop/buyPaimonsBargainsCharacter.ts`, `packages/genshin-world/src/services/shop/buyPaimonsBargainsWeapon.ts`).** Their goods pay in the same currencies as the Fates. The rotation's materials wait on the bag's definitions, since the items they give need them, and their item ids are open (see Open).
- **Paimon's Bargains draws the grid of the reference, not its tabs or art.** The screen mounts the shared shop screen (`packages/genshin-interface/src/components/ShopScreen/Index.vue`) and restyles its goods with `:deep` overrides (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/PaimonsBargains/Screen/Index.vue`): four cards of 354 by 335 from 206 across and 128 down, a name over its limit line, and a price band of 57. The Fates take the gold tone the cell carries (`packages/genshin-interface/src/models/ShopGoodCell.ts`, `tone`), the other cards the plain purple. The measures are read off the reference's own pixels (`"paimons-bargains-screen"`, `scripts/src/services/genshinParity/shared/ParityReferenceMap.ts`). The header stays the current build's title, since the current build draws no tab row.
- **Each good is written with its game-time dates at the game's offset (`scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/shops/toShopGoodRow.ts`).** The table's dates carry no zone, and the game's server is in `GAME_TIME_ZONE`, so each is written with that offset and read as an instant.
- **A shop with no tabs is drawn as the General Goods list (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/Shop/Screen/List/Index.vue`).** Its goods run one under another down the left, the shown good's detail stands beside them with a plain tile for its picture and a strip for the Owned line, the balances sit across the head, and the item-refresh line and the Purchase button sit at the foot. A shop with tabs keeps the grid of the shared `ShopScreen` (`packages/genshin-interface/src/components/ShopScreen/Index.vue`), which Paimon's Bargains draws too. The detail shows the first good until one is picked, and the Purchase button stays dimmed until then, since a purchase counts the picked good alone. The words are the game's keyed ones only: the Shop title, the Left Today line and the Refreshes in line, with the Mora balance drawn as a coin.

## Sources

- [AnimeGameData](https://github.com/DimbreathBot/AnimeGameData), the community's per-patch dump: `ShopGoodsExcelConfigData`, the goods, their prices, limits, refreshes, ranks and dates, and "ShopExcelConfigData", the shops and their refreshes. The table's refresh kinds are none, daily, weekly and monthly.
- "RefreshPolicyExcelConfigData" row 101000 of the dump ([Original Resin](../original-resin/design.md) cites its file), which holds 14400 seconds: the daily refresh's row, grounding the requirement "Refreshes happen at four in the morning game time".
- `{data-root}/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/ExcelBinOutput/ShopExcelConfigData.json`, shop 102: the shop whose monthly refresh falls on the first of the month. It grounds that day but names no hour, so the monthly hour is unsourced (see the spec's Open), and the requirement "Refreshes happen at four in the morning game time" rests on the daily row alone for its hour.
- [Blanche](<https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Blanche_(Mondstadt)>), Genshin Impact Wiki, cached at `{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Page_Blanche_(Mondstadt).json`, lines 25 to 32: eight cooking ingredients at 60, 80, 80, 100, 120, 120, 120 and 100, the prices that the shop 1004 goods hold as their `costScoin` (`{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ShopGoodsExcelConfigData.json` lines 716 to 926), which `scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/shops/getShopGoodCosts.ts` line 8 takes as the Mora cost.

## Open

- [ ] **Shop 1004 is the Mondstadt grocery, which the wiki's Blanche page confirms** — contradicted by `{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ShopExcelConfigData.json`: types shop 1004 as SHOP_TYPE_ACTIVITY_DRAGON_SPINE.
- [ ] **The rotation's other fifteen item ids name no goods row in the dump** — goods 102027 to 102033 of shop 1001 (`{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ShopGoodsExcelConfigData.json` lines 39337 to 39535) name seven of the rotation's twenty-two item ids (1020, 1021, 11404, 12404, 13404, 14404 and 15404, `{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ShopRotateExcelConfigData.json` line 5), and no goods row names the fifteen that remain (1006, 1014, 1015, 1023, 1024, 1025, 1027, 1031, 1032, 1034, 11408, 12408, 13408, 14408 and 15408). The rotation is published all the same, as `shops/paimonsBargainsRotation` in `packages/genshin-world/src/generated/gameDataLock.json` (line 400), built by `scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/shops/buildPaimonsBargainsRotation.ts`.
- [ ] **Each refresh is at four in the morning in UTC+8** — the hour is read off `{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Page_Reset.json` line 2 (04:00 on the server's time zone) and the daily row 101000 of `{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/RefreshPolicyExcelConfigData.json` (14400 seconds); no source gives the zone's UTC offset, so a source that names it would settle it.
- [ ] **The General Goods' own words have no text key** — the English text map (`packages/genshin-text/src/generated/text/English.json`) holds no key for "General Goods", "Purchase up to", "Owned", "Source", "Sold at general goods shops", "Cooking Ingredient" or "Item refreshes in", so the list draws the keyed Shop title, the Left Today line in place of the Purchase up to line, and the Refreshes in line, and leaves the rest undrawn until the game's text ids are found (the reference is `{data-root}/genshin-parity/references/shop-screen.png`).
- [ ] **The rows and the detail draw plain tiles, not the goods' pictures** — the item icons and the Salt picture are not extracted, so the tiles wait on the goods' icon asset in the [roadmap](../../../changes/genshin-roadmap/tasks.md).
- [ ] **The row's quantity badge and the item's description are not drawn** — the goods carry their count (`ShopGood.itemCount`, one for Salt), and the detail's description needs the item's text, but `ShopGoodCell` and `ItemDefinition` carry neither yet (`packages/genshin-world/src/models/shop/ShopGood.ts`, `packages/genshin-world/src/services/inventory/getItemDefinition.ts`).
