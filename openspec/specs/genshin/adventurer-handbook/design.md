# Adventurer Handbook

The game's Adventurer Handbook tracks a player's progress: the experience chapters that reward Adventure Rank tasks, the day's commissions, the domains open, the enemies and where they spawn, the quests it recommends, and the party's elemental lineup. In the world it is F1's screen, opened through the screens' `ScreenKind.AdventurerHandbook`.

## How it works

`HandbookScreen` draws the book with its tabs down the left edge, top to bottom as the English client draws them: Experience, Commissions, Domains, Enemies, Guide and Embattle `{data-root}/genshin-parity/references/handbook-experience.png`. Each tab is named with the game's own word for it `{data-root}/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/TextMap/TextMap_MediumEN.json`. The book opens at Experience: the chapter's checks and its navigator across the head, the investigation panel on the left with its ring, its bar of one segment per investigation, its rewards and their claimed footer, and the chapter's commissions down the right, each with its title, its progress, its rewards and its tick `{data-root}/genshin-parity/references/handbook-experience.png`. A reward is the interface's `ItemTile`, its rarity's tile with its count on the strip across its foot `{data-root}/genshin-parity/references/handbook-experience.png`. The world keeps no investigations yet, and it has no completion event to record one by. No investigation spot is placed or spawned, since the spots are blocked on the PC's scene-group export (`openspec/changes/genshin-gathering/tasks.md`, task 2.1), so nothing is investigated and nothing calls `recordInvestigation` (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/investigation/recordInvestigation.ts`, exported and tested with no caller). What is built is the day's count and cap beneath it ([investigation](../investigation/design.md)); what is missing is the spot and its investigate action, which gives the spot's rewards once (`openspec/changes/genshin-gathering/specs/genshin/gathering/spec.md`, "Investigating a spot gives its rewards"). Until then the Experience page opens on an empty panel with no rewards and no commission rows, and the close button returns to the world.

## Parity

The book is scored over what the page draws against `handbook-experience`, the wiki's screenshot of the English client's Experience tab at 3130 by 1730 `{data-root}/genshin-parity/references/handbook-experience.png`, with every box measured off it in its own pixels. Its mean difference, 3.47% (4.17% over the whole frame before its mask), is over the default approval bar (`scripts/src/services/genshinParity/reference/constants.ts` line 19 names it, and the `handbook-experience` row of `scripts/src/services/genshinParity/shared/ParityReferenceMap.snapshot.md` line 43), and its entry at `scripts/src/services/genshinParity/shared/ParityReferenceMap.ts` line 461 records no approval of its own, so the score is a report and not a pass; the rows, tiles and colours were measured off it. The quill over the cover's right edge is an outline traced in percentages of its box and filled with its measured colours, and the page stacks above the pages are measured fills. The backdrop behind the book is the blurred world: the screen draws it as a gradient of its measured colours and does not blur it. The mask leaves out the game's art, which cannot ship: the commission rows' art zones, the item icons on the reward tiles, the quill and the cover's corners. What is left is art that sits over pieces the page draws, which a rectangle cannot leave out (the ring's ornament over its disc, the tabs' emblems behind their labels), and the cover's page edges. After the art comes the face: Signika stands in for the game's face, which the comment at `packages/genshin-world/src/styles/handbookCards.css` line 40 describes only as wider than Signika, so it is set at the game's cap height and letter-spaced to its widths; then the current chapter's ornate check, not drawn. A commission title of two lines leaves its progress pill 7.5 low against the game's, which rises 22 for a second line (`.commission-progress` in `packages/genshin-world/src/styles/handbookCards.css`, which sits 13.5 under its title's foot).

## Notes

- **The Experience page is measured; the other tabs' pages are not.** The rows are cards 242 tall (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/Handbook/Screen/Index.vue:620`) at the pitch `COMMISSION_STEP` declares (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/handbook/constants.ts:4`), and the tabs' labels shrink to the tab as the game's do, by an estimate from the label's length that holds for five of the English client's six (`Index.vue:350-351`), with Commissions set to 41 by hand (`Index.vue:351` and `:365`). The Experience tab is drawn open at its full width with the game's green edge; the open look of another tab once clicked is the interaction states' work and not drawn.
- **Each tab's pages come with their feature.** The commissions with the daily commissions, the domains and enemies with combat and the enemies' spawns, the guide with the quests it recommends, and the experience and embattle chapters with Adventure Rank and character ascension.
