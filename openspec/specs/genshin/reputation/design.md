# Reputation

Each nation keeps a Reputation, raised by the work done for its people. Mondstadt's is built as data and rules: its levels from the game's table, the reward each level, request and bounty pays, the unlocks a level opens, and the weekly limit on claiming them (`scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/reputation/buildMondstadtReputation.ts`, `packages/genshin-world/src/services/reputation/readMondstadtReputation.ts`). Its keeper, its bounties and the exploration that raises its EXP call these rules from the world, and a bounty's trail is not built.

## How it works

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  CLAIM["A bounty claimed, or a request offered"] --> WEEK{"Under the week's limit of its kind, across nations?"}
  WEEK -->|"no"| SHUT["Not claimable or offered until the weekly reset"]
  WEEK -->|"yes"| PAY["The nation's Reputation EXP and items paid"]
  PAY --> CARRY{"EXP past the level's requirement?"}
  CARRY -->|"yes"| UP["Level up: the new level's reward and unlocks"]
  CARRY -->|"no"| HOLD["Held at the level"]
  UP --> LAST{"The nation's last level?"}
  LAST -->|"yes"| NONE["No more EXP earned there"]
  LAST -->|"no"| CARRY
```

## Data

- **The levels.** `ReputationLevelExcelConfigData`'s rows for the nation, each with `nextLevelExp`, the EXP to leave that level, unlike Friendship's totals (`{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ReputationLevelExcelConfigData.json`, city 1 rows 1 to 8). The table gives the last level no `nextLevelExp` (level 8's row, `{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ReputationLevelExcelConfigData.json` lines 93 to 99), so the reader takes the absence as zero and the last level earns no more EXP (`addReputationExp` in `packages/genshin-world/src/services/reputation/addReputationExp.ts`). The wiki's Reputation page says the same of a city's maximum level (`{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Page_Reputation.json` line 1). Level 2 opens the mining outcrop search and level 4 the merchant discounts, the function ids the table names (`{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ReputationLevelExcelConfigData.json`, `functionId` 1001 on level 2 and 1002 on level 4; `{data-root}/genshin-parity/references/reputation-screen/18s.png` shows both unlock lines at Lv. 2 and Lv. 4).
- **Each reward.** A level's `rewardId`, and a request's or bounty's, read from the reward table (`{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/RewardExcelConfigData.json`, reward 2010001 for level 1, and reward 2010002 for level 2, which pays item 221001, "Instructions: Anemoculus Resonance Stone": line 151037 of that file, `{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/MaterialExcelConfigData.json` line 280490, `{data-root}/genshin-dump-backup-6.6/TextMap/TextMap_MediumEN.json` line 278905). The nation's Reputation EXP is the item `ReputationCityExcelConfigData` names as its `virtualItemId`, so the reward is split into the EXP that item's count gives and the items it also pays (`{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ReputationCityExcelConfigData.json`, `virtualItemId` 315 for city 1, which `MaterialExcelConfigData.json` names Mondstadt Reputation EXP; `scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/reputation/toReputationReward.ts`). A request pays its EXP and Mora, a bounty its EXP and Mora, more of each at the harder difficulties (`{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/RewardExcelConfigData.json`: reward 2010110 for a request, 80 of item 315 and 20000 of item 202, which `MaterialExcelConfigData.json` names Mora; bounty rewards 2010101 and 2010102, 120 and 160 of 315, 20000 and 25000 of 202).
- **Requests, Reputation quests and bounties.** The requests of the groups the levels name, each with its weight (`{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ReputationRequestExcelConfigData.json`, request 1001 with weight 20); the Reputation quests of `ReputationQuestExcelConfigData`, each bound to the quest its `parentQuestId` names, which pays its reward once that quest finishes (`{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ReputationQuestExcelConfigData.json`, city 1 row with `parentQuestId` 311); and the nation's `HuntingRefreshExcelConfigData` bounties with the region each is hunted in (`{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/HuntingRefreshExcelConfigData.json`, city 1 rows with `regionId` 101, whose `finishRewardId` is 2010101 for easy, 2010102 for medium and 2010103 for hard). A bounty is offered ahead of a request, and a request waits while a taken bounty is still hunted, offered once the bounty is claimed (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/Reputation/Screen/Index.vue`).
- **The exploration thresholds.** The exploration table's rows for the nation, each at a percentage of its exploration and paying a reward split the same way (`{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/ExcelBinOutput/ReputationExploreExcelConfigData.json`, city 1 at 20, 40 and 60 percent). The thresholds reached between two percentages are the ones the progress passes, so one already held pays nothing again (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/reputation/computeReputationExploresReached.ts`).
- **The tables the dump lacks.** The dump holds the exploration table but not the level, city, function, request, quest or hunting tables. Those are fetched from the AnimeGameData repository into the dump's `ExcelBinOutput` and never committed.

## Notes

- **The weekly limit is `REPUTATION_WEEKLY_CLAIM_LIMIT` of each kind, across nations.** (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/reputation/constants.ts`) Bounties and requests are counted apart, and each kind's claims are counted from the weekly reset that the [reset clock](../reset-clock/design.md) defines, Monday (`WEEKLY_RESET_DAY_OF_WEEK`) at `GAME_DAY_START_HOUR` on the server's clock (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/time/computeLastWeeklyReset.ts`), a claim at the reset counting toward the new week. The count is the weekly helper the bosses also use, in `packages/genshin-world/src/services/weekly` (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/weekly/countWeeklyClaims.ts`). The keeper offers no request past the limit until the reset (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/reputation/offerReputationRequest.ts`). A request taken before the limit pays when it finishes, past the limit too, and its claim is counted; the week's claims are kept to the newest `REPUTATION_WEEKLY_CLAIM_LIMIT`, which is all the limit reads. The wiki gives the same three a week for each kind, the bounties shared between nations (`{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Page_Reputation.json` line 1), and its weekly reset is Monday at 04:00 (`{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Page_Reset.json` line 1).
- **A discount is `REPUTATION_DISCOUNT_PERCENT`% off, rounded down to a multiple of `REPUTATION_DISCOUNT_ROUNDING` Mora.** The rounding is the player's favour, the `REPUTATION_DISCOUNT_ROUNDING` step beneath the discounted price. The wiki's Reputation page gives the same reading (`{data-root}/cache/genshin-wiki/Page_Reputation.json` line 1), as does the [shops](../../../changes/genshin-shops/design.md) proposal (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/reputation/constants.ts`, `packages/genshin-world/src/services/reputation/computeReputationDiscountedPrice.ts`).
- **The discount's shops are the shop types the grocery and the food shop name.** Which shops a discount covers is named in the function's text. The grocery's shop type 1004 and the Good Hunter's food shop's 1005 are the ones the discount names, and only their goods are written with the level (`toShopGoodSlice`, `scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/shops/toShopGoodSlice.ts`; the shop types in `scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/shops/constants.ts`). The [shops](../shops/design.md) page applies it.
- **The reward page.** The `ReputationScreen` lists each level of the nation as the public clip's reward page does: its level on a pentagon badge under the game's short level word (`LevelShort`, "Lv." in English, the one abbreviated in every language; `{data-root}/genshin-parity/text/TextMap/TextMap_MediumEN.json`, key 2960839764 reads "Lv."), its items in the interface's `ItemTile`, coloured by rarity with their count beneath, the feature it unlocks in the game's words behind an info mark (`ReputationFunctionGameTextKeyMap`, by the level's function id; `packages/genshin-world/src/services/reputation/ReputationFunctionGameTextKeyMap.ts`), and the tick or the not achieved block at its end. Each slot reads its item's name and count to assistive technology, the items' rarity and names coming from the materials catalogue, which carries every item a level pays (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/Reputation/Screen/Index.vue`). The slots draw no art, since the game's item icons cannot ship. The list scrolls, since its rows are taller than the panel, and the page opens on the level it is given (`packages/genshin-interface/src/components/ReputationScreen/Index.vue`). The picked level's name and requirement, and the words the tick is read aloud with, stay empty in the world: its requirement is worded per item, and no game text names the mark.
- **The keeper is built.** `mondstadt.json` places her as a resident with `keepsReputation`, and `getTalkScreenKind` opens the Reputation screen from her talk. The session renders `packages/genshin-world/src/components/Reputation/Screen/Index.vue` for it, with the request she offers drawn as the screen opens (`packages/genshin-world/src/data/regions/mondstadt.json`, `packages/genshin-world/src/services/dialogue/getTalkScreenKind.ts`, `packages/genshin-world/src/components/World/Session/Index.vue`).
- **Not built.** The camp requests run as world quests, a bounty's trail and its spawned target, the discount at the food shop, the reward page's sources tabs, and Natlan's tribes and supply notices. The [proposal](../../../changes/genshin-reputation/design.md) keeps them.
- **The reward page's list is measured off its frame; its head and left column are provisional.** The rows, the badge, the slots, the unlock lines and the row's end are read off `{data-root}/genshin-parity/references/reputation-screen/18s.png`, the 18-second frame of the public reward-guide clip (`{data-root}/genshin-parity/captures/yt-1SqmdXbIhLs-reputation-guide.mp4`). The frame shows only the list, so the title, the way back and the picked level's reward stand as estimates. The list's remaining gap is the item icons' art, which cannot ship, and the face, Signika standing in for the game's HYWenHei, as the [open list](#open) records.
