# Sound effects

The login's door sounds as it opens, and so does ours (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/Login/Music/Index.vue`). The game's own sounds stay on the machine that measures them; each sound's levels and the engine's noise that plays them are what ships (the hosted record, `{cache-root}/content/5523c4b4cee27020c460a2a08a930025fe6b69c05e10ffada78f373da3b8df97`, holds only the door's frame time and, each frame, the left, right and shared levels of 24 bands).

## How it works

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  R["A recording that plays the sound"] --> W["The window it sounds in, over the music before it"]
  P["Minimum.pck"] --> D["Every sound of its banks and its streams, decoded"]
  D --> M["Each scored against the window by its octave bands' levels"]
  W -->|"genshin:assets sounds"| M
  M --> S["The best set of each size, with its residual"]
  S -->|"fit: fitSoundEffect"| L["The login/sounds record: three noises a band every 25 ms"]
  L -->|"computeSoundEffectSamples"| N["Our noise in two channels"]
  N --> A["Web Audio, a measured delay after the click"]
  N -->|"genshin:parity passes --pass Audio"| G["Both channels against the game's own sounds"]
  D --> G
```

- **A sound is found by the recording that plays it.** A sound has no name, only an id, so `genshin:assets sounds` finds it by matching: every sound of the packages it is given, `Minimum.pck` by default ([game data formats](../game-data-formats/design.md)), whose bank holds the login's own sounds, is scored against the recording's window by the cosine of its octave bands' levels from 1 kHz up (`scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/sound/findSoundStart.ts`, with the default `--lowest` of 1000 hertz in `scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/commands/soundsCommand.ts`), over the matcher's own octave bands, which run from 31.5 hertz (`SOUND_MATCH_BAND_CENTRES`: the music's 63 hertz band, and one below it that reads only a frame's first 23-hertz bin, so a tone splits between the two, evenly near 35 hertz; a pure tone's share of its power in the 31.5 hertz band is 35.5 percent at 40 hertz, 21.2 at 45 and 1.2 at 60, by `computeSoundBandPowers` over a second of it, so a 40-hertz rumble puts about a third of its power there, not none), at every start, over the level of what plays in the quarter second before the window. The best five are then played together at their stored levels in every set of them, each start refined a frame at a time, and the best set of each size is printed with what it leaves of the window unexplained: each band's level against the set's, in decibels, over the cells within 30 decibels of the window's loudest, after one gain over every band. The gain is never one a band, since the game's sounds keep their own balance and a gain a band lets one sound stand in for another's octaves: under it the rumble alone reads better than the pair. For the door, the rumble alone leaves 4.19 decibels, the rumble and a broadband rush 75 milliseconds after it 3.31, and any further sound a few hundredths at most, so the door is the pair (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/Login/Music/DoorSound.reference.ts`, its `genshin:assets sounds` investigation). Where each sound sits is the login music's reference (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/Login/Music/Index.reference.ts`), and every search for them its door sound topic (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/Login/Music/DoorSound.reference.ts` beside it).
- **A sound with no pitch is its bands' levels, in both channels.** Neither door sound holds a harmonic series, so each is noise whose level moves band by band. The game's sounds are stereo and wide, their channels correlated about a half, so the fit lays the matched sounds at their offsets in both channels as the game plays them (`readGameSoundEffect`) and reads the mix at each hop of `SOUND_HOP_LENGTH` samples, in a Hann window of `SOUND_FRAME_LENGTH` samples over its own power, so a power is the noise's variance (`scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/sound/computeStereoSoundBandPowers.ts`, with its hop and window at the rate `SOUND_SAMPLE_RATE` sets, in `scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/shared/constants.ts`). Each band's two channels and their cross power become three noises apart: the cross power is what the channels share, and each channel's own is the rest of its power, so each channel's level and how alike the two sound both survive. One channel averaged from the two keeps neither: FFmpeg's mono downmix read the door about three decibels over each channel's own level, and the octave bands it was read in stopped at 11 kilohertz where the game's sound runs to the top of hearing (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/Login/Music/DoorSound.reference.ts`, its investigation of the two sounds in both channels: the channels correlated 0.46, the mono downmix about three decibels over each channel, the sounds run to 20 kilohertz). The bands are `SOUND_EFFECT_BAND_EDGES` (`packages/genshin-engine/src/audio/constants.ts`, where `SOUND_EFFECT_FRAME_LENGTH` is too): a third of an octave each from 178 hertz up, and under that as wide as two of the window's bins, since a narrower band holds no bin of its own.
- **The engine plays its own noise, frame by frame.** `computeSoundEffectSamples` (`packages/genshin-engine/src/audio/computeSoundEffectSamples.ts`) builds each of the three noises from a spectrum flat across each band at its level at that moment, a frame a `HOP_SHARE`-th of the window apart, each turned into samples by one inverse transform and laid over the frames before under a Hann window, whose overlaps sum to a constant; the shared noise is added to each channel's own. A noise of a band each built in one long transform would cost most of a second on the login's thread, where the frames cost a few hundred short transforms. `createSoundEffectBuffer` (`packages/genshin-engine/src/audio/createSoundEffectBuffer.ts`) renders it once into two channels when the login's audio starts. A window reads a band of few bins short of what the noise plays in it, so the fit renders its levels once, reads the render back as it read the game's sound, and scales each band by the game's power over the render's; the render is the same every time, so what is scaled is what plays. The levels keep five decimals down to a hundred decibels under the loudest sample they hold, so the tail dies away as the game's does (`scripts/src/services/genshinAssets/fit/fitSoundEffect.ts`, `LEVEL_DECIMALS`).
- **Scored against the game's own sounds, in the audio pass.** `genshin:parity passes login --pass Audio` renders each effect `DerivedAssetSoundEffectMap` names, the one map of the matched sounds that `fit` reads too, and reads both channels against the game's matched sounds in the effect's own bands on hops of `SOUND_SCORE_HOP_LENGTH` samples, finer than the fit's frames, over the cells within `SOUND_SCORE_RANGE_DECIBELS` of the game's loudest (`scripts/src/services/genshinParity/sound/constants.ts`). The door sits about two decibels off, every band's bias within about a decibel, its channels correlated as the game's are (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/Login/Music/DoorSound.reference.ts`, 2.18 decibels off at the mix volumes, channels correlated 0.58 against 0.53). The pass gates it at how far a second take of the same levels, its noise seeded apart (`computeSoundEffectSamples`'s seed), sits from the first (`scripts/src/services/genshinParity/passes/measureAudio.ts`, its `GRAIN_SEED`), since what is left under that is the noise's own grain, and no finer band or frame closes it: only the game's own samples would, which never ship. When the sound starts is no reading of the pass: its delay is read once off the recording's frames into its constant, with nothing solved over it (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/login/constants.ts`, `LOGIN_DOOR_SOUND_DELAY_MS`, read off the first whitened frame at 14.633 seconds in `packages/genshin-world/src/components/Login/Music/DoorSound.reference.ts`).
- **It sounds when the game's does.** The click sets off both sounds: the login's music component owns the one audio context (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/Login/Music/Index.vue`), and the door's sound starts `LOGIN_DOOR_SOUND_DELAY_MS` after the click, 0.32 seconds (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/login/constants.ts`), the time from the recording's first whitened frame to the rumble's rise, so the rush follows the click by about 0.4 seconds (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/Login/Music/DoorSound.reference.ts`, the click at 14.633 seconds and the rumble from 14.950). The screen hands on to the loading screen a second after the click, when its white is up, and the context stays open until the 1.9-second sound has ended, so that hand-off does not cut the sound short.
- **It plays at the game's mix.** The game does not play its sounds at the level they are stored at: each is laid at the volume its banks give its source through every bus up to the master's (`readGameMixVolumes`), the door's rumble 16 decibels over its source and the rush 9 (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/Login/Music/DoorSound.reference.ts`, its `readGameMixVolumes` investigation). The door then passes a compressor at the effects bus's settings, -9 decibels at 4 to 1 with no attack and a 0.15 second release, its threshold 5 lower since the levels already carry the master's 5 down, and a limiter at the master's -2 decibels, at 20 to 1, the most a browser's compressor takes, where the game's is 50 (`packages/genshin-world/src/services/login/constants.ts`, `LOGIN_EFFECTS_COMPRESSOR_OPTIONS` and `LOGIN_MASTER_LIMITER_OPTIONS`).
- **A heard sound that a full search of the game's audio leaves unmatched is built as a measured layer.** A measured layer is band-limited noise under a measured envelope, and its reference is the measurements and the search's scores it was built from (`packages/genshin-world/src/components/Login/Music/DoorSound.reference.ts`); a sound no search has yet reached stays an open row. The door's forming is the one built so far: the 63 hertz octave band of the engine's own noise (`createNoiseBuffer`, the band the instruments' noise is also given in), at `LOGIN_DOOR_FORMING_LEVEL_DECIBELS`, under the envelope of `LOGIN_DOOR_FORMING_ATTACK_MS`, `LOGIN_DOOR_FORMING_HOLD_MS` and `LOGIN_DOOR_FORMING_RELEASE_MS`, which runs over the recording's layer from 11.0 to 13.9 seconds. No bank sound matches it: all 69,170 sounds of Banks0 to Banks31 score at most 0.798 over 11.0 to 13.6 seconds, against the door pair's 0.91 (`DoorSound.reference.ts`). The music and streamed packages stay unsearched, so the built layer rests on the bank search alone. It is built, and no record ships it, since its levels are the envelope's own. It starts as the door begins to form, a lead before the door rises (`LOGIN_DOOR_FORMING_LEAD_MS`), through the same bus as the door's sound: `getLoginDoorFormingLeftMs` reports the forming once the door's copy is within the walkway's far end plus the distance the glide covers in that lead, with how much of the lead is left at it, and the screen hands it on to the login's music, which anchors the layer's envelope to the rise. The glide holds its pace from the door being due until it rises, so the lead holds whenever the glide has that long to fly after the due; with less left, the layer starts with the due, partway into its envelope at the point a full lead before the rise would have reached, so its hold and release still land at the rise's times; the phase of the walkway's copies at the due decides which case holds. The forming waits on the flight, so a download that holds the flight holds the layer with it.

## Not yet

- **The shared mixer's filters.** Both door sounds pass a low-pass of 20 and a high-pass of 15 on Wwise's scale, whose cutoffs are not published where they can be read, and the compressor's detector is the browser's own; each waits on a recording whose music is the login's own, where the door can be read out from under it.
- **The login's other sounds.** The clicks on its buttons and the wind each wait on a recording that plays them clearly enough to match, and go through the same fit.
