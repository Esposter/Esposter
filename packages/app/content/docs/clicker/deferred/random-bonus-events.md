# Random Bonus Events

Timed clickable bonuses that appear at random intervals and grant a short production or click multiplier — the mechanic that makes players keep the tab focused.

**Why deferred:** Real engagement value, but it needs buff state the game doesn't have (temporary effects with expiry interacting with the effect engine) plus art/sound. [Milestone achievements](/docs/clicker/game-loop-and-saves) have shipped, but the mid-game still has little to spend bursts on while upgrades exist for only 2 of 19 buildings.

**Revisit when:** [all-building upgrades](/docs/clicker/deferred/all-building-upgrades) has shipped, making active sessions rewarding enough to boost.
