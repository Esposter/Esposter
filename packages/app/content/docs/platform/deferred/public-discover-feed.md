# Public discover feed

A public gallery of published resources (dashboards, surveys, webpages) — browse, search, maybe like — turning publishing from link-sharing into an audience surface. The natural long-term direction for a social platform.

## Why deferred

Publishing currently means "reachable by URL", not "listed" — flipping that is a real product decision (owners must opt in to being listed), and the surface needs discovery mechanics (curation, search, abuse handling for public content) that dwarf the explorer work in flight. Building it before any published content circulates would be a feed of nothing.

## Revisit when

Published links are actually being shared ([share to esbabbler](/docs/proposals/platform/share-to-esbabbler) shipping is the measurable start) and there is enough public content that discovery beats direct links. Listing must be opt-in per publication from day one.

## Cheaper interim

Share links directly in esbabbler rooms; OG meta tags already make them presentable.
