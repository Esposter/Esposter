## Open findings

- **`@esposter/db-schema`'s `dayjs` export is unusable by a consumer that needs `.duration`.** `packages/db/src/services/dayjs/index.ts` is a byte-for-byte duplicate of db-schema's, so both packages barrel-export a `dayjs` symbol with its own `duration` extend. It cannot simply be deleted: db-schema's bundled `dist/index.d.ts` inlines the dayjs namespace as a local type, so `declare module "dayjs"` augmentations never reach it and `dayjs.duration` fails to typecheck through the package boundary. Fixing this is a rolldown-dts change (externalising `dayjs`), not a code move.
- **`PostgresJsDatabase<typeof relations>` is spelled out at ~49 sites** across `db`, `db-mock` and `packages/app` with no alias. One `Database` type in `@esposter/db-schema` would carry it, but the sweep is mostly in another agent's tree.
- **`classifyMentions` plus its `getRoleMemberIds` query runs twice per send**, once in `incrementMentionCounts` and once in `getPushSubscriptionsForMessage`, which the callers invoke back to back. Passing an already-computed `ClassifiedMentions` changes both signatures and every app call site.
- **`hasPermission` awaits the owner lookup and `getPermissions` sequentially.** `Promise.all` halves the latency for the common member case at the cost of one query owners currently skip — a trade, not a refactor.
- **`azure-mock` throws `new Error("Method not implemented.")` at ~85 unimplemented-stub sites**, against the `typescript` skill's ban on bare `Error`. Converting them is its own mechanical pass.
- **Table and index names follow no single rule.** `call_sessions`, `resource_accesses`, `resource_favorites`, `storage_blobs` and `resource_publications` are snake_case where 13 other multi-word tables are camelCase, and `users_to_rooms_timeout_until_index` snake-cases a column every other index leaves camelCase. Either migration or a correction to the `drizzle` skill's stated rule — both out of a behaviour-preserving pass.
- **The `LENGTH(col) <= N` CHECK idiom is hand-written at 8 schema sites**, and the numeric `col BETWEEN min AND max` idiom at 5 more in `userSettingsInMessage`, while the trimmed-name variant has `createNameCheckSql`. A `createMaxLengthCheckSql` / `createBetweenCheckSql` pair must emit byte-identical SQL or it forks the migration chain, and proving that needs `db:gen`.
